    en: In the course, most widely used engineering materials will be introduced. Then, metal forming technologies (MFT) for different components are discussed. To obtain deeper understanding on MFT, the satellite main structures are introduced and regarded as forming objects. As main tools in industry 4.0, the tools for components design and process optimization are introduced and trained, including CAD and CAE softwares. Projects about design & manufacture a satellite’s main structure will be completed by the students through using the softwares. This course is taught by Professor Liang Wang from Imperial College London. Professor Wang's team has achieved world-class research in metal forming, particularly in digital manufacturing of metal structures, data science, cloud computing, and efficient forming technologies. They have been awarded the Gold Medal for Outstanding Team at Imperial College London. Through this course, students will gain insights into foreign teaching models, engage with cutting-edge professional knowledge, and be inspired to innovate technologically. Metal Forming and Modelling group (MFG) at Imperial College London, led by Prof. Wang, has world-class reputation on modelling and developing advanced metal forming processes, regarded as one of the world's top leading metal forming groups, as witnessed by the Imperial 2022 President’s Gold Medal for Outstanding Research Team. Prof. Wang’s major research interests include the design and development of advanced metal forming technologies and manufacturing system. His work has made fundamental contributions to the characterization and modelling of materials and interfacial thermo-mechanical and mechanochemical behaviours of engineering materials. Particularly, Prof. Wang’s research has direct impacts on： (1) Digital manufacturing and Data Sciences in metal forming; (2) Innovative warm/hot stamping technologies (FAST) for high strength steels, aluminium and titanium alloys. The innovative FAST technology led to awards by international journals and society. (3) Cloud-FEA technologies for metal forming process design and optimization. (4) Tribology in metal forming. (5) Advanced material characterization of heat transfer coefficients for hot/warm stamping.
