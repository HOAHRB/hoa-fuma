    en: Laser additive manufacturing technology, also known as laser 3D printing technology, is a laser manufacturing technology that processes three-dimensional solid model slices into two-dimensional slices with computer assistance, and the two-dimensional slices are then discretized into one-dimensional lines, the laser is used as the energy source for point-by-point accumulation, and finally the formation of three-dimensional solid parts is realized. Compared with traditional manufacturing technology, this technology has the characteristics of flexibility, no mold, short production cycle, easy to realize intelligence, not limited by part structure and material, and can produce parts with high mechanical properties. The technology has been widely used in aviation, national defense, transportation, energy, metallurgy, mining and other fields, and has shown attractive prospects. It is expected to become a representative technology of the "third industrial revolution". According to its forming principle, laser additive manufacturing technology includes the most representative laser selective melting technology (Selective Laser Melting，SLM) characterized by powder bed spreading and laser metal direct forming technology (Laser Metal Direct Forming，LMDF) characterized by synchronous powder feeding. This course mainly includes the principles and characteristics of laser additive manufacturing technology, laser additive manufacturing equipment, additive manufacturing technology methods, shape control technology, and laser additive manufacturing process quality monitoring and intelligent control technology. Through the study of the course, students will have a detailed understanding of the parts printing method, application characteristics, and application fields of laser additive manufacturing technology, which is currently widely used and has good application prospects, master the printing process and shape control strategy of laser additive manufacturing parts, and can provide solutions for solving practical problems in engineering applications.
