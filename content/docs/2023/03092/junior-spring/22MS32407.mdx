Additive manufacturing has brought new vitality to the manufacturing industry, making it possible to one-time additive manufacturing of diversified small batches of complex and large-scale multi-cavity parts. However, the current mainstream additive manufacturing technology still has certain limitations, such as high manufacturing cost, low forming accuracy, and high defect tendency. Solid phase additive manufacturing technology can effectively avoid defects such as porosity because it has no melting process. It has the characteristics of saving energy, high forming precision, excellent forming performance, high manufacturing efficiency, low production cost, and green environmental protection. Efficient and high-quality manufacturing can be achieved in terms of large structural parts such as arrow body connecting rings and aircraft wing trusses. Solid phase additive manufacturing technology is one of the elective courses in the additive manufacturing engineering specialty of Institutions of Higher Education. The base concept, development history, formation mechanism, microstructure and property regulation, device type and design are taught. The main task is to develop the innovative thinking, analysis ability, scientific research ability and engineering practice ability via classroom teaching, which can support the related index point of graduation requirements. The students are required to comprehend the base theory and knowledge, and use the related knowledge into the complex engineering problems. Meanwhile, the students should possess the all-round abilities in the analysis of solid phase additive manufacturing characteristic, the making of plans and the writing of reports. The rational effective conclusion can be obtained via the information integration, possessing the abilities of preliminary scientific research and technological development.
