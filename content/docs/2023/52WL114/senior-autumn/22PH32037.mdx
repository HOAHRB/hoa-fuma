    en: In recent years, computational materials science is a new quickly developing interdisciplinary science. It is a combination of multi-disciplines, including solid state physics, materials physics and chemistry, theoretical chemistry, math, computer technology, etc. The substance of computational materials science is to simulate the physical and chemical properties of different materials by modern high performance computers, to deeply understand different phenomena and characters for the materials in the size ranged from atomic to macroscopic scale, to theoretically predict the material structure and physical properties, and after that to design the new materials. The current course for Fundamentals and practice of computational materials science is very important for the advanced undergraduate students whose majors are physics, material science, chemistry, and electronic engineering. This course will especially introduce the first-principles density function theory, which is an interdisciplinary theory in computational materials science and is exactly adequate for the investigations of materials in the nanoscopic scale. This course will also introduce other interdisciplinary theories applied for the materials in the microscopic, mesoscopic, and macroscopic scale. At the same time, this course will combine the different computational methods with detailed model buildings, practical operations, actual problems, and the examples in the research publications. Such combination will cause the students to be familiar with the design ideas and the computational methods from atomic to macroscopic scale in computational materials science. Then the students will be deeply impressed with the broad applicability of computational materials science in different fields. This course can train the students to correctly extract and reasonably analyze the physical essence in the fields of condensed matter physics and material science. Moreover, this course can help the students to improve their abilities of autonomous exploration and innovation based on their knowledge and the practical issue.
