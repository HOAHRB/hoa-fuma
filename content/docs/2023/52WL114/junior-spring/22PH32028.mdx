It mainly teaches general physical cognitive methods for understanding and describing fluids. Through the study of physical and mathematical description of fluids (Chapter 2), space-time scale analysis and similarity (Chapter 3), waves and instability (Chapter 4), and nonlinear processes in fluids (Chapter 5) and solution methods (Chapter 6), It will enable students to combine the basic physical laws they have learned with strange and diverse physical processes, effectively improve students' ability to understand, explain, use and transform the natural world with physical laws, deepen their understanding of physical and mathematical knowledge and skills, and lay a foundation for gradually developing independent scientific research in the future.
