    en: Astronomical Navigation Technology Practice is a course for undergraduates majoring in "detection guidance and control". This course not only requires students to master the basic principles and methods of astronomical navigation, but also requires students to be active, inspire students to think, solve problems and hands-on ability, so that students ultimately achieve the purpose of applying what they have learned. Practical links should be introduced into classroom teaching, combining theoretical teaching with practical operation. In the practical operation link, students can operate the debugging and testing modules in the course of related projects completed by the research group in recent years, and students can also set relevant parameters for these debugging and testing modules according to their own needs to output the results between modules and the final results. For example, students can adjust the installation error between lens and detector, lens optical distortion and color difference and other parameters of the "Star Sensor Model teaching demonstration" software, observe the output results before and after adjusting parameters of the navigation camera, and students can further understand the error mechanism and sources in visual navigation. Students can use and operate the "star sensor + single star simulator + two-axis turntable" system in the laboratory, so that students have a further understanding of the concept of visual navigation accuracy, and lay the foundation for subsequent exploration of new methods and technologies. Students can make use of the "star sensor experiment" system in the laboratory. The output attitude and image of the star sensor change with the change of the three-dimensional rotating platform, so that students can further understand the three-axis attitude of the carrier (yaw Angle, pitch Angle and rolling Angle), and also cultivate students' ability to think independently and analyze problems. The strengthening of these practical links enables students to master and apply knowledge from specific case experiments, and at the same time inspires students to apply knowledge to solve practical problems and explore new knowledge.
