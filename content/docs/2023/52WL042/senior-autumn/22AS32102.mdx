    en: “Flight Control and Guidance” is a specialized course for engineering students who are major in Control Science and Engineering or Science and Technology in Aeronautics and Astronautics in technical universities. Via lectures and computer experiments, the purpose of this course is to train the students to have the capability of establishing mathematical models to describe the motions of flight vehicles for the design of flight control systems and the capability of designing flight control systems using knowledge on control theories. The typical background of this course is that an endo-atmospheric interceptor missile intercepts a flying target with maneuvers. Based on some missile motion models and target-missile relative motion models, some methods to analyze and design missile guidance and control systems are presented, and some methods to simulate missile guidance and control systems are practiced here. In essence, this course presents the constitution of a missile guidance and control system and the basic phase of designing the system. In order to practice the design of missile control system, the equations to describe missile motions are simplified and linearized. Some missile autopilot design methods such as classical design method in frequency domain and modern design method using Linear Quadratic Regulator are presented. Moreover, detailed analyses of classical guidance laws are provided, such as target pursuit approach，parallel Line-of-sight approach, Proportional Navigation Guidance Law and its modifications. In meanwhile the principle of how target seekers work is introduced. Finally, some modern guidance laws, such as Linear quadratic optimal guidance law, differential games guidance law, sliding-mode guidance law and nonlinear guidance law are presented.
