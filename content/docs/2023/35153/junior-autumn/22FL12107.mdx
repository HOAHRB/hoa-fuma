The course provides students with English grammar, dialogues, and short passages, and it aims to inform students of language and culture knowledge. The students should master fundamental English language competence and foster the skills in listening, speaking, reading, writing and translation, so as to improve their English speaking ability, reading competence, and intercultural communication competence. The contents include the subjunctive mood, the pronoun it, verb-ing form, inversion, the use of punctuation marks, parallel structure, emphasis sentences, and other important sentences and structures. There are dialogues and 400-word texts in each unit. The course evaluation is cumulative, which includes in-class presentation, after-class assignments, and final exam.
