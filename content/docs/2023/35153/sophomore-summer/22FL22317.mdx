    en: Practicum is an important practical step in the undergraduate education for Japanese majors. It aims to develop students’ enthusiasm for work, earnest work ethic, and ability to apply professional knowledge in practice. Internship helps students in testing and reinforcing professional knowledge, thereby laying a solid foundation for them to adapt to future work and become a qualified graduate welcomed by society. The content of internship involves (1) learning about host company’s organization structure and administrative regulations, (2) familiarizing with job duties, operating rules, and office work procedures, and (3) direct participation in work, especially reception work of foreign affairs. In doing so students will master technical terms frequently used in working context, further enhance their competence in writing and translating official document and other paperwork, as well as improve their oral Japanese and interpretation skills. Course grade is cumulative, consisting of Contribution to practice(20%), internship log (30%), feedback from host company (15%), and internship report (35%).
