The teaching purpose of this course is to cultivate students' Japanese listening and speaking skills, improve their Japanese communication skills, and enrich their knowledge of Japanese society and culture. The main teaching content of this course is the listening comprehension and imitation retelling training of the real and simulated listening questions of the New Japanese Language Proficiency Test N2. According to the five types of questions included in the test, the training is carried out separately, 5 types, i.e., "topic comprehension", "key comprehension", "summary comprehension", "instant question and answer" and "comprehensive understanding". Through this course, students will be able to understand the common expressions of various scenes in daily life, master the skills of listening to and understanding various types of conversations. You will be able to understand listening materials that are longer, more informative and more complex. The exam score for this course is cumulative, consisting of classroom performance, homework,and final exam score.
