    zh: 土壤是地球陆地表面上能够生长植物的疏松表层，具有肥力特征，能够不断地供应和协调作物生长发育所必需的的水分、养分、空气、热量和其他生活必须条件。土壤除了具有生产力、能生长植物以外，还具有缓冲自调和净化两大功能。 目前，世界范围的土壤的污染等问题造成了越来越多的经济损失，土壤污染、水土流失、土壤沙化、退化及土壤次生盐碱化，均是生态失衡所带来的恶果。由于土壤介质的非均质各向异性等造成其物质迁移、转化特点，与其他环境子系统如水环境和大气环境相比有很大差异，因此土壤环境问题有较强的隐蔽性和潜伏性，会造成更大的危害。鉴于土壤污染的危害及土地资源的重要性，土壤环境问题及其治理已成为环境、资源、生态等相关领域的重要研究课题。“土壤化学”是针对环境科学与工程专业本科生开设的一门较为基础的土壤学分支课程，教学内容结合我国的土壤资源与环境这个主题进一步阐述土壤化学过程的基本原理，适当地增加国内外土壤化学研究的新进展。 土壤化学性质影响土壤中的化学过程、物理化学过程、生物化学过程以及生物学过程的进行，其中重要的有土壤的酸碱性、缓冲性、氧化还原性质、吸附性、表面电化学性质与胶体性能等。这些性质深刻影响土壤的形成与发育过程，对土壤的保肥能力、缓冲能力、自净能力和养分循环等也有显著影响。 土壤化学作为环境科学与工程的重要组成部分，其研究内容和相关规律、定律等涉及环境化学、生态学、土壤学 、化工原理等相关内容，具有较强的综合性和科学交叉性。本课程主要涵盖的知识内容：土壤中的有机质、黏粒矿物、胶体表面化学、溶液与溶质迁移、酸碱化学、化学动力学、氧化还原、根际化学及污染化学等基础知识。各章节都围绕主题，有重点地介绍基础知识、并结合我国的实际情况进行讨论，提高学生的学术水平与联系实际的创新思维能力，尽可能地结合学科研究和发展的新动态、选好切入点，既反映该领域的新进展、新知识，又体现本门课程教学的循序渐进和系统性。
    en: Soil is the loose top layer of the earth's land surface that can grow plants. It has the characteristics of fertility and continuously supply and coordinate the water, nutrients, air, heat and other living conditions necessary for crop’s growth and development. Soil not only has productivity and can grow plants, but also has two functions of buffer, self-adjustment and purification. At present, the problems of soil pollution all over the world have caused more and more economic losses. Soil pollution, soil erosion, soil desertification, degradation and secondary salinization are all the bad consequences of ecological imbalance. Because of the heterogeneous anisotropy of soil media, the characteristics of material migration and transformation are quite different from other environmental subsystems, such as water environment and atmospheric environment. Furthermore, soil environmental problems have strong concealment and latent nature, which will cause greater harm. In view of the harm of soil pollution and the importance of land resources, soil environmental problems and the treament have become an important research topic in the related fields of environment, resources and ecology. "Soil Chemistry" is a basic branch course of soil science for undergraduates majoring in Environmental Science and Engineering. The teaching content will further elaborate the basic principles of soil chemistry process based on the theme of soil resources and environment in China, and will appropriately add the new progress in soil chemistry research at home and abroad. Soil chemical properties affect the chemical processes, physicochemical processes, biochemical processes and biological processes in the soil. Among them, soil acidity and alkalinity, buffering, redox, adsorption, surface electrochemical properties and colloidal properties are important. These properties have a profound impact on the formation and development of soil, as well as soil fertility retention, buffer capacity, self-purification capacity and nutrient cycling. Soil chemistry is an important part of environmental science and engineering. Its research content and related laws involve environmental chemistry, ecology, soil science, chemical principles and other related contents, and have a strong comprehensive and scientific intersection. This course mainly covers the basic knowledge of soil organic matter, clay minerals, colloid surface chemistry, solution and solute migration, acid-base chemistry, chemical kinetics, redox, rhizosphere chemistry and pollution chemistry. All chapters focus on the theme, introduce especislly basic knowledge and discuss it in light of the actual situation in our country, so as to improve the students'academic level and innovative thinking ability in connection with reality. In addition, this course combines the new trends of subject research and development as far as possible and selects a good entry point, which not only reflects the new progress and new knowledge in this field, but also reflects the gradual and systematic course teaching.
