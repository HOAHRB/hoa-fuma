Much attention has been put onto drafting and perfecting the city construction regulations since the foundation of the People’s Republic of China. Nowadays, a comprehensive construction laws and regulations system has been formed, which guarantees the normalization of the city and country’s construction in China. At present, the planning construction laws and regulations have been listed onto the required contents menu of Registered Architect and Registered Urban Planner qualification test, which are the necessary elementary knowledge in related work. This curriculum embarks from the actual needs, teaches knowledge about current city construction administration and regulations, which are closely related with Urban Planning and Landscape Architecture. It includes the basic program of engineering construction in China, science of urban planning law, urban & rural planning formulation and implementation administration and regulations, and qualification management in urban & rural planning industry. This course aims to help students to familiar with the institutional framework system which urban and rural construction follows, master the main bodies, key points and procedures in urban & rural planning formulation and implementation, familiar with the principles and legal basis in disposing illegal constructions, form the public interest- oriented values, cultivate students to pay close attention to national public policies, preliminarily possess the ability of analyzing and understanding public policies, lay a foundation for future’s registered urban planner exam.
