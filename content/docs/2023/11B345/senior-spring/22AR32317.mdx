    zh: 城市地理学是研究在不同地理环境下，城市形成发展、组合分布和空间结构变化规律的科学。主要研究内容包括城市形成和发展条件、区域城市空间组织研究、城市内部空间组织研究、城市发展的支撑系统研究、城市问题和对策研究等。它既是人文地理学的重要分支，又是城市科学群的重要组成部分。城市地理学与城乡规划学都是以城市为研究对象，但它们的侧重点和研究方向不同。城市地理学研究区域城乡结构的发展演变，从地理的角度研究单个城市的形成发展，研究一定区域范围内的城市体系的结构关系及产生、发展、演变的规律。城市地理学为城市规划提供理论指导，应用于城市规划的实践；城市规划也为城市地理学提供研究课题、研究素材和实践验证，促进城市地理学理论不断充实完善。城市地理学具有理论和实践相结合的特征，一方面，它可以应用于城市规划、国土整治和区域规划等领域，用于分析城市的发展条件、明确城市的性质和分工、制定各类用地布局方案；另一方面，它也具备直接解决实际问题的能力。因此，城市地理学概论能使学生掌握城市发展的基本理论和规律，是城市规划与设计专业的重要主干课程之一。
    en: Urban geography is a science to study the city of the formation and development, portfolio distribution and variation of spatial structure in a different locale. The main contents include the formation and development of the city, the regional urban spatial organization, the inner space organization of the city, the supporting system of the urban development, and the urban problems and countermeasures. It is not only an important branch of human geography, but also an important part of the urban science group. Urban Geography and Urban Planning are both based on cities, but they focus and research in different directions. Urban geography researches the development of urban structure evolution for the single city from a geographical perspective and studies on the law of urban structure, development and evolution in a certain area. Urban geography provides theoretical guidance and practical application for urban planning. And urban planning provides research topics, research material and practice to enrich urban geography theory. Urban Geography has the characteristics of the combination of theory and practice. On the one hand, it can be applied to some fields, such as Urban Planning, Land Improvement and Regional Planning. It is used to analysis urban development conditions, the city nature and labor division, and the formation of various types of land layout program. On the other hand, it also has the ability to direct to solve practical problems. Therefore, Urban Geography Introduction enables students to master the basic theories and laws of urban development, and it is one of the important foundation courses of Urban Planning and Design.
