It is of great significance for city future sustainable development to fully adapt to different regional climatic conditions and carry out urban planning and construction. The course is a professional limited elective course for undergraduate students of urban-rural planning and landscape-gardening. Taking northern winter city as focus, through a variety of teaching methods, students may learn to understand the interaction between cold climate characteristics and urban planning and understand urban planning and construction experiences of some foreign winter cities including those in planning and management policies, in climate protection, in city's image as well as in key technology fields. Students also learn to understand existing problems and development opportunities in winter city planning and construction of China. Then, theories and countermeasures on urban development policy level and planning and design level under cold climate conditions will be provided in the course. In the process of teaching, students are required to integrate pre-learned knowledge and skills. Combined with a variety of teaching links, further study of the impact of climatic conditions on urban planning and construction will be made to enable to understand the interaction between the climatic characteristics and urban planning. In addition, students can use the course as a platform to integrate the urban planning professional knowledge through winter cities.
