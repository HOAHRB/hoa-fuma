    en: The principle of ecological infrastructure planning supported by AI is a cutting-edge interdisciplinary course in the disciplines of landscape architecture and urban and rural planning. By integrating the principles of landscape ecology, sustainable urban planning and artificial intelligence technology, a trinity teaching content system of "theory - technology - ethics" is constructed. The course is oriented towards the strategic demands of national ecological security and smart cities, focusing on the protection of ecological Spaces and intelligent decision support during the process of urbanization. It aims to cultivate students' innovative ability to solve complex ecological planning problems by applying AI technology. In urban ecosystems, due to the high population density, human activities have a decisive influence. Not only have the structures and compositions of natural ecosystems undergone artificial tendency changes, but also a large amount of artificial technological substances in cities have completely altered the nutrient levels and flow change relationships of natural ecosystems. In the process of input, processing and transfer of matter and energy, human factors play a major role. Ecological infrastructure is an important component of the urban ecosystem and is connected to the ecosystem of a larger area, not only forests, mountains and water surfaces, but also the ecological transformation of various artificial facilities, minimizing the impact of human development and construction activities on the natural ecosystem. The course focuses on the planning, construction and spatial control of ecological infrastructure. On the basis of mastering the basic knowledge of ecology, it combines the characteristics of the urban built environment and urban planning regulations and policies to construct urban ecological infrastructure. Through the technical closed-loop training of "data fusion - intelligent analysis - dynamic regulation and control", students will master the interactive development of ArcGIS Pro spatial analysis tools and Python scripts, be proficient in applying deep learning models to interpret multi-source remote sensing data, and construct planning schemes that combine ecological resilience assessment and intelligent decision support. Practical teaching relies on real projects of school-enterprise cooperation, selects typical scenarios such as intelligent recognition of unmanned aerial vehicle images and generative design of industrial heritage sites, and cultivates students' engineering and technical capabilities in developing AI-assisted toolchains.
