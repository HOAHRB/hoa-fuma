    en: Today forging technology refers to the emerging forging technology represented by isothermal precision forming technology. Isothermal precision forming technology refers to the forging of billets and dies under almost constant temperature conditions, and the formed parts reach or approach the shape and size of product parts. It is mainly used for precision forming of complex shaped forgings in industries such as aviation and aerospace. It is a new technology developed on the basis of traditional plastic processing. The purpose of this course is to enable students to master the principles of isothermal precision forming technology, understand the characteristics of isothermal precision forming technology, broaden their professional knowledge, and focus on cultivating students' ability to analyze and solve problems. Mainly teaching the origin and development process of isothermal precision plastic forming technology, the principles, characteristics, equipment and process design of isothermal precision forming technology, typical forging process design, key technologies such as closed forging forming, split flow forming, local loading and floating die forming, and defect control technology of isothermal precision forming. The teaching focus is on typical forging process design, various key technologies in isothermal precision forming, and forming defect control technology. The teaching difficulty is typical forging process design, the principles, application scope, and advantages and disadvantages of various key technologies.
