Topics include but are not limited to people and ethnicity, cultural integration, multiculturalism, the Chinese character, the Chinese mind, ideals of life, gender differences, the Chinese family system, literature, art, architecture, food culture, economy, festivals, folklore, martial art, wedding and ceremonies, history, and philosophy.
