They simply maintain cooperation with the United Nations in the maintenance of peace and security. At the same time, their members are a number of countries in a specific region, which often have certain ties in history, culture, language and other aspects, or have common concerns or common interests, so they have the need to coordinate, cooperate extensively and form permanent organizations in the peaceful settlement of disputes, the maintenance of peace and security in the region, the protection of common interests and the development of economic and cultural relations.
