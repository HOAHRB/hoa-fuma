The procedure of graduation thesis consists of the selection of topics, oral defense of the proposal, mid-term inspection and oral defense of the thesis. It aims to cultivate students’ ability to use basic academic writing skills and professional knowledge, improve their ability to analyze and solve practical problems, and cultivate their creative thinking. Students will acquire the ability to do preliminary academic research. For writing a graduation thesis, students are required to conduct in-depth studies on specific topics in International Relations, Economics, History, Culture, and/or Area Studies.
