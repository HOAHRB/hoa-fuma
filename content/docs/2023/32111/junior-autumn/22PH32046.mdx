    en: 'Lithium niobate (LN), first synthesized 70 years ago, has been widely used in diverse applications in optical and microwave technologies, ranging from communications to quantum optics. LN is a promising material platform for integrated photonics, and some integrated LN optical devices, such as optical modulators and nonlinear wavelength converters, have already outperformed their legacy counterparts implemented in bulk LN crystals. The successes of manufacturing wafer-scale, high-quality thin films of LN-on-insulator (LNOI) and breakthroughs in nanofabrication techniques have made high-performance integrated nanophotonic components possible. LNOI devices boost the performance of LiNbO3-based traditional applications in optical communications and data centers, integrated microwave photonics, and quantum optics and open up novel applications in metrology, including ranging systems and frequency comb spectroscopy. This course introduces the students to integrated lithium niobate photonics. A series of interesting topics will be covered during the course: LN-based Optical waveguides, LN-based Broadband electro-optic modulators, Applications of lithium niobate modulators, LN-based platform of quantum photonics, and Thin-film LN technology. Beyond this outlook, we will discuss the challenges in integrated LN photonics and the potential solutions.'
