Students are required to search and study the literatures, master the design of experimental scheme, preparation and processing of solid samples / devices, and learn structure, spectroscopy and other characterization methods, and test the physical properties of material or device for applications. All students should complete the compulsory projects of synthesis of oxide materials, X-ray diffraction analysis, scanning electron microscope, Raman spectrum and impedance spectroscopy, then students can select an innovative exploratory experimental project, such as fuel cell electrode material performance test, the porous metal treatment, morphology observation and physical measurement, energy conversion and memory device preparation and performance test, pressure-temperature-displacement measurement experiment, high speed photography and video analysis technology experiment, semiconductor photoelectric properties test, sensor comprehensive experiment, and so on.
