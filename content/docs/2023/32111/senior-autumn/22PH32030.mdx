    en: General relativity is a relativistic theory of gravity, which was proposed by Einstein in 1915 and is now a classical field theory describing gravitational interactions. Under the framework of Einstein's general theory of relativity, the gravitational interaction between matter is shown as the curvature of space-time, which can be well described by Riemann geometry. The curvature of space-time is directly determined by the energy and momentum of the surrounding matter (including radiation). At present, general relativity has passed many experimental tests and becomes an important tool to understand the nature of gravity and describe the universe. Starting from the basic principles of general relativity, this course will briefly introduce the basic mathematical tool of general relativity, "Riemann geometry", the motion of matter in the relativistic gravitational field, Einstein's gravitational field equations, gravitational waves and the preliminary physics of black holes.
