    en: This course embraces the principle of “learning from nature,” centering on micro- and nanoscale bionic system technologies. It systematically reviews the field’s evolution, foundational theories, key processes, and representative applications. By delving into micro-/nanoscale bionic surface and interface energy–field effects, the curriculum highlights design strategies such as drag-enhancement and drag-reduction, as well as cutting-edge cases like droplet manipulation, fog-water harvesting, and low-drag materials, equipping students to grasp both frontier developments and practical contexts. Instruction balances rigorous theory with case-driven, inquiry-based learning, encouraging students to employ advanced fabrication methods—ultrafast laser processing and precision 3D printing—to pursue interdisciplinary exploration and innovative designs. Upon completion, students will not only comprehend the full landscape of micro-/nanoscale bionic systems, master core techniques and applications, but also acquire skills in independent literature research and academic report writing, laying a solid foundation for future research and engineering practice.
