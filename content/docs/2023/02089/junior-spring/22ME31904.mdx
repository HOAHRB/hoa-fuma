It covers interdisciplinary knowledge in robotics, bionics, artificial intelligence, mechanical engineering, control science, and related fields. Inspired by the structures, functions, and behaviors of biological organisms in nature, the course focuses on key topics such as bionic mechanism design, motion analysis, mechanical modeling, intelligent actuation, perception and decision-making, and human-robot interaction. By incorporating real-world applications, including hexapod robots, planetary rovers, and soft robotics, students will gain hands-on experience in the design principles and intelligent control methods of bionic robots. Through a combination of theoretical study and practical training, this course aims to cultivate students' interdisciplinary innovative thinking, laying a solid foundation for future research and development in robotics and related fields.
