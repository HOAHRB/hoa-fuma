It is one of the professional elective courses of bionic science and engineering, aiming to cultivate students' research interest and application ability of bionic mechanisms in the fields of deep space detection, on orbit manipulation, etc, It is an indispensable professional course for mastering the forefront of space technology development and engaging in scientific research in space technology. Through the learning of this section, students will be able to understand the technical characteristics of sensors in space biomimetic mechanisms, understand the technical characteristics of control methods in space biomimetic mechanisms, and master the ability to establish sensors and controllers in space biomimetic mechanisms. Finally, through the systematic learning of this course, students can deeply understand and master the classification, effects, simulation methods of space environment, as well as the design, simulation, perception, control and other technologies of space bionic mechanisms, which will lay a theoretical and technical foundation for future work in the design, control, experiment, scientific research and development of space bionic robots.
