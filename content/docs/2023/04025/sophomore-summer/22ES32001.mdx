    en: Nuclear medicine is a discipline that studies the application and theory of nuclear science and technology in medicine. This course clarifies the basic principles of nuclear medicine. The main content is the radionuclide targeted imaging diagnosis of molecular level, which is targeting and sensitive applied in the medical research. With the mature application of the PET/CT, the SPECT/CT and the molecular level targeted therapy of radionuclides, nuclear medicine has already broken through the traditional content and combined with other disciplines to form the molecular imaging technology. It studies and observes the molecules leading to the occurrence of diseases from the molecular level and shows the pathophysiological changes and metabolic and functional changes of diseases in vitro to achieve early diagnosis. It is a reform of traditional medical diagnosis and an important means to achieve early diagnosis and treatment.
