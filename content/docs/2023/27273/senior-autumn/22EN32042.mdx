    en: Currently, our country is increasingly emphasizing environmental protection, with ecological civilization construction being elevated to a national strategy. Through this course, students will grasp the basic concepts, theories, and fundamental research methods of conservation and restoration ecology. They will understand the causes of ecosystem degradation, principles, techniques, and methods for restoring and rebuilding degraded ecosystems. By elucidating typical cases of ecosystem conservation and restoration, students will acquire practical applications of conservation and restoration ecology. The aim is to enable students to engage in ecosystem conservation and restoration construction, while also providing them with a preliminary understanding of research developments and new trends in this field. This will equip students with the ability to analyze and address issues related to sensitive ecosystem protection and degraded ecosystem restoration, thereby enhancing their awareness of ecological civilization construction and laying the foundation for future learning and research.
