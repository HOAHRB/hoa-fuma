    en: Building Information Modeling (BIM) technology has become a cornerstone of the modern construction industry. By creating and utilizing digital information models, it facilitates more efficient design, construction, and operational management throughout the entire lifecycle of a building. BIM is not merely a 3D modeling tool; it encompasses multiple disciplines including architecture, structural engineering, and mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems, achieving an integration of information that optimizes project management and significantly enhances the productivity and quality of construction projects. This course aims to provide a comprehensive introduction and exploration of BIM technology and its extensive applications in the construction industry. Designed to merge theory with practice, the course enables students to deeply understand the fundamental principles of BIM, master the use of major BIM software tools, and apply them in the design, construction, and operational phases of building projects.
