    en: In recent years, global temperatures have been rising year by year, and the greenhouse effect has become increasingly serious, leading to more and more severe global climate change. During the National People's Congress and Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference in 2021, the terms "peak carbon emissions" and "carbon neutrality" were mentioned in the Chinese government's work report, and China announced to the world that it will strive to achieve carbon emissions peak before 2030 and carbon neutrality before 2060. This demonstrates China's mission and responsibility as a responsible great power. Currently, reaching peak carbon emissions and achieving carbon neutrality have become a widespread consensus globally. 194 contracting parties have approved the Paris Agreement for combating climate change, which sets a long-term goal that the global temperature rise should remain below 2 ℃ by the end of this century, and strive for no more than 1.5 ℃. Over 130 countries have proposed carbon neutrality or net zero emissions targets and have formulated a series of action plans to achieve them. Reaching carbon neutrality involves multiple disciplines, industries, and a wide range of areas. It is a systemically complex technological system. Achieving carbon neutrality is a broad and profound economic and social systemic change that seeks to address the most pressing resource and environmental constraints issues. It directly relates to the sustainable development of the Chinese nation and the construction of a community with a shared future for humanity. This course will mainly introduce the concepts of "climate change" and "carbon neutrality." Through this course, students will be able to understand the close relationship between climate change and carbon neutrality, the hazards brought about by climate change, and the necessity of achieving carbon neutrality. Students will also learn about cutting-edge technology and innovative, systematic ways of thinking concerning carbon neutrality and cultivate a strong sense of patriotism, sustainable development concepts, and a sense of a shared future for humanity. With this knowledge and training, students will be able to handle complex technical and governance problems in the carbon neutrality system, and become high-level, innovative, and composite talents with international competitiveness and global competence.
