The course covers key areas such as the basics of edge computing, its architecture, technologies, embedded system design and implementation, edge intelligence, edge inference technologies, distributed training techniques, and large model optimization. Through lectures and real-world case studies, students will learn how to integrate edge computing with embedded systems to build efficient, intelligent, and scalable computing solutions. Specifically, the course starts with the basic knowledge of edge computing, introducing its architecture, system design, and relevant operating systems, helping students understand the collaborative role of edge computing between cloud and end devices. In the edge computing technology section, the course explains access technologies, offloading techniques, and caching strategies in detail, analyzing how optimizing transmission mechanisms, offloading strategies, and caching strategies can enhance system performance. In the embedded system section, students will learn about common embedded hardware and software architectures, laying the foundation for system design. Then the course focuses on edge intelligence applications, particularly in image, video, and text domains, and explores advanced technologies such as edge inference, distributed training, and large model optimization. Through these topics, students will not only master core technologies in embedded systems and edge computing but also be equipped to tackle various challenges in the evolving field of technology.
