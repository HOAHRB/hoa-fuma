This course requires students to systematically master the basic principles of indirect metal additive manufacturing technology, the preparation and requirements of metal powder materials, various indirect 3D printing technologies, green billet degreasing, sintering principles and processes, and focus on the basic principles indirect 3D printing technology and sintering principle.
