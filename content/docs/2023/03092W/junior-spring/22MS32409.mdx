    en: Electron beam additive manufacturing technology is an innovative manufacturing method formed by combining electron beam welding technology and additive manufacturing principle. It uses high-energy electron beam as a heat source to build three-dimensional parts layer by layer. It has the advantages of multi-material selection, low waste generation, custom manufacturing and adaptation to complex structures. It is used in aerospace, medical, automotive and defense industries. This course includes introduction, electron beam powder bed melting additive manufacturing, electron beam fuse deposition additive manufacturing, electron beam additive manufacturing of typical materials, shape and performance control and application of electron beam additive manufacturing technology, as well as experimental teaching of practical operation of electron beam additive remanufacturing. The principle, characteristics and application of electron beam additive manufacturing technology are discussed. Through the teaching of this course, students will understand the basic principle and working process of electron beam additive manufacturing, master the technical details of electron beam powder bed melting additive manufacturing and electron beam fuse deposition additive manufacturing, and solve the common problems in the preparation of electron beam additive samples and engineering applications, so as to provide a solid foundation for future scientific research or engineering work.
