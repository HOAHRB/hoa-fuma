    en: Additive manufacturing, also known as 3D printing, is a very cutting-edge high-tech, is the integration of computer-aided design, automatic control, material processing and forming technology, based on digital models, through software and numerical control system will be dedicated metallic materials, non-metallic materials and biological materials, according to extrusion, sintering, melting, light curing, spraying and other ways layer by layer. Acquire manufacturing skills for physical objects. Additive manufacturing layer by layer of the unit volume is small, different shapes, the smallest melt pool is only about tens microns, so it can prepare the traditional method can not prepare complex structures, very suitable for personalized design and manufacturing needs, so it is also an important method for future manufacturing. This course mainly studies some basic theories and technical issues involved in the additive manufacturing of metallic materials, including the interaction mechanism between different heat sources and metallic materials, the behavior of metal melt pool, the solidification process of small melt pool, the control method of solidification structure, the additive manufacturing process of metal components, and the surface quality control of metal components in additive manufacturing. The above questions will be combined with arc fuse additive manufacturing, laser selective melting additive manufacturing, laser direct deposition additive manufacturing, electron beam selective melting additive manufacturing, electron beam fuse deposition additive manufacturing and other additive manufacturing technologies and equipment, and understand the development trend of additive manufacturing raw materials and composite additive manufacturing technology.
