The primary mission is to train students’ abilities of innovative thinking, comprehensive analysis, scientific research and engineering practice by classroom teaching, and so as to meet the requirements for corresponding graduation indexes. The course objective is to make students grasp the basic theory and elementary knowledge of welding metallurgic process, and possess the comprehensive abilities of materials weldability analysis, welding technology design, and consolidated report writing. The teaching contents in this course are involved in the two aspects. One is welding metallurgic theory under the conditions of fusion welding, and the other is weldability analysis of typical materials. The detailed contents include welding nomenclature, welding materials, welding chemical metallurgy, microstructure and properties of joints, formation mechanism and control of welding defects, material weldability and its testing means, and weldability analysis and technological focus on the typical materials.
