It explores the cultivation mode and curriculum system that integrate liberal arts, science, and medicine. Treating medical foundational knowledge as a unified whole, the course covers the basic principles from the morphological structure of the normal human body to physiological functions and from morphological changes in pathological states to functional alterations. It integrates multidisciplinary knowledge from subjects such as 《Human Anatomy》， 《Histology and Embryology》,《Human Physiology》,《Pathophysiology》,《Pathology》and 《Pharmacology》. The course focuses on the essence, characteristics, and development laws of basic medicine, elucidating the scientific nature, scientific fields, branch structures, and research scopes of medicine. It aims to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of basic medical knowledge. Initially, students are guided to vertically understand the composition and basic morphology of the normal human body. Subsequently, based on the various systems comprising the human body, students engage in horizontal learning that spans from morphology to functionality, from normal to abnormal, and from disease to drug treatment. The integration of foundational medical knowledge in both vertical and horizontal dimensions facilitates students in gaining a more comprehensive grasp of basic medical knowledge, laying a solid foundation for their later studies in related fields.
