    en: 'Computational biology is a discipline that uses computational methods to study biological problems. Computational biology is an interdisciplinary field that integrates biology, computer science, physics, and mathematics and so on. This course mainly focuses on computational methods to enable students to understand biological phenomena, functions, and mechanisms at a systematic level. Through this course, students will master the use of computational methods to systematically study biological objects, master the construction of common molecular biological networks and complex network analysis methods, be able to establish mathematical calculation models for complex biological systems, and be able to perform computational simulation and analysis on the established models; Train students to use computational methods to analyze molecular networks and related high-throughput biological data, extract biomolecules and their relationships. Enable students to have the ability to model and analyze biological network systems, and broaden their research perspectives. The specific objectives of the course are as follows: 1. Knowledge Objectives Understand the core content, methodological frameworks, and current research landscape of computational biology；Comprehend the fundamental principles governing the interaction and regulatory mechanisms between biomolecules；Familiarize oneself with the standard processes and key knowledge points involved in constructing biological networks and regulatory models；Master the operating principles of basic tools used for building biological models. 2. Ability Objectives Be able to utilize established computational biology tools and bioinformatics software to construct models of biomolecular interaction and regulation；Develop the ability to analyze and interpret the phenomena, inherent laws, and underlying mechanisms of life activities at both the systemic and molecular levels；Learn to mine biologically meaningful molecular components from model calculation results, grounded in biological principles；Be proficient in visualizing experimental and computational results to present findings clearly. 3. Quality Objectives Gain a deep understanding of the working principles and construction methodologies of computational biology models, and master the interconnections between key knowledge areas in the field；Develop the capability to understand and explain common biological phenomena from a systems-level perspective；Cultivate the habit of tracking cutting-edge advancements and advanced analytical tools in computational biology, thereby enhancing students’ scientific literacy, exploratory thinking, and practical spirit.'
