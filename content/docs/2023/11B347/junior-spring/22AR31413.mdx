    zh: 《古代景观史》课程是风景园林学专业重要的专业基础课和理论课，主要讲授19世纪以前中国和西方的古典园林和景观的发展历程、形态特征、代表作品、设计师及其主要理论，内容涵盖：古埃及、古希腊罗马等为代表的西方古代园林和景观、中世纪西欧和伊斯兰的园林、文艺复兴和巴洛克时期的意大利台地园、17世纪法国古典主义园林，以及18世纪英国的自然风景园；中国自先秦开始的各个历史阶段如秦汉时期、魏晋南北朝、隋唐、宋元、明清时期的园林和景观的发展演变，以及各时期的不同类型景观的主要形态及其特征。 通过对中西方在历史上不同时期、不同地域、不同人文背景下的景观发展历程以及经典景观设计案例的剖析，帮助学生系统地掌握古代景观形态的基本特征，把握景观发展演变的基本脉络，理解景观不同层面的内涵，以促进学生景观理论素养的充实与提高，最终达到提高学生专业素养与设计水平的目的。
    en: This course is a fundamental and theoretical course for students majored in Landscape Architecture. It focuses on introducing and analysing evolutions, features of gardens and landscape architecture, as well as designers and their works and theories, both in China and western countries before 19th century, including gardens of ancient Egypt, Greek and Roman Empire, gardens of middle ages and Islamic gardens, Italian terrace gardens in Renaissance and Baroque periods, French gardens in 17th century, and English landscape gardens in 18th century. Meanwhile it analyses different characteristics of Chinese gardens and landscape architecture in Qin, Han, Weijin and Nanbeichao, Sui, Tang, Song, Yuan, Ming and Qing dynasties. This course aims to help students to grasp systematically the basic features and evolutions of landscape architecture before 19th century through case studies from different region, period and cultural background, and to have comprehensive understanding of the nature of landscape architecture, so as to improve their professional abilities both in research and design.
