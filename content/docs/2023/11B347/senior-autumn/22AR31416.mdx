    zh: 城市中建筑物与人群的高密度聚集，能源和资源的高强度消耗，在城市空间形成了特殊的城市室外物理环境——城市热环境、城市风环境、城市光环境、城市声环境等，其既区别于自然状态下的气候环境，也不同于建筑室内物理环境。有必要理解城市物理环境的形成机理，掌握控制和改善城市物理环境的途径。课程主要论述城市空间形态和布局与城市物理环境的相互作用机理，讲述合理运用规划和设计手段，创造健康适宜的城市物理环境，实现节能和低碳城市的目标。课程讲授景观物理环境概况，了解城市环境中人的行为方式、城市物理环境的变迁与城市公害、相关环境法律法规；讲授城市热环境内容，了解城市热湿环境、城市热岛效应成因及特征、城市设计对城市气候的影响，掌握城市热岛效应概念、建筑气候分区及建筑城市设计导则、微气候概念，了解绿地对城市热环境的影响及设计实践；讲授城市风与大气环境内容，了解风环境与大气环境基本概念，掌握规划建筑设计与自然通风的关系，了解控制大气环境污染的规划设计原则；讲授城市光环境内容，了解光环境基础，掌握光环境控制的原则与途径，了解城市光污染的危害与防治及设计实践；讲授城市声环境，了解城市噪声基本概念、噪声危害及防治噪声立法，掌握城市噪声控制原则及噪声控制途径；讲授城市声景观，了解声景观基本概念、城市噪声控制及声景设计实践。
    en: The high-density gathering of buildings and people in the city and the high intensity consumption of energy and resources form a special urban outdoor physical environment in the urban space, as urban thermal, wind, light, and sound environment, which are different from natural climate and indoor physical environment. It is necessary to understand mechanism and ways to control and improve urban physical environment. The course mainly discusses the interaction between spatial form and urban physical environment, and describes planning and design to create a healthy and suitable urban physical environment to realize the goal of energy saving and low-carbon city. General situation of landscape physical environment, the behavior of people in urban environment, changes of urban physical environment and urban public hazards, and relevant environmental laws and regulations would be taught. Content of urban thermal and humid environment, effect of urban heat island, the impact of design on urban climate, building urban design guidelines, microclimate concept, impact of green space on urban thermal environment and design practice. Urban wind and atmospheric environment, relationship between planning and architectural design and ventilation, and planning and design principles for controlling atmospheric environmental pollution. Content of urban light environment, the principles and ways of control, harm and prevention of pollution and practice. Concept of urban noise and soundscape, noise hazards and legislation, principles and ways of noise control, urban noise control and soundscape design practice would be taught.
