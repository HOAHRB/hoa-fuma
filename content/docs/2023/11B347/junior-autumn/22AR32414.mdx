    en: “Landscape Design Vision and Methods” teaches the professional perspective, core knowledge and design method of landscape design. It is an elective course of design theory open to undergraduates majoring in landscape, architecture, planning, art and design. Landscape Architecture integrates the knowledge of ecology, society, art and other fields, which supports sustainable living environment and civilization. It needs to analyze problems, explore knowledge and create design from different perspectives. This course constructs three major modules, namely, "Habitat Construction, Social Perspective, and Art Form ", and seven sub-topics, namely, "Habitat of Water, Habitat of soil, Habitat of vegetation, fantasy and conflict, interaction and connection, history and reality of landscape form, creation and expression of landscape design". This course can help and support students majoring in planning and design in the School of Architecture to explore knowledge, expand vision and design innovation.
