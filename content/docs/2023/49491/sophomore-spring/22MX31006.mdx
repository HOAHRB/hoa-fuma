Using the broad concept of "moral education" as a benchmark, this paper deeply explores and summarizes the moral education ideas of different historical periods, schools, and thinkers in ancient China, and systematically elucidates the historical trajectory of the occurrence and development of ancient Chinese moral education ideas. The Chinese nation has an excellent cultural tradition of five thousand years, and its moral education ideas have a long and profound history, enjoying a unique position in world cultural history. Systematically elucidating the history of the emergence and development of ancient Chinese moral education ideas will undoubtedly help summarize the wisdom and theoretical creations of our ancestors in the field of moral education. A systematic and profound theoretical exposition was made on the ideas of ancient thinkers about the purpose and objectives of moral education, the design of moral education theory and basic content, and various reflections on moral education principles, methods, and approaches. This has reference significance for a comprehensive understanding of ancient Chinese moral education theories, for effectively exploring and grasping the laws of moral education activities with Chinese characteristics, for enriching socialist moral education theories and promoting contemporary moral education practices.
