It not only reflects the relationship between human and nature, but also inevitably refers to the relationship between people and people, and between human and society. There are many profound and important ethical issues inherent in engineering activities. Nowadays, China is undergoing unprecedented engineering construction. It is of great importance to propose, analyze and solve the ethical problems of engineering activities in different industries and fields. “Engineering Ethics and Cognition” is designed for senior undergraduates for their engineering and ethics education. The course includes not only theoretical elements such as engineering connotation, engineering life cycle, engineering ethics, engineering community, but also the analysis of engineering ethics problems from concrete practical cases. Through the active discussion between teachers and students, it is hoped that students’ ethical awareness in engineering will be well cultivated, ethical literacy will be further improved and social responsibility will be more strengthened.
