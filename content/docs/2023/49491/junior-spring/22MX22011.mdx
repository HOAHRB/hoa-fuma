As the SPSS/R is one of the most widespread statistic tools now, proficient SPSS/R operation is the required ability for researchers of philosophy and social sciences. This course introduces the theories of social statistics, and the layout in SPSS and r. It requires students to achieve a solid proficiency in operations of files, data, and charts within the software. Operations of files include establishing, transposing, merging, splitting, and summarizing files. Basic operations of data include ranking, selecting, inserting, re-encoding data, as well as elimination of errors. Data analyzing operations include descriptive statistics, means analysis and T-test, correlation analysis, regression analysis, multi-selection multivariate analysis, and non-parametric test, etc.
