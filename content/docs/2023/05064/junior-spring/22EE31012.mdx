This course introduces the basics of power electronic devices and converters. Working principles, operating modes and analysis of DC-DC, DC-AC, AC-DC, and AC-AC converters would be covered for a variety of loads. Control of power electronic converters would be explained. Certain specialized concepts in power electronics like matrix converter, active rectifiers and SiC/GaN devices would be included, along with some of the popular applications of power electronics such as renewable energy conversion and power quality enhancement. This course lays the foundation for future specialized courses such as motor drive, motion control system, power supply, HVDC technology, power quality control, power electronics devices and systems, etc.
