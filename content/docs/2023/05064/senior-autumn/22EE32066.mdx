Through this course, students will gain a solid understanding of information acquisition and execution systems, information transmission systems, and information processing systems, and develop an initial ability to design the architecture of modern power dispatch automation systems. The course also covers mathematical models and computational methods for state estimation, static security analysis and control, and automatic generation control, enabling students to analyze and solve problems encountered in engineering practice. Students will also acquire skills in literature retrieval and analysis, while keeping up with the latest theoretical and technological advances in the Energy Internet field. The course focuses on cultivating students’ abilities in comprehensive analysis and design of information automation systems, applying energy system scientific knowledge, enhancing innovation awareness, and supporting the achievement of the corresponding program learning outcomes.
