    en: Dynamic Simulation of Integrated Energy System is a elective course for electrical engineering and automation. This course mainly introduces the status, development direction of Integrated Energy System simulation and basic concepts, principles and methods of simulation. The course covers Integrated Energy System modeling, Principle and methods for full digital simulation, Real-Time Semi-Physical simulation, Simulation of sampled-data system, Application of Professional simulation software in Integrated Energy System Simulation etc.. The principle and method of full-digital simulation of Integrated Energy System include Simple alternative method, Bilinear substitution method, Root matching method, the numerical value simulation method and the Euler method , the betterment Euler method and Adams method based on it, Runge-Kutta method based on Taylor Series matching principle. The reason of the error of the simulation method, the precision of and the stability of the simulation method, the variable step principle of the simulation method are analyzed. We will dicuss the concept of real-time hardware-in-the-loop simulation, the operation mode of simulation system, the characteristics of simulation model and the commonly used simulation method in Integrated Energy System simulation. The simulation method of Integrated Energy System with digital controller and the reconstruction method of the digital controller when the sampling period is changed are introduced in the course. The simulation environment of Professional simulation software, programming language , programming method and its application in Integrated Energy System simulation are introduced. Simulation experiments of simple Integrated Energy System are given in the end.
