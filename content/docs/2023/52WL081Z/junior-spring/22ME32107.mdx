This course mainly introduces the basic theory and method of finite element analysis of mechanical structures. The types of elements include beam element, planar element and three-dimensional element. It also introduces the construction of displacement field, the derivation of element stiffness matrix, the assembly of system stiffness matrix and the solution of dynamic and static equilibrium equations. To facilitate students to have a deeper understanding of the finite element theory, and to conduct qualitative and quantitative analysis of the calculation structure of finite element, the force method and displacement method in structural mechanics are taken as the leading content, considering that most mechanical students lack the knowledge of structural mechanics. In combination with SAP84 and ANSYS software, the course makes a preliminary introduction to the application of finite element method in structural statics and dynamic analysis, which is convenient for students to master the general finite element software to solve mechanical structure analysis problems. For basic theories, classroom teaching is adopted, and students are guided to compile simple finite element programs. The finite element simulation teaching adopts the form of flipped classroom. The assessment method is cumulative, with 20 points for major work of finite element programming, 20 points for major work of finite element simulation and 60 points for final exam. This course focuses on cultivating students'' comprehensive analysis ability of mechanical structure through classroom teaching, assignments and computer simulation.'
