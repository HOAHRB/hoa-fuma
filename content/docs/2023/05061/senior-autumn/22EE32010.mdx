The problem of electromagnetic compatibility is paid more and more attention, and electromagnetic compatibility technology is becoming more and more important. This course is for undergraduate students in electrical engineering and automation specialty, teaching basic concept of electromagnetic compatibility and hazards of electromagnetic compatibility, standards of EMC, EMC analysis method, design method of electromagnetic compatibility, measurement method of electromagnetic compatibility and electromagnetic compatibility problems in motor drive system. This course enables students to understand and master the electromagnetic compatibility technology. The teaching requirement of this course is overview of electromagnetic compatibility technology, and various methods of analysis, design, measurement, etc. teachers explain the concept, basic knowledge and basic principle combining the specific engineering practice to improve the students ability of combining theory with practice. At the same time, with the current development trend in the field of electromagnetic compatibility, teachers introduce some new research achievements and trends to broaden ideas and vision of students.
