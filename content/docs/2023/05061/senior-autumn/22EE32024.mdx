This course mainly introduces the status, development direction of power system simulation and basic concepts, principles and methods of simulation. The course covers Power system modeling, Principle and methods for full digital simulation, Real-Time Semi-Physical simulation, Simulation of sampled-data system, Application of MWORKS in Power System Simulation etc.. The principle and method of full-digital simulation of power system include Simple alternative method, Bilinear substitution method, Root matching method, the numerical value simulation method and the Euler method , the betterment Euler method and Adams method based on it, Runge-Kutta method based on Taylor Series matching principle. The reason of the error of the simulation method, the precision of and the stability of the simulation method, the variable step principle of the simulation method are analyzed. We will discuss the concept of real-time hardware-in-the-loop simulation, the operation mode of simulation system, the characteristics of simulation model and the commonly used simulation method in power system simulation. The simulation method of power system with digital controller and the reconstruction method of the digital controller when the sampling period is changed are introduced in the course. The simulation environment of MWORKS, programming language , programming method and its application in power system simulation are introduced.
