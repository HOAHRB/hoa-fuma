    zh: 社交网络是一种在信息网络上由社会个体集合及个体之间的连接关系构成的社会性结构。这种社会结构主要包含关系结构、网络群体与网络信息三个要素。其中，社交网络的关系结构是社会个体成员之间通过社会关系结成的网络系统。个体也称为节点，可以是组织、个人、网络ID等不同含义的实体或虚拟个体；而个体间的相互关系可以是亲友、动作行为、收发消息等多种关系。基于这些关系，社交网络中的个体自组织成各种各样的虚拟社区，虚拟社区是社交网络的一个子集，具有虚拟社区内节点之间关联密切、不同虚拟社区的节点间关联稀疏等特点。在上述关系的基础上，社交网络中个体与个体之间、个体与群体之间、群体与群体之间传递着各种各样的信息，这种信息传递的不断迭代便是社交网络中的信息传播。本课程主要介绍社交网络分析的现状、发展以及面临的挑战，从结构与演化-群体与互动-信息与传播三个方面展开，系统、深入地阐述了社交网络分析中的基本理论、关键方法和技术，并且进行相关实践及技术分享。
    en: 'A social network is a social structure composed of individuals and the connections between individuals on an information network. This social structure mainly includes three elements: relationship structure, network groups, and network information. Among them, the relationship structure is a network system formed between individuals through social relationships. Individuals, also known as nodes, can be entities or virtual individuals with different meanings, e.g., organizations, people, and network ID; The relationships between individuals can be friendships, behaviors, and communication. Based on these relationships, individuals in social networks self-organize into various virtual communities. A virtual community is a subset of a social network, characterized by dense connections within the virtual community and sparse connections between nodes in different virtual communities. On the basis of the above relationships, various types of information are transmitted between individuals and groups in social networks, which is also referred to as the dissemination of information. This course mainly introduces the status quo, development and challenges of social network analysis. It starts from three aspects: structure and evolution - group and interaction - information and communication, and systematically and deeply expounds the basic theory, key methods and technologies in social network analysis, and conducts related practices and technology sharing.'
