    en: 'Wind disaster is one of the main natural disasters acted on building structures. Nowadays, with the advancement of structural analysis and construction techniques, building structures tend to be higher or larger, with a low weight and significant flexibility. And thus they are more sensitive to wind action. Under this circumstance, it is very necessary to advance the understanding of structural wind engineering principles for civil engineering undergraduate students and cultivate their ability to correctly carry out wind resistance design of building structures. The main contains of this course includes: the main characteristics of wind disasters of building structures, characteristics of the atmosphere boundary layer wind and their engineering description, basic concept and determination of wind loading including mean wind pressure and fluctuating wind pressure, classification of wind induced responses, wind resistance design of high-rise buildings, high-rise structures and large span structures, control of wind loading and wind-induced responses via aerodynamic control measures, technology of wind tunnel test. By this course, the students are expected to be familiar with the basic features of wind loading, the principles and methods of wind resistance design, and can carry out wind resistance design for some conventional structures based on the loading code.'
