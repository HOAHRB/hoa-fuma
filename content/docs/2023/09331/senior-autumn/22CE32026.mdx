Unlike urban civil engineering structures, offshore engineering structures suffer more and worse kinds of environmental loads. The task and purpose of this course is to enable students to understand and master the basic theory of hydrodynamics, the environmental load function of offshore engineering (especially the wave force theory and the calculation method of wave load involved in the analysis and design of offshore engineering), as well as the basic structural form and preliminary design of fixed and mobile platforms. This course mainly includes: (1) Introduction; (2) Basic knowledge of fluid mechanics; (3) Marine environmental load; (4) Offshore platform structure. Through the study of this course, students can understand and master the theory of wave, which is an important load type in offshore engineering structure characteristics, analysis and design, as well as the analysis method of offshore engineering structures considering wave loads, so as to understand and preliminary for students on the basis of existing theoretical knowledge of structural analysis. Master the ability of analysis and design of offshore engineering structures such as cross-sea bridges, offshore platforms and ports, and expand students'' knowledge. This course can be used as an elective course for the elite classes of Civil Engineering Specialty in construction engineering, geotechnical engineering and mechanics.'
