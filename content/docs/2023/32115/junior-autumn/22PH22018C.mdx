    en: Quantum mechanics (QM) is an important part of science and physics and it is essential to understanding the behavior of systems at atomic length scales and smaller. QM provides a mathematical description of quantum objects that exhibit the wave nature. The abstract mathematical formulation of QM is best understood through postulates. The state of the QM system is described by the wave function (WF) that is defined as a vector Hilbert space. The behavior of WF is governed by the Schrodinger equation. The main content of this course includes exactly solved one-dimensional models, introduction to the angular momentum theory, central field problem, Hydrogen-like problem, introduction to the relativistic Dirac theory, approximation methods of quantum mechanics, radiation transitions, many-electron system and introduction to the potential scattering theory.
