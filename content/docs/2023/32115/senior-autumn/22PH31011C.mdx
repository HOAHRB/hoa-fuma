    en: The course provides basic information in the field of topical issues in the soft matter physics, namely, in the field of physical chemistry of polymers and liquid crystals. The importance of polymers in nature and human activity is difficult to overestimate. Biopolymers are the basis of all life on Earth. Synthetic polymers are the main materials used by man in various fields. The course sets out the basic ideas about the nature of high molecular compounds, the properties of polymer molecules, which determine the unique characteristics of the materials created on their basis. Structural features of polymers and methods of their synthesis are briefly described; basic information on the statistics and thermodynamics of macromolecules in solutions is given. The basic principles of theories of translational and rotational friction of macromolecules in solutions are described in sufficient detail, allowing one to obtain the basic characteristics of macromolecules. Issues related to the optical, electro-optical properties of polymer molecules are considered. Liquid crystals have long been the basis for the creation of various information display devices (indicators, displays, etc.), as well as various sensors, modulators, and other, primarily optical, devices. The course gives the basics of the classification of LC, outlines their main physical characteristics that underlie the created displays. Briefly presented are the ways of creating and improving liquid crystal cells.
