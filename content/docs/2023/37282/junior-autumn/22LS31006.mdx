The field of cell engineering technology is novel and rich, including cell culture, cell fusion, monoclonal antibodies, stem cells, transgenic animals, embryonic engineering, nuclear transplantation and animal cloning and other technologies. Therefore, the cell engineering becomes much more important in the fields of the bioengineering. The purpose of this course is not only to make the students fully understand the relationship between cell engineering and modern biological technology, but also to enable the students to master the key technology, basic principle and the operation method in cell engineering, and to understand the development of cell engineering and to have the ability of comprehensive use of various technologies.
