Based on the Qt platform, the course introduces the development of typical applications in the field of medical electronic technology. Through this course, students will acquire knowledge of the Qt development environment, object-oriented software development methods, and the development approaches for software related to human physiological parameter detection, medical imaging data acquisition, transmission, and post-processing using Qt.
