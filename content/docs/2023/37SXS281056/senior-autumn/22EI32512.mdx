It aims to cultivate students' interdisciplinary perspectives and future-oriented engineering practice capabilities. This course systematically covers fundamental bioinformatics theories and multi-omics analysis methods, introduces the structure and regulatory mechanisms of biomolecular networks, and elucidates core principles of artificial intelligence and intelligent computing. It focuses on the application of next-generation technologies—including intelligent optimization algorithms, machine learning, and deep learning—in genomics, proteomics, metabolomics, and precision medicine. The course design incorporates classroom lectures, problem-based discussions, and report writing to help students build systematic understanding within an integrated “biological mechanisms + intelligent computing” framework. Through this course, students will not only grasp the theoretical framework and developmental trends of intelligent computing technologies in bioinformatics but also master key methodologies such as cross-omics data integration, complex network modeling, and intelligent algorithm design and implementation. The curriculum emphasizes cultivating three core competencies as follows. 1. Intelligent processing and feature engineering capabilities for biological big data. 2. Interdisciplinary systems modeling and causal relationship discovery. 3. Application and innovation of intelligent algorithms for clinical and industrial purposes. As a key elective for the Biotechnology (with Dual Bachelor's Degree in Intelligent Measurement and Control Engineering) program, this course transcends traditional disciplinary boundaries to explore deep integration between life sciences and intelligent computing. It lays a solid foundation for students' future endeavors in bioinformatics research, smart healthcare development, and interdisciplinary innovation.
