With the rapid advancement of Computer Science and information technology, their applications in biology have become increasingly widespread and profound. Research areas such as medical clinical diagnosis, disease treatment, and bioinformatics are now inextricably linked to computing. Advancements in computer technology have significantly propelled progress in related fields. Particularly in recent years, the explosive growth of biological data resources has created an urgent need for new tools to uncover the underlying patterns of life. Computer science and technology, which fundamentally involve data analysis and processing, perfectly address this demand. This course primarily introduces the application of computers in fields such as computer-aided medicine, medical image processing, and bioinformatics. The curriculum content mainly includes course introduction, overview of basic tools like computers, computer-aided medical technology, telemedicine technology, medical information systems, modelling and simulation in biomedicine, medical image processing, compressed sensing and medical imaging technology, and the application of computers in bioinformatics. Through this course, students will understand the current state of computer applications in biology and emerging research trends domestically and internationally. They will comprehend the role of computers in biological research and master fundamental concepts, theories, principles, and methodologies for conducting biological studies using computational tools.'
