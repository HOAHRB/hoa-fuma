The aim and objective of this course is to begin with service performance of materials, clarify the relationship between composition, microstructure and properties of engineering materials, help students to select and use materials properly, design new materials in the end, according to base theory and knowledge obtained by the students. The main contents of the course contain: the foundation of alloying (alloying of steel, nonferrous metals and alloys such as aluminum alloy, magnesium alloy and copper alloy). Structural steels (classifications and naming methods of the structural steel, carbon structural steel, high strength and low alloy steel). Machine parts steels (carburizing steel, hardened and tempered steel, spring steel, bearing steel, wear-resistant steel). Chisel tool steels (classifications and naming methods, cutting tool steel, die steel and gauge steel). Special steels (stainless steel, heat-resistant steel). Nonferrous metals and alloys (intermetallic, aluminum alloy, titanium alloy, magnesium alloy, copper alloy). Ceramic materials (Introduction to ceramic materials, structural ceramics). Polymer materials ((Introduction to polymer materials, several polymer materials). Composites (Introduction to composites, metal matrix composites, ceramic matrix composites, polymer matrix composites and carbon/carbon (C/C) composites).'
