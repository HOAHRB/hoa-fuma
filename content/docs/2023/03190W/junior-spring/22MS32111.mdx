    en: The aim of the course is to get the interactional rule of materials and surroundings medium, obtain the reason and control measures of materials corrosion under some typical environment along with the natural environment (such as atmosphere, seawater and soil). The aim of the course is to make the students mastering the thermodynamics criterion of material corrosion as well as the testing technique of materials corrosion and principle of differently corrosive types. The contents of this subject include the thermodynamics criterion of material corrosion and its electrochemical parameter along with the kinetic theory of electrochemical corrosion. Some importance corrosion types and accordingly formed mechanism are mentioned. The subject deals with the necessary conditions for corrosion, polarization phenomenon and polarization reason, anodic passivation and character. The subject also deals with the character of materials corrosion at elevated temperature and nature environment. In addition, corrosion control by design and coating are also described. The task of the course is to train students to master the basal conception and theory of corrosion, master the reason of corrosion and control methods. This subject not only has important practicality effect on the students in the future, but also is the important part of basic knowledge for materials science.
