It is a crossed subject of space environment technology, surface science, and materials science. Aiming to space applications, the main contents of the course includes: basic theory for surface and interface of materials; overview of space tribology; lubrication theory, tribological performance, preparation technology, and applications of main space solid lubricant materials (soft metals, laminar solid lubricant materials, polymers and their composites, oxides for high temperature lubrication etc); selection principles of space lubrication materials; space liquid lubricant; new lubrication materials for space tribology. The course is assumed to the undergraduates from materials science. The teaching materials are designed for students to master the following knowledge: requirements for tribological devices and materials used in space; main species and applications of material s for space tribology; basic concepts and principles of space tribology; space environment effects on lubrication materials; selection principles and space environment evaluation experiment methods for space lubrication materials; Development trends and prospects of new space lubrication materials.
