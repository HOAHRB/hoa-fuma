    zh: 无人机相关技术是当前的热点研究领域，无人机在军用及民用领域都实现了较广泛的应用。无人机系统包括飞行器系统、控制系统、地面站系统、作业系统等，其设计、实现与应用均采用系统工程的原理与方法。本课程基于系统工程理论，以无人机系统为研究对象，讲解无人机系统工程导论（无人机系统的分类、组成与飞行原理等）、无人机系统的分析方法（模型驱动法、需求获取法等）、无人机系统的设计方法（动力系统设计、飞行控制系统设计等）、无人机系统的实现方法（飞行器的实现、控制系统的实现等）、无人机系统的集成与试飞等内容，旨在以多学科交叉的方式帮助学生加深对系统工程、自动控制原理、控制系统设计、单片机控制等专业知识的理解和运用，充分调动学生的主观能动性，增强学生的控制系统实现能力和动手能力。 课程包含24个学时的理论授课及8个学时的实验授课，在讲解必要理论及方法的基础上，使用多旋翼/直升机/垂起固定翼无人机系统、无线电遥控系统、飞行控制系统、云台作业系统等实物进行演示讲解与实验操作，并使用STM32单片机及相关硬件进行单片机编程及系统实现。本课程是一门理论与实践相结合的专业选修课，在系统工程与仿真研究方向为本科生提供了基于一个热点应用的学习机会。
    en: UAV (Unmanned Aerial Vehicle) related technology is the hotspot today. UAV is widely used in the fields of military and civilian applications. A UAV system contains aerial vehicle system, flight control system, ground station system, task operation system etc. It adopts the principles and methods of system engineering to realize design, development and application. Based on the theory of system engineering, the lecture takes the UAV system as the studying object, and explains the introduction of UAV system engineering (including UAV system’s category, components and flying principle etc.), the analysis methods of UAV system (including model-driven method, requirement acquisition etc.), the design methods of UAV system (including power system design, flight control system design etc.), the implementation methods of UAV system (including aerial vehicle implementation, control system implementation etc.), and the integration & test flight of UAV system etc. The lecture aims at helping students understand and use the knowledge of system engineering, automation control theory, control system design, MCU (Micro Controller Unit) control etc. more efficiently, with the teaching method of multiple correlated disciplines. Meanwhile, the lecture emphasizes on stimulating the study motivation of the students, and enhancing the student's ability of control system implementation and manipulation. The lecture contains 24 class hours of theory and 8 class hours of experiment. On the basis of explaining necessary knowledge principles and theory methods, the lecture uses the real machines of multi-rotor/helicopter/VTOL fixed-wing UAV system, remote control system, flight control system, and cradle head system etc. to perform demonstration and experiment manipulation, and uses STM32 MCU with related hardware to realize programming and system implementation. The lecture is a theory and practice combined professional elective course, and provides a studying opportunity on hot application of system engineering and simulation technology to undergraduate students.
