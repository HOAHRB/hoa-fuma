    en: This course mainly focuses on wind power grid-connected generation technology and photovoltaic (PV) grid-connected generation technology, explaining the principles, design methods, and applications of wind power and PV power grid-connected systems. The specific content includes an overview of renewable energy generations (REGs), principles of wind power generation and grid-connected system, principles of PV power generation and grid-connected system, key technologies in grid-connected power generation, structure and control principles of grid-connected inverters, design of PV grid-connected power generation systems, and more types of REGs. Through the study of this course, students will be familiar with the basic principles and key technologies of REGs such as PV power generation, wind power generation, and tidal power generation etc., based on their understanding of the concept, classification, and utilization history of energy. They will also master the power electronic topology, working principles, and control methods of grid-connected converters for REGs, and become familiar with the maximum power tracking, anti-islanding strategy, low voltage ride through, and other requirement for the integration of renewable energy into the main power grid. During the teaching process, questions and group discussions are used to improve students' interest, cultivate their ability to analyze and solve problems independently. By introducing corresponding software and tools for grid-connected power generation system design and simulation, students are able to form a deeper understanding of grid-connected REGs technology, and initially obtain the ability to design and develop PV power generation grid-connected systems.
