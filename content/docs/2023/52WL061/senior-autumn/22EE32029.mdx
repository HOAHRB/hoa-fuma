    en: Electrical Power Quality Control Techniques, focusing on the applications of power electronic technology in power system, is a prerequisite for courses in High voltage, High power electronic devices and control. Starting from the basic concept of power quality, this course expounds the power quality indicators and measurements, the international standards and national standards of power quality. The causes and harms of typical power quality problems are analyzed, based on the classification of power quality problems in power system, such as impulsive transient, voltage sag and swell, waveform distortion, harmonic, flicker, and so on. Along with the analysis of power quality control theory, such as harmonic analysis and instantaneous reactive power theory, the power quality control techniques based on power electronics are introduced, including the Active Power Filter, Static Var Compensator (Thyristor Controlled Reactor, Thyristor Switched Capacitor, Static Var Compensator, Thyristor Controlled Series Capacitor ), Flexible AC Transmission (STATCOM, SSSC, UPFC), etc. The circuit topology, control principle and applications of various control techniques are discussed in detail. Through the analysis of some practical cases, this course will cultivate students' ability to apply theoretical knowledge to solve practical problems.
