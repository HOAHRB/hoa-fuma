Meanwhile, it also plays an important role in training the students of electrical engineering in the engineering problem analysis and integrated design capabilities. The course mainly teaches students the basic design method of the typical electrical apparatus, such as relays and contactors. The content of the course includes magnetic circuit analysis and calculation of the electromagnetic system, resistant force calculation of the contact system, analysis of the relationship between the attractive and resistant force, three stages design theory of electrical apparatus (system design, parameter design and tolerance design), finite element method of calculating the electromagnetic characteristics, multi-body dynamics method of calculating the contact system characteristics, etc. Through the classroom teaching and practice in the computer, the course is focused on training students the integrated analysis and design capabilities of the electro-mechanical system.
