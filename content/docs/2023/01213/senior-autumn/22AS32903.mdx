    en: Optical remote sensing satellites can quickly and accurately capture the latest spatiotemporal information of specific areas on the Earth's surface. They have the advantages of wide spatial coverage, short revisit time, multiple imaging spectral bands, rich image texture information, and can be used for long-term staring imaging. They are important means of obtaining situational information in key regions. Optical remote sensing image processing technology has been widely applied in various fields such as digital cities, geographical and national monitoring, environmental protection, and disaster emergency response. The main content of this course includes basic theoretical courses such as remote sensing image radiometric correction, geometric correction, inversion restoration, fusion superresolution, visual interpretation, machine interpretation, and corresponding experimental courses. The aim is to enable students to understand the imaging principles of optical remote sensing systems and the degradation mechanism of image quality, master the basic concepts and methods of optical remote sensing image processing applications such as correction, enhancement, and interpretation, and practice through typical cases of remote sensing image processing, Exercise the interdisciplinary application ability of "optics+information+intelligence" and the ability to apply advanced tools to solve practical engineering problems.
