This course is aimed at the characteristics of environmental science majors, guided by the "dual carbon" strategy, closely related to the practical problems of environmental science majors in engineering practice, and emphasizes the role of practical case teaching in environmental science. The main content includes the basic concepts, principles, methods, typical processes, and related design and calculation issues of environmental pollution control engineering. The key content is the basic principles and processes of air and water pollution control methods: tracking the latest frontier of air pollution control, clarifying the physical and chemical characteristics of different air pollutants, designing feasible research and experimental plans, and cultivating low-carbon, comprehensive, regional, and systematic air pollution prevention and control thinking; Integrating sewage quality indicators, sewage treatment theories, principles, and process technology design calculations closely, comprehensively and systematically interpreting the water quality conditions of different sewage and the applicable water treatment process flow for environmental factors, and training and developing design ideas for comprehensive new water treatment processes. The assessment methods include after-school exercises and final exams.'
