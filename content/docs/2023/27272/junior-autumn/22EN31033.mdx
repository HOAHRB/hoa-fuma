Environmental problems and environmental emergencies accompanied by industrial development have been attached much importance gradually since China''s reform and opening-up. Environmental emergency management in China is growing with the development of ecological environmental protection. Environmental ecological emergency management includes two major parts, namely, environmental risk management and environmental emergency management. This course is divided into 9 chapters.
