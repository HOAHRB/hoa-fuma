    zh: 长期以来，我国以煤为主的能源结构，使得全国城市大气环境呈现高浓度的二氧化硫和可吸入颗粒物污染。近20年来，随着经济的快速发展，城市化进程不断加速，高速发展的城市交通和汽车产业又导致了严重的机动车尾气污染。发达国家经历了近百年的环境污染问题在我国经济发达地区二三十年内便集中爆发，老的污染问题尚未解决，新的污染问题又接踵而至。在我国城市群出现了煤烟型与机动车污染共存的特殊大气复合污染，具有明显的局地污染和区域污染相结合、污染物之间相互耦合的特征，其表现为大气氧化能力不断增强、高浓度细颗粒物（PM2.5）使大气能见度下降。大气环境形势总体上进入了以多污染物共存、多污染源叠加、多尺度关联、多过程演化、多介质影响为特征的复合型大气污染阶段，已经对我国公众健康和生态安全构成巨大威胁，并存在发生环境灾变的隐患。在这样的背景下，我国相继实施了大气污染防治行动计划、打赢蓝天保卫战三年行动计划、深入打好重污染天气消除攻坚战行动方案，以区域性PM2.5污染为集中体现的灰霾问题得到了有效遏制，形成了国际公认的治霾“中国经验”。通过本课程的学习，将使学生深刻认识过去50年间我国大气污染治理的辉煌历程，增强学生的文化自信，培养有志于生态文明建设的培养拔尖管理人才；掌握与空气质量管理相关的基础知识，特别是我国在治霾过程中自主提出的霾化学理论、PM2.5与O3协同治理理论、空气质量改善与气候变化应对协同增效理论。
    en: For a long time, China's coal-based energy structure has caused the national urban atmospheric environment to show high concentrations of sulfur dioxide and respirable particulate matter pollution. In the past 20 years, with the rapid development of the economy, the process of urbanization has been accelerating, and the rapid development of urban transportation and the automobile industry has led to serious motor vehicle exhaust pollution. Developed countries have experienced nearly a century of environmental pollution problems in China's economically developed areas within two to three decades will be concentrated outbreaks, the old pollution problems have not yet been resolved, the new pollution problems and come one after another. In China's urban agglomerations appeared soot type and motor vehicle pollution coexistence of special atmospheric compound pollution, with obvious local pollution and regional pollution combined with the characteristics of pollutants coupled with each other, which is manifested in the increasing oxidizing capacity of the atmosphere, high concentrations of fine particulate matter (PM2.5) so that atmospheric visibility decreased. The atmospheric environmental situation in general has entered the stage of compound air pollution characterized by the coexistence of multiple pollutants, superposition of multiple pollution sources, multi-scale correlation, multi-process evolution, and multi-media influence, which has already posed a great threat to China's public health and ecological safety, and there is a hidden danger of environmental catastrophe. In this context, China has implemented the Action Plan for Prevention and Control of Air Pollution, the Three-Year Action Plan for Winning the Battle for the Blue Sky, and the Action Plan for Eliminating Heavily Polluted Weather, which has effectively curbed the haze problem concentrated in regional PM2.5 pollution, and formed the internationally recognized haze control "Chinese experience". Through the study of this course, students will have a deep understanding of China's brilliant course of air pollution control in the past 50 years, enhance students' cultural confidence, and cultivate top management talents who are interested in the construction of ecological civilization; and master the basic knowledge related to air quality management, especially the haze chemistry theory, the theory of synergistic management of PM2.5 and O3, and the theory of synergistic effect of air quality improvement and climate change response, which have been independently put forward by our country in the process of haze control.
