Through experimental practice to consolidate atmospheric environmental science course knowledge emphases and difficulties, combined with the latest research progress worldwide, training students to analyze the atmospheric environmental quality parameters, gained the abilities to solve the problem of atmospheric pollution control, for the further engagement in atmospheric environmental monitoring and pollution control related scientific research and environmental management work to lay the foundation of the system. The course requires students to master the physical and chemical characteristics of gaseous and particulate pollutants, the relationship between meteorology and atmospheric pollution transmission, the formation mechanism of atmospheric compound pollution, and the technical methods of the chemical component analysis of atmospheric particulate matter. This course focuses on the combination of basic theory study with scientific research practice, and achieves the following goals through experimental teaching. Based on experimental teaching, experimental operation courses are issued before the experimental class, and short videos describe the difficulties and key points of the experiment, to help students preview the content of the experimental class before class. The experiment class is demonstrated by the lecturer, and the students carry out the experiment simultaneously. The lecturer will standardize the experiment operation of the students, and ensure that the students master the experiment methods that comply with the national standards and discipline frontier. The student will gain the credits by submitting 6 experiment reports. The lecturer will remind the students to record the experiment process and make the observation diary during the class.
