Atmospheric environmental science is the study of the earth''s atmosphere from the perspective of the human environment, and is a science compatible with theory and application. It mainly studies the physical and chemical properties of atmospheric components, the laws of transport and transformation, and their interactions with human activities, meteorology and ecosystems. In addition, atmospheric science also includes the interactions between the atmosphere and the land, ocean and biosphere, and has a wide range of applications in various aspects of the environment, oceans, new energy science, human activities and daily life. This course emphasizes the combination of basic theoretical study and scientific research practice. Through classroom teaching, students will be familiar with the sources, effects and transport-diffusion processes of the major components of the atmosphere; understand the chemical processes of global climate change and stratospheric ozone depletion, as well as the essential connection between tropospheric chemistry, stratospheric chemistry and climate change; master the basic theories and methods of atmospheric environmental science, and lay the foundation for flexible application to in-depth professional studies and daily life in the future.'
