It requires students to comprehensively apply their acquired knowledge, integrate with the requirements of practical work positions, and solve real-world problems. Practice also serves as a comprehensive assessment of students'' overall qualities. By visiting enterprises and institutions related to environmental science, students gain an understanding of the characteristics and talent qualities required in the field, enhancing their knowledge of the profession and laying the foundation for future employment. This practice also helps students develop practical skills, cultivate a serious and good work ethic, and establish a solid foundation for their future careers. Simultaneously, practice cultivates students'' healthy outlook on life, worldview, and values, shaping a positive attitude towards learning, employment, and career choices. It forges the spirit of striving for the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation. To align theoretical knowledge with the practical aspects of environmental science, students master relevant learning skills. The content of environmental science practice follows thematic routes, incorporating observation, explanations, interviews, and discussions. Guided by the principle of "walk, think, and learn," students "see" environmental issues during inspections, "reflect" on the causes and solutions to environmental science problems, and "learn" the knowledge contained within. This helps them appreciate the importance and necessity of environmental protection and sustainable development. Specifically, the goals of environmental science practice include: becoming familiar with the basic qualities of the resource and environmental science field, enhancing practical skills, hands-on abilities, problem-solving, and analytical abilities to obtain effective conclusions; cultivating good practical and collaborative abilities to lay a solid foundation for actual work, enabling students to assume roles as individuals, team members, and leaders in multidisciplinary teams.'
