    en: Environmental microbial metabolic transformation and material cycling is a newly introduced course within the revised curriculum for the environmental science undergraduate program. This course is a crucial research area within the environmental science major, encompassing interdisciplinary fields such as microbiology, ecology, environmental science, earth science, chemistry, physics, mathematics, and engineering. The primary objective of this course is to provide students with an understanding of the fundamental principles and processes involved in environmental microbial material cycling, as well as to equip them with relevant research methods and skills. The course delves into topics such as ecosystem material cycling, the basic principles of microbial material cycling, intracellular energy flow, material cycling, and information transfer, as well as the distinctions and connections between material cycling and biogeochemical cycling. Students are expected to comprehend the causes and consequences of ecological environmental pollution, learn about technical measures for treatment and restoration, and apply this knowledge to both natural environments and artificial ecosystems.
