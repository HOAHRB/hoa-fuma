    en: Microstructured functional surface is a new interdisciplinary research field developed in the 1980s. After nearly 40 years of rapid development, it has become an important subject in the field of mechanical engineering. Because of its unique and excellent functional properties, microstructured functional surfaces have been the focus of research in many fields at home and abroad. The fabrication of microstructured functional surfaces is to use specific processing methods to produce functional structures with different morphologies, different scales and different dimensions on the surface of objects. Compared with the traditional smooth surface, the microstructured functional surface has many functions such as enhanced heat transfer, enhanced light production, resistance and wear reduction, antibacterial and desorption due to its special geometric characteristics. There are many kinds of functional surfaces with different shapes and sizes. According to its functional use, it can be divided into surface thermal functional structure, surface light functional structure, surface reaction functional structure, surface drag reduction, desorption functional structure and so on. At present, the microstructured functional surface has been widely used in various fields, and has become an effective means to improve energy efficiency and achieve energy saving and emission reduction in traditional high-energy consumption industries such as metal smelting, petroleum processing and power production. It is the key to improve product performance and ensure system safety in aerospace, high-speed rail, 3C(computer, communication and consumer electronics) and other fields; It is also a breakthrough to promote the development of national strategic emerging industries such as biological industry, emerging information industry, and new energy vehicles, and to enhance China's independent innovation capability and international competitiveness. The course 《Fabrication and Application of Microstructured Functional Surfaces》is a professional elective course that broadens students' relevant professional knowledge, cultivates students' dialectical thinking ability and innovative consciousness, and is an important personalized professional course for engineering students. Through learning the concept and scope of microstructured functional surfaces, manufacturing theories and technical characteristics of the typical microstructured functional surfaces, and application of typical the microstructured functional surfaces, students could master the basic principles of manufacturing and application of microstructured functional surfaces, broaden their horizons and scope of knowledge, and exercise their scientific research thinking. Enhance students' ability to analyze complex engineering problems. And through the experimental demonstration, deepen the understanding of the typical micro-structure functional surface related manufacturing and application theoretical knowledge, improve the ability of combining theory with engineering practice.
