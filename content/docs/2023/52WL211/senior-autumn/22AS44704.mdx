    zh: 中红外光谱范围一般为2-20微米，是分子指纹区，大气透射窗口，热辐射区，以及水、聚合物等材料的高吸收区。中红外激光在痕迹量气体检测、大飞机复合材料构件无损超声检测、人眼安全激光测风雷达、激光生物医疗及光电对抗有重要应用。中红外激光材料种类繁多，如何选择合适的晶体材料，结合设计合理的谐振腔型以实现高性能激光输出，是新波段激光器设计和制备面临的重要问题。本实践课程基于掺铥、掺钬2微米激光材料，通过仿真激光输出性能、晶体在强泵浦条件下的热效应，选择优化激光介质掺杂浓度和尺寸、泵浦方式和泵浦光参数以及谐振腔型和腔镜参数，并通过实验进一步迭代优化，从而实现最佳性能的2微米激光输出。在此基础上，以2微米声光调Q掺钬激光器泵浦源，泵浦ZnGeP2光学参量振荡器(OPO)，产生3~5微米可调谐激光输出。本课程要求学生掌握2微米固体激光器设计和调试方法，掌握2微米激光参数测试方法，特别是光束质量M2因子测量和光束变换，了解非线性光学器件OPO的结构及可调谐波长测量方法。
    en: The mid-infrared spectral range is generally 2-20 microns, which is the molecular fingerprint region, the atmospheric transmission window, the thermal radiation region, and the high-absorption region of materials such as water and polymers. Mid-infrared lasers have important applications in trace gas detection, non-destructive ultrasonic testing of composite components of large aircraft, eye-safety laser wind radar, laser biomedical treatment and countermeasures. There are many kinds of mid-infrared laser materials, and how to select the appropriate materials and combine the reasonably designed resonant cavity type to achieve high-performance laser output is an important problem in the design and preparation of new band lasers. Based on thulium-doped and holmium-doped 2-micron laser materials, this practical course selects and optimizes the doping concentration and size, pumping ways and pumping optical parameters, resonator structure and mirrors parameters of the laser by simulating the laser output performance and the thermal effect of the crystal under strong pumping conditions, and further iterative optimization through experiments to achieve the best performance of the 2-micron laser output. On this basis, a 2-micron acousto-optic Q-switched holmium-doped laser was used as a pumping source, and the ZnGeP2 optical parametric oscillator (OPO) was pumped to generate a 3~5 micron tunable laser output. This course requires students to master the design and adjusting methods of 2-micron solid-state lasers, master the measurement methods of 2-micron laser parameters, especially the M2 factor measurement and beam transformation, and understand the structure and tunable wavelength measurement methods of nonlinear optical devices OPO.
