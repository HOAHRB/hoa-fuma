    en: Physical Optics as an important fundamental and specialized course holds a significant position for students majoring in Electronic Science and Technology. It is a link between the previous and follow-up courses. Therefore, it becomes a necessary base for the students to learn later specialized courses and engage in future scientific researches. After the course, the students will be proficient in the basic ways of analyzing problems and gain the ability of solving the problems which may be encountered in later courses and research work. The main contents of this course are divided into two parts. The first part, based on the electromagnetism theory of optics, is to discuss the propagation of optics, it puts much emphasis on the optical propagation law through isotropy medium and anisotropic medium and the optical characteristics of interference, diffraction and polarization. The latter part discusses the interaction of light and substances, including induced birefringence of crystals, absorption, dispersion and scattering of the optics. It will pay much attention to the interpretation of physical ideas and the introduction of new concepts as well as advanced subjects of modern optics, such as the principle of laser, the basis of Fourier Optics and so on. In addition, the heuristic educational mode will be emphasized to foster the students’ ability of analyzing and solving problems.
