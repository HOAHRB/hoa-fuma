China urgently needs composite talents who are familiar with semiconductor manufacturing equipment and understand semiconductor manufacturing processes. Semiconductor Manufacturing Technology and Equipment is a course that integrates semiconductor manufacturing processes and equipment. The course aims to cultivate composite talents who understand semiconductor manufacturing processes and cutting-edge technologies, and are familiar with semiconductor manufacturing equipment. Through course learning, students will have a comprehensive understanding of the semiconductor manufacturing process from sand to chips, and become familiar with the key equipment components and principles throughout the entire process from wafer preparation to chip packaging . The course not only helps students understand how advanced chips are made, but also familiarizes them with the equipment used to make advanced chips and how these devices ensure chip quality. The course content includes: Introduction to Semiconductor Manufacturing, Introduction to Semiconductor Materials, Wafer Preparation Technology and Equipment, Overall Semiconductor Manufacturing Process, Advanced Lithography Technology and Equipment, Advanced Etching Technology and Equipment, Ion Implantation and Equipment, and Advanced Packaging Technology and Equipment. The course teaches advanced semiconductor manufacturing technology from two lines: manufacturing process and manufacturing equipment, laying a foundation for students to engage in semiconductor manufacturing, semiconductor equipment operation, research and development, production and manufacturing in the future.'
