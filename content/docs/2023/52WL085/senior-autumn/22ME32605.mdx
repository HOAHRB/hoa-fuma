It is a multidisciplinary field with contributions from human–computer interaction, artificial intelligence, robotics, natural language understanding and etc. This course discusses the principle and implementation of interactions between humans and robots through input and output devices. In this course, several commonly used devices and methods for human-robot interaction are introduced. After finishing this course, the students should understand the theoretical foundation, research focus and tendency, be familiar to the development method of commonly used HRI, master the ability of using HRI knowledge in practical projects. The contents of this course includes the development history of HRI, perceptual and cognitive foundations of HRI, commonly used HRI devices, interaction techniques, HRI simulation, human robot physical interaction, HRI based on joystick, glove, speech, gesture, kinect, emotion and SLAM and etc. The examination results are cumulative, including the final exam, regular assignments and projects.
