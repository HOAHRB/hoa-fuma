    en: Compliant robot is a kind of robot that can adapt to various environments and is highly flexible and deformable. They often employ flexible or soft materials and structures in order to freely move and deform in space. Bio-inspired robots are robots that mimic the form, structure and movement of living organisms. They often have a high degree of biomimetic performance that can simulate the perception, movement and interaction capabilities of living organisms. Compliant robots and bio-inspired robots are widely used, including manufacturing, medical care, aerospace, rescue and other fields. The course focuses on the two typical principles of flight and swimming, so that students can understand the inspiration source and mechanism of locomotion bionics, and lay a foundation for innovative design in the future. Fluid-driven compliant robot is a typical application example of the basic knowledge of fluid mechanics and mechanical engineering. It has a good demonstration and guiding role, and has a positive role in cultivating students' innovative consciousness. Through the course study, the students can understand the principle of typical locomotion bionic robot, and master the design and manufacturing method of typical compliant bionic robot.
