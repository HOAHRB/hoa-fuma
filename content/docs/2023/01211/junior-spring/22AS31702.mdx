    en: Optoelectronic technology is the main pillar of modern information technology, widely applied in areas such as communication, industrial inspection, precision measurement, medical field, security, and defense. As one of the core components of optoelectronic technology, optoelectronic devices have rapidly developed, with constant emergence of new technologies and improved performance, effectively driving progress in related industries. This course covers an introduction to optoelectronic devices, the physics foundation of optoelectronic devices, typical optoelectronic detection and imaging devices, optoelectronic modulation and transmission control devices, optoelectronic displays, as well as micro-nano optoelectronic devices. Starting from the basic theory of optoelectronic devices, the course focuses on the working principles, structural characteristics, and applications of optoelectronic devices such as photoconductors, PN junctions, and vacuum electron multiplier-based photodetectors. With advancements in semiconductor technology and device fabrication processes, array detectors have seen rapid development. The course explains the working mechanisms, performance, and applications of mainstream array detectors such as CMOS cameras, array single-photon detectors, and image intensifiers. Additionally, it introduces and analyzes key devices for modulation and transmission control in the field of optical communications, mainstream optoelectronic display devices based on liquid crystal and OLED technologies, as well as emerging micro-nano optoelectronic functional devices represented by photonic crystal metamaterials. This course targets undergraduate students majoring in optoelectronics, aiming to enhance their knowledge structure in optics, improve their professional level and capabilities, and lay a solid foundation of professional knowledge for future work or research in the field of optoelectronics.
