    en: Laser is one of the most important inventions of the 20th century. After years of technological advancements and scientific exploration, lasers have played an irreplaceable role in various fields. This course is based on the current development status of the laser field, and combines theoretical contents of applied optics, physical optics, and laser principles as prerequisites. It introduces students to the cutting-edge technologies and applications in the field of lasers, such as extreme ultraviolet laser sources and applications, the development process and current mainstream engineering design schemes of high-power solid-state laser systems, single-photon imaging technology, and lidar scene image reconstruction technology, based on the research directions of the teaching staff. Additionally, according to laboratory conditions, the course provides opportunities for students to experience the operation process of large-scale short-wavelength laser sources, gain in-depth understanding of damage risks in the operation of high-power solid-state laser installation, grasp the importance of beam quality control during laser transmission and amplification, conduct practical experiments on single-photon measurement of atmospheric attenuation coefficients and single-photon imaging, and learn about the principles of data acquisition for lidar and use 3D reconstruction methods to reconstruct unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) targets in different scenarios, understanding the differences in processing methods for real detection scenarios. Through a combination of theoretical teaching and practical application, this course enables students to quickly grasp the cutting-edge scientific and technological advancements in the field of lasers, cultivate their abilities to design experiments, analyze data, explore scientific problems, and promote teamwork skills.
