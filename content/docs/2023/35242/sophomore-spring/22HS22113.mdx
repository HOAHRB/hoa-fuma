    en: 'As a fundamental course in sociology, this course will outline the basis for dividing the development of sociological theory into three stages: classical, modern, and contemporary times. The Course will elucidate the historical and intellectual background of the emergence of classical sociology in the mid-19th century, and introduce the theoretical propositions of classical sociologists such as Comte, Marx, Durkheim, Pareto, Weber, and Simmel. Then it will also sort out the evolution of modern sociological theories and teach theories such as structural functionalism, social conflict theory, symbolic interactionism, social exchange theory, phenomenological sociology, and Ethnomethodology. Finally Based on the comprehensive orientation of sociological theory development since the mid to late 20th century, the course will explain the theoretical propositions of contemporary sociologists such as Giddens, Habermas, Bourdieu, Elias, Foucault, Beck, and Baudrillard.'
