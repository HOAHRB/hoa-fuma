    en: Art and culture have a wide range of implications in the world, but when problems arise, those who understand art and culture do not understand the law, while legal professionals with strong legal knowledge do not understand the profound essence of art, ultimately unable to use the law to uphold art. There is a significant difference between art and culture and a single patent issue, a single copyright issue, etc. Therefore, art law should be a professional and high perspective industry law. Currently, art law is mainly broadly classified as intellectual property, copyright law, etc., which cannot solve and avoid some problems in detail. The most superficial function of art law is to resolve disputes in areas such as artistic creation and art collection, safeguard rights and interests, and prevent legal red lines from being violated. The actual responsibility of art law is to protect national intangible cultural heritage, artistic and cultural heritage, and maintain national cultural security. Through the study of this course, law students have a deeper understanding of the art, culture, and legal industry categories in intellectual property law, copyright law, and other areas of law. Non law students can protect their own rights and interests in their work and creation, and prevent them from touching the legal bottom line. Some students may become leaders in national cultural education in the future, and have deeper thinking and decision-making in the protection of national intangible cultural heritage, art, and cultural relics.
