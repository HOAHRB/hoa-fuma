Since China implemented the policy of reform and opening up, foreign economic exchanges have become increasingly frequent, especially after China became a member of the WTO, and the country is in greater need of foreign-related legal talents. Therefore, the basic goal of this course is to cultivate foreign-related legal professionals who meet the needs of foreign economic exchanges and understand domestic law, WTO rules, and international business practices. By comprehensively applying the relevant knowledge and skills learned in the prerequisite courses, students need to focus on mastering the basic concepts, principles, and knowledge of the WTO, especially the content of some important agreements and classic cases. They will learn to analyze cases and problems encountered in practice based on the basic theory of the WTO, so that the theory can be truly applied to practice. The main teaching content of this course includes: the organizational law of the WTO (the emergence and development process of the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade, as well as the establishment, functions, institutions, decision-making and voting mechanisms, purposes and roles of the WTO), the basic legal principles of the WTO (most favored nation treatment and national treatment and exceptions), the substantive law of the WTO (relevant legal systems of tariff and non-tariff measures and trade remedies, such as "two countermeasures and one guarantee", GATS and TRIPS), the dispute settlement system of the WTO etc.'
