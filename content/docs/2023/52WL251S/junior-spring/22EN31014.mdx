    en: Water Quality Engineering is one of the core courses for the major of Water and Wastewater Engineering. The course is developed based on the discussions of water property, water quality and related parameters, water pollution, principles of water treatment. It also introduces the selection and design of water treatment systems based on raw and required purified water quality. The course will introduce the principle of natural water cycle and social water cycle, as well as the water quality variations in the process. The course will introduce in detail the physical chemical principles of water treatment such as coagulation and flocculation, sedimentation and flotation, filtration, adsorption, and disinfection, iron and mangenese removal, ion exchange, membrane technology, corrosion and scaling etc. Related experiments on various processes are arranged. Field work for course and graduation design will also be arranged.
