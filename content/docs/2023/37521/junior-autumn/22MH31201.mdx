    en: Medical imaging is a medical discipline that applies modern medical imaging technologies, such as X-rays, magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), ultrasound, and others, to diagnose diseases in the human body. It also involves guiding minimally invasive interventions in medical diagnosis and treatment using imaging techniques. It is a crucial component of clinical medicine. Medical imaging integrates and processes signals obtained from different imaging principles using advanced scientific technologies. This integration results in objective image information, allowing the visualization of the morphology and physiological functions of organs and tissues within the human body. It enables the differentiation between normal tissue structures and pathological changes. The objective and accurate judgment based on these images facilitates the observation of variations in internal human anatomy and function, significantly aiding human understanding, prevention, and treatment of diseases. This course primarily focuses on the fundamental principles and diagnostic applications of commonly used clinical imaging methods such as X-ray, CT (computed tomography), ultrasound, MRI, and PET (positron emission tomography). It emphasizes the basic imaging principles of these devices and their applications in common diseases. The goal is to assist students in constructing a comprehensive knowledge structure of medical imaging, understanding the significant role of medical imaging in clinical practice. Additionally, the course provides a brief introduction to the application of artificial intelligence technology in medical imaging, particularly in image processing. It aims to cultivate and stimulate students' abilities to identify and solve problems in practical applications, laying a crucial theoretical foundation for interdisciplinary collaboration between medicine and engineering. By building a systematic knowledge network, the course encourages students to apply knowledge from various disciplines and research methods, explore developments in the forefront of medical science, inspire and cultivate scientific literacy and research thinking, and train students in comprehensive analysis and problem-solving skills.
