Developed in collaboration with Associate Professor Zhao Ou from Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, “Behaviour and Design of High Strength Steel Structures” is an elective course tailored for undergraduate students who have completed prerequisite courses “Steel Structures”, “Design of Steel Structures” and “Stability of Steel Structures: Theory and Design”. This course aims to provide students with a thorough understanding of the behaviour and design of high strength steel structures, including the material properties of high strength steels, residual stresses of high strength steel welded sections, design of different types of high strength steel structural members and design of high strength steel connections. The course is divided into 4 parts, with each part lasting 4 class hours.
