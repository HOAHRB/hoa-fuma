The purpose of the course is to let the students master the basic theory knowledge of GNSS which is required for the subject of Instrumental Science and Technology, and knows the principle, technology, system application and development trend of satellite navigation system. Some typical satellite system, GPS、GLONASS、GALILEO, and Compass, are introduced in detail, including navigation algorithm, error analysis, system composition, signal structure.
