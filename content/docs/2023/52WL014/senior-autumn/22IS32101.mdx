    en: This course aims to meet the requirements of students in various fields of instrument science and technology. Starting from the application of engineering technology, it focuses on the optoelectric detection devices involved in the optoelectric conversion process of optoelectric detection systems. It provides a simple and easy explanation of the optoelectric effect principles and phenomena of various devices such as optoelectric emission devices, photoconductive devices, photovoltaic devices, thermoelectric devices, imaging devices, light modulators, etc., with a focus on introducing the characteristics, typical parameters, usage points, input circuits, and other contents of optoelectric devices. Using heuristic case teaching method, establish an intuitive and effective connection between typical optoelectric detection systems and optoelectric devices. In the analysis and design phase of the optoelectric detection system, guide students to think in reverse, select suitable optoelectric detection devices, and introduce the calculation methods of noise characteristics and frequency characteristics of the optoelectric detection system. Through the study of this course, students will have a profound understanding of the selection, use, and design of optoelectronic detection devices, and will have a preliminary ability to design, build, and analyze optoelectronic detection systems comprehensively.
