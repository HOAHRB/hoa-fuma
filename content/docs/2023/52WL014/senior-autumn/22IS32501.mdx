    zh: 工业计算机断层扫描技术简称工业 CT或 ICT，利用射线穿过物体发生衰减这一性质，将传感器获得的数据进行重建从而获得被检测物体的三维灰度图像，清晰、准确、直观地反映物体的内部结构、材料密度和缺损状况，在无损检测和无损评价领域得到了广泛运用，是公认的最佳无损检测手段。工业 CT成像技术结合了计算机技术、自动控制、机械、光学、图像学等多学科知识，拥有一套较为完整的理论体系。因此，本课程的开设对学生融会所学知识、提升专业能力、坚定“仪器报国”决心等将起到重要作用。 本课程将全面介绍工业CT的组成、原理、应用等相关知识，内容包括：X射线物理基础、CT扫描机的发展历史与核心元部件、CT成像与重建算法、伪影分析、重要性能参数介绍、计算机仿真与分析、相衬CT成像设备与原理、应用实例介绍，以及实验课等。 精密仪器、测控技术与仪器、智能感知工程等专业本科生及辅修本科生通过对本课程的学习，可系统掌握工业CT在体视觉检测、测量领域的相关知识，达成以下目标：①提升光电测量与仪器设计领域复杂工程问题所需的光学基础理论知识；②能够应用吸收衬度成像、相位衬度成像等CT成像基本原理，对精密测量与仪器设计领域的复杂工程问题进行条件假设、模型构建和知识表达；③能够基于X射线成像基本原理搭建实验平台、采用科学的实验方法获取实验数据，并分析实验数据的合理性；④能够通过文献检索工具，获取CT基本原理在精密测量与仪器设计领域的最新应用及研究进展。
    en: 'Industrial computer tomography (ICT) utilizes the property of radiation attenuation through objects to reconstruct the data obtained from sensors and obtain a three-dimensional grayscale image of the object being tested, which can clearly, accurately, and intuitively reflect the internal structure, material density, and defect status of the object. ICT has been widely used in the fields of non-destructive testing & evaluation and, now, is recognized as the best non-destructive testing method. As we known, ICT imaging technology combines multidisciplinary knowledge such as computer technology, automatic control, machinery, optics, and imaging, and has a relatively complete theoretical system. Therefore, this course will play an important role in helping the students to integrate their knowledge learned, enhance professional abilities, and strengthen their determination to serve the country. This course will comprehensively introduce the composition, principles, and applications of industrial CT, including the fundamentals of X-ray physics, the development history and core components of CT scanners, CT imaging and reconstruction algorithms, artifact analysis, introduction of the important performance parameters, computer simulation and analysis, introduction of phase contrast CT imaging equipment and principles, application examples, and experimental courses. Through the study of this course, undergraduate students majoring in precision instruments, measurement and control technology and instruments, intelligent perception engineering, etc. can systematically master the relevant knowledge of industrial CT in the field of body-vision detection & measurement, and achieve the following goals: (1) Enhance the optical basic theoretical knowledge required for complex engineering problems in the field of optoelectronic measurement and instrument design; (2) Capable of applying basic principles of CT imaging such as absorption contrast imaging and phase contrast imaging, to make conditional assumptions, model construction, and knowledge expression for complex engineering problems in the fields of precision measurement and instrument design; (3) Be able to build an experimental platform based on the basic principles of X-ray imaging, use scientific experimental methods to obtain experimental data, and analyze the rationality of experimental data; (4) The latest applications and research progress of CT basic principles in precision measurement and instrument design can be obtained through literature search tools.'
