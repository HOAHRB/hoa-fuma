With the rapid development of computers, more and more complex calculations have become possible. Utilizing computers for scientific computing has brought about significant economic benefits and has also fundamentally transformed science and technology itself: traditional science and technology only included theory and experimentation as two components, but with the use of computers, computation has become an equally important third component. The computational process mainly involves three stages: establishing mathematical models, developing computational methods for solving them, and implementing them on computers. This course introduces the basic methods and recent advances in modern scientific computing in a unified and concise manner, including numerical solutions to equations, as well as the concepts and methods of simulation. Students with a university-level mathematics background in science and engineering can choose this course, and it is also very beneficial for students involved in scientific and engineering design calculations.'
