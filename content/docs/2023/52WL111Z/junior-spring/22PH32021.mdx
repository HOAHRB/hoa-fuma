    en: Fiber Optics is one of the fundamental courses which is tightly related to some fields of science and technology of optical information，including optical network，optical communication and modern optical sensoring techniques. Via the course, the clear understanding for the classification, the mechanism of guiding light, the structure and composition of the fibers will be established. The students will comprehend the mode theory of fiber and master the transmission characteristics of light inner fiber waveguide. The students will make acquaintance of the principles of some basic fiber-optic components, such as passive (negative) components (connector, coupler, grating, interferometer, polarizer and polarization controller), active components (optical amplifier, fiber laser) and exchanging (switching) components (modulator, optical switch, OADM - Optical add-drop multiplexer, OXC – optical cross connector ). Through the course, the students will be familiar with the fiber-based sensor principle and master the construction of modern (optical) network and its development tendency. It is believed that this course will provide a certain assistance in determing the career direction to the students who will be engaged in the further study or to that who will take the position in the enterprises in the future.
