Modern Algebra also called abstract algebra, its research content including algebraic structure or algebraic system, with numbers, polynomials, matrix, transformation and their algebraic operation as concrete models, gives the concept of semigroup, group, ring and field. Mathematical logic concerns with the research of formal theory of man's reasoning with mathematics.
