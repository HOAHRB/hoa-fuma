Energy is an important material foundation for the survival and development of human society, and gas is an important component of urban energy. Gas supply is necessary for urban modernization construction and energy conservation and environmental protection. At present, the application field and scope of gas in China are constantly expanding. In the context of "dual carbon", gas will serve as a powerful supplement to renewable energy, helping to achieve stability and sustainability of energy supply. This course fully combines the current development status of gas supply systems in China, systematically and briefly teaches the basic theories and knowledge of urban gas supply systems, and appropriately introduces new technologies, processes, equipment, and materials of gas supply and application systems. Through the study of this course, students will understand and master the basic classification and characteristics of urban gas, the composition of gas pipeline network systems, the processes and main equipment of non pipeline gas transmission stations, the role and architecture of smart gas systems, and civil and commercial gas application equipment. Through the classroom teaching, discussions, and design of this course, students will further understand the overall composition of urban gas systems, with a focus on cultivating their ability to design and manage urban gas supply systems, analyze problems, design comprehensive solutions, team cooperation, language expression, research ability of complex engineering problems, and enhance innovation consciousness.
