    en: Gas combustion and application is a required course for undergraduates majoring in Building Environment and Energy Application Engineering. Gas is one of the important infrastructure of urban construction, but also an important part of urban energy, at the present stage in our country, combustion is the main way of energy utilization, gas is non-renewable energy, its clean and efficient combustion is of great significance for saving energy, improving atmospheric environment, and achieving the goal of " the carbon peak and carbon neutrality ". Combining with the current development and application of the gas industry in our country and based on the current norms, standards and regulations in our country, this course systematically introduces the basic theory and combustion methods of gas combustion, the design and calculation of burners and the basic knowledge of combustion equipment for civil, commercial and industrial use. The main contents of the course include gas combustion calculation, gas combustion reaction dynamics, gas flow mixing process of gas combustion, flame propagation of gas combustion, gas combustion methods, diffusion burners, atmospheric burners, fully premixed burners, gas interchangeability, civil gas appliances, gas industrial furnaces, gas-fired boilers, etc. Through classroom lectures, exercises, and assignments in this course, students can master the theoretical knowledge required for the application of gas combustion, understand the design and calculation methods of diffusion burners, partial premixed burners, and fully premixed burners, cultivate their comprehensive ability to analyze and solve problems, and be able to design, transform, and operate management of civil and industrial combustion equipment.
