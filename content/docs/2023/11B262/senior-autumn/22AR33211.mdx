The internship units are mainly design institutes that align with the students'' graduation project directions, which include heating, gas supply, and air conditioning. The primary tasks of this internship course are as follows: through practical activities such as learning standard practices in engineering design units, assisting in calculations, supporting design work, and conducting construction site investigations, students will develop initial abilities to comprehensively apply the knowledge they have learned to analyze engineering and technical issues.
