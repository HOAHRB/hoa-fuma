    en: In this course, interesting functional properties and their inner mechanisms in ferromagnetic functional materials will be introduced. At the first part of this course, the functional properties and their potential application of ferromagnetic functional materials will be briefly introduced. After that, the effects of chemical composition, microstructure, martensite transformation and the sample size and dimension on the functional properties of the materials will be discussed in detail. In this course, the effects of chemical composition on the martensite structure and physical properties, with emphasis on the microstructure and properties of different martensites in ferromagnetic shape memory alloys will be discussed. Then the inner relationship between different transformation processes and functional properties will be covered. The main body of this course is to introduce the synthesis methods of this material with different dimensions and sizes, including 3-dimensional porous foams, 2-dimensional ribbons/films, 1-dimensional microwires/fibers and 0-dimensional powders. The effects of the size and dimension on the according functional properties will be discussed. Furthermore, combined with the results based on different size and dimension of the materials, different properties in single crystalline, bulk polycrystalline, textured polycrystalline, micro-sized polycrystalline ferromagnetic functional materials will be presented. Finally, functional properties including magnetic-filed-induced strain, magnetocaloric effect, elastocaloric effect, thermal induced shape memory effect and superelasticity of different alloys will be introduced based on the intrinsic microstructure and martensite transformation of them.
