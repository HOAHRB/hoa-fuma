    en: Space environment simulation and experimental methods is a major course, which meets the development of space technology. In this course, focused on the environmental adaptation of materials and devices used in spacecrafts, the main techniques for the ground-simulation of near-earth-orbit space environment and the corresponding experiment methods are introduced. In order to ensure spacecraft reliability, it is essential to evaluate the space materials, components, mechanisms, or even the spacecraft system itself by various ground-based space environment simulation tests. This course describes the requirements, principles, facilities, and corresponding experiment methods for simulating thermal vacuum (vacuum effect), solar electromagnetic irradiation (thermal and ultraviolet effects), atomic oxygen (erosion effect), space charged particle radiation (ionization and displacement effects), space meteoroid and debris （impact and erosion effects）, space microgravity (microgravity effect), and lunar environment. Some important concepts are given, such as environmental adaptation？of materials and devices for spacecrafts, equivalent effect of space environment, equivalent condition region, accelerated test, acceleration factor, comprehensive space environment simulation etc. The applicability and limit of common space environment simulation tests are listed. The processes of the typical space environment simulation tests are illustrated, such as solar electromagnetic irradiation, space charged particle radiation, atomic oxygen exposure etc. Some in-situ dynamical measurement techniques during space environment simulation test are also introduced. The course is assumed to the undergraduates from materials science. The teaching materials are designed for students to train their ability of selecting space environment simulation testing items and methods for some typical spacecraft-used materials or devices served under special orbital environment. The knowledge in this course can also help conduct space environmental tailoring, test procedure, and experiment design of on-ground equivalent test and accelerated test for the typical space environment factors. The course is credit 1.5 and total class hours are 24 consisting of 24 lectures hours
