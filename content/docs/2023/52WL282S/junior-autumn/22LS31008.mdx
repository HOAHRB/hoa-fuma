Immunology is a branch of biology that covers the study of immune systems in all organisms. The study of the molecular and cellular components that comprise the immune system, including their function and interaction, is the central science of immunology. Clinical immunology is the study of diseases caused by disorders of the immune system (failure, aberrant action, and malignant growth of the cellular elements of the system). It also involves diseases of other systems, where immune reactions play a part in the pathology and clinical features. At the same time, the cross-study of immunology and oncology, structural biology and other disciplines is also becoming more and more in-depth. Based on the study of the general biology, biochemistry, animal physiology, genetics, molecular biology, cell biology curriculum theory, immunology can help students learn how to understand the biological macromolecules such as composition, structure and function in the life, especially for an organism and the environment interaction effect mechanism.
