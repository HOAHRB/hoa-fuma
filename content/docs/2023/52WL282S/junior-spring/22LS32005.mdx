    en: The comprehensive experiment of bioengineering is an important practice link to achieve the training goal of bioengineering specialty. Referring to some teaching methods in undergraduate thesis, the comprehensive experiment course is defined as a series of experiments with a scientific research nature. By organically integrating the contents of related courses, the students' knowledge is expanded, their ability of consulting data, thinking innovation and teamwork is trained, and their scientific research ability and innovative spirit are preliminarily cultivated, which is beneficial to the improvement of students' comprehensive quality. In this experiment, the scattered professional experiment teaching is separated from the theoretical teaching and an independent comprehensive experiment course of biological engineering is set up. On the basis of the basic, scattered and verifiable experimental courses in the early stage, it is reintegrated, expanded and broadened to upgrade into a comprehensive and designed "independent" experimental curriculum system, which, in turn, goes hand in hand with the theoretical curriculum system and becomes two pillars that are closely related and relatively independent in terms of talent training objectives. The course is arranged after the end of the professional theory teaching, that is, after the completion of the study of biochemistry, microbiology, genetic engineering, microbial fermentation engineering, biological matter separation, biological engineering equipment and other required courses. It is a systematic operation of a professional experiment conducted by the undergraduates majoring in biological engineering. At this stage, students are required to master the basic experimental operations of bioengineering and consolidate the basic theoretical knowledge of the profession. The experimental class is arranged in a concentrated and continuous manner, so that the previous experiment is prepared for the next experiment. The experiment content is interlocked to ensure the continuity of the experiment, so that more accurate experimental results can be obtained. The experiment content covers the whole process of bioengineering, including the preparation of medium, small amount of microorganism culture, plasmid extraction, PCR amplification, electrophoresis detection, DNA double enzyme digestion, ligation, transformation for engineered bacteria, covers the complete process of fermentation and post-processing, including fermentation to a larger batch in fermenters, induced expression, cell disruption, chromatographic separation, purification and identification, including genetic engineering, protein engineering, and fermentation engineering and other operating technologies, and cultivated comprehensive skills, reflecting a complete discipline system.
