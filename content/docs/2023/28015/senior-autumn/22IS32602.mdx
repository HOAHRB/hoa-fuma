    en: The magnetic levitation motor is a specialized type of motor that operates without contact between the stator and rotor. This course focuses on the magnetic levitation planar motor. Through the study of this course, students will gain a comprehensive understanding of the basic structure, principles, and characteristics of magnetic levitation planar motors. They will master the modeling of electromagnetic forces and electromagnetic torque in magnetic levitation planar motors, along with key methods for model validation. Additionally, students will acquire proficiency in the commutation methods for coil arrays in magnetic levitation planar motors, including the fundamental principles and methods of the six-degree-of-freedom electrical drive. The course aims to provide an understanding of the dynamic characteristics of each degree of freedom in the motion of magnetic levitation planar motors, enabling students to design suitable controllers for motion control requirements. Students will also master the control of suspended motion under the conditions of six degrees of freedom for magnetic levitation planar motors. The course covers an exploration of the future development trends and applications of magnetic levitation motors. Upon completion, students will be well-equipped to comprehend the intricacies of magnetic levitation planar motors and apply this knowledge in designing and controlling these systems. Additionally, they will be informed about the evolving trends and potential applications of magnetic levitation motor technology.
