    en: This course is for the students from school of energy science and engineering majoring in energy storage, which is a very important basic course for this department. This course is aiming at teaching the modelling and control design process of thermal storage systems. Through the teaching process, the students should have basic understanding of how to establish a model of linear system, simplify the system and get the transfer function. The students should understand the concept of “feedback control”. They also should master the time-domain analysis method of linear steady systems, the algebraic and frequency domain stability criteria for linear systems, and the frequency characteristic analysis method of linear steady systems. They would also study to analyze the dynamic and static performance of systems with Bode diagrams, understand the closed-loop frequency characteristics and their performance estimation. and use frequency method to synthesize systems and to correctly choose calibration steps and parameters.
