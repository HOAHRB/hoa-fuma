As an important module in the energy system, energy storage can make up for the congenital defects of strong randomness and low dispatchability of new energy power generation such as wind and solar, promote the development of new energy vehicles and energy Internet, and realize the replacement of the main energy from fossil energy to new energy on the production side and consumption side, which is considered to be the core physical means to support the energy revolution and achieve "carbon peak" and "carbon neutrality". The goal of this course is to enable students to understand the principles of various energy storage technologies, equipment, materials and systems, establish a set of energy storage system and environmental coupling analysis system through in-depth analysis of the working principles and characteristics of various energy storage technologies, conduct in-depth analysis and explanation of the system, deeply couple environmental engineering and energy storage technology, analyze the environmental impact mechanism of energy storage systems, and explore environmental protection methods in the whole life cycle of energy storage systems, which can help students have an in-depth understanding of various energy storage working principles and systems. To help students better grasp the application theory of energy storage system science and engineering, expand their horizons and reserve knowledge, stimulate students' interest in research, and guide students to carry out systematic learning.
