It is one of the important elective courses in related disciplines such as energy storage, new energy science and engineering, and energy internet, with a wide range of applications. This course is aimed at students in third year or above, and is designed based on their understanding of basic knowledge of electrical engineering, thermodynamics, and automatic control. Course purpose: The purpose of the course is to start from the analysis of multi capacity, multi-scale, and multi-level demand regulation in the new energy power system, such as peak shaving, frequency regulation, backup, black start, and demand response support.
