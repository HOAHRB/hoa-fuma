    zh: 本课程的主要目的是使学生系统掌握材料发生摩擦磨损的基本原理、规律和测试技术。学生在了解固体表面几何形状特性、表面物理化学性质及其表征方法、固体表面接触时的相互作用和变化的基础上，需掌握基本的摩擦理论及影响摩擦的因素，懂得选择合适的理论模型（如分子-机械理论、粘着理论、能量理论）来分析和解决工程实践问题。针对不同类型磨损形式，特别是常见的磨粒磨损、粘着磨损、疲劳磨损、腐蚀磨损、剥层磨损、微动磨损等，学会选用相应的材料和采取不同的减摩抗磨表面设计，掌握研究不同类型磨损的实验方法和检测技术。本课程培养学生从材料科学的角度，结合磨损表面的摩擦力学和摩擦化学特征，来分析磨损形貌特征、磨损程度与磨损机理之间的内在关系，建立起“根据工件的实际工况条件来选择材料类型、组织及处理工艺”的概念，为今后从事材料摩擦磨损的检测、磨损机理和防护研究奠定基础。本课程除了摩擦与磨损机理、摩擦学测试技术和减摩耐磨材料等传统领域外，还介绍当今空间摩擦学、纳米摩擦学、生物与仿生摩擦学等技术领域的最新进展。
    en: The objective of this course is to provide the undergraduate students basic knowledge on fundamental principles, characteristics and testing methods of friction and wear, and to develop their ability to analyze and solve the practical problems in friction and wear of materials. The topics covered in this course include surface physical and chemical characteristics on solid surfaces, tribo-stressing contact of solids, basic friction principles such as molecule-mechanical theoretical model, adhesion contact model and energy dissipation theory etc., different wear modes and basic mechanisms of materials such as abrasive wear, adhesion wear, fatigue wear, corrosive wear (include oxidation wear), delamination wear, fretting wear, etc., friction-reducing and wear-resistant materials, different friction and wear testing methods, failure analysis of worn surfaces, and recent research progress on advanced tribology, especially such as space tribology, nanotribology and biomimetic tribology. It also provides the students an opportunity to widen their sights on tribomaterials selection, friction-reducing and anti-wear strategies after obtaining a better understanding on the relationship among tribomaterials selection and wear-resistant surface design, processing, microstructure, wear degree and mechanisms.
