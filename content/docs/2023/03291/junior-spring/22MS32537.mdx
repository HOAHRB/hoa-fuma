    en: 'At present, most of the research on material bonding is focused on structural materials, and only the structural reliability of the joint is considered in bonding process. Compared with structural materials, the bonding of functional materials should not only consider the structural reliability, but also consider the bonding process and the introduction of the joint (especially for miniaturized devices) can not affect the performance of the material function. So the influencing factors are more complex. However, there are few studies on the bonding of functional material, and there are no courses specifically for functional material bonding in our university. In order to promote the application of advanced functional materials, it is very important to carry out in-depth research on functional materials bonding and open relevant courses to stimulate students'' interest in this research. With the background of advanced bonding technology and application of functional materials, this course follows up with the latest research progress and focuses on: 1. Advanced bonding technology and application of magnetic functional materials; 2. 2. Advanced packaging technology and application of all-solid state battery materials; 3. Advanced bonding technology and application of transparent ceramic materials; 4. Advanced bonding technology and application of thermoelectric materials; 5. Advanced bonding technology and application of biological materials; 6. Advanced bonding technology and application of structural and functional composite materials. Through the study of this course, students are trained to think about problems from the engineering practice, to deal with problems and experiment ability, to cultivate their innovative spirit and practical ability, to improve their comprehensive scientific research ability.'
