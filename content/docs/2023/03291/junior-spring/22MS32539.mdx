    en: Metal 3D printing is an advanced manufacturing technology that uses metal materials to produce complex-shaped products through layer-by-layer manufacturing based on computer-aided design models. It’s an advanced manufacturing technology with significant advantages, including high material utilization, short production cycle, customization and the ability to produce complex structures. It is widely used in aerospace, defense, automotive, electronics, and biomedical fields. However, due to its specific manufacturing process, the product still has many quality problems, especially in terms of repeatability and reliability, which seriously restrict its large-scale industrial production and application. Therefore, it is urgent to develop quality control technology, monitor the additive process in real time, and provide theoretical guidance for stabilizing the additive process through numerical simulation. The content of this course mainly includes the concept and principle of metal 3D printing process, monitoring methods and industrial applications, the basis of heat flow transmission, defect and deformation simulation, tissue simulation and digital twin technology of metal 3D printing process. This course aims to guide students to master the basic concepts and principles of metal 3D printing, various monitoring methods of metal 3D process and its monitoring principles and be able to propose reasonable monitoring methods for different engineering application fields and improve them according to specific application needs. Furthermore, students are guided to accomplish the numerical simulations of deformation, structure and defects, which may provide theoretical guidance for practical engineering application problems.
