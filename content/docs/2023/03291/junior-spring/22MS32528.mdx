This course mainly focuses on explaining the application of advanced materials joints and dissimilar materials joints as the welding of advanced materials and dissimilar materials has always been the research hotpot of the electron beam welding. The electron beam cutting, electron beam surface treatment and other special processing methods of electron beam are also introduced in this course. The course includes two parts: application of electron beam welding and electron beam processing technology. The process principle of electron beam welding and the application of advanced materials joints and dissimilar materials joints in the industrial fields are the main content of “application of electron beam welding” part. The electron beam welding quality control technology will be introduced by the explaining of welding process of several typical advanced materials and dissimilar materials.The technical principle and application status of electron beam cutting, electron beam drilling and electron beam surface treatment are explained in the “electron beam processing technology” part. Students are required to understand the electron beam weldabilities of typical advanced materials and dissimilar materials, grasp the basic methods of electron beam process, know the applications of electron beam process technology in the hot work area. The course, in a total of 16 hours, uses a combination of traditional blackboard and multimedia teaching methods.'
