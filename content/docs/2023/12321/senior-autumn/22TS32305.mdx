However, the development of transportation infrastructure materials with asphalt/cement-based materials as the core has long relied on empirical trial and error methods, which are inefficient, ineffective, and costly, becoming an international challenge that restricts the improvement of transportation infrastructure resilience. In recent years, computer technology related to materials science has been steadily shifting from "technology development and pure computational research" to "discovery and design of new materials guided by close collaboration between computational results, machine learning, and data mining and computation". At present, digital design methods for materials such as new energy and alloy materials have been adopted in the research and development process. However, there is relatively little research on traditional transportation infrastructure materials, and it is necessary to strengthen research to promote the digital transformation of the transportation infrastructure materials industry. Therefore, offering a specialized course on digital design of transportation infrastructure materials to provide students with systematic theoretical knowledge and practical skills training is an inevitable measure to meet the research and development needs of transportation materials in the new era. Through the study of this course, students will master the digital design methods of transportation infrastructure materials, break through the traditional trial and error mode, and reserve interdisciplinary talents to enhance the independent innovation ability of transportation infrastructure materials in China. The task of this course is to enable students to master the theory and basic knowledge of digital design of transportation infrastructure materials through teaching, and to apply digital tools such as high-throughput computing, machine learning modeling, and big data analysis in the research and development of asphalt/cement-based materials.
