    en: This course systematically introduces the principles of highway performance variation, subgrade maintenance technology, highway performance detection and assessment technology, highway maintenance technology, and highway management systems. Integrating the new concept of "smart highways" the course emphasizes intelligent detection technologies for highways, such as multifunctional pavement detection vehicles, lightweight detection technology, and Robot inspection technology based on video detection. Building upon traditional highway maintenance technology, the course incorporates new concepts of "green" and "carbon peaking and carbon neutrality goals" with a focus on introducing green maintenance materials and technologies, including pavement recycling technology, low-carbon maintenance materials, and smart maintenance materials. Furthermore, the course integrates new ideas such as big data and deep learning into existing road management systems, introducing scientific maintenance decision methods and systems for highway networks. Through the explanation of these new materials, technologies, processes, and methods, students will gain a comprehensive understanding of the current trends and directions in highway maintenance and management, broadening their perspectives. The teaching of this course aims to deepen students' understanding of road alignment design, subgrade and pavement design from the perspective of pavement usage and systemic engineering.
