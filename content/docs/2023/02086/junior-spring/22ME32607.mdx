    zh: 《空间机器人》是机器人工程专业本科生的专业选修课之一，旨在帮助学生深入理解空间机器人在空间探测和在轨服务中的核心技术及其未来发展趋势。课程内容涵盖空间机器人的基本原理、关键技术和空间应用，重点讲解空间机器人系统设计、运动控制、力控制和仿真测试等方面的内容。 主要内容： 1.空间机器人概述：介绍空间机器人在航天领域的重要作用，阐述空间机器人系统的组成、工作原理及当前研究热点。分析空间机器人技术的发展历程，探索未来的技术发展方向和应用前景。 2.空间机器人运动学与动力学：讲解空间机器人在微重力环境中的运动学和动力学建模方法，为运动规划和控制提供理论基础。 3.空间机器人运动规划：研究空间机器人关节空间运动规划、笛卡尔空间运动规划，及冗余空间机器人避关节极限与奇异问题的处理方法，确保任务的顺利执行。 4.空间机器人运动控制：探讨空间机器人在执行任务过程中的运动控制策略，确保机器人精准、高效地完成任务。 5.空间机器人力控制：介绍空间机器人与外部环境交互时的力控制技术，特别是在操作任务中的力-位混合控制方法。 6.空间机器人系统：了解空间机器人机械系统、控制系统、感知系统和遥操作系统的组成与工作原理。 7.空间机器人系统仿真及测试：讲解空间机器人系统的仿真技术和实验测试方法，帮助学生掌握如何验证空间机器人性能。 8.大型空间机械臂设计：重点研究我国空间站大型机械臂的设计原理及其验证技术。 通过本课程的学习，学生将掌握空间机器人设计、运动控制、仿真测试等方面的核心技术，培养解决实际应用问题的能力。课程结合理论与实际案例，注重动手实践和创新思维的培养，为学生从事航天工程、空间机器人研发等相关领域的工作奠定坚实基础。 课程目标： 1.了解空间机器人在空间任务中的应用与发展趋势； 2.培养学生在空间机器人系统建模、运动规划、控制与仿真测试等方面的综合能力； 3.掌握空间机器人基础理论与设计方法。 适用对象： 本课程适合机器人工程专业的本科生，尤其适合对空间机器人技术感兴趣并希望从事相关领域研究与应用开发的学生。
    en: '"Space Robotics" is an elective course for undergraduate students majoring in Robotics Engineering. The course aims to help students deeply understand the core technologies of space robotics in space exploration and on-orbit servicing, as well as its future development trends. The content of the course covers the basic principles, key technologies, and applications of space robotics, with a focus on system design, motion control, force control, and simulation/testing methods. Course Content: 1.Overview of Space Robotics: This section introduces the importance of space robotics in the aerospace field, describing the components, working principles, and current research hotspots of space robotic systems. It also analyzes the development history of space robotics technology and explores future technological trends and application prospects. 2.Kinematics and Dynamics of Space Robots: This part explains the kinematic and dynamic modeling methods of space robots in microgravity environments, providing the theoretical foundation for motion planning and control. 3.Motion Planning of Space Robots: This section studies joint-space motion planning, Cartesian-space motion planning, and methods for handling redundant space robots, joint limits, and singularity avoidance to ensure the smooth execution of tasks. 4.Motion Control of Space Robots: This section explores the motion control strategies of space robots during task execution, ensuring that robots can respond accurately and efficiently to mission requirements. 5.Force Control of Space Robots: This part introduces the force control techniques for space robots interacting with external environments, especially force-position hybrid control methods for operation tasks. 6.Space Robot Systems: Students will learn about the components and working principles of space robot mechanical systems, control systems, perception systems, and teleoperation systems. 7.Space Robot System Simulation and Testing: This section covers the simulation technologies and experimental testing methods for space robot systems, helping students understand how to validate the performance of space robots in practical applications. 8.Design of Large-Scale Space Manipulators: This part focuses on the design principles and verification technologies of large-scale space manipulators used in the Chinese Space Station. 9.Through this course, students will master core technologies in space robot design, motion control, and system simulation/testing. The course combines theoretical instruction with practical case studies, emphasizing hands-on practice and the development of innovative thinking, thus providing a solid foundation for students to pursue careers in aerospace engineering, space robotics research, and related fields. Course Objectives: 1.Understand the applications and development trends of space robotics in space missions. 2.Develop comprehensive abilities in space robot system modeling, motion planning, control, and simulation/testing. 3.Master the basic theories and design methods of space robotics. Target Audience: This course is suitable for undergraduate students majoring in Robotics Engineering, particularly those interested in space robotics technology and wishing to pursue research and application development in this field.'
