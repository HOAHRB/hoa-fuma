With the rapid progress of modern technology and industry, these vibrations have brought unprecedented challenges to many fields, especially aerospace, precision manufacturing and optical instruments. Therefore, the precision vibration isolation technology came into being and became the key to ensure the stable performance and high precision of the equipment. Precision Vibration Isolation Theory and Technology, as an interdisciplinary comprehensive course, skillfully integrates the essence of mechanics, control science, materials science and other disciplines. This course not only focuses on the depth of theoretical knowledge, but also emphasizes the mastery of practical skills, and is committed to cultivating the innovation and interdisciplinary mindset of students. The content of this course follows closely the latest research results and trends in precision vibration isolation technology, while maintaining the importance of practicality and engineering applications. Therefore, it provides students with a solid foundation of professional knowledge and practical support for their future scientific research and engineering practice. Through the study of this course, not only can students gain an in-depth understanding of the core principles of precision vibration isolation technology, but they will also master its practical application skills. This course is based on theory teaching, with a total of 24 credit hours and 1.5 credits. In terms of teaching content, it mainly introduces single-degree-of-freedom vibration systems, multi-degree-of-freedom vibration systems, numerical calculation methods of vibration and its control, and focuses on discussing their basic theories, analysis methods and their applications in engineering. In terms of teaching methods, the combination of lectures, case studies, follow-up tests and conclusive report enables students to fully grasp the core knowledge and practical skills of precision vibration isolation technology, as well as cultivate their innovation and problem solving ability, and then enhance their comprehensive quality. In the evaluation part, we adopt a method combining attendance, classroom interaction, conclusive report and PPT presentation to measure students' mastery of precision vibration isolation technology and practical application ability. Through well-designed theory teaching and evaluation system, this course aims to provide students with an opportunity to learn precision vibration isolation technology in a comprehensive way, and at the same time, train them to become high-quality talents with innovative spirit and practical ability.
