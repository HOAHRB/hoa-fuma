    en: 'The course " Micro and Nano Sensing and Measurement Technology" is an elective course primarily designed for undergraduate students majoring in instrument-related disciplines or those pursuing a minor in instrument-related subjects. The course aims to cultivate students'' understanding of the fundamental principles, instruments, and methods of micro/nano sensing and geometric measurement technology, as well as their ability to solve practical precision coordinate measurement problems. The course covers the following aspects: (1) Origin and development of geometric measurement: Introduction to the purpose and measurement objects of machining measurement technology, as well as the development of coordinate measurement technology and coordinate measuring machines. (2) Objectives of coordinate measurement: Introduction to the objectives of geometric measurement, standardization of geometric features, interchangeability verification, filtering, fitting, and other key aspects of geometric measurement. (3) Basic principles and equipment technology of geometric measurement: Introduction to the basic principles of coordinate measurement technology, classification and structure of coordinate measuring machines, and auxiliary equipment. (4) Core of coordinate measurement - sensors: Introduction to classical sensors used in coordinate measurement, including classic contact measurement sensors, vision sensors, non-contact distance sensors, scanning probe microscopy technology, etc. (5) New coordinate measurement instruments: Introduction to basic information about laser trackers, articulated arm coordinate measuring machines, 3D nano measurement, and nano positioning devices. (6) Error evaluation in coordinate measurement: Introduction to the error evaluation process in coordinate measurement and the main methods for determining measurement result uncertainty. Through this course, students will gain an understanding of coordinate measurement technology, including its development history, classical structures and key components, mainstream detection principles and measurement methods, data processing, as well as precision and error evaluation. Upon completion of the course, students should possess the ability to comprehensively apply principles and methods of geometric coordinate measurement in the design, experimentation, and data analysis of coordinate measurement systems. They should also demonstrate innovation awareness during the process of system analysis and design.'
