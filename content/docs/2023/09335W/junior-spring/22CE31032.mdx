The course lays a solid theoretical foundation for students in their engagement in future work of construction management and cost estimation. This course introduces the theory and method of cost estimation in a comprehensive and systematic way. It embodies the two parts of theory and practice. The former part mainly focuses on the theory of estimation, i.e., current composition of cost estimation in China, construction quota, the valuation method of cost estimation based on bill of quantities, the budget of construction drawing and the concept and method of construction settlement. The latter part mainly focuses on cost estimation practice, systematically introducing calculation rules and methods of building area based on the Code of Valuation with Bill Quantity of Construction. Combined with the actual situation, the rules for calculating bill quantities are also introduced. The contents of the course are significant in business work of the registered cost estimators and consultants. This course aims to train students be familiar with the construction quota, understand the basic principles of construction quota making, master the composition and calculation method of construction cost, and be equipped with preliminary cost estimation ability of civil engineering and decoration engineering
