    en: 20th Century English Literature is an elective course offered to sophomore English majors. Focusing on major writers and their major works in the first and second halves of the 20th century English literature, this course aims to explore how significant social events and movements and socio-cultural thoughts, such as Modernism, Post-modernism, Post-colonialism and Feminism, have impacted the literary tradition and inspired literary endeavors. This course engages students in textual analysis of poems, short stories, novels, and dramas in the 20th century. Students will read for relatedness with historical background, themes, characterization, techniques, and language style. This course is to cultivate students’ ability to understand and appreciate English literary works and improve their basic language skills and critical thinking. It also aims to develop students’ ability to do research and literature review as well as write critical essays following the MLA format. Major authors include Yeats, Auden, Thomas; Bernard Shaw, Beckett; Woolf, Joyce, Mansfield, Lessing, Lodge, etc. Accumulative assessment includes classroom participation, report, group study, papers and final exam.
