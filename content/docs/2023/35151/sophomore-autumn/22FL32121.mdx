Being designed as both extension and expansion of the students’ Chinese cultural knowledge in English, this course aims to enhance the students’ understanding as well as their identification with the Chinese culture, integrating students’ learning of English and Chinese culture with the development of their intercultural communicative competence. Upon completion of this content-based course, students should be able to express，negotiate and construct their Chinese cultural identity and promote Chinese culture in the English language with critical cultural awareness and from both Chinese and global perspectives. The content of this course highlights the major themes of Chinese culture ranging from the traditional to the contemporary, including geo-economic conditions, historical heritage, philosophical and spiritual heritage, language and literature, government and society, educational system and culture of learning, ethnicity and customs, and popular culture etc. Course evaluation is based on formative assessment of the students’ overall performance in class participation, assignments, course project and final exam.
