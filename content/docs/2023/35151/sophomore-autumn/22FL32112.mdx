Course Description History of European Literature is a selective course for the sophomores of English Department. It is a preliminary course for other literature courses. In the chronological order, taking into consideration the historical background and the cultural context, this course traces the historical development of European literature. It introduces students to the various literary schools in the different stages of European literature and their respective features, explores the style of the representative writers of different literary periods as displayed in their masterpieces, analyzes the major themes and artistic features of the important works, and explains some basic literary terms and their general features. With the history of European literature as the framework, supplemented by textual analysis, this course aims to facilitate students’ overall view of the history of European literature and mastery of the fundamental knowledge of literature so as to improve students’ capability of comprehending and appreciating literary works and cultivate their literary temperament. It also aims to instruct students to understand literary works from the historical and cultural perspectives and inspire students’ philosophical reflection of the common problems of humanity to help students establish correct life value. The course consists of the following chapters; the ancient Greek and Roman literature, the renaissance literature, the 17th-century Neoclassical literature, the 18th-century Enlightenment literature, the 19th-century Romanticism, the 19th-century Realism, the 20th-century Realism, Modernism and Postmodernism.
