    en: History of English Literature is a compulsory course offered to the English juniors. This course aims to help the students get an overview of the development of English literature, and systematically grasp the knowledge of English literature and literary genres as well as the basic methods of literary criticism. It also aims to cultivate students’ ability to understand and appreciate English literature, do some preliminary research independently and improve their critical thinking. This course chronologically traces the history of English literature within the historical, social and ideological context, explores the characteristics of the dominant literary schools, representative writers and works, with a close textual analysis of the selected works from different periods in the development of English literature. The outcome of the course will be evaluated in an accumulative way, including classroom participation, quizzes, report, group project study, term paper, and final exam.
