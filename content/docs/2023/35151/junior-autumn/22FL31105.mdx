The course aims to develop students' ability to communicate with people of different cultural backgrounds with the ultimate goal of building a community of human destiny in the era of globalization. The main content covers the main features, core concepts and basic principles of intercultural communication as well as its socio-cultural practices in the context of globalization. Through this course, students will learn about the basic concepts and principles of global intercultural communication, experience its diversity and complexity, recognize the insights that the anthropocosmic worldview, values and ethics of traditional Chinese culture offers for global intercultural communication. The students are required to understand the basic characteristics, dynamic mechanisms and ways of intercultural dialogue in the context of globalization, and develop their abilities and skills in intercultural criticality, intercultural interactions and intercultural adaptation in the era of globalization and diversity, and be able to deal with intercultural conflicts and barriers in their real-life intercultural encounters. This course will provide students with a solid professional foundation for intercultural personhood development and prepare them for active participation in building a global community of a shared future. The course assessment is based on the overall performance of students’ class participation, class presentations, course project report and final examination.
