Based on the nature of the course and the science of the English language, the design idea of this course is to carry out the introduction of corpus, grammar discussion and learning practices gradually so that students are able to formulate a complete understanding of the English language rules, and cultivate independent thinking and practical problem-solving abilities. In terms of the teaching mode, with the consideration to several teaching concepts such as heuristic, discovery, interactive and situational, the course requires students to do independent observation, pair work and group discussion as well as share the language rules together. Due to the fact that students must have obtained a certain degree of mastery of English grammatical rules in their pre-university stages, this course focuses on some key points of English grammar instead of every aspect of it.
