    en: 19th Century American Literature is an elective course offered to senior English majors. This course aims to cultivate students’ ability to understand and appreciate American literature in the 19th Century, improve their basic language skills, critical thinking and humanistic quality. It also aims to develop students’ ability to do research, and write critical essays about literature in English following the standard format. This course covers the representative works of some important American writers in the 19th Century. It focuses on two literary trends, namely American Romanticism and Realism, examines the interconnection between these literary activities and socio-historical and cultural factors and explores the literary imagination of the American identity, American past and its present. This course engages students in close textual analysis of selected works in the historical and cultural context so as to enable them to grasp the characterization, themes, techniques, and language style and grasp the basis skills and format for writing critical essays about literature in English. Accumulative assessment includes classroom participation, report, group project study, term paper and final exam.
