It is an important part of English majors’ literature courses and paves the way to other literature courses to be learnt later. This course covers briefly the historical development of American literature with its respective historical, social and ideological context. In the chronological order, taking into consideration the historical background and the ideological context, this course systematically traces the historical development of American literature. It introduces students to the various literary schools in the different stages of American literature and their respective features, explores the writing styles of the representative writers of different literary periods as displayed in their masterpieces, analyzes the major themes and artistic features of the important works, and explains some basic literary terms and their general features, so that the students can have an overall view of the history of American literature and grasp the fundamental knowledge of literature. This course aims at cultivating students’ interest in English literature and enhancing their understanding of American culture through the reading and analysis of individual literary works. History of American Literature helps to improve students’ sensibility towards English, promote their basic language skills and enhance their understanding of American literature and culture. The course consists of the following chapters：Literature of Native Indians, Literature of Colonial Period, American National Literature, Romanticism, Realism, Naturalism, Modernism, Post-Modernism, Women Writings, Afro-American literature, American Jewish Literature and Chinese American Literature. The grading system consists of class participation, homework, quizzes, and the final examination.
