    en: College German (4), this course is a second foreign language course for English majors. The total class hours are 224 and it is completed in five stages. 192 class hours in the first four stages, which are professional compulsory courses; The fifth stage is 32 class hours, which is a professional limited elective course. College German (4) is a compulsory course for third year students majoring in English in foreign language schools, and it is also the basis for students to continue to learn German as a second foreign language. The course mainly teaches common German vocabulary and basic German grammar, focusing on enabling students to understand basic German language knowledge and basic national conditions of German speaking countries, be familiar with basic German vocabulary and sentence patterns in daily life, and master the basic skills of German listening, speaking, reading, writing and translating. The course mainly includes topics such as occupation, health, banking, mass media, wishes and news. Grammatical points include comparative adverbial clauses, process passive, state passive, special verbs and subjunctive forms. Through the study of grammar, students can focus on understanding the dynamic and virtual usage rules of German, and then more comprehensively understand the frame structure, morphology and syntax of German sentences. The content of the textbook is closely related to the Chinese and German conditions and campus life. By learning the text and introducing German customs, geography, history, culture and other aspects, students can enrich their international vision and improve their cross-cultural communication ability. At the same time, the communicative teaching method is used in the classroom to determine a communicative intention around a theme, and to organize a dialogue in groups, so that students can speak German in class and participate in classroom teaching. The classroom will also make use of the comparison of vocabulary and grammar between English and German, so that English students can quickly and better accept German based on their own English knowledge. The test scores of this course are cumulative, including class participation, homework and final exam. 4 class hours per week.
