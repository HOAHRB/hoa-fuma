Through this course, the students will have an overall, systematic and in-depth knowledge about English vocabulary, will master the ways to analyze and the mechanisms to explain lexical phenomena in the English language, and thus will be able to study into the rules and laws governing the English vocabulary. Meanwhile, through the study of etymology, the students will have a deeper understanding of British and American society, history and culture. This course also facilitates further English language learning and courses in linguistics. This course mainly involves: basic concepts and theories of lexicology; the classification, organization and development of English vocabulary; borrowing and sources of borrowing; ways of word formation; types of motivation; word meaning, meaning relations and meaning changes; word cognition, understanding and context. Besides, in order to improve the students’ interest in the course, one or two “stories of etymology” involving large numbers of English words, idioms and sayings are provided at the beginning of each lecture concerning various aspects of the society, history and culture of English speaking nations, which will also enrich their cultural knowledge.
