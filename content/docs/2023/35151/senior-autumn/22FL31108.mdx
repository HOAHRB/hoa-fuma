Course Description The course of Chinese-English Interpretation is open to the senior year English major students. In this course, students will get their training on techniques and skills of Chinese-English interpretation. After the study, students are expected to be able to interpret general material on conferences and business negotiations. The top of them can reach the level required by the Intermediate Level of Interpretation Certificate Test of SISU. The course provides training to students on word equivalence, and lexical and textual transformation. Students will also learn localization principles under Skopo theories. The text book used is An Intermediate Course of Interpretation (fourth edition) compiled by Mei Deming，published by Shanghai Foreign Language Education Press in 2019. Updated complimentary materials are applied to class training as well. The course covers the following topics: the interpretation of titles, proverbs, quotations and number, and interpreting materials on reception service, conversations, interviews, ceremonial speeches, informative speeches, persuasive speeches, academic speeches, business speeches, and popular science speeches.
