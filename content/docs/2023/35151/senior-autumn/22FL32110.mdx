    en: Language and Society is an optional course for Seniors majoring in English, which is designed to provide students with critical English language skills, cognitive abilities, and to enable students to understand and explain social linguistic phenomena from a scientific perspective. This is an introductory course of Sociolinguistics. It covers major topics about language and society, such as language and social stratification, language and social groups, language and gender, language and context, language and social interaction, language and country, and language and geography. Students will be trained through reading relevant literature materials, learning the basic concepts and terms of sociolinguistics, with special focus on cultivating students' language awareness concerning analysis and criticality, and understanding the close relationship between language use and society. Students will also be enabled to develop their cultural awareness and enhance their intercultural communicative competence in different scenarios in the age of globalization. The assessment is multidimensional, incorporating class participation, essays, project defense, and course paper.
