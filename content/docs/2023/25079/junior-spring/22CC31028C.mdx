Due to short duration the course includes only the main methods of TA, mostly without detailed technical analysis: it gives the opportunities to present the essentials of TA for students of various training levels. The following methods of TA will be considered: thermogravimetry (TG), differential scanning calorimetry (DSC), simultaneous thermal analysis (STA), dilatometry(DL) and thermomechanical analysis (TMA), dynamic vapor sorption (DVS), visual thermal analysis (VTA). Using of some supported methods will be also considered: mass spectrometry (MS) and IR spectroscopy (FTIR) for the investigation of gas evolution. The course also focus on training the students on interpretation of thermograms for different physical-chemical processes and the thermal behavior of modern materials. The final test scores are cumulative including the final test, ordinary performance and assignments. At the end of this course, students should: 1) Understand and acquire the main methods of thermal analysis. Have ability to apply the above knowledge to solve practical problems by methods of thermal analysis and to interpret thermograms, to determine thermochemical characteristics of different processes and thermodynamic properties of compounds and materials. 2) TA is a vast field of analytical chemistry and has applications in all branches of natural science. TA deals with real processes in wide range of temperature and is an important and necessary methods of modern scientific researches. Accordingly, students will obtain the ability to propose a solving of different basic and applied problems using methods of TA.'
