    en: Computer Numerical Control of Machine Tools is one of the most important courses to the students majoring mechanical engineering, which will lay a knowledge foundation for the design of digital manufacturing system, flexible manufacturing system and intelligent manufacturing system. This course introduces the basic principles of NC machining, CNC programming, the theory of interpolation, CNC unit, techniques about servo drive and measuring，etc. The main content is as follows. Chapter 1 includes the basic concepts, principles, composition, classification, methodologies about the CNC machining, introduces the background, origin and development trends of CNC technologies. Chapter 2 discusses the CNC programming process, includes the NC encoding system, criterion, CNC machining processes, mathematics for NC programming, and automatic programming, presents several programming examples for turning, milling, and hole-making. Chapter 3 introduces the principle of interpolation (control of tool paths), functions and implementation about reference pulse interpolation and data sampling interpolation. Chapter 4 introduces the functions, hardware, software, interface, and PLC about CNC unit. Chapter 5 includes the principle and application about the NC drive system. Chapter 6 discusses the composition, function, speed control, position control of DC and AC servo drive system. Chapter 7 introduces the basic principles of intelligent machine tool and intelligent machining. Grade will be based on 3 reports, 2 experiments, and one final exam with the relative weight being 15%, 10%, and 75% respectively.
