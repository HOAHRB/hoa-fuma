The purpose is to apply the theoretical knowledge taught by the school to practical project development through internship experience in enterprises, so that students can deeply experience the software development process in a real environment, feel the corporate culture of the internship company, improve the comprehensive practical ability of software development for complex software systems, improve professional technical level, and enhance observation. After entering the internship company, students undergo a short period of training, choose internship projects, and enter the project team. The course duration is 4 weeks, but students are required to work continuously at the internship company for no less than 3 months. The earliest start time for industrial internships is the first week of the summer semester in grade 3, and the latest end time is one month before the graduation thesis defense in the spring semester of grade 4 (usually at the end of April each year).
