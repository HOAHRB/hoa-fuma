    en: The course is mainly taught the basic principles characteristics and basic knowledge of control theory and its application especially in mechanical engineering. The methods of modeling, analysis and design are introduced combined with a typical electromechanical control system, for example, an position servo system. The mathematical modeling method, transfer function and block diagram of electromechanical systems are provided. The time-domain analysis of a typical first-order, a second-order and a higher order systems are presented; the polar diagram, logarithmic plot and the analysis of performance indicators of frequency characteristics for a typical control system are presented; the control system stability criterion and the relative stability of the composition of the computer control system analysis, the calculation of the steady-state error are introduced; the correction method and device design of electromechanical systems are also provided. In addition, the PID discretization method and improvement are introduced.
