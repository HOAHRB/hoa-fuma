However, the features of big data, i.e. volume, velocity and variety, bring new challenges for effective and efficient computation. Big data computation requires special theory and techniques facing these challenges. Therefore, the talents in data science and big data technology require to get specialized knowledge of big data computation. The basis of big data computation is a professional course for the direction of data science and big data technology. The goal of this course is to provide preliminary knowledge for big data computation. This course builds a solid basis for students to solve scalable data-centric problems. Students could learn state-of-art research approaches and applications of data science and big data technology from this course and know the approaches for solving data-centric problems. They could furthermore understand the ability requirement for data science and big data technology. Thus, the competitiveness of students in this area is increased. The contents of this course include basic knowledge of big data, big data algorithm, data structure for big data, computational architecture for big data, big data management system as well as the application of big data. Its emphasis is the difference between big data computation and traditional computation on algorithms, systems, data management and applications to provide solid basis for further study of big data computation.
