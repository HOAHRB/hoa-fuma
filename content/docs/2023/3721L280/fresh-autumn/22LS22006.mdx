    en: The first part of this course is laboratory safety training for freshmen. The safe and orderly management of the laboratory is the basic guarantee for the normal operation of the experimental work. The personnel who enter the laboratory to work and study must comply with the relevant requirements of the laboratory. In recent years, experimental accidents happen frequently, so it is urgent to strengthen laboratory safety education. The "Laboratory safety" part of this course develops students' safety awareness, develops the habit of attaching importance to safety, and obtains the ability of safety guarantee, including the safety of experimental operation and laboratory environment such as water and electricity, fire protection, chemical reagents, biological materials, and the operation and use of instruments and equipment. The second part of this course is basic laboratory training for freshmen, with a focus on standardizing students’ use of common equipment in biological laboratories, as well as training in common molecular biology experimental techniques across different laboratories. The goal is to enable students to truly participate in annual projects and laboratory rotations, rather than just observing and assisting. The basic operations are standardized, the data obtained is repeatable, and students gradually participate in the core parts of the experimental projects, gaining an improvement in scientific literacy and confidence in scientific research abilities.
