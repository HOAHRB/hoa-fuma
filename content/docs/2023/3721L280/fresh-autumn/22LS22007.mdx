    zh: 生物学特色实验是面向大一本科生开设的富有生命科学特色的实验课程，集合了植物学、动物学、微生物学以及分子生物学等多方向多学科的富有趣味的实验内容。本门课程为第一部分，包含《植物的组织培养》、《模式动物斑马鱼》两个实验项目。 《植物的组织培养》是植物学方向的特色实验。与动物细胞不同，植物体内的任何一个细胞，都包含有发育成一株完整新植株的全部信息与潜力，这种潜能称为全能性，是植物组织培养的理论基础。本实验通过在无菌条件下，在合适的光照与湿度环境中，在人工制作的培养基质上，培养剪成小段的植物器官，诱导具有分裂能力的不定形的细胞团（愈伤组织），分化出新的嫩叶幼芽等新器官的过程，展示成熟的植物组织培养技术。 《模式动物斑马鱼》是动物学与发育生物学方向的特色实验，斑马鱼是鲤形目鲤科短担尼鱼属的鱼类，是生命科学研究中常用的模式生物的一种。实验中，学生不仅可以在体视显微镜下观察成年鱼体的生理结构，还能近距离观察受精卵的发育过程。
    en: Biological Characteristic Experiment is an experimental course with life science characteristics for first-year undergraduates. It combines interesting experiments in various directions such as botany, zoology, microbiology and molecular biology. This course is the first part, including two experiments, “Tissue Culture of Plants” and “Model Animal Zebrafish”. “Tissue Culture of Plants” is a characteristic experiment in the direction of botany. Unlike animal cells, any cell in a plant contains all the information and potential to develop into a brand new plant. This is called totipotency. Totipotency is the theoretical basis for plant tissue culture. In this experiment, mature plant tissue culture technology was demonstrated by cultivating small segments of plant organs, under aseptic conditions, in suitable environment, and on the artificially medium, which would induce amorphous cell masses with division ability (calluses), and then differentiate into new organs such as new young leaves and shoots. “Model animal zebrafish” is a characteristic experiment in the direction of Zoology and Developmental Biology. Zebrafish is a fish of the genus Brachymonith in the family Cyprinidae, is a type of model organism commonly used in life sciences. In this experiment, students can observe not only the physiological structure of adult fish under a stereomicroscope, but also the development of fertilized eggs.
