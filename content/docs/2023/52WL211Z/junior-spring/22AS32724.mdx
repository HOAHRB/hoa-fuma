    en: The course takes the solid-state laser amplification and transmission system as its framework, with a wealth of unit-level and system-level engineering technology case studies as support, guiding senior undergraduate students in electronic science and technology, optoelectronic information science and engineering and other related majors to conduct a comprehensive cross-disciplinary practical exercise with their previous professional foundation knowledge. The course explores the typical engineering problems faced in the design, development and operation of solid-state laser systems, and the basic technical methods required to address these problems. Through the study of the key technology principles and engineering implementation forms of the current mainstream solid-state laser amplification and transmission systems, the course guides students to develop their ability to think and judge about actual engineering problems and their ability to analyze and solve them, deepen their understanding and mastery of professional knowledge, and broaden their professional horizons, thereby enhancing their ability to apply their professional knowledge comprehensively. The course consists of classroom lectures and laboratory practicals. The lecture part provides an in-depth introduction to solid-state laser engineering, dividing it into system and unit technologies in a hierarchical manner, enabling students to understand its basic principles, key elements, and engineering implementation methods, and to keep abreast of the latest international trends in the field. All key knowledge points are illustrated with practical engineering case studies. The practical part includes a field discussion of the 100-joule-level large neodymium glass laser device at Harbin Institute of Technology, where students complete cognitive practice on various key technology principles based on the device; it also includes several representative unit experiments to cultivate students' hands-on ability for the future operation of complex laser systems.
