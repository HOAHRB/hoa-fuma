    en: Laser remote sensing, a forefront technology for active long-range detection and monitoring the changes of the natural environment, is mainly refer to that by transmitting laser pulses actively which will interacted with atmospheric air, oceans or some substances with fixed shapes, and taking the advantage of the optical characteristics of absorption, scattering or reflection, after collecting and analyzing optical signal arising from that, the meteorological data will be obtained, including the 3D distributions of the atmosphere, oceans or fixed targets, the propagation and diffusion of pollutions, the atmospheric vector wind field with small or medium scale, etc. With the high spatial resolution and high temporal resolution, laser remote sensing system is not only a powerful new concept instrument for monitoring the atmosphere air, measuring the chemical and physical parameters changes of the atmosphere, but also a new direction and new technology growing up in the development of modern remote technologies which includes the existing microwave, infrared radiation, GPS and so on. This course describes the basic theoretical knowledge of laser remote sensing technology. It covers the interaction process of laser and matter, laser atmospheric transmission characteristics, lidar composition and lidar equations. Typical lidar systems are introduced briefly including laser ranging radars, wind lidar, differential absorption lidar (DIAL), and Raman lidar.
