This experiment aims to assess the impact of the ZIF-8/Cas9 gene knockout system on a cell line expressing the connected GFP green fluorescent protein. The objective is to evaluate the gene-editing effects by observing changes in fluorescence intensity through fluorescent microscopy. This approach allows students to comprehend how gene editing technology, as a crucial research tool in precision medicine and personalized therapy, can alter cellular functions and reshape life.
