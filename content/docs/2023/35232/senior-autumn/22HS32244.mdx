    en: 'Course Positioning and Significance "China''s Financial Reform" is an elective course in a specialized direction offered to senior undergraduates majoring in finance and economics. This course takes the reform process of China''s financial system since 1978 as the main thread, and systematically analyzes the internal logic and practical paths of the transformation towards financial marketization, internationalization, and technologization. Against the backdrop of China''s high-quality economic development and high-level opening up of the financial industry, this course is committed to helping students understand the path of financial development with Chinese characteristics, grasp the deep connections between financial reform and national strategies, and cultivate composite talents who meet the needs of the modern financial system. Course Content and Features The course content covers four core areas of China''s financial reform: Market-oriented Reform of the Financial System: Focus on key issues such as the process of interest rate liberalization, the reform of the RMB exchange rate formation mechanism, and the shareholding system reform of commercial banks; Innovative Development of the Capital Market: Conduct in-depth analysis of major measures such as the registration system reform, the establishment of the Science and Technology Innovation Board, and the unified supervision of the bond market; Evolution of the Financial Regulatory System: Systematically sort out the changes in the regulatory framework from "one central bank and three regulatory commissions" to "one central bank and two regulatory commissions", and explore the construction of the macro-prudential management framework; Financial Opening-up and Internationalization: Study cutting-edge topics such as the internationalization process of the RMB, the opening of the capital account, and financial cooperation under the Belt and Road Initiative. The features of the course lie in: Adopting a trinity teaching model of "theory-policy-practice"; Introducing the latest reform cases (such as the pilot of the digital RMB and the establishment of the Beijing Stock Exchange); Emphasizing the methodological training that combines quantitative analysis and qualitative research. Teaching Methods and Assessment The course adopts diversified teaching methods: Case Teaching: Conduct discussions through real cases such as the risk disposal of Baoshang Bank and the suspension of Ant Group''s listing; Policy Simulation: Organize students to play the roles of the People''s Bank of China, the China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission, etc. for decision-making drills; Field Research: Encourage students to visit local financial institutions to understand the implementation of reforms. Learning Objectives and Expected Outcomes Through the study of this course, students will be able to: Systematically master the historical context and theoretical logic of China''s financial reform; Skillfully use tools such as SWOT analysis and event study method to evaluate the effectiveness of reforms; Write professional policy analysis reports and put forward constructive reform suggestions; Cultivate financial risk awareness and sensitivity to macro policies. Target Audience and Development Value This course is especially suitable for: Students planning to work in regulatory institutions such as the People''s Bank of China and the China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission; Academic talents aspiring to engage in financial policy research; Practical talents intending to enter institutions such as investment banks and funds to engage in macro analysis.'
