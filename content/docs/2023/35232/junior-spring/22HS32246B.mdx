How to ensure the sustainable development of agriculture in major countries and build a strong food security defense line has become a global issue. This course is based on Chinese practice and combines an international perspective to systematically analyze the strategic logic, policy tools, and future paths of agricultural modernization and food security. The specific goals include the following three aspects: (1) Knowledge construction: mastering the scientific connotation, evaluation system, and global governance framework of food security, understanding the historical evolution and institutional innovation of China''s agricultural policies, analyzing the evolution of China''s agricultural policies, technological changes, and food security strategies.
