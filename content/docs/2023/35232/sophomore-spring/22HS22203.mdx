Through more rigorous logical reasoning and more concise mathematical language, economic problems are described and described. At the same time, it tries to combine the basic principles of economics with the reality of China''s economic reform and development to inspire students to analyze and solve practical economic problems. Through the study of this course, students can deeply understand the simplified and modeling thinking methods of economics, master the skills of quantitative analysis of economic problems, develop interest in thinking about practical economic problems and have the ability to solve practical problems. Elementary Microeconomics analyses and teaches the main models of microeconomics analysis in modern Western economics, market equilibrium and comparative static analysis, elasticity theory, production techniques and production functions, cost functions, economies of scope, imperfectly competitive firm theory, general equilibrium theory, and welfare economic theory. The content is from simple to deep, from empirical to normative, combining qualitative analysis with quantitative analysis, combining theory with practical application. Considering that the content of western economics is very complicated and there are many schools, the primary microeconomics mainly focuses on the basic content.
