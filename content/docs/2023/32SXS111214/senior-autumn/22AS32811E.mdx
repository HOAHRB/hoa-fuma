    en: 'Biochip technique is an important branch for the growing trend in the field of electronic information science and technology. With the help of integrated circuit fabrication technology, the biochip integrated some un-continuous analysis process on the substrate surface, which based on the principle of intermolecular specific interactions in the field of life science. Therefore, the biochip offers exciting possibilities for the miniaturization of analytical instruments, called micro total analysis system (μ-TAS). Based on MEMS engineering and biochemistry, this course will mainly introduce the basic theory in the field of biochip, as well as practical process, the main research results, the latest progress and trend in the future. The student should master the basic principle for all kinds of biochip and the fabrication methods. The studying of biochip course is beneficial for the students, improving their ability that use their learning to solve and analysis some practical problems, which lay the theoretical foundation for the application and further study in microelectronics field. Micro total analysis system (μ-TAS) offers the potential for highly efficient, simultaneous analysis of a large number of biologically important molecules in genomic, proteomic and metabolic studies. Students should be familiarized with the types and fabrication methods of biochip widely used in current production, including: microarray chip, protein chip, microfluidic chip, silicon bulk machining, corrosion, bonding technology, function film fabrication; grasp the working principle of various types biochip, common problems and solutions. With the new technology, new equipment constantly emerging, biochip technology raises fascinating possibilities for new analytical assays in various fields such as bioelectronics assembly, biomechanics and sampling techniques, as well as in chips or micro-devices. This course aims to describe the present state-of-the-art of microsystems for use in biotechnological research, medicine and diagnostics. The cumulative scoring method was adopted in this course. a course report about one kind of biochip, should be submitted, no less than 3,000 words, with main references，at least 5 papers reading, accounting 60% of the total score; usually 10% of the results (discussion, curriculum difficulties, lectures, etc.); experimental scores accounted for30% (experimental operations and experimental reports, etc.).'
