    en: The course provides students with a practical understanding of the various types of flow data obtained from experimental measurements and numerical calculations. This course requires students to master the basic theoretical and practical skills of visualization and analysis of flow data, and to be able to obtain flow data based on simulations and experiments. In addition, it is necessary to be able to process the flow data through popular post-processing software, obtain visual data results, and perform secondary processing and analysis. This course lays a theoretical and experimental foundation for future research and work related to fluid mechanics.
