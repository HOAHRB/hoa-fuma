    en: As a gas-liquid two-phase flow phenomenon, cavitation is harmful in most cases. For example, the occurrence of cavitation in fluid machinery will induce negative effects such as noise, vibration, and cavitation erosion of components. Under the guidance of the idea of "if we can't avoid it, then use it", many technologies using cavitation effects have been applied to engineering practice in recent years. "Fundamentals of Gas-Liquid Two-Phase Flow in Fluid Machinery" is an engineering technology course that studies complex flows in fluid machinery and the applications. This course mainly focuses on the fundamental concept、theory、method and phenomenon of cavitating flows and cavitation bubble dynamics. The purpose of this course is to let students grasp the fundamental characteristics and mechanisms of cavitation and cavitation bubble dynamics. The course not only provides the necessary basic theoretical knowledge for students to engage in the design and testing of fluid machinery (such as pumps, turbines, etc.) after graduation, but also lays the groundwork for students to continue their advanced studies in the future. Combining with its characteristics, this course focuses on cultivating students' interest and their abilities of judgment, calculation and comprehensive analysis for complex flows inside fluid machinery, and training them to have the ability to engage in numerical simulation, experimental test and post-processing.
