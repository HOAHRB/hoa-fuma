    en: With the increasingly severe global climate change, the demand for new energy and environmental protection is growing. The Chinese government has proposed strategic goals of building a "marine power" and achieving "dual-carbon targets", aiming to promote economic transformation and sustainable development by accelerating the development of offshore new energy and the construction of maritime transportation infrastructure. In this context, the accurate calculation and analysis of marine environmental loads are of great significance for ensuring the safety of ocean engineering structures. The unique characteristics of marine environments, such as waves and currents, bring great challenges to the design, construction, and maintenance of ocean engineering. Therefore, the accurate calculation and analysis of marine environmental loads are important prerequisites for ensuring the safety and stability of ocean engineering structures. This course mainly involves the basic wave theory and hydrodynamic calculation methods for marine structures. Through the course, students can grasp the characteristics of marine environments, including the parameters of wave frequency, wave height, and wavelength. They can understand the basic wave theory and master the basic principles of wave propagation, reflection, and refraction. They will learn the wave force calculation method for cylindrical structures and be familiar with the characteristics and calculation methods of wave loads on cylindrical structures. At the same time, the course will also introduce shallow water wave theory, structural hydrodynamic numerical calculation methods, commercial software, and other contents. Students will learn how to use mathematical models and computer simulation techniques to accurately calculate and analyze the loads on ocean engineering structures under different marine environments. Through the course, students will have the basic ability to design and analyze ocean engineering, laying a foundation for the practical application of ocean engineering.
