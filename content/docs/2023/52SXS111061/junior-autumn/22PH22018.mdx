    en: “Thermodynamics and Statistical Physics”is one of the four main courses of theoretical physics for all students of physics department. Thermodynamics is the macro-theory of thermal motion, while statistical physics is the micro-theory of thermal motion. The purpose of the course is to let the students systematically master the macro- and micro-ways of describing thermal motion of theory physics, correctly understand the physical concepts of thermal motion and the differences between thermal motion and other physical motions, and solve some basic problems of thermal motion using mathematical tools more skilledly. The main contents of the course include the basic laws of thermodynamics, the thermodynamical properties of uniform system, the equilibrium of non-single phase system and phase transition, the statistical theory for quasi-independent system, ensemble theory.
