    en: Additive manufacturing, also commonly referred to as 3D printing, is a manufacturing technique that is very different from traditional manufacturing methods of cutting or removing material. The core concept of additive manufacturing is to gradually build three-dimensional objects by stacking or adding material layer by layer, rather than cutting or removing material from a block to obtain a desired shape. The core idea of this process can be simplified as “adding, not removing”. Through the study of this course, students can master the basic principles of additive manufacturing, understand the current situation and development direction of the field, make students master the modeling, data analysis and dissection of additive manufacturing and other processing methods, and be able to complete the pre-design, analysis and calculation of additive manufacturing alone, so that students can master the application of the typical additive manufacturing technology to achieve the knowledge reserve for scientific research in this field.
