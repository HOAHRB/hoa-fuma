    en: As a net-shape forming process, additive manufacturing technology has the advantages of without mold, no limitation of component complexity, high material utilization rate, energy saving and environmental protection. Additive manufacturing technology has high application value in the fields of aerospace, automobile and ship, micro nano manufacturing, biomedical engineering and so on. However, the special layer by layer printer system of additive manufacturing technology makes have large tendency of defects in the manufacturing process, and there may be problems such as size and shape difference, surface quality defect, density difference, anisotropy, local deformation, internal quality defect and stress concentration. Therefore, in order to ensure the stability of the quality of additive manufacturing components, reasonable and efficient quality inspection technology is needed for defect detection and quality inspection. As a professional compulsory course for additive manufacturing engineering, the course enables students to understand and master the forming process of additive manufacturing and component quality detection related technologies, measurement methods and specific applications by analyzing the principle, advantages, limitations and typical applications of additive manufacturing component quality detection technology. The course content mainly includes on-line detection principle and classification of additive manufacturing process, on-line detection method and measurement technology, intelligent control technology of forming quality, detection technology of shape and size of additive manufacturing parts, surface quality detection, internal quality detection and use quality performance detection, so as to understand the testing principle and method of various parameters in additive manufacturing process in essence, It can also formulate corresponding test plans, lay a solid foundation for Undergraduates' follow-up study and related work in the future, and support the achievement of corresponding index points in professional learning achievements.
