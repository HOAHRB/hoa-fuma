    en: Review of human exploration of low speed to high speed to supersonic and then hypersonic flight, the intermittent leap in propulsion development contains the inevitable logic of superiority and inferiority. Rocket engines are an integral part of aerospace missions. Air-breathing engines have the advantage of high specific impulse when flying in the atmosphere. Meanwhile, tasks such as long-endurance reconnaissance, hypersonic flight, space shuttle, and reusability have put forward new requirements for the vehicle design, and future-oriented vehicle power technology is in urgent need of innovation. Therefore, it is necessary to sort out the development of aircraft power technology, learn the performance advantages of different engines, and study the innovative combined power forms. This course analyzes the physical basis of momentum conservation in jet propulsion, taking the demand for aircraft power and the actual cost constraints as the traction. The course focuses on the development of various aircraft power units and teaches innovative thinking research methodology from discovering to solving new needs with the knowledge learned. Students will deepen their understanding of the development of current new aircraft power technology, understand the thermal cycle of various engines, and can independently analyze the operating characteristics of various types of aircraft power units. Meanwhile, the course will develop students’ innovative thinking and independent thinking and improve students’ ability to analyze and solve problems using innovative means. Further, students will be encouraged to seek new forms of aircraft power units for new scenarios and new needs.
