    zh: 近年来航天电推进技术快速发展，国内外多个大规模星座均使霍尔和离子电推进进行轨道提升、位置保持、碰撞规避和快速离轨。阴极作为电子源与中和器，是现有航天电推进系统的关键核心部组件，被誉为电推进的心脏，阴极电子发射特性的衰减会直接导致推进系统的永久性失效，因此阴极已经成为航天电推进技术的关键支撑。 本课程以此为背景并结合当前国际上电推进阴极技术的研究前沿，开展空心阴极、射频与微波阴极以及无工质阴极等方向的教学，重点阐述空心阴极的工作原理、描述模型、失效机理和不稳定特性等，讲解空心阴极与霍尔推力器、离子推力器的耦合原理，补充介绍射频与微波电离产生等离子体原理，了解冷等离子体电子引出机制及其应用场景，介绍真空场发射电子引出原理、工作特性及应用领域，使学生掌握多种电推进电子源与中和器工作原理、基本构型和工作特性，培养学生逻辑思维能力、创新意识和工程设计能力。课程主要通过专业理论、文献调研与大作业以及实践教学相结合的形式展开，在培养学生科研能力的同时，还着重强调拓宽学术视野，加深学生对科学研究和工程实践之间的联系与认识。
    en: Electric propulsion technology is developing rapidly. Hall thrusters and ion thrusters are being deployed for orbit transfer, position-keeping, collision avoidance and de-orbit of satellites in many constellation programs. Onboard the thrusters, the cathode, known as the heart of electric propulsion, is a key component. It functions as the electron source and the neutralizer. The decay of the electron emission characteristics of the cathode will instantly lead to the permanent failure of the propulsion system. Therefore, the cathode has always been the key support of aerospace electric propulsion technology. This course gives lectures on hollow cathodes, radio-frequency cathodes, microwave cathodes and propellant-less cathodes in combination with the current international research frontier of electric propulsion cathode technology based on above background. Then this course will discuss the working principle, description model, failure mechanism and instability characteristics of hollow cathodes. After that, this course will supplement the coupling principle of hollow cathodes with Hall thrusters and ion thrusters, and the principle of plasma generation by radio-frequency and microwave ionization, the extraction mechanism of electrons in low temperature plasma and the field emission in vacuum surrounding. The scope of this course is to enable students to grasp the working principles, structures and characteristics of several kinds of plasma neutralizers, and to cultivate critical thinking, innovation mindness and engineering capabilities. The course includes theoretical explanation, article reading, essays and practical operations. These will cultivate the scientific potiential and academic overview of students, and deepen the connection between research and engineering.
