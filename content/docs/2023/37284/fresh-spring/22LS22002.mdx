It is of great research value and application prospect in the fields of life science, medicine, and agriculture to observe the structural characteristics of cells by microscopy and to study them qualitatively and quantitatively in combination with immune technology. In experiment 1, we learned to observe the cell membrane and nucleus of animal cells with fluorescence microscope, initially understood the basic components of fluorescence microscope and the principle of fluorescence imaging, and mastered the application of immunofluorescence technology in the field of life science. The research and application of life science have entered the molecular level. DNA can be extracted from a small number of shed cells, such as oral mucosal epithelial cells, and amplified in large quantities under the action of specific primers, so that specific genes can be detected. This technology is widely used in gender identification, paternity identification, criminal detection, and other aspects. It has great research value and application prospect in production and life. In experiment 2, we learned the principle and technology of sex detection by DNA extraction, PCR gene amplification and electrophoresis. Master the relevant molecular biology technology initially.
