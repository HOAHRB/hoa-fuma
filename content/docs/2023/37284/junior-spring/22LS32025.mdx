The use of nanotechnology to develop nanomedicine is of great importance in the prevention, diagnosis and treatment of human diseases. Compared with traditional drugs, nanomedicine has the characteristics of high efficiency and low toxicity, and has become a hot topic in the medical field at home and abroad. This course systematically describes the frontiers of nanomedicines, including the overview of nanomedicines, Physichemical properities of nanomaterilas, the nano drug delivery system (such as liposome, polymeric nanoparticles, nanohydrogel, solid lipid nanoparticles, inorganic nanoparticles etc), smart drug delivery systems, nanomedicine for gene therapy, the quality evaluation of nanomedicine agents, and the route of administration, toxicity mechanism, safety evaluation, the pharmacokinetics of nanomedicine. This course adopts instruction in class combined with literature reading and special subject investigation after class. In this course, students are required to master the basic concepts, research methods, preparation methods of various nano delivery systems, quality evaluation of nanomedicine, and understand the frontiers and challenges in the field of nanomedicine, in order to make them have a comprehensive understanding of nanomedicine, systematically grasp the latest developments in this field, and to develop the ability for independently designing experiments, analyzing and processing results.
