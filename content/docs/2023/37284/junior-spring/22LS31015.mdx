As medical image big data has redundancy and diversity, how to use it to help doctors diagnose diseases has become one of the hotspots of international research. New methods based on big data technology, machine learning (ML), and artificial intelligence (AI) have become effective tools for analyzing medical image big data and are of great significance for improving the accuracy of disease diagnosis. The purpose of this course is to enable students to understand the current situation and trends of the development of medical image big data and the problems it faces, master the preprocessing methods of medical image big data, the basic PyTorch deep learning implementation framework, common convolutional neural network structures, and understand the application of deep learning in radiographic image analysis, pathological image analysis, and optical image analysis. Through teaching and demonstration, students will have the ability to combine theoretical knowledge with practice, broaden their horizons by introducing cutting-edge technology development trends, and lay a foundation for their future scientific research or work. The aims of this course require the following capabilities of the students. Objective 1 Mastering the basic knowledge of big data analytics in medical imaging. Objective 2 Mastering the implementation of the common framework of deep learning using PyTorch Objective 3 Developing students’ ability to analyze image data using deep learning.
