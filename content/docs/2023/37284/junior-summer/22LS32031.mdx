    en: 'The ecology that studies the interrelationship between organisms and their environment has received unprecedented attention as ecological problems have become more serious. “Green mountain is gold and silver mountain”. The construction of ecological civilization requires us to establish ecological concepts and ecological consciousness in our lives. Based on the process of the cognition, taking the ecology and the ecosystem as a whole, this course puts main emphasis on two aspects. The first is the law of ecology existing among different biology systems, such as the individual organisms, the populations, and the communities. The second is the introduction of the ecology in different aspects, including the structure, the function, the regulation of ecosystem, the appliance of the ecological theory, and the ecological methodology. The contents of this course are described as follows: 1. The relationships between living things and the environments (which contains energy environments and substance environments); 2. Population ecology, with respect to the variation, the evolution, and the life history of the individual organism and the population; 3. Community ecology, with an eye to the construction, the dynastic, and the sort of the biology community; 4. Ecosystem ecology, with respect to the basic characters of the ecosystem, the energy flow, the material cycle, and the classification of the ecosystem existing on the Earth; and 5. Application ecology, which describes the environmental protection, the species diversity and the control of pests.'
