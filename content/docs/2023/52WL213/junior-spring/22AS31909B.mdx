The theoretical part includes the basic working principle of the typical optical system and the fundamental aberration theory. The practical part adopts the form of computer operation. The basic working principle of typical optical system mainly deals with the basic working principle of typical optical instruments such as human eye, magnifier, microscope, telescope and photographic system. The depth understanding of the optical characteristics and functional indexes of typical optical systems is necessary for determining the overall scheme of optical instruments at the initial stage of optical instrument design. The basic aberration theory includes geometrical aberration theory and wave aberration theory. Geometrical aberration theory is often used in optical design stage. It can qualitatively and quantitatively reflect the image quality of optical system and guide the aberration correction. The theory of wave aberration is mostly used in the testing of optical system and the formulation of aberration tolerance. In the practical part: combining the typical principles of optical system and the knowledge of aberration in classroom teaching, students will initially learn to design some simple optical systems by using optical design software Zemax.'
