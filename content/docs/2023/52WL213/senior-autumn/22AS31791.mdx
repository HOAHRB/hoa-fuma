    en: PRINCIPLES OF LASERS B is a basic course for undergraduates majoring in Optical Engineering. In this course, the basic concepts and principle of laser, optical resonator, propagation & transformation of Gaussian beam, control & improvement of laser properties are mainly taught. The aim for this course is to make students master the basic principles of lasers, learn about the production mechanism of laser, the properties of laser beam and theory of optical resonator. In the chapter of the basic principle of laser, the conditions required to generate the laser, laser structure, formation of laser, and characteristics of laser are mainly taught. In the chapter of optical resonator, stability conditions of coaxial spherical cavity, loss of the resonator, modes of stable cavity, unstable cavity are introduced. In the chapter of propagation & transformation of Gaussian beam, characteristics & description methods of Gaussian beam, ABCD equation, focusing & collimation of Gaussian beam, reconstruction of Gaussian beam, matching of Gaussian beam are mainly taught. In the chapter of control & improvement of laser properties, basic concepts of laser mode selection, laser frequency stabilization, laser Q-switch, laser mode locking, laser amplification are introduced.
