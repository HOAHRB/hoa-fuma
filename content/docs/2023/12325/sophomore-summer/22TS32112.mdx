At the same time, through the study of this course, students can understand and master the principles of the use and design of sustainable development materials, and identify the great challenges facing the traffic environment. Through this course, students will have an understanding of the scope of transportation infrastructure, basic knowledge of sustainable development, the relationship and impact of transportation and environment, and how to establish a sustainable development of transportation infrastructure. Also, students should be familiar with the definition and connotation of life cycle assessment, and understand the definition and characteristics of functional units and system boundaries. Under the framework of life cycle assessment, the autonomous exercise of life cycle assessment is carried out according to three key steps. Through practice, students have clarified the concepts of LCA input and output, mastered the methods and indicators of LCA impact assessment, and understood the concepts and strategies of group, standardization and interaction in LCA. It also introduces the use of sustainable materials and their impact on the environment. Finally, students have the ability to analyze and solve the sustainable development of transportation infrastructure in engineering practice.
