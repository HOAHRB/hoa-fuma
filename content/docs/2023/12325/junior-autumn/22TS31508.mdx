    en: The course of “Transportation System Simulation and Applications” mainly introduces the basic principle and flow of traffic simulation technology, and the operation flows and application methods of one typical traffic simulation tool, which is VISSIM. This course firstly introduces the concept of traffic simulation, the meaning of simulation and basic steps, and road facility model, traffic generation model, vehicle following model, lane changing model and pedestrian simulation model. Then take VISSIM as an example, the road network modeling method, vehicle and driving behavior modeling method, signal control scheme setting method, traffic information collection method, signal control scheme evaluation method are introduced. At last, the application examples of the traffic simulation tool are illustrated, which can help the students understand the flow of applying the tool to traffic control scheme improvement, traffic state evaluation and traffic plan scheme evaluation. Based on the knowledge illustration, the course takes teaching the traffic simulation models as the core. By combining class teaching and computer experiment, the students can have profound understanding of the traffic engineering, traffic plan, traffic management and control knowledge.
