    en: Centering on the core perception system of intelligent connected vehicles, this paper systematically explains the technical principles and applications of multimodal sensors. The content covers the working principles and signal processing algorithms of cameras, millimeter-wave radars, lidars, ultrasonic sensors and inertial navigation devices. Through the analysis of typical cases, this paper explores multi-sensor data fusion technology, target detection and tracking algorithms, and strategies for improving perception reliability in complex scenarios. The focus is on cultivating students' key technological research and development capabilities in the perception layer of autonomous driving. Combined with the latest industry trends, the innovative application of V2X sensing technology in vehicle-road coordination systems is analyzed. Through the study of this course, students will possess comprehensive capabilities in the design, performance evaluation and engineering implementation of intelligent vehicle sensor systems, laying a core technical foundation for their research and development in autonomous driving.
