    en: This course is open to all undergraduate students across the university and aims to illustrate the fundamental concepts and application potential of the interdisciplinary integration between modern engineering and cutting-edge data science. Starting with engineering reliability and uncertainty analysis, the course guides students through computational thinking, machine learning, deep learning, and modern probabilistic methods to develop a comprehensive understanding of the theoretical foundations and technical frameworks underpinning intelligent engineering systems. The course content ranges from traditional reliability theory to advanced sampling-based methods for failure probability assessment, and extends to widely used engineering techniques such as surrogate modeling, deep learning, few-shot learning, Bayesian inference, and intelligent optimization. Through this comprehensive curriculum, students will systematically acquire the ability to apply data science tools to enable rapid simulation, intelligent design, and risk-informed decision-making in complex engineering systems. The final module focuses on full lifecycle management of aging infrastructure, guiding students in exploring emerging frontiers such as digital twins, intelligent monitoring, and AI-driven smart civil engineering. This foundation will support the advancement of engineering disciplines toward greater digitalization and intelligence.
