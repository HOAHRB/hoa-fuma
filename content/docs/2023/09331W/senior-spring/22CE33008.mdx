    en: Graduation internship is a significant practical teaching link for civil engineering undergraduates. The internship includes visiting typical and special projects already built or under construction, attending special lectures, collecting information on projects in combination with the graduation projects, completing special reports, writing internship diaries and completing internship reports. Through the internship, civil engineering undergraduates can deepen their understanding of the latest development and prospect of both domestic and overseas civil engineering, consolidate their professional knowledge, apply their professional knowledge to solve practical problems. Furthermore, they can systematically understand the process of planning, design, construction and management of civil engineering and cultivate the responsibilities and professionalism of engineers and project managers, which would help them to lay the foundation for the graduation design and adapt to professional technical or management work as soon as possible after graduation. The aim is to strengthen the passion of undergraduate civil engineering students for their majors, motivate their determination to develop civil engineering, which inspire them to devote themselves to the modernization of the motherland and the realization of the Two Centenary Goals.
