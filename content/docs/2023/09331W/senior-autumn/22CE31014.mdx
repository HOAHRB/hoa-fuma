    en: 'Wind disaster is one of the main natural disasters acted on building structures. Nowadays, with the advancement of structural analysis and construction techniques, building structures tend to be higher or larger, with a low weight and significant flexibility. And thus they are more sensitive to wind action. Under this circumstance, it is very necessary to advance the understanding of structural wind engineering principles for civil engineering undergraduate students and cultivate their ability to analyze and solve practical problems by using mechanical basic theories. The main contains of this course includes: origin of wind and basic categories, the main characteristics of wind disasters of structures, characteristics of the atmosphere boundary layer wind and their engineering description, mean and fluctuating wind speed characteristics, wind speed conversion under non-standard conditions, Bernoulli equation and its applications, flow separation, reattachment and vortex shedding, the Reynolds number effect of flow around circular cylinder, basic concept and determination of wind loading including mean wind pressure and fluctuating wind pressure, classification of wind induced responses, wind induced response analysis of structures in the windward and crosswind directions, wind resistance design of high-rise buildings, technology of wind tunnel test. By this course, the students are expected to be familiar with the basic features of wind loading, grasping the principles and methods of structural wind-induced responses analysis, and can carry out wind resistance design for some conventional structures based on the loading code. Furthermore, the students are expected to understand the principles and methods of wind tunnel tests.'
