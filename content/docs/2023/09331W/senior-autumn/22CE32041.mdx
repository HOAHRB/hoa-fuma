    en: In engineering practice, some special concretes besides common concrete material play important roles and broadly used in general concrete structures and especially some great infrastructures. In order to satisfy special requirements of new types of infrastructures, construction technology, environmental conditions under real services and special functions, special types of concrete materials are then developed and broadly applied. The performances and properties of special concrete materials are of course determined by their constitutes and micro-structures. In this course, based on the basic knowledge of modern cementitious materials and common concrete materials, high/ultra-high strength concrete, self-compacting concrete, high performance concrete, shrinkage compensating concrete and mass concrete are introduced with great emphases on mechanical property, workability, durability, dimensional stability, and the technical ways to achieve these special performances. Additionally, pervious concrete, foam concrete and polymer-modified concrete are announced with special constitutes and performance. According to the practical need of concrete materials, one is required to acquire the approaches to design the constitutes, adjust its microstructures, construction technique and sometimes other techniques in the preparation and production of these fundamentally special concrete materials. Moreover, the engineering applications of them should be also mastered with respect to their special performances.
