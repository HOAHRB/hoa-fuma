On the base of University physics, theoretical mechanics and mechanics of materials, undergraduate students need to further study the course of experimental mechanics. Through practical teaching, students in the direction of architectural engineering or building material in civil engineering need to familiar with the error analysis and data processing，strain gauge, optical measurement method, and grasp the basic theories and experimental skills. This lays foundation for further learning the professional courses such as structural health monitoring and structural modal analysis, and especially provides experimental measurement method for graduation thesis of students. The objective of the course is to solve the problems of engineering structures and solid mechanical strength by using modern experimental technique and equipment which are suitable for measuring such mechanical parameters as stress, strain, displacement, load, force, etc. Students should grasp the working principles and usages of experimental instruments, improve the capability of solving engineering problems, and settle the problems of engineering structures and mechanical strength in solid mechanics. The main contents include basic theories of stress – strain relationship of components and resembling design of experimental models, basic theories and solutions of experimental data process, introduction to strain gauge and its applications, techniques and solutions of modern optical measurements, such as digital image correlation method and stereovision measurement method, etc.
