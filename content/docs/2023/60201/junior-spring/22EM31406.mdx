Since the 1990s, a new wave of economic globalization tidal wave has shown an irresistible force. International enterprises, as a mainstay to push forward global economic integration, are playing an increasingly important role----two-thirds of the quantum of world trade and investment is fulfilled by them. The rapid development of economic globalization and China's entry into the WTO has almost integrated the Chinese market and world market, and Chinese enterprises will face more fiercely competition in the international market. If we still remain in foreign trade, general labor export and project contracting, it not only can not share the benefits of globalization, but will continue to lag behind, and even be marginalized into brinkmanship. Therefore, Chinese enterprises must be integrated themselves into the global economic tide, find their own comparative advantages, and position the international division of labor accurately. International Business Management Course provides a variety of international business theories and methods according to the real need and evelopment practice of China's enterprises, through a large number of successful and failure case analysis of Chinese and foreign enterprises internationalization, so as to improve students’ ability of thinking independently, analysing and solving problems, cultivate large numbers of talents who unites international vision and administrative capacity.
