    en: Recently, corporate governance has become one of the hottest topics in both academic and industry. Corporate governance is an institutional arrangement which include a formal or informal, internal or external mechanism in order to coordinate the interests of the company and all stakeholders. The core of corporate governance is the decision-making so as to safeguard the interests of all parties and to achieve the everlasting development of the corporation. This course is open for third year student majoring in Business Administration, which aims to develop their professional knowledge, skills and ability. The main contents of corporate governance include internal governance such as shareholders 'meeting and shareholders' equity, board governance, board of supervisors governance and managerial incentives and constraints, as well as the external governance which is related to the regulation of government, the constrain of product markets, capital markets and manager markets and the governance of creditors and community.
