    en: This course is a professional course which has strong comprehensiveness. It is multidisciplinary in theory and practice. It lays stress on engineering designing of control system. This course studies the driving principle and motion control system design of electrical machines, including direct current motor and asynchronous current motor, builds the kinematics and dynamics model of motor motion control system ,and analyzes the control system by applying the theory and analysis method of Automatic Control Principle. For the direct current motor, the main contents include the speed closed loop design of electrical machine, the structure of the current and speed double closed loop and the engineering design methods. For the asynchronous current motor, the course mainly analyzes the speed control method and studies the principle of the vector control and direct torque control. And on these bases, this course studies one kind of practical Servo motion control system taking Turn-table as an example This course looks at improving the students’ ability of analyzing and solving realistic engineering problems of motion control system.
