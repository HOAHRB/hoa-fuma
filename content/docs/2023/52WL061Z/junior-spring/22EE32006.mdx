    en: At present, the micromotors and control systems have become indispensable components in various fields such as the intelligent manufacture, electrified transportation, new energy generation, energy storage. They can be used as the control or drive components and have applications in a variety of systems. For the students of the electrical engineering department, it is necessary to understand the basic knowledge and implementation methods of various micromotors, and the practical applications of various control methods as well. This course will be taught to undergraduates in Electrical Machines and Apparatus on the structure, principle and control technology of several typical micromotors. By this course, students can understand and master the corresponding basic knowledge, and also can have the preliminary design and application ability. This course gives priority to the basic concepts, knowledge and principles of motors based on actual motors and control systems, so that the students can understand the basic concepts and key knowledge of motor control technologies. At the same time, this course is combined with the development and trend of motor control technology, and introduces some new structure, principle and control methods of motors, so as to open the thinking and vision of the students, increase the sense of innovation.
