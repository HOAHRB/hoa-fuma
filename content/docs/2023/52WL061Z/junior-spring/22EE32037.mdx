    en: In this course, with the research field of power electronics and power converting, the establishing approaches of dynamic models of switching power converters based on pulse width modulation (PWM), and the design methods of its control system are taught. Based on the steady-state and dynamic characteristics analysis of high-frequency power devices (diodes, MOSFET, IGBT) and different power conversion topologies (mainly DC-to-DC power converters) composed of the mentioned devices. In this course, the circuit and mathematical and average models are established, both the devices and topologies. Then this course introduces the controller design methods based on the classical automatic control theory to realize different operating modes of voltage and current closed-loop control. Moreover, the course analyzes the effects of the duty cycle, the input voltage and the load current on the output voltage and current, as well as the frequency-domain characteristics of the control-to-output and input-to-output transfer functions to design a switching power converter system with high stability and fast dynamic response. Through studying this course, students will master the methods for establishing both the circuit model of power-switching devices and the average and small-signal model of power-switching circuits. In addition, students should be able to analyze and design the devices, the circuits and the closed-loop system by means of circuit simulation software (Pspice) and closed-loop system simulation software (Matlab or Plecs). The study of this course will lay the foundations for the modeling and control of more complex power electronic topology and systems in the future.
