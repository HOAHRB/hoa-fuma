    en: As an elective course for electrical engineering students, wireless power transfer technology is a comprehensive interdisciplinary application of circuit theory, electromagnetic field theory, embedded systems, power electronics, and automatic control, which students have previously studied. By using wireless power transfer technology as an example, this course aims to consolidate students' basic theories, cultivate their ability to use multiple technologies to solve practical problems, and guide the integration of theory and practice. This course first introduces the development history of wireless power transfer, comprehensively compares the advantages and disadvantages of various wireless power transfer technologies, and enables students to truly understand the reasons and consequences of inductive wireless power transfer technology becoming the mainstream technology. Based on this, the course sequentially explains the key technologies of wireless power transfer, including high-frequency switch power supply technology, reactive power compensation technology, efficient magnetic coupling technology, rectification and filtering technology, feedback control technology, and energy signal synchronous transmission technology. Through a gradual introduction, students gain a comprehensive understanding of wireless power transfer technology, master the design methods and processes of wireless power transfer systems, and become proficient in using circuit and magnetic circuit simulation software. This course emphasizes the practical application of theoretical knowledge, focuses on cultivating students' ability to use various technologies to solve practical problems, enhances their innovation consciousness, and cultivates research-oriented talents with both technical and theoretical capabilities.
