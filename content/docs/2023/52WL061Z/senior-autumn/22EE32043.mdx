This course mainly teaches the basic principles of electromagnetic measurement technology, the structure and operating characteristics of electromagnetic measurement tools, commonly used sensor technologies and usage methods in electromagnetic measurement, modern advanced digital measurement and development trends. It enables students to have a deeper understanding of the basic scheme of equipment electrical parameter testing, master the basic methods of electromagnetic measurement, and possess the ability to process and analyze electromagnetic measurement data in engineering research and industrial production. The main content includes: basic knowledge of electromagnetic measurement and data processing methods; Direct reading and comparative electrical measuring instruments (traditional measurement methods for electrical parameters); Electronic measuring instruments (electronic oscilloscopes and digital oscilloscopes); Digital measuring instruments (structure and characteristics); Magnetic measurement (basic theorem of magnetic field measurement, measurement methods for static and dynamic magnetic characteristics); Non electrical measurement (principles and applications of several types of sensors); Basic training of virtual instruments; Engineering application examples, interference and suppression techniques for measurement systems.
