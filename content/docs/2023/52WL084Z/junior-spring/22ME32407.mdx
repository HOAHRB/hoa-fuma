Management and information science and technology have penetrated into various aspects of society and economy, and the knowledge, methods, and tools of manufacturing management information systems have become essential basic skills for management and engineering talents. This course aims to enable students to comprehensively apply the theories, methods, and tools of manufacturing, management, information, systems, and computer science and technology they have learned. On the basis of understanding the manufacturing process, they will understand management theory, system theory, and the systematic, formal, and structured planning, analysis, design, implementation, operation, maintenance, and evaluation methods of management information systems. Understand the functions, information, business, and management methods involved in the manufacturing process and their interrelationships, learn to collect, store, organize, analyze, process, and apply information in decision-making, management, business, and processes in the manufacturing industry, and learn to apply management and information technology to design and implement the manufacturing process. Understand the management principles, system structure, functions, and business processes of manufacturing management information systems represented by ERP in the fields of production planning, inventory management, equipment management, quality management, cost management, and workshop operation control. Proficient in applying the management information system established by manufacturing enterprises to handle related businesses, and based on this, enhancing students' innovation awareness and ability.
