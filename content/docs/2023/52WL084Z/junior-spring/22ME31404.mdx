To reduce costs, shorten production cycles, improve the quality and efficiency of a company, and keep up with changes in market demand, it is crucial to effectively manage the allocation of materials, equipment, human resources, and capital. These are the core functions of production planning and control. The course is divided into nine chapters, starting with an introduction to the relationship between manufacturing and workflow, modes, and management, followed by a comprehensive overview of production planning and key production schemes. The course also covers topics such as storage analysis and control, material planning, production capacity planning, workshop task planning, project scheduling, and maintenance management. Various methods for production planning and control are presented from different perspectives. To keep up with industry developments, the course also includes a discussion of the history and key technologies of intelligent manufacturing, as well as the application of industrial engineering knowledge in the context of big data manufacturing factories. This course is graded out of a total of 100 points, and the cumulative assessment method is used.
