    zh: 半导体是一种特性介于导体和绝缘体之间的物质，其电阻率易受掺杂、温度、光照等因素的影响而改变。基于半导体制作而成的各类电子元件（传感器、储存器、CPU等）及其上的各类电器（计算机、移动电话等），已广泛存在于人们日常生活的各个角落，在日新月异的信息时代中扮演重要的角色。半导体材料的理化性质，尤其是电学和光学特性，决定半导体器件的功能，因此有必要对其进行定量测试和分析。本课程旨在为学生讲解半导体测试技术，主要针对半导体的电学和光学特性，通过对其电阻率、载流子浓度、少数载流子寿命、霍尔效应等的测量，加深对半导体器件工作原理的理解。课程涉及到的测试分析技术包括：四探针法测电阻率、微波光电导衰减测试、霍尔效应测试、红外光谱测试、能级测试等。课程将对测试技术的基本原理和实验操作进行简要介绍，不涉及过深的原理和过多的实验操作细节；目的是帮助学生了解半导体测试的基础知识，培养学生对半导体研究的兴趣。
    en: 'A semiconductor is a substance whose electrical resistivity can be modified by factors such as doping, temperature, and light. Various electronic components and electrical appliances built on semiconductors are playing an important role in people’s life. The physical and chemical properties of semiconductor materials, especially the electrical and optical ones, determine the function of semiconductor devices, so it is necessary to quantitatively test and analyze them. This course aims at explaining semiconductor testing technology and equipment to students to deepen their understanding of the working principle of semiconductor devices through the measurement of resistivity, carrier concentration, minority carrier lifetime, Hall effect, and so on. The testing technologies covered in this course include: four-probe resistivity measurement, microwave photoconductive attenuation test, Hall effect test, infrared spectroscopy test, energy level test, etc. The course will introduce the basic principles and experimental operations of testing technology. The purpose is to help students understand the basics of semiconductor testing and cultivate students'' interest in semiconductor research.'
