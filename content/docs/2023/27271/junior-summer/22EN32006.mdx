    en: Landscape ecology as a rapid rise emerging disciplines since 1970s, consists not only the basic principles and ideas of traditional ecology, but also the geography, ecology, system theory and many other disciplines. Landscape ecology offers a new theoretical and scientific methods for the comprehensive solution to the problem of resources and environment and the construction of ecological civilization. It also opens up a new way of thinking. This course focus on combining the landscape in geography and ecology in environmental science. It takes a horizontal study on the spatial interaction of geographical phenomenon in geography and a longitudinal study on the interaction of ecosystem function in ecology as one to study. The course takes the landscape as object, providing the spatial structure and function of the landscape through the material flow, energy flow, information flow and species flow’s migration and exchange in the Earth's surface, and the interrelationship between the various parts. It also introduces the dynamic changes in the landscape and the principles and ways to landscape optimization and protection. In particularly, the course introduces landscape design and its methods based on landscape ecology concept, taking the landscape as an organic life to consider, consuming less energy and material, through the system of material circulation and energy conversion to obtain greater ecological and social benefits and to achieve the goal of natural and human harmonious development. This course enables students to master the basic concepts, basic theories and basic principles of landscape ecology, to comprehensively and thoroughly understand and master the application of landscape ecology principles, to scientifically solve the practical problems in the process of landscape design, and to improve their abilities to solve complex engineering problems.
