    en: Biotechnology is the primary method to treat the environmental issues. The rational use of modern biological technology is the basis for implementation of environmental protection projects and development of the innovative environmental protection technology. Environmental biotechnology is an emerging discipline which combines biotechnology and environmental engineering. The discipline can directly or indirectly utilizes certain components or certain functions of a complete organism to establish a reduction or elimination of pollutants in environment. This course introduces the principles of modern biotechnology such as genetic engineering, cell engineering, and environmental microbial community detection techniques to enable students to apply biotechnology to solve environmental problems. Applications of environmental biotechnology are also introduced in this course to help students analyze environmental issues and determine rational solution by using biotechnology. By introducing the cutting edge of environmental biotechnology in this course, students can understand the development trend of environmental biotechnology, designing and developing new technologies for environmental protection.
