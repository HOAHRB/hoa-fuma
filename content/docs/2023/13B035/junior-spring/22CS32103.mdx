    en: Currently, with the rapid development and widespread application of high-throughput biological experimental technologies such as life genomics sequencing, biological big data continues to experience explosive growth, becoming one of the largest datasets. This trend has led the fields of life science and biomedicine to shift towards a data-driven development model, entering a new era of rapid growth. In this context, the development and utilization of biological big data have become a new driving force for the development of life science and biomedicine. The core lies in the creation of algorithms that can effectively process, analyze, mine, and apply biological big data, aiming to address the analytical challenges of PB-ZB scale biological big data. This course is a core foundational course for the bioinformatics major. Its primary objectives are to enable students to understand and comprehend computational issues in life sciences, the representation and organization of biological data, basic algorithms for biological data management, analysis, mining, and application. Additionally, students will gain insight into the fundamental principles, components, and operation of bioinformatics algorithm systems. The course aims to prepare students for designing, constructing, and applying bioinformatics algorithms and systems to solve computational problems in the fields of life science and biomedicine, particularly in the realm of big data analysis and applications. The main content of the course covers data structures for representing, organizing, and indexing biological bigdata, core algorithms for large-scale sequence alignment, searching, variant calling, as well as basic methods for assessing the quality of biological data. Emphasis is placed on hands-on algorithm experiments to cultivate students' abilities in the design and practical application of bioinformatics algorithms, laying a solid theoretical and technical foundation to achieve the course objectives.
