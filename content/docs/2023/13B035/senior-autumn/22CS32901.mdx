The "Biomedical Image Processing" course synthesizes cutting-edge research outcomes from scientific practices in biological and medical image processing and analysis, gene microarrays, and macromolecular structure determination. The course content encompasses the core principles and innovations of over a dozen Nobel Prize-winning advancements in modern medical imaging domains such as X-ray, CT, MRI, and PET/CT, while also engaging with the latest developments in computational imaging, notably those involving deep learning methodologies. This curriculum is designed to unveil an academic blueprint of the discipline, to inspire and broaden students' scholarly perspectives, and to empower them with a precise understanding of the technological trends within the field.
