At present, the calculation and analysis of engine strength and vibration is one of the important contents of engine design, research and maintenance. With the continuous improvement of engine performance, the problem of strength and vibration of engine structure is becoming more and more important, which has become the key technology affecting the success or failure of advanced engine technology. This course is one of the core fundamental courses of Aircraft Power Engineering, mainly for undergraduates majoring in Aircraft Power Engineering. The course teaches the theoretical knowledge and calculation methods of engine strength and vibration, including blade strength analysis, disc strength analysis, theoretical basis and analysis method of blade vibration, theoretical basis of rotor dynamics, vibration characteristics of the whole engine, experiments for blade and rotor vibration, etc. Through the study of this course, the students are able to know the problem of aero engine structure strength and vibration, master the calculation and analysis method of stress and vibration, understand the latest research progress and the key technology in this field, train students to apply the knowledge of strength and vibration, grasp the engine structure strength design method to graduate design and the future engine design, experiment and research.
