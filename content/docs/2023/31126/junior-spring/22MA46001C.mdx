This course is a compulsory basic course for undergraduate students who major in the applied mathematics, and is also necessary tools for mathematical modeling of practical problems in other disciplines such as mechanics, engineering, economics, etc. This course systematically introduces the basic theoretical knowledge and research methods of dynamical systems, and is divided into three parts: The first part mainly introduces the relevant knowledge of planar dynamical systems, including important concepts of dynamical systems such as limit sets, the closed orbits, and Poincaré maps; The second part mainly introduces the relevant content of nonlinear differential dynamical systems, including stream functions of nonlinear equations, the linearization theory, Poincaré-Bendixon theorem, etc; The third part mainly introduces the relevant knowledge of discrete dynamical systems.
