    en: In this class, the fundamentals of embedded system hardware and firmware design will be explored. Issues such as embedded processor selection, hardware/firmware partitioning, design methods of embedded system, development tools, firmware architecture, firmware design, and firmware debugging will be discussed. ARM microprocessor and Linux are one of the most popular embedded microprocessors and embedded operating systems, respectively. By using an ARM9 experimental box, each student will experience how to develop and download bootloader, transplant Linux operating system, and develop application programs and drivers of peripheral equipments. The course will culminate with a significant final project which will help students to deepen their comprehension to most knowledge of this course. Depending on the interests of the students, other topics may be covered.
