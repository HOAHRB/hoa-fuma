    en: The high-speed digital circuit design is a very complex process. With the complexity and integration highly increased, the traditional methods do not work due to the transmission line effects and signal integrity issues. So the high-speed digital circuit design technique has been essential for electronic engineers, especially with the rapid development of the communication network and electronic technology. The “high-speed circuit design” course is opened for the undergraduate student of the school of electronics and information engineering in Harbin institute of technology. This course focuses on the terms of the digital circuits, high-speed characteristics of the basic logic circuits and standard high-speed measurement and some other issues that affect signal integrity in high-speed digital design, such as transmission line, clock skew and jitter, termination, etc. Through the study of this course, students can master the basic concepts, principles, basic analysis methods, typical circuits and design methods in the process of high-speed digital circuit design. They can understand the signal integrity concept which are related to the high-speed digital design, and master the high-speed characteristics of the basic logic circuits, measurement techniques and other issues about high-speed digital design that should be paid attention to, and being able to design of some typical high-speed digital circuits. This course not only improve the ability of the students in the field of electric measurement in theory and circuit design, but also build the foundation for the students who would be electronic system engineers in communication and testing area to study, research and development the high-speed digital design in the future.
