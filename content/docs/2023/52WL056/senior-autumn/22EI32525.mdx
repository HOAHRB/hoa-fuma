    en: AI large models are leading technological changes. This course explores the innovative applications of AI large models in the field of testing and experimentation. In the field of test, large models has shown broad and far-reaching application prospects. In the product testing link, the large model can build an intelligent fault prediction model based on past product testing data, fault cases, etc., to accurately predict possible failures of components in advance, effectively reducing the failure rate of products in actual use and saving maintenance costs. In terms of experimental design, large models can use algorithms to optimize experimental plans, choose the most representative samples from a large number of potential experimental combinations, reduce the number of experiments, shorten the development cycle, and ensure the reliability of experimental results. For the processing and analysis of experimental data, large models can quickly process and analyze complex, high-dimensional experimental data, uncover hidden patterns and correlations behind the data, and assist researchers in making more scientific decisions. This course will systematically explain the basic theory of large models, conduct in-depth analysis based on rich testing and experimental field cases, and arrange experimental projects for students to personally experience the application process of large models in professional scenarios. By combining theory with experimentation in teaching, we aim to help students master the application skills of large models in the field of testing and experimentation, cultivate their ability to use cutting-edge technology to solve practical professional problems, and lay a solid foundation for their future career development and innovative research in this field.
