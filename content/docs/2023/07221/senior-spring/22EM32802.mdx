    en: Venture Capital is a basic course of Finance Major. It mainly studies the equity capital which the professional financier invests in the enterprise which is emerging and quick-developing, and has strong competitive potential. Firstly, it introduces the history of the emergence and development of venture investment as well as the objective necessity for developing venture investment in China. Secondly, it introduces the basic organization form of venture capital, the supervision and management of venture capitalist on venture entrepreneurs, the effective mechanism design of risk control, and the specific operation methods of auditing business plan and implementing venture control by stages. Thirdly, it systematically introduces the operation process characteristics of venture investment, analyzes the life cycle and capital requirement characteristics of high-technology enterprises, and introduces in detail how the high-technology enterprise drafts project proposals and business plan and how to compete for venture capital. Finally, this course summarizes the market characteristics and transition system of American NASDAQ, and its positive effects on venture investment. This course aims to make students understand the operation characteristics of each stage of venture investment and the related regulation design by introducing the whole process of venture investment.
