    en: International finance is a basic financial theory course with the background of economic globalization and financial integration, international currency circulation, international financing and investment activities as the main body. It studies the operation rules and functions of international capital flow in international economy, balance of payments, foreign exchange and exchange rate, as well as foreign financial activities of different countries. One of the teaching objectives of this course is to enable students to master the theoretical system of modern international finance, including the theory of balance of payments adjustment, foreign exchange rate theory, the theory of exchange rate system selection, the theory of international capital flow, and the theory of currency crisis. While learning the theory, students should pay attention to the cultivation of critical thinking, make an objective evaluation of the existing theories based on the reality and avoid following blindly. The second purpose of the teaching is to require students to master the international financial practical knowledge and operational skills, such as the trading mode of foreign exchange market (including the traditional way and the innovative way), foreign exchange risk composition and risk prevention methods. The third teaching purpose requires students to be familiar with the characteristics of international financial markets, the principles of international financial regulation and the development overview of the current international monetary system.
