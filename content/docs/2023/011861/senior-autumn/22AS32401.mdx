    en: This study aims on the solid mechanics and physics of polymeric condensed-matter. In this course, we will focus on the long-term molecular structure, relaxation behavior of macromolecules and their aggregates, to formulate the constitutive relationships between molecular structure-relaxation-performance of the polymeric condensed-matter. In combination of theoretical basis and research outcome of solid mechanics and physics of polymeric condensed-matter, the nature of glassy matter, thermodynamics in glass transition, polymer solution theory and scaling law, thermodynamic phase separation of polymeric condensed-matter, dynamic relaxation, Gaussian distribution of molecular weight and transition probability, Maxwell equation for viscoelastic relaxation, Boltzmann superposition and mechanics of soft matter. Their potential applications will be also discussed. With aid of this course, it is helpful for students to know the cutting edge researchers in solid mechanics and physics of polymeric condensed-matter, which is then be used as a fundamental basis for further study and research.
