It aims to cultivate interdisciplinary talent with both energy system expertise and applied AI capabilities. By focusing on core scenarios such as wind-PV-storage collaborative forecasting and intelligent frequency regulation of thermal power units, the course systematically explains the engineering implementation of AI algorithms in power generation forecasting (e.g., LSTM) and equipment fault diagnosis (e.g., 1D-CNN). Students will acquire hands-on skills covering the entire workflow—from data cleaning and model deployment to result analysis—and learn to use tools like Scikit-learn and MATLAB for modeling and optimizing typical power generation scenarios. They will develop foundational engineering capabilities to address small and medium-scale "source-grid-load" coordination challenges, preparing them to contribute to the intelligent upgrading of the energy sector as technically skilled professionals.
