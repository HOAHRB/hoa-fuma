    zh: 发展高效、稳定、成本低廉的储能蓄热技术，对提升不稳定热源的能量利用效率、增加电网对可再生能源的消纳具有重要作用，是提高能源利用效率、增加可再生能源占比的重要途径。相比显热储热及相变储热，热化学储热技术通过化学键的断裂重组实现能量的储存和释放，具有更大的储能密度（约为显热储能的10倍及相变储能的5倍）和更广的储能温度范围，且能量损失小，可实现能量的季节性存储而不需特殊的绝热措施。本课程的目标是通过讲授热化学储能技术基本原理，使学生了解热化学储热相比显热及相变储热在储能密度、热损失、储能温度、储能周期等方面的优点；通过讲授各种典型热化学储热体系的原理、材料、储热规律、系统优化，使学生掌握各典型体系的关键材料及工艺流程；通过2次课堂研讨，使学生掌握运用所学基础理论知识综合分析典型热化学储热体系的方法。本课程主要培养学生以下几方面能力： （1）分析计算能力：正确理解热化学储热技术的基本原理及特点，运用数学知识对体系热力学和动力学相关理论公式进行推导和应用；计算各类热化学储热技术的体积/质量储能密度等关键技术指标；（支撑毕业要求1、2、5） （2）思辨能力：热化学储热技术尚处于实验室研究向工程示范的过渡阶段，部分理论仍处于百家争鸣阶段。通过作业及课堂讨论的设计，本课程着力培养学生基于基本科学原理，发现各类热化学储热体系中问题的能力，通过合作学习搜集素材进行论证及展望；（支撑毕业要求4、6、7、10、12） （3）解决工程问题能力：通过讲授各类典型热化学储热体系的技术原理、工艺流程及单元装备，使学生初步具备运用数学、工程热力学、化学等所学基础知识解决热化学储热技术相关的复杂工程问题的能力；（支撑毕业要求1、3、4）
    en: 'Developing efficient, stable, and low-cost heat storage technologies plays an important role in improving the energy utilization efficiency of unstable heat sources and increasing the consumption of renewable energy by the power grid. It is an important way to improve energy utilization efficiency and increase the proportion of renewable energy. Compared to sensible heat storage and phase change heat storage, thermochemical heat storage technology achieves energy storage and release through the breaking and recombination of chemical bonds, with a larger energy storage density (about 10 times that of sensible heat storage and 5 times that of phase change storage) and a wider range of energy storage temperatures, and with less energy loss, it can achieve seasonal energy storage without the need for special insulation measures. The goal of this course is to teach students the basic principles of thermochemical heat storage technology, and to enable them to understand the advantages of thermochemical heat storage over sensible and phase change heat storage in terms of energy density, heat loss, storage temperature, and storage period; By teaching the principles, materials, thermal storage laws, and system optimization of various typical thermochemical thermal storage systems, students can master the key materials and process flows of each typical system; Through two discussions, students will master the method of applying their basic theoretical knowledge to comprehensively analyze typical thermochemical thermal storage systems. This course mainly cultivates students'' abilities in the following areas: (1) Analytical and computational abilities: Correctly understand the basic principles and characteristics of thermochemical heat storage technology, apply mathematical knowledge to derive and apply theoretical formulas related to system thermodynamics and kinetics;？Calculate key technical indicators such as volume/mass energy storage density for various thermochemical heat storage technologies; (2) Speculative ability: Thermochemical thermal storage technology is still in the transitional stage from laboratory research to engineering demonstration, and some theories are still in the early stage.？Through the design of assignments and discussions, this course focuses on cultivating students'' ability to discover problems in various thermochemical thermal storage systems based on basic scientific principles. Through collaborative learning, materials are collected for argumentation; (3) Ability to solve engineering problems: By teaching the technical principles, process flow, and unit equipment of various typical thermochemical heat storage systems, students will have the ability to apply basic knowledge such as mathematics, engineering thermodynamics, and chemistry to solve complex engineering problems related to thermochemical heat storage technology.'
