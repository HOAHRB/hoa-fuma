    en: Architectural Heritage Conservation and Sustainable Renewal is an undergraduate course for students in architecture, urban planning, and related disciplines. It introduces the fundamental theories, value identification methods, spatial analysis perspectives, and sustainable renewal strategies for historic buildings, historic districts, and existing built environments. The course is jointly developed by an architectural scholar from the University of Adelaide and faculty members from the School of Architecture and Design at Harbin Institute of Technology. Drawing on international expertise in architectural history, sustainable retrofit, adaptive reuse, and design practice, the course combines theoretical teaching with case-based learning. Through lectures, case studies, and discussions, students will learn to understand architectural heritage conservation from the perspectives of historical value, spatial quality, contemporary use, environmental performance, and sustainable development, laying a foundation for further study in architectural design, urban renewal, and historic environment conservation.
