The course scope covers both (a) basic technical aspects, such as indicatively Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer, Energy Conversion Technologies, Renewable Energy Systems, Energy Storage, Power Systems, etc., and (b) management and economic aspects, such as indicatively Energy Markets and Economics, Sustainability and Environmental Impact, Project Management, etc. in order to bridge the gap between technology and business. The aim is not only to acquaint STEM students and professionals with the methods needed for the integrated interdisciplinary approach of individual energy issues, but also to spark their potential interest in the research field and enable them to generate new knowledge and innovation.
