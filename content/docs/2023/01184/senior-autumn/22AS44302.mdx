    en: The development and utilization of biomass materials are effective ways to achieve “carbon peaking and carbon neutrality” goals. Bio-based materials have been included in key material areas that require major breakthroughs explicitly in the “The Green Plan for Industrial Development in the 14th Five-Year Period”. This course will guide students to understand the preparation and modification methods of the biomass-based carbon materials and make them skilled in the operation of the equipment and familiar with the evaluation methods of key performance of the biomass-based carbon materials through experiment. Simultaneously, the course will guide students to prepare high-efficiency heat-insulating carbon foams and high-performance electromagnetic shielding materials using wood and bamboo as raw materials. This course aims to make students feel the charm of scientific and technological innovation closely and cultivate students' scientific research mind and lay a foundation for their future study of the majors related to materials science. Meanwhile, this course will actively guide students to participate in subject competitions, help them accumulate competition experience and improve their relative skills.
