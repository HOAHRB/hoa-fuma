    en: Rapid Control Prototype (RCP) and Hardware in the Loop (HIL) Simulation Technology is a highly applied and practical course designed for the development of students in the field of control science and engineering and related majors. It mainly cultivates and popularizes the international advanced development concept of model-based design, and is an important part of the practical process of control systems. The teaching content is currently an important method for control system design internationally, seamlessly connecting model based functional design and subsequent practical verification to improve development efficiency. RCP is a system that compiles and downloads algorithm models to a specific simulation hardware platform for real-time operation and control of actual objects, enabling rapid verification and modification of design algorithms, reducing development cycles and costs. After the controller design is completed, it needs to be tested in detail in a closed-loop system. Due to potential challenges such as extreme testing, failure testing, or high testing costs in real-world environments, HIL simulation technology has become an effective approach. HIL utilizes computer hardware platforms to simulate the motion process of objects under actual working conditions in the laboratory, in order to comprehensively verify the quality of controller development and the reliability of control algorithms, reduce testing cycles and costs, and improve testing safety. The main task of this course is to enable students to master the methods of establishing RCP and HIL simulation technology, as well as their application in control system design, through classroom teaching and hands-on practice, and to cultivate their ability to connect theory with practice and solve problems.
