    en: Ecology is a science that studies the relationship between biological organisms and their surroundings, and is the embodiment of life science at the macro level. In an ecosystem, each species is not cluttered and stacked, but constitutes an orderly spatial pattern, and they are interdependent and mutually restricted to form an organic whole. These knowledge can only be deepened in the natural environment. The practice lasted for one week, and the practice site was a representative boreal forest. The practice contents included the investigation of community type lines, community structure characteristics, community renewal, succession trend, and corresponding statistical analysis and induction. Practical teaching is an important part of cultivating college students' practical ability. On the one hand, ecological practice can consolidate and enrich classroom teaching content, enhance perceptual understanding and broaden knowledge field; On the other hand, it can also cultivate students' love of nature, love of life, teamwork, hard-working spirit, enhance the ability of independent observation, thinking, problem solving, as well as the ability to survive and work in the field, and improve the comprehensive quality of students. With the development of the country, ecological damage and environmental pollution have been paid more and more attention, and have also become an important part of ecology and environmental ecological engineering. Ecological practice can intuitively observe and study ecological damage and restoration, understand the corresponding national policies and regulations and their practical performance, trigger inspiration, arouse interest in ecological environmental protection, inspire ideas to solve ecological environmental problems by combining knowledge of other disciplines of life science, and expand the direction of future research and work.
