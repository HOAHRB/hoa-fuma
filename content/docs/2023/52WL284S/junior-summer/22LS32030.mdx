    zh: “神经生物学前沿进展”课程将提供有关膜结构和神经细胞中膜兴奋性机制的最新进展。本课程将讨论转运蛋白、膜受体、基于结构的离子通道激活机制。同时探讨它们对引发电信号传导的作用和在细胞通讯中所起的重要作用。此外，将讨论神经营养因子，生长因子，肽或胺类神经递质的信号传导机制。接受各种因素刺激后受体会产生多种信号传导途径，细胞组分通过整合各种信号形成最终信息，并该信息将被传递至目标神经元。作为受体的激活因子其主要由神经末梢分泌。本课程将讨论突触小泡与靶膜之间的融合过程，以了解胞吐作用和内吞作用调节神经传递的机制。然后，本课程将提供有关视觉，听觉和躯体感觉等感官信息处理的讲座。最后，本课程将提供研究神经退行性疾病（包括阿尔茨海默氏病和帕金森氏病）的最新研究进展，这些疾病是大脑衰老的主要危险因素。
    en: The course will cover topics which are not discussed in Neuroscience course. Advanced neurobiology course will provide in-depth knowledge about membrane structure and the mechanism of membrane excitability in neuronal cells. There are many kinds of transporters in the membrane such as pumps, exchangers, and ion channels. The structure-based activation mechanisms of transporters and ion channels will be discussed and their contribution to elicit electrical signaling will be discussed in detail. There are also many different types of receptors in the membrane to play an important role in cell communication. The signaling mechanisms of neurotrophic factors, growth factors, peptides or amine neurotransmitters will be discussed. There are diverse signaling pathways generated from receptors stimulated by the above factors and various signaling should be integrated to make finalized information which will be delivered to target neuron. The receptor activating factors are mostly secreted from nerve terminal. The fusion process between synaptic vesicles and target membrane will be discussed to understand the mechanism of exocytosis and endocytosis for regulated neurotransmission. Then I will give lectures for sensory information processing such as vision, hearing, and somatic sensation. Finally the course will provide recent research progress in studying neurodegenerative diseases including Alzheimer’s disease and Parkinson’s disease which are major risk factors for aging brain.
