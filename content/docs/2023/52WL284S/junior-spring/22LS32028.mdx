In the course, the Earth history will first be explained, and then the basic concepts, the evolution thoughts and theories. The evolution process and regulations were narrated from two aspects. On the one hand, the history process of evolution was explored according to the time vector. on the other hand, the evolutionary laws and the mechanism of species formation are elaborated from the different levels of biological organizations (the evolution of plants, animals and human beings); at the same time, the factors affecting biological evolution are elaborated from the genetic perspective.
