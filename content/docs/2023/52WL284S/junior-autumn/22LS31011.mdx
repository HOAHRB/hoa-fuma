As biology transforms into a field that increasingly values quantitative analysis and intersects with diverse scientific discipline, this course prepares students to excel in such integrated environment. Central to the course are fundamental biophysical concepts that are vital across various biological contexts. It encompasses an array of exciting topics, including molecular biophysics, membrane and cell biophysics, sensory and neural biophysics, photo-biophysics, bio-electromagnetism, biomolecular electronics, and biosensing techniques. This course not only equips students with essential techniques to study the structure and function of biological macromolecules and cells using modern physics but also encourages an interdisciplinary approach to biological challenges. It’s designed to enhance students’ ability to comprehend, analyze, and solve complex biological issues through a nuanced and profound lens, making it an ideal choice for those looking into deepen their understanding of life sciences through the fascinating perspective of physics.
