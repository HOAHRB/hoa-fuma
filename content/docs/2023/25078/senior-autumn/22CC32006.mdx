This course is intended to serve for the national defense research. Through the explanation of the practical problems encountered in the field of solid propellants in China as well as the shortcomings of the existing domestic research and the important frontier directions, the course will stimulate the enthusiasm and enthusiasm of the students to solve the problems, the purpose of patriotic education and classroom civic teaching will be achieved. This course systematically teaches the definition, classification, basic composition and functional indexes of solid propellants, especially for the energy performance, combustion performance, mechanical performance, stability and danger of solid propellants. Based on the background of actual national defense needs, the course will introduce the chemical principles involved in solid propellants, study the intrinsic relationship between the material composition of energy-containing materials and the performance of solid propellants, and cultivate the students' awareness of serving national defense scientific research and encourage their thinking of problem solving.
