    en: The teaching objectives of this course are to enable undergraduate students, after studying courses such as University Physics, Engineering Fluid Mechanics, Heat Transfer, and Engineering Thermodynamics, to master the measurement principles and methods of commonly used thermal parameters (such as temperature, heat flux, physical property parameters, wide-spectrum radiant energy, etc.) in processes like combustion flames, high-temperature thermochemistry, high-temperature heat equipment, and thermal environment control in the field of energy and power. Students should acquire a good understanding of the working principles, performance, error propagation theory, and signal conversion processes of sensors. Based on this foundation, they are expected to master thermal information perception technologies for different objects based on various principles and devices. Moreover, students should be familiar with the measurement, perception, and inversion reconstruction principles and procedures of common thermal physical quantity fields such as temperature, velocity, and concentration in the field of smart energy and power. They will also undergo training in the design of thermal physical parameter/thermal information measurement systems and gain an understanding of the frontiers and development trends of thermal information perception technologies in the smart energy field in the new era.
