Thermal energy conversion devices could heat the working fluid to a specific parameter using chemical, nuclear, or solar energy. The course''s primary objective is to enable students to master the structure of thermal energy conversion devices and the thermal calculation process and to correctly apply the knowledge gained to the design process of thermal energy conversion devices. In this course, we comprehensively use the relevant knowledge and skills learned in the previous courses, combined with classroom discussions and experimental teaching, to carry out the basic training required by energy and power engineering technicians. This course focuses on cultivating students'' ability to design, calculate and analyze the comprehensive application of knowledge in thermal energy conversion devices and cultivating students'' innovative spirit and practical ability. Specific training goals: (1) Solid grasp of chemical energy - heat energy conversion device (boiler) combustion chamber structure and thermal calculations, gas - steam, gas - water, gas - gas, steam - water, steam heat exchanger structure and its thermal calculations. (4) To master the experimental method of the heat balance of chemical-thermal energy conversion device (boiler) and enhance the experimental skills. (5) Students will be able to exercise their practical engineering skills, logical thinking, innovation consciousness, energy saving, environmental protection consciousness, and scientific research ability.
