Compressed Sensing is a new developed theoretical framework for information acquisition and processing, which is based on optimization and statistical probability theory and so on. The high-dimensional signal can be recovered from the low-dimensional and sub-Nyquist sampling data based on the compressibility of signals. This course explains the theories and methods can be applied to wireless communication, radar detection, pattern recognition, image compression, image super-resolution reconstruction and many other fields.
