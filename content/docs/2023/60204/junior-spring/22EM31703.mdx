In the current economic environment, the importance of internal controls and corporate governance is increasingly prominent, playing an irreplaceable role in the steady development of enterprises and risk prevention. This course aims to combine theory with practice, enabling students to comprehensively grasp the principles and frameworks of internal controls and corporate governance, and deeply understand the relationship between the two and their core roles in enterprise management. Simultaneously, it cultivates students' systematic thinking and practical abilities, allowing them to apply the principles of internal controls and corporate governance to solve practical problems. Firstly, in terms of knowledge learning, students are taught the basic concepts, principles, and frameworks of internal controls and corporate governance. This includes the five elements of internal controls (internal environment, risk assessment, control activities, information and communication, internal supervision), as well as internal and external corporate governance mechanisms. Students will also gain a deep understanding of the relationship between the two and their core roles in enterprise management. Secondly, in terms of ability cultivation, the course aims to cultivate and improve students' ability to analyze the current situation and problems of enterprise internal controls and corporate governance using their learned knowledge, and propose targeted solutions. Additionally, it fosters students' innovative and critical thinking, enabling them to examine problems from multiple perspectives and propose new viewpoints and methods. Finally, in terms of quality cultivation, the course helps students deeply understand the importance of professional ethics and integrity awareness in internal controls and corporate governance. This encourages students to consciously establish a good professional image and moral demeanor. Simultaneously, it cultivates students' sense of responsibility and mission, guiding them to establish correct values and professional views.
