    en: Microprocessor architecture mainly introduces the principles and methods to design the microprocessor architecture. The course first gives an overview of the development history and characteristics of microprocessors, computer architecture and organization, and contemporary advanced system level chip architecture. Then the definition and evaluation methods of microprocessor performance and cost are focused on. Based on this, the design principles of instruction set architecture are introduced in detail, and the design techniques of data path and control logic are analyzed. RISC technology is the mainstream technology of microprocessor design. This course focuses on the characteristics of RISC architecture, taking MIPS processor as an example, analyzes the characteristics and composition of MIPS’ instruction set, gives the methods and rules of implementing assembly language with this instruction set, analyzes and introduces the design methods of the data path and control structure of single-cycle processor, multi-cycle processor and pipeline processor based on the MIPS instruction set in detail. And principles and design methods of memory hierarchy system and cache are introduced. The course lays a theoretical foundation for designing the application-oriented system solutions.
