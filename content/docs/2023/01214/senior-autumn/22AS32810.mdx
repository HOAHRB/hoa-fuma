    en: The course will first introduce space radiation environment and the harm to the device / circuit, and how to select device / circuit material, process and hardened design technique. Then the special requirements for integrated circuit and design method under the radiation environment will be proposed. The content of the course is divided into five chapters. The radiation environments are introduced first, including the space radiation environment, atmospheric radiation environmental and terrestrial radiation environment. The hazards of the space radiation environment to the integrated circuit, which is mainly total ionizing dose effects and single event effects are summarized. In the second chapter, the material and radiation characteristics used for integrated circuit are introduced, and the hardened techniques of integrated circuit, including silicon material and SOI material, are introduced. The third chapter focuses on memory hardened design techniques, the system level is mainly error correction code technique, Hamming code hardened method, which can correct one bit error in a memory. At the circuit level, the hardened method for memory cell is introduced. Detection and correction technique is mainly through adding detection circuit to achieve the purpose of error detection and error correction. The fourth chapter focuses on transient error propagation in combinational circuits, including electrical masking, logic masking and window masking effects, and suppression techniques to transient error propagation. For sequential circuits mainly deals with the hardness of latches and triggers. The fifth chapter focuses on the evaluation methods of total dose radiation performance and single event effect radiation performance of the circuits, including computer environment evaluation technique, ground simulation test system and test methods.
