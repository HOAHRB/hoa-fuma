    en: Design for Rocket Control System is a specialized elective course for the fourth year of undergraduate students majoring in vehicle design and engineering. This course firstly introduces the composition, significance and realization of the launch vehicle control system, gives the detailed establishment process of the rocket motion equations based on the implicit relation and its promotion form, and the processing method of the rocket control model based on the linearization of small perturbation. Then, it focuses on the method of analyzing the stability of the uncontrolled motion of the rocket in order to clarify the free motion characteristics of the control object. After that, the basic knowledge and design methods of attitude stabilization control, deviation minimization control and load minimization control, which are widely used in the engineering practice of rocket design, are taught step by step, and their understanding is strengthened by combining them with actual rocket model design examples. Finally, several typical rocket advanced control methods are introduced, focusing on the composition framework and design method of adaptive broadening control, which will be adopted by NASA's main heavy launch vehicle. Through the study of this course, students can comprehensively and systematically master the control characteristics of the rocket as a complex object analysis method, in-depth understanding of the basic principles of the rocket control system design, can be engaged in the overall design of the rocket or rocket control system design for subsequent work to provide the basis. And through the study of rocket advanced control methods, broaden the horizons, cultivate innovative thinking.
