Space debris threatening the safety of spacecrafts is originated and generated from artificial space activities. Concern for it is growing in space engineering. This course covers essential knowledge of space debris environment and spacecraft shielding. Students can learn the sources, environmental characterization, the hazards, and the mitigation technologies of space debris, and spacecraft shielding technologies. They also will enhance the environmental and sustainable consciousness in space engineering. This course includes introduction, space debris environment and its source, engineering model of space debris environment, space debris mitigation, protection against hypervelocity impact of space debris, and risk assessment of space debris impact.
