    en: This course mainly aims at undergraduate and graduate students majoring in electronic information. Through thematic discussion, three core themes of "industrial Internet", "intelligent manufacturing" and "new quality productivity" are focused. Starting with the increasingly prominent demand for intelligent analysis and modeling of industrial big data for major equipment and complex industrial systems, students are guided to understand the close relationship between vertical scene lightweight intelligent small models, full chain enabling generalization large models and the latest industrial systems and equipment from the perspective of life-cycle professionals, and focus on the research, application, development and challenges of machine intelligence and computing intelligence represented by industrial Internet and industrial big data in testing and experiments in different industrial fields. The seminar topics will cover the core technologies necessary for intelligent industrial systems, such as intelligent perception, intelligent transmission, intelligent computing, and intelligent control decision-making.
