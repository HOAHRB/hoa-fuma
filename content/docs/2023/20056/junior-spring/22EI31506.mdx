    en: Test and evaluation is an activity to understand product performance or event results. It is a theory and method widely used in scientific research, engineering technology and management. With the increasing complexity of products, such as vehicles, aircraft, ships, intelligent systems, distributed systems and other complex products are widely used. It is necessary to complete the evaluation of their performance, effectiveness and system capability through test. The course of "The Theory and Method of Test" is mainly offered for undergraduates majoring in intelligent measurement and control engineering. The course is oriented to the equipment level, system level and system of system (SOS) level product test evaluation in the field of electronic information (including network). The theory part of the course teaches the basic concepts related to the test and evaluation of electronic information products, as well as the basic theories such as small sample test and evaluation method based on Bayesian method, sequential test method, regression analysis test method, and Monte Carlo test method. It focuses on the basics, common methods, and the common basis of electronic information products. The implementation part of the course teaches experimental design methods, construction methods/technologies of test systems based on real resources, all-digital simulation and mixture of virtual and real, operation control methods/technologies, test data analysis and processing methods, performance evaluation and effectiveness evaluation methods, focusing on electronic information products, web-based information systems, and more large-scale distributed system level products. The content of the experiment course is themed with weapon equipment system/SOS test and evaluation, which not only reflects the complex characteristics and wide area characteristics of product test, but also conforms to the characteristics of our school running and talent training. Through the course study, students will initially have the ability to design complex system product test systems and evaluate complex system products, so as to establish a foundation for conducting complex and large-scale system product test and evaluation.
