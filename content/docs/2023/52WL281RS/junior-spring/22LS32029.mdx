Based on this course, students can understand the growing concern of the ecological environment, understand the fundamental way to solve environmental problems is to regulate the relationship between human social activities and the environment, and lay a solid foundation for exploring the solution for the future environmental protection, ecological management and rational use of resources and so on from the perspective of engineering. Based on this course, students can master the basic types of environmental biotechnology, the basic knowledge and methods of environmental ecological engineering design, master the biological treatment technology of waste water, solid waste, air pollutants, master producing alcohol, hydrogen, methane, master bio-fuel cells, bio-metallurgy, biological desulfurization, environmental biological monitoring technology, understand the characteristics and self-purification capacity of farmland, lake, river, wetlands, desert, soil and other ecosystem, master ecosystem design, management and engineering techniques to reduce pollution generation and to improve ecological restoration.
