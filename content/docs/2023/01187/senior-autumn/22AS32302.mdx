    en: This course aims to broaden students' understanding of aerospace, emphasizing the crucial role and significance of thermal protection materials and structures in aerospace vehicles. It focuses on enhancing students' skills in experimental methods and material characterization, as well as understanding and mastering the preparation methods and characterization techniques of relevant thermal protection materials and structures. The goal is to stimulate students' interests in learning and cultivate a lifelong learning mindset, contributing to the development of highly skilled talent in the field of national aerospace. Lecture teaching mainly focuses on teaching the background and application, research progress, design concepts and methods, modern preparation processes, performance testing methods and evaluations, ground simulation environmental test assessments and flight demonstration verification of ultra-high temperature thermal protection materials and structures, etc. On site teaching relies on the ultra-high temperature thermal protection laboratory of the Institute of Composite Materials and Structures, the main content of on-site teaching is the ultra-high temperature thermal protection material UHTCC in special environments, the new generation spacecraft re-entry thermal protection material PICA, and the integrated thermal insulation material TUFROC for aerospace vehicles. The design concepts and preparation methods of ablative and non-ablative thermal protection materials in the course content are explained, and the principles and usage methods of various basic instruments and equipment are taught, enabling students to deepen their understanding of the course content, laying a solid foundation for their professional learning and graduation design experiments.
