It plays an important role in cultivating students’ ability of aircraft dynamics modeling and analysis, control system analysis and design, and solving practical engineering problems. This course mainly includes three parts : flight vehicle structural dynamics, control theory basis and attitude dynamics and control. In the part of flight vehicle structural dynamics, by teaching the scientific theoretical knowledge of structural dynamics, students can master important concepts such as natural frequency, mode and frequency response function of typical structures, as well as structural vibration analysis methods based on modal decomposition and superposition. In the basic part of control theory, the composition, working principle and function of each component of the control system are taught. It enables students to master the method of establishing the mathematical model of the control system and several methods of analyzing and synthesizing the control system, such as time domain method, root locus method, frequency method, state space method and phase plane method. In addition, it leads the students to understand the analysis and synthesis method of the typical linear control system and nonlinear system. What’s more, it leads the students to establish the concept of system, the concept of mathematical model, the concept of dynamic process, the concept of closed-loop feedback and the concept of comprehensive design. In the part of attitude dynamics and control, the attitude dynamics and attitude control of spacecraft are taught. In the part of attitude dynamics and control, by teaching the related concepts of spacecraft attitude dynamics and attitude control, students can understand and master the basic principles of spacecraft attitude dynamics modeling, spacecraft attitude determination and spacecraft attitude control, as well as the attitude dynamics modeling method and attitude control method of spin spacecraft and three-axis stabilized spacecraft. While teaching professional knowledge, this course cultivates students'' ability of aircraft dynamics modeling, analysis and control system design. While strengthening scientific literacy, this course also cultivates students'' ability to solve practical engineering problems.'
