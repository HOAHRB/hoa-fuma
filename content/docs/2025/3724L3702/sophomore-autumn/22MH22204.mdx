    en: Bioinformatics applies mathematics, informatics, statistics and computational and scientific methods to study biological problems and is one of the major frontier fields of life sciences and natural sciences today. Focusing on the study of theories and methods of mining and extracting information from molecular biological data, the application and development of computational methods and the full sharing of resources are realized. On the basis of popularizing the basic knowledge for bioinformatics (including basic theories and methods), this course focuses on introducing the basic technologies, methods and tools of data analysis, and grasping the knowledge of bioinformatics to lead the forefront of biological big data analysis around the hot issues of life science research. This course consists of two parts. The first part covers common molecular-level data acquisition, analysis methods and techniques, including genome sequencing, single-cell sequencing, proteome sequencing, and epigenome sequencing, which enables students to learn the advanced basic theoretical knowledge and skills of bioinformatics and master the commonly used database and data analysis tools through computer practice; The second part combines biomedical practice to teach the application of bioinformatics, and introduces the research progress of bioinformatics in cancer biology, immunology, drug design, etc. with examples. This course focuses on developing students' ability to solve scientific problems. Through this course, students can master the common methods and data resources of omics data analysis ranging from genome, transcriptome, epigenome to proteome, as well as the application of multi-omics data in the fields of disease, immunity, drug design and so on.
