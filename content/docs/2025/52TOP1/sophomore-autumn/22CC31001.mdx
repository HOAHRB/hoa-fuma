In Chapter 2, the short-range structure, the far-range structure and the conformation statistics of polymer chains are introduced. In Chapter 3, the dissolution of polymers, thermodynamic properties of polymer solutions, polymer concentrated solutions and Flory-Huggins theory of polymer solutions are introduced. In Chapter 4, the amorphous structure models, glass transition and viscous flow of amorphous polymers are introduced. In Chapter 5, the morphology and structure of polymer crystals, the structure model of polymer crystals, the crystallization process of polymer, the melting of polymer crystals, the influence of crystallinity on the physical and mechanical properties of polymer, and the liquid crystal state of polymer are introduced. In Chapter 6, the tensile behavior of polymers, the fracture theory and theoretical strength of polymers, and the factors affecting the actual strength of polymers are introduced. In Chapter 7, rubber elasticity of polymers, the mechanical models of polymer viscoelasticity are introduced.
