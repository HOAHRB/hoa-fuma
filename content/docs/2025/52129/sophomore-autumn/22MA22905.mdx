    en: 'Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs) form a foundational subject in mathematics, physics, and engineering, focusing on equations that involve unknown functions and their derivatives. The course typically begins with analytical techniques for first-order ODEs, including separable variables, homogeneous equations, linear equations, and exact equations. It then progresses to higher-order linear differential equations, emphasizing methods like characteristic roots and Laplace transforms for constant-coefficient systems. Students also explore systems of ODEs solved via matrix exponentials and gain introductory insights into stability theory, such as phase plane analysis and Lyapunov functions. Practical applications are a key component, with ODEs modeling dynamical systems, population growth, electrical circuits, and mechanical vibrations. Through problem-solving and modeling exercises, learners develop the ability to translate real-world phenomena into mathematical frameworks, highlighting the interdisciplinary power of ODEs in science and technology. The key topics as follows: First-order ODEs、Higher-order Linear ODEs、Systems of ODEs involving eigenvalue solutions method. Stability & Qualitative Theory、Numerical Methods、Real world applications.'
