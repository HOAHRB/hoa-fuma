    en: Intelligence and autonomy are important characteristics of future spacecraft. This course primarily focuses on intelligent spacecraft overall design methods and new concept spacecraft design and simulation techniques. It elaborates on theoretical content, including the overall design process, conceptual design methods, dynamics and control, and spacecraft semi-physical simulation systems. Using quadcopter drones as a practical example, students gain a comprehensive understanding of spacecraft control system design and simulation. They also have hands-on experience in course projects, achieving practical results. Through this course, students establish a theoretical foundation and practical experience for future research and work in the aerospace field, especially in spacecraft overall design, attitude control system design, and simulation.
