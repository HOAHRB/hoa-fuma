    en: 'Integrated Skills of English (II) is a a compulsory course designed for first-year undergraduate students in the School of Future Technology who are enrolled in the Development Level of College English. It aims to cultivate their academic English proficiency and literacy within a higher education environment. The course focuses on key academic skills such as reading scientific literature, writing and presenting academic papers, and participating in academic discussions, with an emphasis on enhancing students’ critical thinking in academic English and practical application skills. Employing an outcome-oriented approach to academic English instruction, the course guides students through the processes of academic writing, submission communication, and participation in international conferences. The course covers four core areas: 1. Academic English Literacy Development – Mastering the linguistic norms required for international academic communication; 2. Scientific Paper Reading and Analysis – Understanding and analyzing the structure of scientific research papers; 3. Academic Paper Writing – Composing research papers that adhere to academic conventions; 4. International Academic Communication – Engaging in academic paper submission, responding to reviewers’ comments, creating academic posters, and delivering academic presentations. The course adopts the textbook Advanced Academic English for Science and Engineering, which includes modules on research preparation, paper structure analysis and writing, and academic communication. Through interactive classroom activities, case studies, and project-based learning, students will gain a comprehensive understanding of the academic research process, from topic selection to presenting their findings. This course has the following key features. Firstly, it adopts discipline-oriented approach aligned with engineering fields. Secondly, it is project-driven learning integrated with competition practice – the course follows a “course-project integration” and “course-competition integration” model, allowing students to write academic papers based on their first-year research projects and data. Thirdly, it simulates international academic exchange process. By completing this course, students will acquire the ability to effectively communicate in English within academic research, international scholarly exchange, and technological innovation, equipped with a strong linguistic foundation for future scientific research and career development.'
