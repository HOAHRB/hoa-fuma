This course plays a pivotal role in fostering students' scientific thinking abilities and their capacity to comprehensively apply circuit and electronic technology theories to analyze and resolve practical issues. The course primarily focuses on explaining semiconductor devices, basic amplifier circuits, field-effect transistors, filter circuits, DC power supplies, combinational logic circuits, sequential logic circuits, as well as digital-to-analog and analog-to-digital conversion circuits. Through classroom instruction, online MOOC learning, and post-class assignment practices, it enables students to gain an understanding of the development trends in electronic technology and the application overview of new knowledge, devices, and technologies. The course places particular emphasis on cultivating students' comprehensive analytical and design capabilities in both analog and digital electronic circuits, as well as their computer simulation skills. It strengthens the connection between theory and practice, enhances students' awareness of practical application and innovation, and inspires their patriotism and sense of mission to contribute to the nation through technological advancements, thereby supporting the achievement of corresponding indicator points in their professional learning outcomes.
