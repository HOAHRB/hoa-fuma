During the course the students are supposed to learn new grammar rules and do grammar exercises, to learn new words for everyday life situations, express their opinions and discuss general issues. The students are also supposed to expand their vocabulary in the sphere of their scientific research. In the course the students will learn to write short summaries about their research and will practice making oral scientific reports and discussing the topics of their research with their fellow students. Apart from expending students’ vocabulary and their knowledge of grammar, the key takeaways of the course will be a written abstract and a script of an oral report the students will be able to use in their further scientific career while participating in international conferences or submitting articles to international science magazines.
