    en: Integrated Skills of English （I）is a a compulsory course designed for first-year undergraduate students in the School of Future Technology who are enrolled in the Development Level of College English. It aims to develop students’ comprehensive abilities of the English language (listening, speaking, reading and writing) so that solid foundation will be laid for the students to communicate and interact internationally in various fields and on different occasions. This course covers four parts (listening, speaking, reading and writing) and teaching materials are carefully collected strictly according to the requirements of IELTS tests. Listening materials are chosen from different situations in real life; speaking materials include all kinds of related topics; reading materials closely model IELTS tests; writing materials cover seven possible variations for task 1 and three possible variations for task 2. This course also helps students to develop lexical and syntactic skills specifically focusing on the building of vocabulary and the understanding of complex sentence structures as well as the broadening of their English background knowledge. At the end of this course, students’ comprehensive abilities are good enough to understand daily conversations, academic lectures and seminars, etc. in English; speak English fluently and clearly; read English journal articles and books with accurate comprehension; write English essays cohesively and coherently and describe information given in charts and graphs logically and clearly.
