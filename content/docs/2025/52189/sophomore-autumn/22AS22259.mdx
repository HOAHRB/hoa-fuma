    en: 'The object of this course is to impart a systematic undersdanding of the classical mechanics and its modern development for students. The course mainly expounds classical mechanics theory. This course consists of 3 volumes. Part 1 introduces the content of statics(Including the free-body diagram,planar force systems and couple systems, etc.), kinematics(Including the kinematics of a particle, the simple motion of a rigid body,resultant motion of a particle,etc.), dynamics(Including the particle dynamics,theorems of linear momentum,angular momentum and kinetic energy of particle systems ). Part 2 discusses the basic concepts of Lagrangain mechanics, including the general equations of dynamics, Lagrange equations and its first integrals: covers analytical mechanics (Including the fundamental equations of dynamics, Lagrange''s equations of the first and the second kind, etc.)and mechanics vibration.Part 3 introduces the kinematics and dynamics of rigid body about a fixed point, including the integraled cases of Euler and Lagrange. The general motion of a rigid body is discussed by example.'
