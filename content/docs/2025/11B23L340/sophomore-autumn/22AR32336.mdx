    en: Have you ever truly seen the city you live in? The streets you walk every day, the old buildings on the corner, the bustling squares, the quiet former factories and courtyard gardens—they are more than just dots on a map. They carry a city’s memory, warmth, and heartbeat. Understanding Cities is a course that takes you on a journey to rediscover the city around you. You will learn to read streets with your eyes, break down spaces with drawings, uncover patterns through data, and feel the pulse of urban life. From strolling through neighborhoods to gazing at skylines, from decoding a heatmap to understanding the “15-minute living circle,” you will gradually grasp why cities are fascinating, how they work, and for whom they are built. No boring theories—just real-world cases, genuine urban questions, and plenty of engaging discussions. At the end, you and your team will present your very own urban discovery report. If you are curious about the city beneath your feet, this course will give you a brand new pair of eyes.
