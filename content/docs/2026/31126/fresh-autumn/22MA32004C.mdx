    en: The course systematically introduces the general reasoning tools and fundamental methodologies common to various branches of mathematics, aiming to cultivate rigorous mathematical thinking and enhance students’ abilities in logical deduction and abstract generalization. The syllabus covers basic set theory, propositional logic and quantifiers, mathematical induction, the axiomatic construction of real numbers, sequences and inequalities, complex numbers and their geometric representation, polynomials and partial fraction decomposition, as well as the properties of numerical functions and mappings. Special attention is given to computational optimization techniques and mental arithmetic rules, reinforcing students’ efficiency in calculation and their capacity to tackle practical problems. Instruction combines lectures, handwritten assignments, independent study, and discussion sessions. Assessment is based on a comprehensive final project that evaluates students’ understanding of core concepts, reasoning methods, and application skills. This course prepares students for advanced studies in analysis, algebra, algorithm theory, and other related fields by providing a solid logical and methodological foundation.
