    en: This course is designed for undergraduate students and provides a systematic introduction to the fundamental theories, practical models and emerging developments in social governance. It examines the historical development, core concepts and the social governance system with Chinese characteristics, while introducing classical theories such as social control, governance theory and social capital. The course also explains the principles, operating mechanisms and practical approaches of social governance, and offers applied analyses of key areas including community governance, poverty governance, social organizations and public security. In addition, the course focuses on the transformations brought about by the digital age, examining governance innovations enabled by big data and Internet technologies, and exploring emerging topics such as data governance, virtual social governance and intelligent social governance. Combining theoretical foundations with real-world case studies, the course helps students develop a comprehensive understanding of contemporary social governance practices in China and cultivates their ability to observe and critically analyze social issues from a governance perspective.
