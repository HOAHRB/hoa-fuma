    en: Electrodynamics is an important theory of the physics and also a required major course for undergraduates in physics programs. Electrodynamics researches on the properties of the electromagnetic field, as well as the interactions between the electromagnetic field and matter. Electromagnetic interaction, which is currently the most well-understood, is one of the four fundamental forces in nature. There are lots of problems related to electromagnetic phenomenon in scientific research, practical activity and everyday life. It is very important and necessary for students to understand and master well the fundamental concepts, theories and methods of electrodynamics. In order to well master the fundamental theory and logical construction of the electrodynamics, the content is divided into two parts. In part one, we introduce mainly the fundamental concepts and theory, including electromagnetic field, electromagnetic wave, electromagnetic potential and electromagnetic radiations. In part two, we teach the solutions to boundary value problems in electrodynamics, in which some methods in mathematical physical are required. The course includes the special theory of relativity as well. The relativistic covariance of electrodynamics and the interaction between electromagnetic field and charged particles are introduced in the framework of relativity.
