Through this course, the students can acquire the following knowledge: some concepts about structured photonics, eigenvalue problems for transmission and reflection in photonic structures, calculation method of photonic band structures, multipole expansion and Mie problem in two- and three dimensional cases, coupled dipoles method, and rigorous multiple scattering theory.
