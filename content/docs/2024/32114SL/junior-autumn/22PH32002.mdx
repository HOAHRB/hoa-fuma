    en: Guided wave optics (GWO) emphasizes on the properties of light propagation in partially confined spaces or structures, which are quite different from those in free spaces. GWO is one of the key core courses for the photonic chip technology and fundamental researches in nanophotonics, and is also an indispensable part in knowledge system for majorities in physics. Through the study of this course, students will deep understand the conceptions and methods in GWO, mastering the operation principles and propagation properties of light in waveguides. Also, the design of waveguides should also be understood by the students. The main contents of this course include the fundamental theories and applications of GWO, including the propagation and coupling of planar waveguides. In this part, the students should derive and solve the dispersion equation of the waveguide, and clearly understand the conception of eigen mode and effective propagation constant, and the functional devices based on the optical waveguides. Further, the properties of rectangular waveguide and circular fiber are also vital in this course. In addition, for the surface plasmonic polariton waveguide, photonic crystal waveguide, topological waveguide proposed recently, the exist conditions of guiding mode, dispersion equations, and the properties of eigenmodes will be covered also. The final score of this course is accumulated by two parts of usual performances and the final examination.
