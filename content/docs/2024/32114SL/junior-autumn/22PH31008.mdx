    en: The course 《Principle and Technology of Modern Optical Measurement A》combines theory with technical applications, and focuses on the methods and their characteristics of optical precision measurement. It’s important for applications in industry, military, life science, medicine, and so on. In this course, we firstly introduce the fundamental of modern optical measurement technique, including the categories, system composing and the future trends. Typical optical measuring systems are analyzed with examples, and light sources, detectors and the match for both are highlighted. Then we introduce the laser interference, hologram, diffraction, ranging and speed measurement technologies. The course will give the students a theoretical background for analyze and design an optical measurement system independently, and improve the ability of analyzing and solving problems during optical testing by one’s own.
