    en: Pollution Control Microbiology is a fundamental and compulsory course for students who major in environmental science-related specialties. Morphology, physiological characteristics, metabolism, growth and reproduction, genetic variation and ecology of microorganisms in natural and controlled environments are introduced and discussed as the basic knowledge in the course. To have a better comprehensive and in-depth understanding of microbiology theory and engineering technology for preventing environmental pollution, microbiology principles of pollution control and ecological restoration are further instructed. Purpose of this course is to enable students to 1) identify and express complex problems in biological treatment of wastewater, exhaust gas and solid waste, as well as ecological restoration, to obtain the effective conclusions in view of the principles of microbiology for environmental pollution control; 2) understand the general experimental methods of environmental microbiology, and have the ability to design and implement experiments for resolving environmental engineering problems; 3) have the ability to solve complex engineering problems using the principles and experimental methods in Pollution Control Microbiology, laying the foundation for future practice, research and management in environmental science and engineering.
