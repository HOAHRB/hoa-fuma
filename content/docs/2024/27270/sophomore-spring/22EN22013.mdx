    en: Principles of Environmental Engineering is an important specialized core course for Environmental engineering in colleges and universities. This course systematically elaborates the basic technical principles in Water treatment engineering, Air pollution control engineering, Solid waste treatment and disposal engineering, Physical pollution control engineering, Ecological recovery project. It also extracts the common basic principles, phenomena and processes, and proposes the problem-solving methods. This course is also the theoretical basis of the following courses and reflects the special features. The main contents include Foundation of environmental engineering principles, Separation process principles, Chemical and biological reaction engineering Principles. The first part mainly contains Unit and dimensional analysis, conservation principles of material and energy, heat transfer and mass transfer, etc. The second part contains absorption, adsorption, filtration, evaporation and crystal, ionic-exchange, extraction, membrane separation, etc. The third part mainly contains stoichiometry and kinetics of chemical and biological reaction, reactors normally used in environmental engineering. Moreover, it also includes three experiments, such as plate frame filter experiment, fluidized-bed drying experiment and heat transfer experiment. By learning this course, the students can obtain the ability of analyzing the environmental process with basic scientific theory, design the research plan independently, construct the experimental devices and finish the experiments with scientific research methods. The students can also have the ability of analyzing the experimental process with the modern analytical testing equipment.
