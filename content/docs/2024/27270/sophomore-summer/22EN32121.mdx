    en: This course aims to introduce the complex global environmental problems and the underlying energy-environment nexus. The physicochemical processes and phenomena that are related with the energy-environment nexus will be introduced and discussed. This course explores the dynamic relationship between energy systems and global environmental change, with a focus on the scientific and technological dimensions of sustainable energy development. Students will examine the environmental impacts of conventional and renewable energy sources, including their roles in climate change, air and water pollution, and ecosystem disruption. Topics include the carbon cycle, atmospheric science and global warming, redox processes in energy conversion and the related environmental processes, various energy-environment nexus issues, and international environmental agreements. Through lectures, case studies, and group discussions, students will gain a foundational understanding of how energy production and consumption shape global environmental outcomes.
