    en: 'As a subject engaged in preventing environmental pollution and improving environmental quality, the core of environmental science and environmental engineering is the control of environmental pollution. Most environmental pollutants are organic compounds, therefore, organic chemistry is a discipline closely related to environmental science and engineering, playing an important and crucial role in controlling environmental pollution and improving resource utilization feild. Organic chemistry is a subject that studies the composition, structure, properties, synthesis, and related theoretical issues of organic compounds. This course emphasizes both theoretical and practical aspects. This course first systematically introduces the basic theoretical knowledge of organic chemistry, including valence bond theory, electronic effects, and organic chemistry acid-base theory. Based on this basic theory, the different structures of organic compounds and the physical and chemical properties of organic compounds caused by these structures are analyzed. It further introduces the impact of different organic compounds on the environment and how to use organic chemical reactions to treat environmental pollution and improve resource utilization.Based on the characteristics of the environmental engineering major, closely linking with practical problems in the field of environmental engineering, while emphasizing the role of environmental science and engineering case teaching in theoretical learning, the following goals are achieved through classroom teaching and experimental teaching: (1) understanding the basic theoretical knowledge of organic chemistry such as valence bond theory, electron effect theory, and acid-base theory, mastering the relationship between the structure and physicochemical properties of organic compounds, analyzing the key influencing factors of environmental pollutant control process, and researching complex environmental engineering problems; (2) By applying the basic principles of organic chemistry and utilizing the characteristic reactions of various organic compounds, feasible experimental plans for controlling environmental pollutants can be designed or constructed, and scientific experimental methods can be used to conduct experiments safely.'
