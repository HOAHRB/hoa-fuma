    en: 'This lecture course aims to give a discursive narrative of a branch in the theoretical science. It embarks from the set of experimental laws, who, in first instance, give rise to a set of the principal notions of thermodynamic parameters and constraints linking them (equations of state). Consecutively, formalization in a part of these laws (0th – 3rd laws) validates analytical corollaries about existence of a set of formal objects termed state functions (temperature, internal energy, entropy, thermodynamic potentials). Certain criteria will be formulated in terms of the said quantities about the behavior of the macroscopic system in the proximity of equilibrium, these criteria having the principal importance in science, nature and information fluxes. The latter assignment will be exemplified by an issue of heterogeneous equilibrium in a system with no chemical transformations, conditions of diffusion fluxes and a homogeneous system with a chemical reaction. There will come about concise algorithm of finding numerical inter-relation of various thermodynamic properties with the standard parameters, viz.: heat capacities, isothermal compressibility, thermal expansion coefficients, etc., basing on a clear-cut Jabobi determinants algorithm. This lecture course will also give a view-point on the stability of thermodynamic equilibrium regarding various fluctuation processes occurring in the system. The students will handle the concepts of equilibrium and ways to macroscopically describe it in terms of thermodynamic parameters for a number of viable cases in the thermo-physics and thermo-chemistry.'
