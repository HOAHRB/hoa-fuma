    en: 'Chemistry (primarily, chemistry of transformations of organic compounds) is the foundation of healthy functioning of living systems. At the same time, various diseases – from common cold to such debilitating ailments as cancer and terminal viral infections – have underlying mechanisms whose knowledge can help design drugs. The course will provide a general overview of chemical aspects of live cell functioning, from translation of genetic information into biomolecules all the way through these biomolecules functioning as biocatalysts which themselves are necessary for producing essential molecules in the human body. The other part of the course will focus on discussing examples of these well-tuned systems beginning to malfunction thus triggering the disease state. Finally, understanding chemistry behind these diseases can aid in designing novel treatments. At the end of this course, students are expected to: 1)understand chemical foundations of the functioning of the eukaryotic cell; 2)understand the relationship between functioning of various biomolecules and the role of small molecule cell components; 3) understand modern approaches to finding treatment to debilitating diseases and foundations of medicinal chemistry and drug discovery; 4)Learn and memorize the chemical structures of essential small-molecule constituents of the cell and common bioactive chemical compounds.'
