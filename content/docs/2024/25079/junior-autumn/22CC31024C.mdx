In this course students will study the typology of texts (description, narrative, reasoning and proof), types of plans (thesis, quotation, question, nominative), read and analyze special texts from scientific publications. The study of grammatical constructions of general scientific style, vocabulary, terminology is assumed. Written speech is practiced by writing own texts of various genres (theses, abstracts, reviews, essays, etc.). Examples of the design of a scientific paper will be given (structure, parts, list of references, sources, citations, etc.), the principles of preparing and presenting presentations will be considered. For work will be actively used translation, a variety of independent assignments, work in the classroom under the guidance of the teacher. Training is planned to be organized jointly with specialist teachers. As a final task it is proposed to prepare a presentation on a topic of interest to the student, but within the framework of the specialty in Russian.
