    en: 'The course is aimed at making students acquainted with the basic principles of chemical sensors: a fast developing branch in the applied science based on (mostly) clear fundamental regularities. Students will see how fundamental laws which “belong” to different sciences: mathematics, physics and chemistry interplay when it comes to practically relevant devices. The course objectives are to give 1.a systematic description of various sorts of chemical sensors ( with a special attention to ionophore-based sensors), and a brief characterization of the mechanism of the sensor response; 2.an overview of the advantages and disadvantages of particular kinds of chemical sensors and biosensors in particular applications; 3.an overview of the modern trends in chemical sensors; 4.an understanding of multisensor systems for gas and liquid analysis; 5.an understanding of multivariate data processing techniques; 6.an understanding of the applicability domain of multisensor systems.'
