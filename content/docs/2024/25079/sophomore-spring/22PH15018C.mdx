    en: This course is a basic experimental course aimed at Sino-Russian jointly cultivating students in various related majors. The course teaches the basic knowledge and methods of physics experiments, cultivates basic ability of scientific experiments, and stimulates spirit of exploration and innovation. The course selects 18 classical physics experiments and characteristic independent inquiry physics experiments covering many fields such as force, heat, sound, light, electricity, magnetism and modern physics. Through the hierarchical experimental teaching arrangement and experimental process, the students can deepen their understanding of the basic knowledge of physics theory and experiment, cultivate their scientific practice ability and innovation awareness, expand their scientific thinking, and improve their comprehensive scientific literacy. The course is designed for 24 credit hours. One 3-hour lecture period about the introduction of the course is to help students to understand the error estimation and data analysis theory at the beginning. The other 21-hour lab periods are for students to do experiment. Each student must select and complete 7 experimental projects and submit a complete experimental report. There is no final exam for the course, and the total score is accumulated by the scores of preview, experiment operation and experiment report. Students who have completed less experiments should retake the course. The experiments are arranged in an open style and students can independently choose the experiment projects to meet their individual needs.
