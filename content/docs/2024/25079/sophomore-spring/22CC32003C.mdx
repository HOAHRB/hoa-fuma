The aim of this course is to expand the student's vocabulary up to 2000 words, to familiarize him/her with additional structures and grammatical forms (types of verbs, complex sentences, participles), word formation, peculiarities of construction of texts of different styles, general scientific vocabulary. Specially prepared academic and authentic texts, textbooks on grammar and vocabulary, exercise books, as well as video and audio materials serve as materials for classes. Special attention is paid to interactive forms of work and testing of knowledge. Collective forms of work are predominantly used. Familiarization with Russian culture, art and literature is based on available material on the Internet. Students prepare their own messages, reports and presentations on topics of interest to them.
