    en: Course Objectives 1.to know the Russian Language System for successful oral and written communication out of language environment 2.to acquire conceptions of general academic style referring to specific area At the end of this course, students should ？in reading – understand social, cultural and economical texts as well as special texts of general topics ？in writing – create texts in everyday, academic and official styles enough to meet communicative needs ？in listening – understand in general texts created in popular, everyday and academic style ？in talking – support dialogues on general social and science themes, make monologue on different topics. This course assumes familiarization with the basic models of Russian grammar, knowledge of the basics of Russian pronunciation, and mastery of a vocabulary of 1500 words. In oral speech, students learn to understand and compose simple dialogues (4-5 lines) on everyday topics (family, friends, free time, hobbies, sports, travel), build small monologues of 5-7 sentences. In writing, students learn to write greeting cards, small notes and text messages. Short narrative stories (200-300 words) are offered for reading. Videos, audio recordings, film clips, and programs introduce students to the culture of the country. As students study the material, they write quizzes and take a final test at the end of the course.
