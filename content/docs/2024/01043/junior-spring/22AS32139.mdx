Their autonomous execution of tasks requires solving problems related to perception, localization, planning, decision-making, and control. This course will systematically explain the core technologies of intelligent autonomous mobile robot systems and, combined with real-world cases and application scenarios, help students gain an in-depth understanding of the principles and application methods of related technologies. The course content mainly includes: an overview of intelligent autonomous mobile robots, environmental perception and localization, autonomous navigation, autonomous decision-making and motion planning, motion control, and cutting-edge technologies such as embodied intelligence. This course is primarily designed for undergraduate students majoring in automation, intelligent equipment, and detection, guidance, and control technology who are interested in fields such as intelligent autonomous mobile robots and artificial intelligence. The course aims to equip students with core AI technologies in the field of intelligent autonomous mobile robots. By integrating cutting-edge technologies such as deep learning, large language models, and embodied intelligence, it seeks to enhance their theoretical knowledge and practical abilities in perception, localization, navigation, control, and embodied intelligence. Students will learn and master fundamental concepts, relevant theoretical methods, and the latest research findings, enabling them to design and develop intelligent robotic systems capable of autonomous learning and decision-making. The course emphasizes the integration of theoretical knowledge with practical applications, focusing on foundational theories while also highlighting frontier and exploratory aspects.'
