This course mainly tells the basic concepts, properties and principles of atomic nuclear physics, including the composition and properties of atomic nucleus, the basic laws of radioactive decay, the basic principles of α decay, β decay and γ decay, nuclear reactions and neutron physics. In addition, students will also understand the basic principles of modern nuclear energy utilization, such as fission and fusion. The study of this course will lay a good theoretical foundation for subsequent courses, such as nuclear reactor physical analysis, radiation protection, radiation detection, etc.
