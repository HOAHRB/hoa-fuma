It combines theory with practice to teach key systems, equipment, and phenomena in the key research fields of nuclear engineering and nuclear technology. The course is based on the SIREP 1300MW four loop nuclear power plant principal machine and ALICE real-time simulation software platform for teaching. This simulation platform is developed according to the teaching requirements of the French INSTN Institute of Engineers and is a major course in the practical teaching platform for energy and power engineers in higher engineering colleges in France. The course teaching content consists of four modules, including thermal engineering module, reactor physics module, nuclear safety analysis module, and operating condition module. The thermal module includes four parts: regulator and safety valve group, steam generator, chemical volume and control system, and waste heat removal system; The reactor physics module covers important reactor physics phenomena, including xenon toxicity, moderator temperature effect, Doppler effect, negative temperature, boron concentration, and burn-up; The nuclear safety analysis module simulates two accidents, including a small break accident in the primary circuit and a heat transfer tube damage accident in the steam generator; The operating mode module is 2 out of 1, and one is selected from the plant power system and cold start to full power for practice. Through the study of this course, students will firmly grasp the main system and equipment knowledge, process flow of pressurized water reactor nuclear power plants, accurately understand the control relationships of various subsystems and variables under different operating conditions, and master professional knowledge and practical skills such as nuclear power plant experimental procedures. At the same time, it will cultivate students'' abilities in engineering practice, scientific innovation, and collaborative work.'
