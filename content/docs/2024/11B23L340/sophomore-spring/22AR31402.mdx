With plants as the main line, it systematically introduces the basic theories of botany and plant landscape design methods. For students majoring in landscape architecture, the goal of the course is to cultivate their ability to identify common landscape plants, understand the relationship between plant morphological structure and function, develop their ability to analyze and research the rational selection of plant materials in specific project practice, and use the knowledge they have learned to analyze and solve problems related to botany. For students of other majors, this course can be used as a general course to guide students to understand the colorful plant world, understand the methods of plant landscape design, and cultivate their right values of loving nature, establishing environmental protection awareness and sustainable development of nature. The course content is presented in the form of videos, manuscripts, animations and pictures. Students are required to carefully watch and read the course materials, complete unit tests and assignments on time, actively participate in discussions, share learning experiences and exchange learning experiences.
