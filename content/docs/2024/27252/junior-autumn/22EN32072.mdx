Combined with industry development trends, it systematically elaborates the intelligent upgrading system of building water supply, drainage, fire protection, hot water and other subsystems. Centering on key technologies such as the Internet of Things, sensor monitoring, automatic control and big data, the course analyzes the selection of intelligent water service equipment, system integration and operational logic. It also integrates cutting-edge concepts including green buildings, sponge cities, water resource recycling and source separation, and incorporates engineering application directions such as BIM collaborative design to connect theoretical knowledge with practical scenarios. Upon completion of this course, students will improve their professional knowledge system, master core technologies of smart water services, and consolidate the professional foundation for engineering design, operation and maintenance management, as well as technological research and development, so as to adapt to the industry development demands in the fields of smart cities and green buildings.
