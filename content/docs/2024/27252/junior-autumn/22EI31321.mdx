    en: 'Geographic Information System (GIS) is an elective course in the field of Remote Sensing Science and Technology, and it is an interdisciplinary subject that intersects information science, spatial science, and management science. It is a computer system that utilizes geographic data, employs geographic modeling and analysis methods, and provides various dynamic spatial information for management and decision-making processes. GIS finds important applications in land resources management, environmental assessment, regional development planning, public facilities management, traffic safety, urban big data, smart cities, and emergency response and disaster reduction. With the integration of the Internet, Global Positioning System (GPS), wireless technology, and web services, GIS has extensive applications in location services, interactive maps, car navigation, and precision agriculture. Through the study of this course, students will acquire a grasp of the basic concepts of GIS, spatial data acquisition, the structure and interrelationships of spatial data, editing and spatial information processing methods, principles and methods of GIS spatial analysis, and technical methods for GIS design. They will also master the application and operation of basic GIS software. This course plays an important role in developing students'' comprehensive analysis and design capabilities in data information processing systems. Through classroom teaching and practical exercises, students will focus on cultivating their abilities in comprehensive analysis and design of information systems, as well as the application of knowledge in information system science, and enhancing their innovative awareness. The teaching objectives of the GIS course are focused on professional skills development and practical operation training. The content emphasizes a balance between concepts and practice, achieving the integration of theory and application to meet the demand for practical application of geographic information technology. Course Objective 1: Understand and master the basic principles and main structure of GIS, possess the knowledge foundation for geographic information data analysis, understand the theoretical foundation, research hotspots, development trends, and cutting-edge applications of GIS. Course Objective 2: Understand and master the basic characteristics and main analysis methods of spatial data, grasp the concept and design methods of spatial databases, understand the implementation technologies of spatial databases. Gain knowledge of application models of GIS, such as location selection and development prediction. Acquire the design methods of GIS and possess the ability to analyze and solve spatial data processing issues in engineering practices. Course Objective 3: Familiarize and master the general software of GIS and basic experimental skills for spatial data analysis, receive basic training in experimental plan design and skill acquisition, and gradually develop the ability to apply advanced tools to solve practical engineering problems.'
