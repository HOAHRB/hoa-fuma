This course mainly introduces the related concepts and key technologies of Intelligent Water Conservancy and intelligent water affair in water system information technology, as well as the basic structure and application scenario of urban water system monitoring and early warning model. The aim is to develop students' ability to solve practical systems engineering problems through a comprehensive application of various information-based means, and to further develop their innovative thinking and ability to keep abreast of the times and develop new methods and technologies.
