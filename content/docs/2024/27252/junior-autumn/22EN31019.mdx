    en: Urban Water Environment Planning is one of the main major courses for urban water system engineering undergraduates. This course takes the development of the urban water environment as the mainline. It focuses on analyzing material circulation, energy flow, and information transmission in the water environment, aiming at the urban development with harmony between human and water environment. Importantly, the relationship between the city and the water environment, including urban water security, water resources, water ecology, water landscape, water culture, water management, and water economic system construction, would be expounded and explored in this course. Through classroom teaching, students need to learn about healthy cycling of water, urban water environment planning principles and methods, urban water environment capacity and its restoration, urban water environment cycle strategy, urban water environment ecological regulation, construction and restoration of healthy urban water environment, ecological water economy, and inheritance of water culture. In addition to mastering the basic concepts and planning ideas of urban water environment, students must also further understand a healthy urban water environment by learning the actual planning case and planning the urban water environment system. Finally, students are hoped to analyze the evolution of urban water issues by themselves with a macro, advanced and basic perspective, and independently design the urban water environment based on the latest theories, ideas, methods and technologies.
