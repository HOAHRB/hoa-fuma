    en: Heat engine is a machine that can convert part of the heat provided by a heat source into mechanical energy for external output. Since the heat source that supplies thermal energy to the engine can thus be powered by virtually any kind of energy, the heat engine covers a wide range of applications and plays an important role in human life. From internal combustion engines used in modern vehicles to steam engines in thermal power plants, the application and development of heat engines have promoted the rapid development of society. Starting from the basic theory of thermodynamics, this course introduces in detail the theories and applications of two types of heat engines, gas cycle and steam cycle, and focuses on the development and application value of a special type of Rankine cycle—Organic Rankine Cycle. Finally, the application of heat engine in nuclear energy is introduced. Through the study of this course, students will be able to understand the development process, working principle, application background and research frontiers of various heat engines, and lay a solid foundation for their future scientific research in related fields.
