    en: The course covers the fundamental processing principles and techniques involved in additive manufacturing, including how to correctly select manufacturing processes and materials, as well as the rational analysis of surface quality, dimensional accuracy, and post-processing techniques in additive manufacturing, along with proposed control methods. It also provides a foundational understanding of the basic knowledge required for designing metal and non-metal additive manufacturing. Additionally, it delves into the key factors influencing processing dimensions and accuracy in techniques such as SLM, DED, SLA, and FDM. The lecture further explains the selection principles for CAD-based 3D structural design and slicing software trajectory planning, along with the fundamental design calculation process. It analyzes the performance optimization of materials like superalloys (e.g., Inconel 718), titanium alloys (Ti6Al4V), and ceramic matrix composites in aerospace environments. Through ANSYS and COMSOL simulations, it models molten pool dynamics and residual stress distribution to predict printing defects.
