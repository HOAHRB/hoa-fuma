    en: Bionic Science lies at the intersection of mechanics, materials science, robotics, artificial intelligence, life sciences, and related fields. Fundamentals of Bionics focuses on drawing inspiration from the structures, functions, and evolutionary mechanisms of nature, and transforming them into engineering technologies and innovative designs. The course covers the basic concepts of bionics, bionic design and applications, interdisciplinary extensions, and emerging technologies. It emphasizes typical bionic phenomena found in nature, such as bio-inspired motion, materials, perception, and information processing, and analyzes frontier application cases in architecture, medicine, robotics, and aerospace. This course aims to cultivate students’ ability to observe nature, extract functional principles, and translate them into engineering problems. It also encourages students to apply interdisciplinary knowledge comprehensively, develop preliminary bionic design ideas, spark interest in integrating nature with engineering, and foster an awareness of interdisciplinary innovation.
