Firstly, the basic theories of electromagnetic fields and electromagnetic waves are reviewed, and the Huygens-Fresnel principle, Fresnel zone and the diffraction theory of electromagnetic waves are explained; secondly, the relevant theories and concepts of wave propagation are emphasized when electromagnetic waves are affected by the earth and the atmosphere.
