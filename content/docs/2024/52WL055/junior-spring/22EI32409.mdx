    en: With the development of information technology, the design of microwave circuit becomes more and more complex, which makes it hard to be realized by traditional methods. Using microwave EDA (Electronic Design Automation) softwares to design microwave components and microwave systems has become an inevitable trend of microwave circuit design. At present, various commercial microwave EDA softwares are emerging in foreign countries, which play different roles in microwave engineering according to their respective characteristics. On the basis of the course "Electromagnetic Field Theory" and "Microwave Technology", this course explains the characteristics and design methods of commonly used microwave circuits and devices. It realizes the design and simulation of microwave components and microwave systems by using three-dimensional electromagnetic field simulation software, which is CST Microwave Studio.
