    en: Contemporary hydrology and hydrogeology are widely used in water supply and drainage engineering. "Hydrology and Hydrogeology" is a compulsory basic professional course for majors related to water supply and drainage science and engineering, consisting of two parts, "Hydrology" and "Water Supply Hydrogeology". "Hydrology" focuses on surface water, while "Water Supply Hydrogeology" focuses on groundwater. According to the professional training objectives and programs, "Hydrology" systematically introduces rivers and their runoff, basic theories of hydrological statistics, annual runoff, high and low runoff, collection and sorting of precipitation data, calculation of rainfall intensity formula, calculation of storm peak flow in small watersheds, urban rainfall runoff, etc. "Water Supply Hydrogeology" introduces basic geological knowledge, groundwater storage and circulation, physical and chemical properties of groundwater, basic theories of groundwater movement characteristics, distribution characteristics of groundwater in different landform areas, hydrogeological survey of water supply, etc. The main feature of this course is its strong theoretical and logical nature. The content of hydrological analysis and groundwater movement calculation is complex and extensive, and is closely related to water supply and drainage engineering. It is one of the important basic courses for majors related to water supply and drainage science and engineering.
