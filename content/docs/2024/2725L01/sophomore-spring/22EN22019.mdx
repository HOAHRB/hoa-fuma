    en: The course of " Biology of Water Treatment " is one of the 10 main courses in the new curriculum system proposed by the Steering Committee of Water Supply and Drainage Science and Engineering. It is a compulsory course for drainage science and engineering. In recent years, with the continuous development of water treatment and environmental water quality purification technology, the application of aquatic organisms and aquatic / wet organisms in water purification and pollution control and aquatic ecological restoration projects has been paid more and more attention. In order to adapt to the new development of water purification technology, in the original "water treatment microbiology" courses based on the development of water treatment biology. Biology of Water Treatment mainly studies the biological problems involved in the process of water treatment and environmental water purification (ie, the migration, decomposition and transformation of pollutants in water), especially the microbial problem, which is a common biology, Microbiology, environmental microbiology and water quality engineering, in order to meet the water treatment and environmental water quality purification project needs to develop a marginal discipline. Water treatment biology in the discipline system belongs to the application (micro) biology category, in the research object and the content and environmental microbiology have a certain cross. This course aims to help students master the shape, structure and function of microbes, nutrition, respiration, material metabolism, growth and reproduction, genetic and variation of microbes and the role of microorganisms in the purification of environmental engineering such as water treatment and remediation of contaminated soil. On the basis of the basic knowledge of organic chemistry and biochemistry theory, we can use the knowledge to analyze the quality of sewage, and put forward the rough processing opinions with microbial theory. Through the study of the basic theory of this course, basic knowledge and have an independent design, the implementation of the basic ability of the relevant experiments, lay the foundation for the follow-up study. At the same time to understand the latest developments in the field, enhance the ability to adapt, and consciously put the curriculum development and related professional development linked.
