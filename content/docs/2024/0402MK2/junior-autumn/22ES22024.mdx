    en: Under the background of carbon neutrality, energy storage technology is one of the key technologies to improve the flexibility and safety of the power grid, and to support the development of renewable energy. Among various energy storage technologies, electrochemical energy storage is the most widely used and promising energy storage technology. This course is aimed at the field of energy storage, guiding students to broaden their horizons in the research frontier of electrochemical energy storage technology through the integration and sorting of knowledge and progress in multiple disciplines, and forming a coherent thinking from basic knowledge to the forefront of technology. In terms of basic knowledge, the course integrates and crosses related disciplines such as mathematics, physics, chemistry, materials, energy, etc. from the aspects of electrochemical thermodynamics, kinetics, and typical principles and technologies of electrochemical energy storage, leading students to understand the scientific principles and development trends of electrochemical energy storage, and forming a multidisciplinary overall knowledge system. The course cultivates students' ability to deepen their understanding from basic knowledge to the forefront of technology. In terms of problem orientation, guide students to analyze and summarize the current development status and key bottlenecks of electrochemical energy storage technology. Based on this, students can propose new ideas for typical technologies using basic principles, literature research, and other methods. Through mathematical, computational simulation, experimental testing and other means of verification, this course aims to cultivate students' ability to use relevant tools for problem analysis, scheme design, and research. In terms of discussion and exchange, based on the basic principles of typical electrochemical energy storage technologies, emphasis is placed on group discussion teaching to guide students to collect information, broaden their horizons, and propose ideas around topics of interest. This course aims to stimulate students' interest in learning, cultivate their ability for self-directed and lifelong learning.
