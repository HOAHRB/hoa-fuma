On the other hand, the mathematical modeling of probabilistic phenomena and the rigorous proofs of mathematical results are often complex and abstract. This course will guide students in understanding how to characterize and study "random phenomena," establish probabilistic thinking, and master key mathematical results through classroom instruction and discussions.
