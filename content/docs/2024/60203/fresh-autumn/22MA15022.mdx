    en: 'This course is intended to introduce fundamental methods and concepts of linear algebra and analytic geometry for economic management students. It is an organic combination of linear algebra and analytic geometry. Topics are usually selected from: the definition of a determinant, computations and properties of determinants; operations of matrices, partitioned matrices and the rank of a matrix; vector algebra, vector coordinates, the equations of planes and lines, and the relations between plans and lines; n-dimensional vectors, linear independence among vectors; vector spaces, the bases, dimensions of vector spaces and changing of basis; the structure of the solution set of a linear system; eigenvalues and eigenvectors, similar transformations, the conditions that determine if a matrix is diagonalizable, and the method of diagonalizing a matrix using eigenvalues and eigenvectors; applications of linear algebra in economic management. The grade policy is as following: homework and performance in class 20%, quizzes in class 10%, and final exam 70%.
