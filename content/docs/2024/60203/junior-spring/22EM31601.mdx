    en: 'Cost and Management Accounting can be divided into two parts in teaching: cost accounting and management accounting. Cost Accounting is a very important core course for accounting students, following basic accounting and financial accounting. Together with intermediate and advanced financial accounting, it forms the core course of the financial accounting system. It plays an important role in cultivating students'' practical skills and problem-solving abilities. This course aims to familiarize students with the classification of expenses, understand the general procedures of cost accounting, master the accounting of factor costs, auxiliary production costs, manufacturing costs, waste and downtime losses, methods for allocating production costs between finished and in progress products, preparation of cost reports and cost analysis, proficiently master the basic and auxiliary methods of product cost calculation, be able to solve practical problems in cost accounting, and cultivate students'' ability to solve accounting related problems. Management Accounting is an important branch of modern enterprise accounting, which combines accounting and management, utilizes the achievements of modern management science, and achieves comprehensive management of enterprises through decision-making, budgeting, control, assessment, and evaluation of business activities, serving to strengthen enterprise management and improve economic efficiency. This course mainly aims to help students understand the meaning of management accounting information, master various methods and techniques for information processing such as management accounting decision-making, budgeting, control, assessment, and evaluation, and understand the impact of management accounting information on management. Knowledge objective: Understand the content and development of cost and cost accounting, master the collection and allocation of various expense elements, auxiliary production costs, and manufacturing costs, and master the handling of waste losses. Master the distribution methods of finished products and work in progress products. Master the basic methods of cost calculation and understand auxiliary methods. Be able to understand the basic concepts and principles of management accounting, and possess relevant computational abilities in management accounting. Course objective: To be able to apply concepts, principles, and methods to solve practical problems. Be able to master the use of quantitative management accounting tools to analyze and solve management problems. Quality objective: To possess the professional ethics of cost management accounting, and to have the awareness and concept of adhering to legality and compliance. Have the awareness and concept of lifelong learning of cost accounting and management accounting knowledge.'
