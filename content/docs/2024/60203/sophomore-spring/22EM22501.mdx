Market survey and analysis are important for modern enterprises to understand the market and customer needs and are the basis for enterprises to make various decisions. The course "Market Survey and Analysis" is a subject with strong practicality. It mainly introduces the basic theoretical knowledge and specific methods of market research, including the basic concepts of market survey, market survey design, qualitative survey methods, questionnaire surveys, and experiments methods, secondary data and online databases, standardized information sources, data platforms, descriptive analysis, differential analysis and predictive analysis, and writing of market research reports, etc. This course integrates the knowledge of cutting-edge information science into market research, expands the scope of research data, enriches the methods of research design, excavates the influencing mechanism behind the research, effectively analyzes the results of the research, and clearly expounds the findings of the research. At the same time, these theories, knowledge and methods are closely combined with the market research practice of the enterprise, which is very practical and operable. By familiarizing with and mastering the basic methods of modern market research and analysis, students can learn to use this basic tool effectively and apply it in market research to solve practical marketing problems.
