    en: This course is designed for undergraduate students who study advanced ceramics in the major of materials science and engineering. It is based on the prerequisite courses including Thermodynamics of Materials, Fundamentals of Materials Science and Engineering, and focus mainly on the basic concepts, principles and methods of ceramic processing and sintering. The topics covered in this course include mainly fabrication and characterization of ceramic powders, fundamentals of colloid science, shaping and forming, sintering and densification theory, microstructural formation and evolution, sintering and processing methods etc. It provides students a broad-based understanding on the relationship among chemical composition, processing and sintering, microstructure and properties of advanced ceramics and components. This contributes primarily to develop the ability of the students to select proper ceramic materials and to design the forming and sintering processes that yield desired microstructures and controlled properties for high-performance ceramic components of industrial applications.
