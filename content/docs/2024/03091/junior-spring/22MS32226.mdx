By atomization process, the obtained powder materials have unique compositions, phases and microstructures, which cannot be achieved by conventional solidification method and will improve the properties of the materials. Therefore, the materials fabricated by atomization route exhibit widespread applications. In this course, the basically theoretical principle of atomized solidification for the powder materials, such as heat transferring, thermodynamics, dynamics and microstructural evolution, the techniques of the fabrication and densification for the atomized powders, and the typical properties and applications of materials prepared by atomization method will be introduced.
