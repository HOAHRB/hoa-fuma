    en: 'In recent years, as emerging disciplines, big data and artificial intelligence have achieved rapid development and promotion. In the field of materials science, they are manifested as the "Materials Genome," becoming a future trend in the research paradigm of materials science and engineering. This trend is conducive to accelerating material R&D and reducing costs. To adapt to this paradigm shift, align with the national strategy for scientific and technological innovation, and follow the developmental trend of materials science, this course is specially designed for undergraduate students. The main teaching content covers an overview of data-driven materials science, databases and data standards, high-throughput computing, experimental methods and case studies, as well as a systematic introduction to machine learning algorithms and their applications. The course aims to enable students to: 1) understand the connotations and trends of data-driven materials science; 2) comprehend and master the basic concepts of artificial intelligence technologies in material processing; and 3) acquire the ability to solve material design problems using artificial intelligence techniques in material processing. It serves as an effective pathway for undergraduate students in materials science to obtain fundamental skills in material processing AI technologies and connect with international research frontiers.'
