Modern photochemistry and the theory of the interaction between light and matter are achievements of the development of modern physics. The new concepts, theories, and methods established in the study of electronic excited states have greatly improved understanding of matter. Achievements in the fields of photochemistry and photophysics are playing an increasingly important role in high-tech fields.This course is an interdisciplinary course for undergraduates majoring in Photoelectric Information Engineering and technology in the Department of Physics. It aims at introducing the optical phenomena and chemical reactions caused by the interaction between light and matter from the point of view of physics, especially optics, and expounding the important optical phenomena and the mechanism of photochemical reactions. The relationship between material structure and optical properties of materials is established. According to the characteristics of physics, this course will selectively introduce the basic knowledge of structural chemistry and chemical reaction, and appropriately introduce the preparation methods of materials chemistry which are interesting in the field of physics. The aim is to cultivate students'interest in photochemistry and improve their skills in solving chemical problems.
