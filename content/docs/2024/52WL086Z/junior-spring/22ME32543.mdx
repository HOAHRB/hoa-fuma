    en: From the viewpoint of system, this course emphasizes the four design principles including integrity, dynamics, modeling and optimization of the mechanical and hydraulic system design, and takes system integration and optimization as objectives. With the aid of the model based design method, and the support of muti-discipline software tool chains, this course introduces the dynamic characteristics of typical components analysis and optimization design method of system integration of the mechanical and hydraulic system. In this course, the basic concept, research contents, study methods and tools are introduced for design and dynamic analysis of mechanical electrical and hydraulic systems. Then, the fundamental theoretical basics of mechanical electrical and hydraulic systems are briefly described. We take some typical elements of mechanical electrical and hydraulic systems, such as servo motors, self-feedback hydraulic components (relief valves and constant pressure variable displacement pumps ) , force-feedback two stage servo valves, hydraulic actuators and controllers etc. as examples, and demonstrated how to build mathematical models, and to simulate and analyze its dynamic performance. Finally, we use an engineering project to demonstrate the integrated design method of mechanical electrical and hydraulic systems.
