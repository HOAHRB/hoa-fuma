    en: Housekeeping system is one of the most important subsystems among the six major satellite systems. Housekeeping system is both the data center and control center of the satellite. This system takes the integrated electronic system hardware as the carrier, and is connected with all components of the satellite through different data interfaces. Each component sends the generated engineering parameters to the housekeeping system according to the agreed protocol. The satellite system judges the correctness and effectiveness of each engineering parameter and makes corresponding disposal. Then the satellite system parses and arranges the engineering parameters according to the protocol, and sends the rearranged engineering parameters to the corresponding component or ground system according to the requirements of satellite management and control. The hardware and software logic involved in all aspects of the above management and control process is the core of this course. It involves on-board embedded systems, measurement and control systems, computer networks, software engineering and other disciplines. It is a comprehensive course. The quality of housekeeping system design directly determines the degree of control of satellites by ground control personnel, as well as the ease of use and ease of use of satellites. Through the study of this course, we can not only master the design method of the housekeeping system, but also have a deep understanding of the overall design of the satellite. ' Design of Housekeeping System of Microsatellite ' is an important professional elective course for aircraft design and engineering. It is a course closely related to engineering practice. It plays an important role in cultivating students ' creative thinking, comprehensive design ability and practical ability.
