This course begins by reviewing the basic concepts of artificial intelligence, aircraft, and aircraft control, and it covers commonly used criteria and methods in the design of aircraft control systems. Subsequently, the course teaches the foundational knowledge of artificial intelligence required for the study, including neural networks, fuzzy systems, reinforcement learning, and more. Following that, the course integrates examples such as the design of missile autopilots, rocket attitude control systems, and re-entry aircraft attitude control systems. It covers intelligent control methods such as BP neural network control, RBF neural network control, fuzzy control, reinforcement learning control, adaptive dynamic programming, and others. Through the study of this course, students will be able to comprehensively and systematically grasp the methods of designing aircraft control systems and the foundational theoretical knowledge of artificial intelligence. By combining engineering examples, they will gain an in-depth understanding of the application of intelligent control and be equipped with a solid foundation for future work in the design of aircraft intelligent control systems. Additionally, the course enables students to explore advanced artificial intelligence methods, broaden their perspectives, and foster innovative thinking.'
