Through the classroom teaching, subsequent experimental operations, and computer simulation of this course, the focus is on cultivating students' comprehensive application ability of missile flight mechanics, automatic control principles, and related basic knowledge of autonomous and integrated navigation of aircraft. The comprehensive analysis and design ability of quadcopter unmanned aerial vehicle flight control system, as well as the ability to develop and apply hardware systems, will also be emphasized. This course can not only cultivate students' ability to design software for scientific research problems, but also train their ability to develop hardware, laying a solid foundation for graduation design and future scientific research work. The course lecture focuses on introducing the current development status, overall situation, and design methods of subsystems such as integrated electronics, power systems, structure and vibration reduction, navigation and control systems, etc. of quadcopter unmanned aerial vehicles. In the experimental phase, students will be divided into several small groups to independently undertake the design and development of a certain subsystem. Finally, the prototypes of each subsystem will be integrated and tested to form a quadcopter drone designed, processed, developed, debugged, and tested by the students themselves. This course is open to students majoring in aerospace, mechanics, control, communication, electromechanical, microelectronics, and many other aerospace related majors.
