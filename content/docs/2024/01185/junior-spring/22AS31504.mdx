    en: Flying into space has been a human dream for thousands of years, and the successful launch of the first artificial Earth satellite has turned this dream into a reality. Over the past half century, space technology has achieved leapfrog development, and spacecraft have also reached a considerable level. Human spacecraft can fly farther and be built more complex to complete more tasks. Orbital dynamics of spacecraft is the science that studies how spacecraft move under the influence of gravity and other external forces, and is the foundation of aerospace engineering. The technical issues of orbit design, orbit determination, orbit control, orbit interception, rendezvous and docking, formation flight, and return orbit in aerospace engineering all require the support of orbital dynamics theory. This course mainly studies the basic concepts, principles, and applications of spacecraft orbital dynamics. Its main task is to cultivate students to establish the basic concepts of spacecraft dynamics, understand the relationship between the motion and force of spacecraft, and master the basic analysis methods of spacecraft orbital dynamics problems; Master the basic theory of spacecraft orbital dynamics and the basic skills to solve general spacecraft orbital dynamics application problems; Understand the latest developments in the theory, methods, and applications of spacecraft orbital dynamics; Master the ability to use relevant references and computer application software for dynamic problem research and analysis.
