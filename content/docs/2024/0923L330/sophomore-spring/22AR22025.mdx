    en: Fluid Mechanics B is a primary supporting technology course of civil engineering and theoretical and applied mechanics. This course aims at raising students' specialized theoretical level and providing necessary basic knowledge for their professional learning. After this course, students can master the specific application and manifestation of the basic classical mechanics theory, based on Newton's three laws, in fluid mechanics. This course will calculate students’ ability of not only combining theory with practice when dealing with specific engineering application related to fluid such as wind engineering and seepage but also solving practical problems by experiment. Focusing on the three basic equations of fluid mechanics system, this course systematically introduces the basic theory and application of hydrostatics, hydrokinematics and fluid dynamics, flow resistance and head loss, pressurized pipe flow, open-channel flow, dimensional analysis and similarity principle. Students can choose two from four experimental teaching classes. The course adopts the mixed teaching method of classroom teaching, self-study and experiment teaching. The assessment results of the course are cumulative. The assessment links include classroom tests, homework, experiments and final exams. The total score is measured in 100 points.
