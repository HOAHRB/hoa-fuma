The mission of this course is to teach the basics of mechanics analysis of different structures in civil engineering for those who major this profession. The contents of this course include plane geometry configuration analysis, calculation methods of internal forces and deformation of statically determinate structure, calculation methods of internal forces and deformation of statically indeterminate structure. Through the study of this course, the students should master the configuration law, mechanical properties and reasonable forms of structures, as well as the analysis principles and calculation methods of stiffness, strength and stability of structures. After studying this course, the students can have the basic technical skills in analysis, design and construction of building and geotechnical structures by using the fundamental knowledge of structural mechanics, to lay the foundation for follow-up courses.
