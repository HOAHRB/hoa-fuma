    en: Multi-omics for disease (disease omics) is an interdisciplinary field that comprehensively utilizes high-throughput biological technologies such as genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, and metabolomics. Its aim is to comprehensively understand and reveal molecular-level information related to diseases. Through comprehensive analysis of molecules such as genes, proteins, and metabolites, disease omics seeks to elucidate the molecular mechanisms underlying the occurrence, development, and treatment of diseases. Precision medicine is a novel approach to disease prevention and treatment that takes into account individual differences in genetics, environment, and lifestyle. Essentially, it is "personalized medicine," with the goal of predicting, diagnosing, treating, and preventing diseases more accurately and effectively. Precision medicine often relies on omics technologies, especially genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, and microbiomics. These omics technologies provide a wealth of molecular-level data in medicine, aiding in a more comprehensive understanding of an individual's biological information and facilitating more precise medical and therapeutic approaches. For example, precision medicine utilizes genomic techniques to analyze genetic variations in an individual's genome, identifying potential disease risks and drug response differences to make personalized medical decisions. Transcriptomic data analysis helps understand gene expression patterns associated with diseases, providing a basis for personalized treatments. Metabolomics technology analyzes an individual's metabolites, revealing the activity of metabolic pathways and assisting in customizing nutrition and treatment plans tailored to individual characteristics. This course will provide a detailed explanation of the relationship between disease-related genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, epigenomics, microbiomics, single-cell omics, imaging omics, and precision medicine. It will also include practical examples and discussions on the analysis of omics data and the integration of multi-omics data in the application of precision medicine. Additionally, this course enhances students' understanding of omics and precision medicine through the integration of practical coursework. Through this course, students will gain a profound professional understanding in the field of disease omics and precision medicine, laying a solid foundation for future academic research and medical practice.
