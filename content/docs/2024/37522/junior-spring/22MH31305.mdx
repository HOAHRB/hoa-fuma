Medical immunology is a scientific that study the structure and function of the human immune system, elucidate the rules of immune response and antigen clearance after the immune system recognizes antigens, and explore the pathological processes and disease mechanisms caused by immune dysfunction. Immunology intersects and permeates with multiple disciplines, it has become a cutting-edge discipline in life sciences and one of the supporting disciplines in modern medicine. The course "Medical Immunology" explains the immune response process, immune function regulation, and application of immune diagnostic techniques, enabling students to master the basic theories and knowledge of immunology, cultivate their ability to analyze, synthesize, and independently solve problems, and lay a solid theoretical foundation for students to apply immunological knowledge to diagnose and treat diseases in safe and effective medical imaging diagnosis, intervention therapy, and medical imaging technology in the future.
