    en: This course is an elective offered to students majoring in Biomedical Sciences, aiming to establish a "health-centered" medical philosophy and strengthen a holistic mindset toward disease prevention and health promotion. The course systematically introduces the fundamental theories, core content, and practical methods of preventive medicine, covering topics such as environment and health, nutrition and food hygiene, occupational health, basic epidemiology, health statistics, chronic disease prevention and control, public health policy, and health management. Through this course, students will not only master the identification of population health determinants and intervention strategies but also understand the paradigm shift from individual clinical care to population health. Aligned with the characteristics of biomedical sciences, the course integrates cutting-edge content such as big data-driven disease prediction models, applications of artificial intelligence in public health surveillance, wearable devices and health data collection, and digital health management system design, guiding students to deeply integrate engineering technologies with preventive medicine. Emphasizing the integration of theory and practice, the course employs case studies, project design, and interdisciplinary discussions to cultivate students’ abilities to apply intelligent technologies in addressing public health challenges. It lays a solid medical foundation for their future involvement in smart healthcare system development, health data analysis, and personalized health management platform construction, supporting the strategic transformation from a "disease-centered" to a "health-centered" approach.
