    en: The uncontrolled discharge of industrial wastewater is the main cause of water environment pollution. If industrial wastewater is not properly treated, if will affect the sustainable development of national economy, sustainable conservation and use of natural resources. In order to ensure the people's physical and mental health, sustainable development of society and economy, and the sustainable survival of future generations, the industrial wastewater pollution must be strictly prevented. The course of Water Pollution Control Engineering 2 is mainly directed for industrial wastewater treatment and water reuse after processing, with the contents including industrial wastewater source, characteristics, harm and control ways, physical treatment processes (equalization, centrifugal separation, oil removal and filtration), chemical treatment processes (neutralization, chemical precipitation, oxidation and reduction), physical and chemical treatment processes (coagulation, air flotation, adsorption, ion exchange, membrane separation), and biological treatment processes (biodegradability, aerobic treatment, anaerobic treatment, hybrid treatment) etc. The unit operation and process for wastewater treatment and reuse and application are also separately introduced. Based on the teaching of basic concept, principle, processes and application of industrial wastewater treatment and reuse, the students can finally choose the suitable treatment processes and design the corresponding structures based on the water quality status of industrial wastewater and the recycling targets of the treated water.
