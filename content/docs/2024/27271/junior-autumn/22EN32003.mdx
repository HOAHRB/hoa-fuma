Smart water management is an essential part of smart cities, representing a new model that comprehensively applies modern science and technology, empowered by innovative thinking and technology, to enhance and coordinate urban planning, construction, and management. Through digital means, it involves real-time monitoring of the city's water supply and drainage system operations, promptly identifying and solving problems. This approach aims to improve the city's resilience with smart measures and is a crucial aspect of advancing the construction of urban lifeline safety projects. Introduction to Smart Water Management is an interdisciplinary subject that intersects information technology with environmental science and engineering. It explores how to better utilize modern information technology to address issues such as urban waterlogging prevention, urban water resource protection, and the management of polluted and foul-smelling water bodies. This course is tailored to the current state of information technology development and the characteristics of the environmental science and engineering discipline. It closely relates to the main urban water issues emerging today, emphasizing the role of actual environmental science and engineering case studies in theoretical learning. Through classroom and practical teaching, the course aims to support the training of top innovative talents in interdisciplinary fields within the water industry.
