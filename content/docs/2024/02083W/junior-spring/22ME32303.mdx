    en: 'Reverse engineering is a new technology developed based on CAD/CAM and computer measurement technology. Previously, the engineering drawing and CAD/CAM courses offered by our university''s aircraft manufacturing major mainly focused on teaching basic theoretical knowledge of curve and surface modeling or the use of 3D modeling software such as CATIA, UG, PRO/E, with little or no involvement in product reverse design. In recent years, with the increasing demand for reverse engineering applications in aviation enterprises, they hope that graduates can not only use CAD software for product 3D modeling design, but also understand the basic theories and working methods of reverse engineering, and be proficient in using coordinate measurement equipment and reverse engineering software for product data collection and innovative product design. The teaching of this course is mainly carried out through classroom theoretical teaching, which requires tracking the forefront of subject development and teaching general basic knowledge of reverse engineering. The main content includes: introduction to various measurement equipment, working principles of various reverse engineering measurement technologies, measurement data preprocessing, curve and surface modeling of measurement data, arbitrary topological surface modeling, and introduction to the functions of reverse engineering software applications. Through the study of theoretical courses, students can master basic theories such as main measurement methods, measurement principles, measurement data preprocessing, and curve and surface modeling based on measurement data. In the presence of experimental conditions, experimental teaching should also be carried out, with a focus on cultivating students'' practical application abilities. Students are required to learn how to use advanced coordinate measuring machines/3D laser scanners to collect physical data, and then independently use the mainstream reverse engineering software Imageware to process the measurement data and reconstruct the CAD model of the product, in order to improve their ability to use reverse engineering technology to solve practical problems. It should be said that mastering reverse engineering technology is very helpful for engineering students to move towards work positions.'
