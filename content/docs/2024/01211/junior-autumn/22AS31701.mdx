    en: PRINCIPLES OF LASERS A is a basic course for undergraduates majoring in Electronic Science and technology. In this course, the interaction of light and matter, basic principle of laser, optical resonator and typical applications of laser are mainly taught. The aim for this course is to make students master the basic principles of lasers, learn about the production mechanism of laser, the properties of laser beam and theory of optical resonators. In the chapter of the interaction of light and matter, the concepts and formulas about spontaneous emission, stimulated emission and stimulated absorption are taught. It lays a foundation for the study of the basic principle of laser. In addition, the contents about the broadening of spectral lines also laid the foundation for the quantitative description of the subsequent laser principle. In the chapter of the basic principle of laser, the conditions required to generate the laser, laser structure, formation of laser, and characteristics of laser are mainly taught. In order to quantitatively describe, the rate equation of laser, gain coefficient of medium, threshold conditions of laser oscillation, output energy and power of laser and so on are mainly introduced. In the chapter of optical resonator, stability conditions of coaxial spherical cavity, loss of the resonator, characteristics of Gaussian beam, propagation and transformation of Gaussian beam, unstable cavity are introduced. The characteristics of laser beam and quantitative description of laser beam are mainly taught. It provides theoretical guidance for transmitting and controlling laser beam in laser applications. At the same time, the typical applications of laser are briefly taught.
