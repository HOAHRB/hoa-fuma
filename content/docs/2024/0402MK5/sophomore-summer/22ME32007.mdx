    en: This course provides a systematic introduction to the fundamental concepts, process principles, material systems, and engineering applications of additive manufacturing technology. The course covers the development history and classification of additive manufacturing, with emphasis on typical processes such as Laser Powder Bed Fusion, Electron Beam Melting, Fused Deposition Modeling, and Stereolithography. It also discusses metals, polymers, ceramics, and composite materials used in additive manufacturing, and analyzes the relationships among material selection, process parameters, and part performance. In addition, the course addresses process optimization, common defects, post-processing techniques, and quality control methods. Through case studies in aerospace, biomedical engineering, automotive manufacturing, and industrial production, students will understand the application value of additive manufacturing in advanced manufacturing. Lectures, case analyses, practical tasks, and group presentations will be used to develop students’ ability to analyze and solve engineering problems related to additive manufacturing.
