This helps students better understand the engineering foundations of design and product implementation. This course is the key to strengthening the integration of product design with industrial technology, expanding design thinking derived from engineering technology, enhancing students' comprehensive design quality and ability to solve complex design problems, and integrating art and technology into products. This course is taught in two units. The first unit mainly covers the basics of engineering mechanics, materials and processing technology, electrical circuits, advanced energy, and their applications. The second unit mainly covers mechanical principles, mechanical design fundamentals, process equipment, and their applications. This course unit summarizes various engineering fundamentals related to structural design and production in design implementation. As an interdisciplinary course based on engineering technology for product design, it emphasizes inspiration and representativeness. In addition to building a complete engineering and technical knowledge system related to product design, many excellent cases from teaching and research processes have been added, especially outstanding works in engineering technology application and innovation, to enhance students' interest in learning the basic knowledge of product design engineering.
