    zh: 当今的产品不仅仅满足人们的使用要求，更重要的还要做到好用、易用。而量化用户体验对有效提高产品的使用质量至关重要。本课程详尽介绍了有效且可靠地收集、分析和呈现典型的用户体验度量数据：操作绩效（正确率等）、用户体验问题（频率和严重程度）、自我报告式的满意度及生理/行为数据。同时对“综合性量化度量数据”等问题进行了专门介绍，而且结合案例等形式对当前与用户体验相关的新内容进行了讲解。 通过该课程的学习，一方面，使学生深入理解和掌握用户体验的要素、评估方法、度量方法及统计学分析方法，能够应用以用户为中心的产品设计方法进行设计，使设计不再仅仅依靠想象力，而是通过对多种因素的综合考量，创造一种良好的用户体验，使设计工作更加有效，物有所值；另一方面，使学生能够运用所学知识从定性和定量两个方面对产品进行综合评价，培养学生综合运用用户体验相关知识指导设计，设计出符合用户、市场和环境的产品，进而提升学生自主学习和终身学习的意识，提升学生不断学习和适应发展的能力。
    en: Today，a product not only should meet the users’ demands for utilization, but also make users operating it friendly and easily. Quantizing user experience is very important to advance the quality of produce in use. This course will introduce the methods of collecting and analyzing and presenting the measurement data of typical user experience effectively and reliably in details, which includes operation performance (such as accuracy etc.), user experience problems (such as frequency, order of severity etc.), satisfaction degree from user self-report, and physiology or behavior, and so on. At the same time, this course also introduce some problems specially, such as integrated and quantized measurement data. Combined with case studies, the course will give the latest development related to user experience. This course will give students great insight to understand and master the factors of user experience and the methods for evaluation, measurement and statistical analysis. And it will help students to carry out a design from the view of User-Centered Design, thinking series of factors, creating a good user experience to improve the effect of design except designers’ imagination. On the other hand, the course will help students to give overall evaluation quantitatively and qualitatively using the knowledge learned in the related course, and it will develop students' design capacity to finish the design of a produce under the guidance of user experience knowledge, which meets the requests from users, market and environment. And then, it will improve students autonomous learning ability and consciousness of life-long study, and promote students the ability of continuous learning and adaptive development.
