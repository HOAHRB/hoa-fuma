    en: The course mainly the teaches knowledge about the basic concept、principle and application of human factors engineering. The aim for studying human factors engineering is to improve the work efficiency, to enhance the comfort and safety of equipment, apparatus and working environment. The grasping of the principle and guide line of human-machine engineering is key for designer to design good products to meet the human’s psychological, physiological need and science rule. The course consists of 10 chapters which introduce the basic concept, origin and development of human factors engineering; the body and function measurement, the psychological traits in human factor part; the analysis and design for ordinary operating environment; the classification measurement prevention of human fatigue; the basic concept of safety, the basic principle and method of safety analysis, the safety prevention and safety design for product; the design for typical human-machine apparatus which include display-control design, work post and work place design, the handle design of tool; the total system design and example of human-machine system; the introduction to the advanced research and technology of human-machine engineering. By class teaching, experiment and product-improvement design, we manage the students have a deep understanding for the human-machine engineering course.
