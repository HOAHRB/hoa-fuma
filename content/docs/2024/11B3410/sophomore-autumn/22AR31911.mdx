It mainly focuses on cultivating students computer application ability in product design, improving students' three-dimensional design expression ability in conceiving products, and on this basis, cultivating students self-study ability in three-dimensional CAD application software. This course uses Creo parametric software to comprehensively and systematically introduce its basic knowledge and application. At the same time, combined with a large number of typical actual products, it comprehensively and systematically explains each functional module of Creo, including software installation, setting, sketch design, part design, surface design, modeling surface design (ISDX), free-form surface design, assembly design, engineering drawing design, advanced rendering Modules such as product animation design enable students to deeply understand and master Creo's basic modeling methods and technologies such as assembly, decomposition, animation, rendering and engineering drawing; Master the top and bottom design idea, skeleton model, component disassembly and other contents, and then be able to skillfully use the knowledge to express the conceived product in three dimensions, so as to improve students' comprehensive design ability; Cultivate students to comprehensively use CAID related knowledge to design products that meet the requirements of production and processing, have the awareness of independent learning and lifelong learning, and have the ability to continuously learn and adapt to development.
