At the same time, the course will give an overview of application and development for 3D printing technology in construction, medical, commercial, industrial and other different fields, and guide students to better understand the 3D printing technology development from the market, development history, application and technical challenges aspects. To broaden the understanding of 3D printing key technology in industrial products, some typical components in aerospace, medical equipment are chosen as the examples to explain their manufacturing process and major technical challenges in detail.
