    en: 4D printing technology has aroused widespread attention in the academic and industrial communities since its conceptualization in 2013. It is an additive manufacturing technology for fabricating intelligent components and a revolutionary manufacturing technology based on the high degree of cross-integration of the disciplines including materials, mechanical engineering, dynamics, information, etc. This course clarifies the concept and connotation of 4D printing and introduces the technical feature and limitation factors of this technology. Different “driving”effect of external stimuli on the material are presented as well. The state of the art of intelligent component design, simulation, manufacturing processes & equipment, and functional evaluation of intelligent components in 4D printing are also introduced. The technical characteristics of 4D printing technology for polymer and its composite materials, metal and its composite materials, ceramic and its composite materials and other different materials are described in detail. Finally, this course introduces the application prospects of 4D printing in aerospace, automotive, biomedical, software robots, etc. The main task of this course is to make students have the ability to analyze and solve practical problems in graduation project experiments with appropriate theories, and have good expression ability and scientific research ability to solve problems independently.
