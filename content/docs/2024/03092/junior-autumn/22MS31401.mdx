    en: This course mainly introduce the basic knowledge, concept, features and system about the heating sources in additive manufacturing process including arc heating source, laser heating source and electron-beam heating source. Arc heating source part mainly introduce the generation, heat and force characteristics, metal melting and formation of arc. The concept, features, system and applications of non-consumable arc and consumable arc and submerged arc as well as DC arc, AC arc, pulsed arc and droplet transfer are also introduced. Laser heat source includes laser physical characteristics, laser and material interaction, laser additive manufacturing heat source characteristics, etc. The types and application of different lasers, the energy transfer mechanism between laser and material, the heat source characteristics of laser melting deposition and selective laser melting process are taught. The purpose is to help students mastering beam characteristics, material melting behavior and other related knowledge of laser additive manufacturing. The electron beam heat source part includes the electron optics basis, system and principle of electron beam, the interaction with materials and application in additive manufacturing process. The physical process of electron beam formation, the composition and principle of electron gun, and the additive manufacturing method by electron beam heat source are introduced. Characteristics of the electron gun for additive manufacturing, the heat generation principle and heating characteristics during electron beam additive manufacturing processes are emphasized.
