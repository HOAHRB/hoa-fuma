    en: 'The course firstly introduces the complete production flow of additive manufacturing process, and guides students to understand the main technologies involved in front-end design and its role in additive manufacturing process, as well as the main design criteria and considerations of additive manufacturing. The specific professional knowledge is divided into three parts: The first part explains the structural design and modeling methods of different types of parts in detail, including the topology optimization, bionic structure, porous structure, assembly-free structure, and reverse engineering technology and its application; The second part explains the front-end design knowledge of additive manufacturing parts, including path programming, slice layering, support design, and document establishment methods of different steps. In addition, explain the design concepts and mathematical methods of complex shape structures systematically, and master the application of design methods through specific design cases. The third part, based on the characteristics of laser additive manufacturing process, explains the application of simulation technology in laser additive manufacturing quality control and deformation prediction, and the analysis characteristics of different simulation methods, including the calculation and simulation of thermal field, flow field and stress field. Finally, through the typical application cases in the industry, the application of the above knowledge points, as well as the new development of structural design and simulation technology of parts are comprehensively introduced. Finally, in the assistance of experimental teaching of three-dimensional modeling, model processing and additive manufacturing processes, students would deepen their understanding of the knowledge through practical operations.'
