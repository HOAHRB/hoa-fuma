The main objective of this course is to explore the key applications of spectroscopy techniques in IC research and manufacturing. The course will focus on how to use various spectroscopy techniques to characterize and analyze semiconductor materials, thin films, defects, and microstructures. Students will gain an understanding of how spectroscopy can help enhance the performance, reliability, and manufacturing efficiency of integrated circuits.
