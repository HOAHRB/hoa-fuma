    en: Hardware description language is the basis of top-down and SoC design method. Verilog HDL is one of the three hardware description languages supported by electronic design automation (EDA) tools at present. This course is a basic course for the design and implementation of large-scale digital integrated circuits. The purpose of this course is to enable students to complete Verilog HDL modeling of actual hardware behavior and apply it to the design process of large-scale digital integrated circuits. The course contents include background knowledge in the field of integrated circuit design, Basic cell structure, characteristics, design, and application of CMOS digital integrated circuits; Verilog HDL framework structure, syntax basis, programming style, tasks and functions, RTL modeling of basic components, verification platform structure and design, RTL modeling, verification and synthesis of complex hardware functions, etc.
