    en: The Functional Genomics course provides comprehensive coverage of many aspects of the genome sequencing program, the tree of life, and methods and techniques for genome program analysis. Students will gain an in-depth understanding of the functions and interrelationships of genes within an organism, including the full spectrum from transcription to the protein level to protein interactions. The core concepts of functional genomics and the use of animal models in research are emphasized to provide students with an initial understanding of how functional genomes are studied. In addition, the course covers the Human Genome Project, disease pathogenesis, and the application of genetic engineering technologies in medicine, biology, and agriculture. Students should become proficient in the principles of genetic engineering through hands-on practice, understand the concepts of proteomics, and utilize databases for cancer genome and gene expression data analysis to develop hands-on and data interpretation skills. This course aims to provide students with a comprehensive and practical knowledge of functional genomics and deepen their understanding and application of life sciences.
