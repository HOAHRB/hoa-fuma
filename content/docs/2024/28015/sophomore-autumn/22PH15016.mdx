    en: In essence, physics is an experimental science. The understanding and mastery of the laws of physics begin with experiments. Physical experiment is the pioneer of scientific experiment, which reflects the commonness of most scientific experiments. It is the basis of scientific experiments in various disciplines in terms of experimental ideas, experimental methods and experimental means. It plays an important role in cultivating students' rigorous scientific thinking and innovation ability, cultivating students' ability to connect theory with reality, especially the comprehensive ability to adapt to the development of science and technology. The total class hours of this course is 48, including 3 class hours of introduction course, which mainly covers error and experimental data processing, as well as basic theory and knowledge of scientific experiments. The experimental part adopts open teaching, and 26 classical physics experiments covering mechanics, thermology, acoustics, optics, electrics, magnetics, modern physics and other fields and characteristic independent inquiry physics experiment projects are selected for students to choose. 13 experiment projects are opened in autumn and spring semesters respectively. The experiment lasts 45 class hours, and 15 experimental projects are completed in two semesters. Each student completes 7 experimental projects in the autumn semester and provides complete experimental reports, while in the spring semester, complete 8 experimental projects and provide complete experimental reports. In addition to some required experiments, students can make their own choices according to their personal conditions, and are encouraged to do more experiments.
