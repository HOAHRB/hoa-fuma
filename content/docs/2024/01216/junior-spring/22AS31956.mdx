    en: Space optical satellites can quickly and accurately capture the latest spatiotemporal information of specific areas on the Earth's surface. They have advantages such as wide spatial coverage, short revisit time, multiple imaging spectral bands, rich image texture information, and the ability to gaze for a long time. They are an important means of obtaining situational information in key areas. Space optical image processing technology has been widely applied in various fields such as digital cities, geographical and national monitoring, environmental protection, and disaster emergency response. The main content of this course includes basic theoretical courses such as spatial optical imaging principles, spatial optical image radiometric correction, geometric correction, inversion restoration, fusion super-resolution, visual interpretation, machine interpretation, as well as corresponding experimental courses. The aim is to enable students to understand the imaging principles of optical remote sensing systems and the degradation mechanism of image quality, and to master the basic concepts and methods of spatial optical image processing applications such as correction, enhancement, and interpretation. Through the practice of typical cases of remote sensing image processing, students will develop the interdisciplinary application ability of "optics+information+intelligence" and the ability to apply advanced tools to solve practical engineering problems.
