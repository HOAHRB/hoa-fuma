    en: Deep learning and artificial intelligence technologies have revolutionized the traditional frameworks of target recognition and stand as the predominant methodology for addressing target recognition issues within digital imagery, both currently and for the foreseeable future. This course serves as the fundamental curriculum of Intelligent Vision Engineering, and aims to impart the basic concepts and knowledge for intelligent target recognition. It seeks to cultivate the students' analytical skills in analyzing target recognition problems and to enhance their competency in employing intelligent methodologies for problem-solving. The instructional strategy of this course is primarily lecture-based, supplemented by demonstrative teaching methods. The lectures encompass a broad array of topics, including the definition and fundamental framework of target recognition, intelligent recognition based on matching techniques, intelligent recognition based on feature learning, DL-driven intelligent recognition, optimization mechanisms for intelligent recognition, and the performance evaluation of intelligent recognition algorithms. Demonstrative teaching sessions include experiments on typical large-scale and small-scale target recognition, continuous target tracking, and performance evaluation all showcased to students during the lectures. Through the study of fundamental theories and algorithm demonstrations, students are expected to gain a rudimentary command of the basic theory of intelligent target recognition, classical recognition methodologies, and corresponding evaluation techniques. The course is designed to broaden the students’ horizons and to equip them with the foundational skills required to address target recognition challenges.
