    en: This course is specially designed for undergraduate students majoring in chemistry, focusing on the interdisciplinary field of "AI + Materials Chemistry." It aims to cultivate a new generation of talent with both data science thinking and solid chemistry knowledge. Taking real-world chemical problems as entry points, the course integrates machine learning techniques with fundamental theories of materials chemistry. Through a "learning-by-doing" teaching model, it helps students master core methods for AI-powered materials research, fostering innovative thinking that meets the demands of intelligent chemical research. This builds a cross-disciplinary foundation for future work in cutting-edge areas such as new materials development and green chemistry. The course starts with the basics of machine learning and Python programming, gradually advancing into cheminformatics and digital analysis of multiscale material features. It incorporates frontier technologies such as knowledge extraction using large language models and the integration of first-principles calculations with AI. The course emphasizes typical scenarios like alloy design and porous material development. Through hands-on projects that follow the full cycle of "data collection — feature extraction — model building — validation," students will learn to use AI tools to optimize material property prediction and synthesis pathway design, helping to overcome traditional bottlenecks such as high trial-and-error costs and challenges in cross-scale analysis in chemical research.
