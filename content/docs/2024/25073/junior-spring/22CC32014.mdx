    en: Structural chemistry, as one important branching of physical chemistry, is a major course for chemistry studies in university. It probes the electron and space structures at the atomistic and molecular levels and relates them with physical properties. With the development of interdiscipline, structure chemistry provides fundamentals for the latest issues and techniques in theories and experiments. It investigates the relationship between the structures and properties of substances based on the fact that structures determine properties and in turn the properties reveal structures. In this course, knowledge of quantum mechanics and structures and characteristics of atoms and molecules are introduced, concerning quantum rules for microscopic particles, distributions and energy levels of electrons in an atom, components, space structure and configuration, electron distributions, bonding, and energy states of molecules. After finishing the course students should know the intrinsic rules of motions at a microscopic state, learn the knowledge and basic principles about the structures and features of atoms and molecules, better understand the contents of inorganic and organic chemistry they previous studied, and have a knowledge foundation for the latter specialty lessons and scientific research.
