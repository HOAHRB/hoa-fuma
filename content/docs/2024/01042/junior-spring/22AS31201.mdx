    en: Robust and adaptive control is one of the frontiers in the control science and engineering discipline, also serves as an important mandatory course for automation students. Taking automatic control theory as a prerequisite, the course focuses on systematic methodology dealing with the uncertainties widespread in actual control systems. As a result of a deliberate consideration on the undergraduate knowledge scope, the course covers robust and adaptive control, both in theory and application, for single-input single-out-put systems, the exposition of which takes a gradual way, easy to involved, to help comprehension. The course mainly consists of three parts. The first part reveals the causation and properties of uncertainties in control systems through case studies on various control system examples taking from real life. The second part deals, via robust control theory, with uncertainties in systems, which are mathematically described. Control system sensitivity analysis is utilized to cope with uncertainties, and therefore leads to the robust control system analysis and design methos. The third part discusses adaptive controller design for systems with uncertainties through online identification of unknown parameters and accordingly adjusting in real time controller parameters. Through the course study, including lecturing in class and experiments outside class, it is expected that the overall capabilities are cultivated for students in theoretical analysis and design of control systems. In addition, it is also hoped that the sense on innovation of students is improved to lay a sound foundation for solving practical engineering problems.
