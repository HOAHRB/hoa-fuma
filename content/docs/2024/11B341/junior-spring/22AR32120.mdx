It focuses on the underlying concepts and mechanisms for creating performance-based parametric systems. The principle platform is the parametric modeling plug-in Grasshopper for Rhino 3-D with additional supporting plug-ins and add-ons as required. Students learn the overall structure of parametric modeling systems and acquire fluency in visual programming environments. They also gain experience in the embedding of design knowledge into a parametric model and in the use of appropriate parametric techniques for conveying formal elements of the design process..
