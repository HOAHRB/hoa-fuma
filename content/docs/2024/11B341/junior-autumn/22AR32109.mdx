The purpose of curriculum is to explore underlying stories behind images of architecture from the perspective of civilization, to enable students to analyze cultural elements associated with architecture form and notions of space. Furthermore, students are instructed to explain the phenomena that architecture space form and their evolvements vary with different civilizations, and they are required to using comparative study to grasp generation and development of architecture space in different culture background with the cross-cultural view. The curriculum includes four topics: Eastern and Western cultural characteristics, Chinese architecture and Confucianism, Buddhism and Taoism culture, architecture and philosophy, and architecture and anthropology. The curriculum combines topic-based teaching with discussion in class to build an overall framework of interaction between Chinese and Western civilizations and architecture is established.
