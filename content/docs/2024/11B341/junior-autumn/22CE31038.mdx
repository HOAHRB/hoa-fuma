Architectural mechanics and building structure is an integral whole to support each other. Building mechanics provides people a method to recognize and analyze building structures. It is useful to enrich and develop architectural mechanics by compacting building structure models. Through this course, students will be able to master the physical and basic mechanical properties of building materials such as construction concrete, construction steel and construction timber. Students will be able to master the conception, calculation and accessor methods of dead load, live load, wind load, earthquake effect, temperature effect, and etc. After mastering the calculation methods 0f the internal forces of simply-supported beams and continuous beams, the design and calculation methods of the bearing capacity of concrete beams and steel beams will be mastered by students. After mastering the internal force analysis and calculation method of the building, students will learn to design method of floor system. After introducing internal force analysis methods of truss, students will be aware of engineering application and development status of the grid.
