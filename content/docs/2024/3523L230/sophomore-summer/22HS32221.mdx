    en: In recent years, artificial intelligence and automation technologies have developed rapidly and have reached an inflection point. This will undoubtedly have a profound impact on the future of mankind. How will rapid advances in artificial intelligence and automation affect business, jobs, the economy, and most importantly, you? What can you personally do to stay competitive in the workforce? A market where more and more jobs are being replaced by AI? In this course, we will explore the economic and social impact of this powerful new technology. We will discuss social, economic, and academic topics related to AI, such as is chatbot intelligence the same as ours? Will they replace jobs previously held by highly skilled human workers, such as lawyers, doctors, and software engineers? Will we fall in love with chatbots? Will they fall in love with us too? Why are so many experts developing chatbots worried that they might destroy humans? What can we do to stop them from destroying humanity? Will we one day be able to "upload" our thoughts to a computer server, in effect becoming chatbots ourselves? We will combine knowledge from multiple disciplines, including computer science, psychology, sociology, and economics to explore these questions. Students' assignments will focus on original research into existing AI-based technologies as well as critical reflection on how we want AI to shape society in the future.
