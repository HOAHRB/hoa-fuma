    en: Energy Internet and Big Data is an interdisciplinary field that encompasses power systems, energy dynamics, and information science. It is based on Internet technology with a focus on the power system as its core. Horizontally, it achieves the "multi-source complementarity" of electricity, gas, heat, renewable energy, and more. Vertically, it ensures highly coordinated links between "source, grid, load, and storage," facilitating two-way interactions between production and consumption. This approach is centralized within a distributed yet integrated energy service network. This course provides a comprehensive introduction to the background, definition, functions, features, and technical architecture of Energy Internet and Big Data. It elaborates on the key technologies required for Energy Internet, including crucial technologies related to the power system, energy infrastructure, information and communication, power electronics, and platform technology. Through the analysis of typical cases, the course explores the key technologies and applications of Energy Internet, equipping students with an understanding of the fundamental principles, core technologies, current industry status, and the development trajectory of Energy Internet and Big Data. This foundation provides students with the necessary groundwork for pursuing professional careers and engaging in scientific research in the field of Energy Internet and smart energy construction after graduation.
