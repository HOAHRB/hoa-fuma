By considering the experimental requirements and using the related theoretical knowledge, the students should design the experimental program and complete it independently EXPERIMENTS IN ENGINEERING MECHANICS can not only fulfill the teaching of basic mechanical experiments but also help the students to make a thorough understanding about the basic theories in mechanics. In addition, through this course, both the basic scientific qualities and the creative thinking abilities of the students will be initiated and enlarged. Through this course, the students will not only be capable of observing the mechanical phenomenon, but also will be trained with advanced experimental techniques.
