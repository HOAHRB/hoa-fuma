    en: Social Statistics and its computer application are the elementary and necessary knowledge and skills for sociology undergraduates. As one of the widespread statistic tools, proficient SPSS operation is the required ability for sociologists and social workers. This course introduces windows, menus, dialog boxes, options, and buttons in SPSS. It requires the students to achieve a solid proficiency in operations of files, data, and charts. Operations of files include establishing, transposing, merging, splitting, and summarizing files. Basic operations of data include ranking, selecting, inserting, re-encoding data, as well as elimination of errors. Data analyzing operations include descriptive statistics, means analysis and T-test, correlation analysis, regression analysis, multi-selection multivariate analysis, and non-parametric test.
