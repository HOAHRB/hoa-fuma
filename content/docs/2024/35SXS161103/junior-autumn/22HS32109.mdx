    en: This course will guide students to rethink the primary pairwise problems of sociology, such as culture and society as well as meaning and structure, by emphasizing the special position of culture in sociological theory. By exploring key issues of culture sociology, such as analysis of symbols and rituals, cultural hegemony, post-colonialism, subculture, research of fashion and consuming, production and reception of culture, and culture of body and gender, it requires students to understand the cultural dimension of sociology. It also requires them to read the classics of cultural sociology so as to improve their abilities to analyze cultural phenomena.
