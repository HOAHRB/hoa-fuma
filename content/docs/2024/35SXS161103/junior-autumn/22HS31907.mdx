    en: 'Anthropology has two major tasks: one is to understand what it means to be human, and the other is to understand how human nature is expressed through diverse cultures. The rapid development of digital technology has brought new forces to both. The Digital Anthropology course, through the study of anthropological theories and the exploration of their relationship with digital technology, attempts to reach a conclusion: what does digital technology mean to humanity? Based on a review of relevant theories in digital anthropology and combined with research methods in this field, this course explores the new developments of anthropology in the digital age. It delves into the human society in the cyberspace, the impact of digital technology on human culture, and digital anthropology in the political life domain, among others, to investigate the influence of digital technology on human society. It also uses certain cases for teaching, aiming to enable students to truly understand the relevant knowledge of anthropology in the digital age through the course, helping them better understand humanity and the world.'
