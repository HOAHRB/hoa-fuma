    en: This course focuses on the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) and finance, systematically teaching the technical framework and practical methods of intelligent finance. It aims to cultivate interdisciplinary talents with innovative thinking, technical expertise, and social responsibility in the field of financial technology. The course explores the core applications of AI in finance, combining game theory, machine learning, data science, and financial engineering to equip students with the knowledge and skills to bridge theoretical modeling and commercial implementation. The curriculum covers cutting-edge topics in AI-driven finance, including game-theoretic modeling of market strategies, the application of linear and nonlinear models in financial forecasting, association rule analysis for product recommendations, and graph neural networks (GNNs) and privacy-preserving technologies such as federated learning. Through a combination of theory and practice, students will learn to apply algorithms like deep learning, reinforcement learning, and semi-supervised learning to address real-world financial challenges, such as robo-advisory systems, quantitative trading, risk control, and anti-fraud mechanisms. The course emphasizes ethical compliance and practical implementation, guiding students to design AI-based financial solutions that balance commercial value with social impact—for example, using federated learning to protect user privacy or leveraging generative AI to optimize financial decision-making processes. The teaching approach combines project-based learning (PBL) and case-driven instruction. Students will use Python/R toolchains to implement algorithms and validate models through simulation platforms. Working in teams, they will complete end-to-end tasks spanning data preprocessing, model development, and business solution design, enhancing their ability to integrate technical knowledge and solve complex problems. Additionally, the course addresses ethical challenges and regulatory frameworks in AI finance, fostering critical thinking about technological risks and advocating for "technology for good" principles to promote sustainable development in areas like inclusive finance and green finance. By the end of the course, students will not only master the core technologies at the intersection of AI and finance but also gain the capability to translate innovative ideas into practical value, preparing them for careers in financial technology, quantitative investing, risk management, and related fields.
