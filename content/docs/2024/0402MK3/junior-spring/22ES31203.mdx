The teaching requirements of this course are as follows: First, the main structure and brief history of development of gas turbine power system as well as the main problems to be faced for further development should be explained clearly, so that students can have a certain understanding of the development status of gas turbine power system. On this basis, the basic cycle theory of gas turbine power system is introduced, so that students can independently analyze the cycle of gas turbine power system with different cycle modes, skillfully draw the cycle process on the temperature-entropy diagram, and perform thermal calculation at the inlet and outlet of each component according to the cycle process. This course focuses on the structure and function of the combustion chamber of the gas turbine power system and the basic combustion theory, so that students can understand the measures to make the combustion stable. Finally, it is required to focus on the variable working condition performance of the gas turbine power system, and analyze the shaft combination schemes of different forms of gas turbine power system according to the variable working condition performance of each component, so that students can understand the adaptation conditions of various combined gas turbine power systems. Through the study of this course, students will have a certain understanding of gas turbine power system, which will lay a foundation for graduation design and future work. This course is mainly for students majoring in energy and power engineering. Students are required to have learned the basic content of Engineering Thermodynamics, Gas Dynamics and other courses. Status and role of this course: The development of the modern thermal power industry urgently depends on the continuous improvement of the gas turbine engine, so mastering the principles of the gas turbine is essential for those who are interested in working in this field.'
