    en: 'Advancements in E-commerce, social network, cloud computing, mobile Internet, and FinTech have brought the human society into a new era of data-driven business. The capability of assisting business decision-making from the acquirement, management, and analysis of multi-dimensional data has become one of the core competencies for firms in the era of big data. As a fundamental course for the undergraduate students in the discipline of economics and management, this course aims to guide the students to learn the essentials of business data analysis from both the technological and business perspectives. We will cover the basic concepts of BDA: data mining, business intelligence, machine learning, statistical modeling, etc., and lecture on the theory basis and applications of the key data analysis tools including summary statistics, classification, regression, association rules mining, data visualization. The course will also feature the introduction to practical cases and academic research in various business management scenarios such as online recommender systems, financial big data analysis, and production line management, and discussions of contemporary extended topics including misinformation, privacy computing, ethics and regulation of algorithms, etc. The goals of this course are to help the students utilize the basic concepts, knowledge, and tools of BDA to solve practical problems, and to train their ability of using basic functions of R language in RStudio to perform data analysis and assist business decision-making.'
