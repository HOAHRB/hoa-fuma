The course also fosters autonomous learning and cultural awareness, laying a solid basis for further study and international exchange. Upon completion, students will: command basic French sounds (35 phonemes and 26 letters) and intonation patterns (declarative, interrogative and emphatic sentences); possess fundamental vocabulary and grammar (verb conjugation, gender of nouns, etc.) and be able to use basic sentence patterns, dialogues and short written texts; recognize and contrast French and Chinese usage at the lexical, syntactic, grammatical and discourse levels, and carry out simple, flexible transfer when producing French; understand a scientific approach to language learning, begin to study independently, gain initial insight into French culture, and demonstrate elementary cross-cultural understanding and communication skills.'
