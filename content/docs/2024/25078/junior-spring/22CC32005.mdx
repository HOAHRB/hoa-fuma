Students are required to master the basic theories involved in the synthesis of inorganic compounds and master the principles and methods of high-temperature synthesis, low-temperature synthesis, high-pressure synthesis, etc., master the synthesis design methods and skills of organic compounds, learn how to design their synthesis routes by reverse cutting according to the known molecular structure, and master the synthesis and characterization methods of organic-inorganic hybrid materials.
