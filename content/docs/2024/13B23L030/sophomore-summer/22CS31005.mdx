    en: Industry intern and practice is a practical course that enables students to contact society, enterprises and professional research, and is a compulsory course for computer majors. Students can choose from the following methods to complete this course. (1) Students contact enterprises independently and complete 2 weeks of practical training. (2) College/professional recommended enterprises, students go to enterprises to complete more than 2 weeks of practical training. (3) Participate in the summer camp of the university or other C9 college summer camps and complete 2 weeks of practical training. (4) Complete 2 weeks of practical training in the national experimental teaching demonstration center or the research room of each research center of the college. (5) Short-term international exchange programs contacted through open channels of the school, and the certificate of acceptance of the foreign school stating the content of the courses or research projects taken abroad can obtain credits. (6) Other ways. Subject to the consent of the Dean of Teaching of the College, credits may be obtained in other suitable ways. Students can also use the winter and summer vacation of sophomore or junior year to complete short-term practical training according to their own situation.
