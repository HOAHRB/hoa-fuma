The actuator is one of the core units of a robot, determining its basic characteristics, functions, and application areas. Traditional actuator, pneumatic, and hydraulic actuating technologies for robots are limited by factors such as structure, principles, and characteristics, and are no longer able to meet the development needs of various emerging fields. Based on this, various new actuating and control technologies have been widely researched and applied.
