Big data computing platforms are capable of efficiently processing and analyzing massive amounts of data and unearthing the value hidden within. This ability to process and analyze data is of great guiding significance for corporate decision-making, policy formulation and scientific research. This course will combine the data analysis algorithms provided by the Big Data computing platform to develop students' ability to combine research and practice. Through the study of this course, students can master the basic principles of various algorithms in big data platforms, the basic skills of computing platforms, understand the latest development, cutting-edge research areas, and applications in different disciplines, and lay a preliminary foundation for future engagement in big data research and practice.
