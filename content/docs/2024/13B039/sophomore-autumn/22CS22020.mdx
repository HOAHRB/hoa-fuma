    en: This course consists of two parts, including Modern Algebra and Mathematical Logic. Modern Algebra also called abstract algebra, its research content including algebraic structure or algebraic system, with numbers, polynomials, matrix, transformation and their algebraic operation as concrete models, gives the concept of semigroup, group, ring and field. Also gives the substructures and quotient structures of these algebraic structures and homomorphism and isomorphism between these structures. Mathematical logic concerns with the research of formal theory of man's reasoning with mathematics. It has widespread applications in computer science. we give concept of propositions and logical connectives, formalization of natural languages, formal axiomatic system of propositional logic, formal axiomatic system of first order predicate logic and theory of reasoning.
