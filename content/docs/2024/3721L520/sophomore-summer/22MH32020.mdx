    en: Developmental biology is the science that studies the basic processes and mechanisms of growth and development in multicellular animals. With the prerequisite of biochemistry and molecular biology, cell biology, introduction to medical fundamentals, medical genetics and other courses, this course focuses on the fertilization process, embryonic development, post-embryonic growth of multicellular animals and related molecular mechanisms, and focuses on human and model animals as the object of study. Students are required to master the basic content of the syllabus, understand the main processes, laws and molecular mechanisms of multicellular animal development; recognize the animal organism from the perspective of development, and combine with other courses to achieve a comprehensive understanding of multicellular animal development. Through the study of Developmental Biology, students will learn the basic laws and mechanisms of animal development, and the new progress and discovery in the field of developmental biology, which will lay the foundation for future biomedical scientific research and related work.
