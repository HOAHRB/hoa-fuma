Gene therapy, as a revolutionary technology in modern medicine, is reshaping the pattern of disease treatment at an unprecedented speed. The core of this technology is to correct pathogenic gene defects or enhance targeted functions at the molecular level through gene editing, gene replacement, or gene regulation, in order to treat diseases or improve physiological conditions. With the rapid development of molecular biology and genetic engineering technology, gene therapy has gradually become a research hot spot in the biomedical field. More and more diseases have been confirmed to be closely related to gene mutations or frequent expression, providing broad application space for gene therapy. This course mainly discusses the core technology of gene therapy, and through clinical applications and case analysis, enables students to understand the future development trends of gene therapy, laying a solid foundation for learning other related courses.
