    en: Data mining is one of the most important driving technologies for the rapid development of big data and artificial intelligence industries in the past decade. Currently, almost all big data analysis relies on data mining techniques, which are widely used and highly demanded in the industry. Data mining integrates core knowledge from multiple computer science fields such as probability and statistics, machine learning, artificial intelligence, data visualization, algorithms, and more, making it an essential research area in information technology. Mastering relevant knowledge and skills in data mining is crucial for students' current employment and future advanced studies. In this course, we will focus on the most practical aspects of data mining, including data understanding, data preprocessing, association rule mining, clustering mining, regression analysis, Logistic Regression, decision trees, Naive Bayes, support vector machines, ensemble learning, and more. The teaching object of this course is the Engineering Experimental Class (Medical Category 1), and students have already taken such major basic courses as Calculus B, Probability Theory and Mathematical Statistics C, Algebra and Geometry C, etc. After these introductory courses, students have acquired basic statistical knowledge and skills. However, the Medicine and Health Sciences major is a highly interdisciplinary field, and in the medical domain, students need to master various methodologies that extend beyond medical expertise to include statistics, computer science, biology, and other disciplines. Additionally, while students may have had a preliminary introduction to their major's related topics during their freshman year, they lack a deep understanding of areas such as massive medical data computation, analysis, modeling, and the application of relevant principles and knowledge. They often remain at a superficial level of understanding concepts and cannot effectively comprehend the significance and impact of medical big data analysis within a systematic and organized framework. They may also struggle to integrate professional knowledge with domain-specific issues, fully appreciate the importance of data modeling in healthcare, or employ statistical methods and R language tools for problem analysis, evaluation, solution, and independent thinking. These capabilities are core to the professional development of students and are essential skills they need to acquire. This course is an elective that combines classic data mining algorithms with R language tools, providing a solid foundation for students to delve deeper into scientific research and prepare for suitable career paths.
