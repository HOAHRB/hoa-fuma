    en: Intelligent Bionic Medicine is an interdisciplinary elective course offered by the School of Medicine and Health for undergraduate students. Integrating biology, medicine, materials science, electronics, and control science, this course covers fundamental topics such as bionics principles, smart materials and structures, biosignal sensing and interaction, neural bionics and brain-computer interfaces, as well as bionic robotics. It is designed for students majoring in Biomedical Engineering, Intelligent Medical Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science and Technology, and related disciplines. The course aims to bridge basic medical knowledge with cutting-edge engineering applications, focusing on the use of bionic principles, smart materials, biosensing, and control technologies in medicine. Through a combination of theoretical instruction and case studies, students will develop a holistic understanding of bionic systems and critically examine ethical challenges and societal impacts associated with intelligent bionic technologies. The course seeks to cultivate interdisciplinary integration and innovative design capabilities. By building a cross-disciplinary knowledge framework, this course supports students' academic and professional development in rehabilitation engineering, intelligent medical devices, precision medicine, and other emerging fields. It serves as a vital component in educating innovative talents in medical engineering, preparing them to contribute to the future of healthcare technology.
