Through the course, for the students, not only the basic principles of physics can be understood deeply, but also the scientific ability and quality for practice is enhanced. The aim of this course is to acquaint the students with some typical knowledge for experimental techniques and some methods to judge and analyze the results, to let them to master some basic instruments for scientific experiments. We hope this effort may benefit to them to engage the scientific research, R&D, fabrication with a solid base. Besides some basic experiments, the course include may types of comprehensive experiments and some topics belonging to the sort of challenges that needs special design，the aim of which is to enhance the students' interests and encourage them to posses the ability to resolve problem independently. Some experiments, which gained the Nobel Prize in Physics are intentionally introduced into the course to expect the students to understand the significant meaning of some important finding for changing concepts or cognition to the Nature and the style of production and daily life of human being via the scientific physics experiments. As the foundation of many courses, this course will provide important assistance to the teaching process for the other practice courses.
