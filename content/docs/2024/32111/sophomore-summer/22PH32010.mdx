    en: The course of Introduction to Biophotonics is a one of the optional courses for the major of optoelectronic information science and engineering. Biophotonics deals with the interactions between light and biology specimens. It is an interdisciplinary subject combining photonics and biology, which is an extension of photonics into biology. Photonics can be used in optical diagnosis and optical therapy. Meanwhile, biomaterials provide a new optical medium for future technological applications. The course is to systematically teach biophotonics, which includes photobiology, traditional bioimaging, scanning confocal microscopy, nonlinear optical microscopy, optical biosensors, photodynamic therapy, laser tissue engineering, laser tweezers, laser scissors and biomaterials for photonics. The objective is to help students understand the various technology and the basic principles and know the recent progress, which could lay a strong foundation for biophotonics researches and applications. Meanwhile, this course will help students know how to do scientific research, and stimulate their interests in scientific researches.
