The content is including the comprehensive application of street type, spatial form, slow traffic and other professional knowledge to resolve the problems of street space renewal and utilization in order to meet the needs of urban renewal and stock development. That enables students to improve their ability to solve urban, functional, aesthetic, social and other urban and rural residential environment quality problems. The course aims to master the specific methods and approaches of street guide preparation, street planning and design, and street landscape design through theoretical study and field investigation. The course is two parts. The first part is the principles of street landscape design, including basic concepts, research methods and laws and regulations. The second part is to collect basic data for research and judgment and complete the planning and design scheme through the street field survey.
