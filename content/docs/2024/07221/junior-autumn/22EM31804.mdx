Theoretical reviews and examples of how the theories are applied in practice are presented in an easy-to-understand manner and serve as a guide for students to further investigate specific ancillary central banking topics and as a means to make informed judgments about central bank actions. Important topics covered in the course include: Evolution of central banking functions; theoretical backgrounds that are the foundation to the modern practice of monetary policy; monetary policy regimes, including exchange rate targeting, money supply growth targeting, the risk management approach, inflation targeting, and unconventional monetary policy; actual practice in market operations and transmission mechanisms of monetary policy, etc.'
