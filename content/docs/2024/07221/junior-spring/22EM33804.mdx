    en: Since the introduction of the Capital Asset Pricing Model in the 1960s, empirical asset pricing has undergone over 50 years of development in the academic field, forming a comprehensive research system and yielding abundant empirical results. At the same time, factor investing developed from these studies has now become an essential tool for asset managers at home and abroad, and it has great potential in asset allocation and obtaining excess returns. Therefore, this course will systematically introduce research methods and investment practices of factor investing, and guide students in mastering the application of various analytical methods in studying anomalies and multi-factor models. The course includes three modules. The first module builds a unified perspective for studying factor-based investing. The second module introduces the statistical methods most commonly used by academia in factor research, such as portfolio sorting, Fama-MacBeth regression, etc. The third module focuses on constructing factor-based investment strategies. Specifically, students will delve into the latest academic research literature, collect data, organize samples, construct factor selection strategies, and present the final portfolio results through research reports.
