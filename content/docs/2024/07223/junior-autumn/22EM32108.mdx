    en: The course takes the cutting-edge applications of Generative AI technology in the field of business analysis as the main line, systematically teaching the basics of large language models (LLMs), the development methods of agents, and their applications in business analysis. It trains students to use GenAI technology to complete business analysis tasks such as sentiment analysis of review texts, expert evaluation, and knowledge-based recommendations. It also guides students to build agents based on the LangChain and Dify.AI platforms that can independently complete business analysis tasks. The course aims to enhance students' innovative thinking and practical abilities in the era of large models, helping them grow into digital and intelligent business analysis talents for the future.
