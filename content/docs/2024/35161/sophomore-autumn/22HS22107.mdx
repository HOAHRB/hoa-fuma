    en: This course introduces the development of Chinese social thoughts from Qin Dynasty to modern China, which can be divided into six parts. In the first part, it introduces the social thoughts in the Spring and Autumn Period, including the social thoughts of Lao Tzu, Confucius, Mozi, Mencius, Zhuangzi, Xunzi and Han Fei. In the second part, it focuses on social thoughts of Dong Zhongshu and Wang Chong in Qin and Han dynasties. In the following part, social thoughts of He Yan and Wang Bi who lived in Wei, Jin and the Southern and Northern Dynasties will be discussed. The fourth part, Sui, Tang and the Five Dynasties, takes Han Yu’s social thoughts for instance. In the fifth part, this course explains the social thoughts of Cheng Hao, Cheng Yi, Zhu Xi, Wang Shouren, Li Zhi, and Wang Fuzhi in Song, Yuan, Ming, and Qing Dynasties. In the last part, it provides an overview of social thoughts in modern China by examining the social thoughts of Gongzizhen, Wei Yuan, Hong Xiuquan Kang Youwei, and Sun Yat-sen. In addition, it explores the modern significance of all social thoughts.
