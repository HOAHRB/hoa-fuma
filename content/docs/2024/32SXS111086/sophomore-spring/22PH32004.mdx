This kind of imaging technology is formed by the intersection of optics, computer, information and other disciplines. Computational optical imaging technology is developing in the direction of higher resolution, longer operating distance, wider field of view, smaller structural volume and stronger environmental adaptation. The course “principles and techniques of Computational Optical Imaging” introduces the principle, technology and application of computational optical imaging on the basis of mathematical analysis, linear algebra, optics and other courses. The specific content of this course includes the theory, technology and application of computational optical imaging. The theoretical part includes the computational optical imaging method based on fast Fourier transform, angular spectrum and so on. Computational optical imaging includes correlation imaging (also called as ghost imaging), compressed sensing imaging, CT imaging, computer holographic imaging, non-field imaging, coded aperture imaging, phase imaging, light field camera, adaptive optical imaging, synthetic aperture imaging, excited loss microscopic imaging, polarization imaging, etc., as examples, teach the principles and techniques of computational optical imaging.
