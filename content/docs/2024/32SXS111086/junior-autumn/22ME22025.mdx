The time-domain analysis of a typical first-order, a second-order and a higher order systems are presented; the polar diagram, logarithmic plot and the analysis of performance indicators of frequency characteristics for a typical control system are presented; the control system stability criterion and the relative stability of the composition of the computer control system analysis, the calculation of the steady-state error are introduced, PID correction, control of nonlinear system, controller discretization method, design and calibration of digital control system, fuzzy control, neural network and automatic control based design and analyze of control system.
