This course primarily focuses on radar systems, explaining their detection principles and countermeasure technologies. Specific topics include the basic composition and working principles of radar systems, radar ranging principles, radar angle measurement principles, radar velocity measurement principles, radar imaging technology, radar countermeasure signal environments and frequency measurement receivers, direction-finding technology and radiation source localization, as well as radar jamming techniques. Through a combination of teaching and experimentation, students will not only acquire relevant theoretical knowledge but also gain more intuitive understanding through hands-on experiments. This enables students to recognize the importance of radar in modern warfare and cultivate their ability to solve complex engineering problems in radar detection and countermeasure systems.
