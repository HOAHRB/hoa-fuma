    en: Selected Works of Russian Literature is a specialized elective course for senior students majoring in Russian. Its focus lies in the study of specific literary works. This course accords independent status to the texts, emphasizing direct engagement with the works to cultivate a perceptual and intuitive understanding of Russian literature, thereby achieving the goals of moral edification and the enhancement of aesthetic taste. It guides students to elevate their humanistic qualities through the appreciation of Russian literary classics, fostering their literary cultivation and critical abilities. Simultaneously, it encourages an understanding of Russian culture through literary phenomena, laying the groundwork for further in-depth research into Russian literature. The course is structured along the developmental trajectory of Russian literature, primarily selecting classic works from key periods of 19th-century Russian literature (covering poetry and fiction), including representative works by writers such as Pushkin, Lermontov, Turgenev, and Tyutchev, as well as poetry from the 20th-century period of the Great Patriotic War.
