    en: Aimed at cultivating students’ ability to use the English language, this course is a required one for sophomores majoring in Japanese and Russian. The ultimate goal is to lay a solid foundation for students’ further English learning in the future. By learning fundamental English grammar such as modal verbs, passive voice, infinitives and clauses, dialogues with settings of everyday life and texts of 200 to 300 words, students will be enabled to grasp related linguistic and cultural knowledge, understand the communicative and informative meanings of dialogues and texts, develop skills of listening, speaking, reading, writing and translation through effective use of the textbook and various in-class activities, improve their comprehensive abilities of using the English language, and thus cultivate their cross-cultural communicative competence. Students’ performances will be evaluated with regard to class participation, assignments, quizzes, and the final exam.
