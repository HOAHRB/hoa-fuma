    en: This course is an experimental course designed for undergraduates majoring in aerospace and energy & power. It focuses on teaching the design methods, performance experimental principles, and aerodynamic performance testing methods for the main aerodynamic components of aircraft engines, as well as the structural disassembly and assembly of aircraft engine models. The main objective of the course is to help students establish the basic concepts of aircraft engine systems, master the experimental methods and principles for main aerodynamic components of aircraft engine, throughflow structure characteristics, and design & evaluation methods of aerodynamic performance parameters. Additionally, it enhances undergraduates' cognitive ability for aircraft engine systems, deepens their understanding of the underlying logical structure for aircraft engine throughflow design, and lays theoretical foundation for their future studies in this field. This course applies a teaching method that combines classroom theory teaching, experimental devices design, engine model disassembly & assembly and numerical simulation. It combines the throughflow design theory and development process of aircraft engines with the physical disassembly and assembly of engine models for practical training. Not only it enables students to acquire theoretical knowledge, but also it also enhances their comprehensive ability to solve engineering practical problems.
