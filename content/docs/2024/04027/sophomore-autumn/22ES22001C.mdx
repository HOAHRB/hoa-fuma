    en: Aircraft propulsion is undergoing a transformation from old aerospace to new aerospace, with new technologies emerging endlessly, accelerating the rise and fall of enterprises, and the inherent logic is difficult to find. The cost perspective is one of the core perspectives for understanding the survival of the fittest in technology and the rise and fall of industries, but it is also a blind spot in science and engineering teaching. Based on the long-term perspective, the Energy College has decided to offer this course to cultivate students' basic awareness of cost control, as well as a basic perspective of examining the advantages and disadvantages of technology and choosing development paths. This course first introduces the concept of cost management, its different forms in the systems of Europe, America, and the former Soviet Union, as well as the cost management of China's aerospace industry in the new era. Afterwards, this course will take four lectures to introduce the different classifications of costs and their main calculation methods, including the balance between fixed and variable costs, the balance between procurement and production costs, and the calculation of capital costs. Afterwards, taking into account the different cost components mentioned above, we will explain the calculation methods of breakeven points and breakeven points in cost volume profit analysis. On the other hand, we will spend one lecture explaining the concepts of target cost and standard cost in cost control, and demonstrate the importance of cost control through examples. Finally, we will spend one lecture, combined with propulsion sub field, to demonstrate the process of comprehensive budget preparation, cost composition analysis, and the main direction of cost control. The main assessment method for this course is a final essay, combined with students' self selected new technologies, and analyzing the technological prospects from a cost analysis perspective.
