    en: Digital Signal Processing is a fundamental course in the fields of electronic information, information countermeasure technology, and remote sensing science and technology. Its application areas involve communication, radar and sonar, image processing, seismic signal analysis, acoustic engineering, intelligent measurement and control, and many other aspects. The teaching purpose of this course is to enable students to master the basic theory of digital signal processing and cultivate their ability to correctly apply digital signal processing technology to solve practical problems. The main content may be divided into three parts, that is, discrete-time systems, digital spectrum analysis and digital filter. Discrete-time systems contain sampling of continuous time signal, discrete-time systems, z-transforms, inverse z-transforms and so on. Digital spectrum analysis contains Discrete Fourier Series, Discrete Fourier Transform，Inverse Discrete Fourier Transform and Fast Fourier Transform. Digital filter contains digital filter design and digital filter realizations. Design methods of finite impulse response filter and infinite impulse response filter are introduced in digital filter design. Design methods of FIR filter contain window method, frequency sampling method. Design methods of IIR filter contain impulse-invariance method, dual linearity method and so on. Net forms of finite impulse response filter and infinite impulse response filter are introduced in digital filter realizations, including direct form, canonical form, cascade form and so on.
