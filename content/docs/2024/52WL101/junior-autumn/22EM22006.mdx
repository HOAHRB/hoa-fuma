    en: This course aims to cultivate the students’ capability of conducting quantitative analysis on business and economics problems. Grounded in probability theory and applied statistics, this course will introduce the basic statistical methods and the most popular open-source statistical software used in data mining -- R to the students majored in E-commerce. The course will make the students understand the main techniques for manipulating the multi-dimensional big data. These techniques can be used for clustering and classification, data reduction, correlation analysis and forecasting. The course will teach cluster analysis, discriminant analysis, principal component analysis, factor analysis, and regression analysis methods. Through lab experiments and explorative study, the course also seeks to teach the students the implementation of different analysis methods in R software, and enhance their understanding of the different methods and their capability to manipulate data and construct statistical models via software. Finally, via case study approach and real data of business and economics problems, the course helps the students learn how to choose the appropriate multivariate analysis technique for different types of business problems, thus enhance the students’ ability to find, analyze, and solve problems in extensive data.
