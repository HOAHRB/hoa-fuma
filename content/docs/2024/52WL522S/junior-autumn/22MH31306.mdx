    en: Outline of Diagnostics is a discipline that studies how to apply fundamental theories, basic knowledge, essential skills, and diagnostic reasoning to diagnose patients. It serves as a bridging course connecting basic and clinical medicine, as well as a crucial foundation for clinical medical practice. The purpose of this course is to enable students to master the fundamental concepts of diagnostics, methods of inquiry, and differential diagnosis for common symptoms; acquire systematic physical examination techniques and the ability to recognize normal signs; understand the basics of common laboratory tests and electrocardiography; and develop clinical logical thinking and standardized medical record writing skills. Through this course, students can gain a solid theoretical foundation while receiving excellent training in scientific research methods and logical thinking models, laying a strong knowledge base for future research in related fields.
