    en: This elective course is designed for undergraduate students majoring in Intelligent Medical Engineering, aiming to cultivate a "health-centered" medical philosophy and strengthen a holistic mindset toward disease prevention and health promotion. The course systematically introduces the fundamental theories, core content, and practical methods of preventive medicine, covering topics such as environment and health, nutrition and food hygiene, occupational health, fundamentals of epidemiology and biostatistics, chronic disease prevention and control, public health policy, and health management. Through this course, students not only learn to identify population-level health determinants and design intervention strategies, but also understand the paradigm shift from individual clinical care to population health. Aligned with the characteristics of intelligent medical engineering, the course integrates cutting-edge content such as big data-driven disease prediction models, artificial intelligence applications in public health surveillance, wearable devices for health data collection, and digital health management system design, encouraging deep integration of engineering technologies with preventive medicine. Emphasizing the combination of theory and practice, the course employs case studies, project-based learning, and interdisciplinary discussions to develop students’ abilities to address public health challenges using intelligent technologies. It lays a solid medical foundation for their future involvement in smart healthcare system development, health data analytics, and personalized health management platforms, supporting the strategic transformation from "disease-centered" to "health-centered" care.
