    en: Nanomedicine is the application of nanoscience and nanotechnology in the field of medicine, including the prevention, diagnosis, therapy, and rehabilitation of diseases. In the new century, with the fast development of nanoscience and nanotechnology, they have been continually applied in biotechnology and clinical medicine, replacing some traditional testing, diagnostic, or therapeutic techniques and producing huge economic and social benefits. For example, the use of gold nanoparticles improves the efficiency and sensitivity of AIDS diagnosis. Magic nanoparticles can be used as a targeting agent to reduce the toxicity of the drug for the treatment of cancer or other diseases. As a new method of surgical treatment, nanohybrid materials can be applied in tissue repair and regeneration. In this course, we will first illustrate the basic concepts, materials, and techniques of nanoscience and nanotechnology. Furthermore, their utility in the diagnosis and therapy of cancer, infectious diseases, cardiovascular diseases, diabetes, and orthopedics as well as in food, drug, and cosmetics manufacture will be introduced. This course aims to let the students know the new ideas and methods that can solve the urgent problems in biology and medicine provided by nanoscience and nanotechnology, as well as the new industry produced by nanotechnology. This course will also encourage students to devote themselves to the development of bio- or medical technology.
