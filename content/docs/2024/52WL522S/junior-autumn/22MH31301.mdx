    en: Medical Microbiology and Immunology is the core course of the biomedical science major and plays an important role in medical courses. Medical microbiology mainly elaborates on the general biological characteristics, life activity patterns, pathogenicity and immunity, laboratory testing methods, and prevention and control principles of pathogenic microorganisms. Medical immunology mainly introduces the organizational structure, physiological functions, immune response patterns, immune response products of the human immune system, as well as the immunological pathogenesis, immunological diagnosis and prevention of certain diseases. This course aims to enable students to master the basic theories, knowledge and skills of microbiology and immunology, to explain common immune phenomena and the pathogenesis, laboratory tests, and specific prevention and treatment of immune diseases and infectious diseases, to deepen the understanding of the interrelationships between pathogenic microorganisms and the human body and environment, to establish a sterile concept and apply it specifically in disinfection, isolation, and prevention of hospital infections, to lay a solid foundation for learning other related courses.
