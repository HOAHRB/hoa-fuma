    en: Differential geometry is a mathematical discipline which uses the techniques of calculus to study problems in geometry. The first subject of differential geometry is to study curves and surfaces in the 3-dimensial Euclidean space developing from the beginning of the establishment of Calculus which is systematized by Gauss now often called the classical differential geometry. Gauss also found the intrinsic geometry. Roughly speaking, he found some properties of 2-dimensial surfaces independent of 3-dimensial Euclidean space so that we can study surfaces without the introduction of 3-dimensial Euclidean space. Riemann develop this theory to curved space of higher dimension which is called Riemannian geometry later. With the promotion of many mathematicians such as Riemann, Ricci and Levi-Civita, the concepts of manifold, tensor, connection and curvature are established which is the beginning of modern theory of differential geometry. The course is for senior undergraduates which is focused on the classical differential geometry whose purpose is to study curves and surfaces embedding in the 3-dimensial Euclidean space using the techniques of calculus. The main parts of the course include the theory of curves and surfaces. Besides, we also lead the students to explore the properties of hypersurfaces embedding in Euclidean spaces of higher dimension and the concept of differentiable manifold. Our aim is to make the students master the main idea of using techniques of calculus to study geometric problems, understand the basic contents of the theory of curves and surfaces. We also want to arouse the interests of students and make them realize the beauty of geometry. Furthermore, this course is also a guide to Riemannian geometry and modern differential geometry.
