Numerical calculation has become the third basic method of scientific research, along with theoretical method and experimental method. This course introduces the method of discretizing continuous mathematical models and obtaining numerical approximate solutions in finite steps through computer programs. Through a series of experiments to help students to master basic error analysis method, solving the nonlinear equation (system) and the method of linear equations, using interpolation and approximation to calculate the function value, continuous function square approximation, computing the approximate definite integral, as well as the method for solving initial boundary value problems of differential equations, etc. Through the study, students will master the basic theory and skills of classical algorithms, and be able to apply them flexibly to solve practical problems.
