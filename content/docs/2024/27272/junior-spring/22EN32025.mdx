The selection and organization of content aims at describing the basic principles of nanoscience, structure preparation, characterization of properties and applications in interface, materials, catalysis and environmental sciences through nanochemistry. The course follows the keywords of bottom-up orderly self-assembly and patterned nanostructure preparation, and shows various applications, especially from environmental science. It introduces totally nine parts, which includes the development history of nanomaterials, chemical patterning and soft printing, layer-by-layer self-assembly, nanoprinting, preparation and application of typical one-dimensional materials, synthesis and application of nanocrystals, growth and regulation of microporous and mesoporous materials, polymer self-assembly, bioinspiration and bionics. The course provides a broad, interdisciplinary learning experience, students therefore can get the ability of forward-looking, scientific consideration in the fields of nanomaterials, nanostructures, nano-characterization and nanodevices, so as to make students have better potential in the future.
