    en: '"Development and Practice of Artificial Intelligence Software" mainly focuses on experiments. Its objective is to cultivate students'' thinking in data automation, mining, and utilization, as well as optimization of artificial intelligence algorithms. It aims to develop students'' abilities in big data analysis and processing, designing and implementing artificial intelligence algorithm programs, and utilizing open-source tools for data analysis, processing, and algorithm optimization. The course also aims to enhance students'' skills in acquiring information, conducting research, self-learning, and self-improvement through modern information technology. The course objectives include understanding the data-driven nature of artificial intelligence, comprehending the relationships between data, knowledge, and algorithms, and recognizing the impact of data scale and quality on artificial intelligence software. Students will gain an understanding of the key steps in the development of artificial intelligence algorithms and software, as well as the influence of application and iterative development processes on algorithm and software performance. The teaching methodology for this course adopts a combination of "experimental teaching" and "flipped classroom." In the experimental teaching approach, the teacher provides relevant experimental materials and operation guidance in advance. Students familiarize themselves with the experimental content and procedures through reading and researching. In the classroom, students perform the experiments while the teacher provides necessary guidance and support. Through independent exploration and practice, students consolidate and apply their theoretical knowledge, develop problem-solving skills, and gain practical experience. The teacher helps students understand experimental phenomena, analyze experimental results, and answer questions encountered during the experiments. Throughout the teaching process, student presentations, discussions, debates, and other interactive activities are considered as elements of the flipped classroom. This teaching approach allows students to actively learn and think through practical exercises, promotes understanding of artificial intelligence concepts, and enhances their comprehensive and practical application abilities. Meanwhile, teachers can pay more attention to students'' practical operations and thought processes, providing timely guidance and feedback.'
