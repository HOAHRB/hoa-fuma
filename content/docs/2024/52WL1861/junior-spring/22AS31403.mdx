The course of Smart Soft Matter Materials and Devices mainly teaches the basic concepts, development status and trends of soft matters, and introduces in detail typical soft matters such as electroactive polymers, shape memory polymers, gels, liquid crystal elastomers, electromagnetic rheological material, and their applications in the devices.
