This course is also the first course for IE undergraduate. The objectives of this course include the following three aspects: (1) To establish the Industrial engineering consciousness of “Continuous Improvement”, to master the basic knowledge of work study and worksite management, and be able to forecast and evaluate the production system and the service system. (2) To possess the ability to carry out research on the planning, implementation and control methods of business operation of the management personnel of the production system and the service system, so as to effectively utilize manpower, materials and economic resources, ensure the efficient operation of the production system and the service system. This course mainly teaches the basic concepts of industrial engineering, the classic content of the method study (including program analysis, operation analysis and motion analysis), operation measurement method (including time study by stopwatch, work sampling, Predetermined time System, standard data, etc.), the worksite management method, and the development trend of industrial engineering.
