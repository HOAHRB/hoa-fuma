It facilitates the students to lay a good theoretical foundation of quantitative analysis for the successive courses. The course covers the main branches of operations research, such as linear programming principle and method, dual theory, transportation problem, integer programming and 0-1 programming, goal programming, graph and network analysis, dynamic programming, etc. This course is oriented to the undergraduate students in Industrial Engineering by means of analyzing models mainly. Through the study of this course, the students are aimed to master some primary theories and methods of operations research and are taught to apply them to solve the practical problems in industrial processes. It also enables the students to understand the concept of global optimization and the capability of systematical analysis via the introduction of the principles and the explanation of algorithms as well as the case study. And some practical software such as LINDO and EXCEL are also inducted to provide the students with tools for model solution and result analysis, which help them to learn for application. This course will lay a foundation for students to learn the other courses of industrial engineering.
