    en: Mathematical Foundations of Computer Science (B) contains tropical (idempotent) mathematics, which is a rapidly growing branch of mathematics. It deals with the theory and applications of algebraic systems with idempotent operations. The application of tropical mathematics allows various problems, which are nonlinear in the ordinary sense, to be transformed into linear problems in tropical algebra, and hence facilitates the formal analysis and simplifies the derivation of solution. Tropical mathematics utilizes basic mathematical concepts but exploits different ways to handle problems than the conventional mathematics. It offers students opportunities to see both how basic concepts of conventional mathematics may work in a different setting and how to solve classical and novel applied problems by using new mathematical language and methods. As a result, it is helpful to train students to obtain innovation ability.
