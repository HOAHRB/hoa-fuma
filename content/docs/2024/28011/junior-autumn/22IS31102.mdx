Through this course, students will acquire essential knowledge of automatic control components, including the structural characteristics and working principles of components involved in execution, amplification, and measurement within automatic control systems, and applying them in engineering practice. The teaching process emphasizes the depth of professional knowledge and the organic integration of theory with practical applications, so as to lay a solid foundation for theoretical research and engineering design in the future.
