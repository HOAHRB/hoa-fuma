    en: Business Data Analytics and Advanced Quantitative Methods is a 1-credit (16-hour) elective course offered by the School of Economics and Management at Harbin Institute of Technology. The course is jointly delivered by Associate Professor Yi Li from the University of Sydney Business School and Professor Wei Liu from HIT. Adopting a bilingual teaching model, this course aims to equip students with a systematic understanding of business data analytics and advanced quantitative research methods, cultivating the ability to make data-driven decisions in both academic research and business practice. The curriculum is structured into two interconnected modules using a "foundations + frontiers" approach. The first half, taught by the HIT faculty, covers the fundamentals, including course introduction and data analytics basics, regression analysis and model diagnostics, panel data and time series analysis, and a hands-on business data analytics project to consolidate practical skills. The second half, delivered in English by the overseas expert, focuses on cutting-edge quantitative methods, including textual analysis and natural language processing, sentiment analysis and topic modeling, machine learning fundamentals and classification/prediction, and qualitative comparative analysis (fsQCA). The course emphasizes the deep integration of theory and practice through case-based teaching, software labs, and project-driven learning. Using Python and Stata as the primary analytical tools, students will complete the full workflow from data cleaning and model building to result interpretation. By the end of the course, students will not only grasp the principles and appropriate application scenarios of core methods—such as descriptive and inferential statistics, regression analysis, panel data models, textual analysis, machine learning, and fsQCA—but also be proficient in independently conducting business data analytics projects or empirical analysis for academic research. Assessment is based on a group project report, which emphasizes problem definition, data acquisition, method selection, result interpretation, and report writing, while also highlighting the academic norm of code reproducibility. This course serves both to prepare students for graduate studies and to meet the practical needs of data analytics roles in industry, making it highly relevant and applicable to contemporary developments.
