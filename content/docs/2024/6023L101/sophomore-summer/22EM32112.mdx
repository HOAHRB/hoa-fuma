    en: 'Global Strategic Management in the New World Economic Landscape is a 1-credit (16-hour) elective course offered by the School of Management at Harbin Institute of Technology. The course is jointly delivered by Senior Lecturer (Associate Professor) Wen Helena Li from the UTS Business School, University of Technology Sydney, and Professor Wei Liu from Harbin Institute of Technology. It adopts a bilingual teaching model designed to provide undergraduates with a systematic introduction to the core theories and cutting-edge practices of global strategic management, equipping students with the logic and methods needed for formulating and implementing competitive strategies in a complex international environment. The curriculum focuses closely on the evolving world economic landscape and consists of four key modules: (1) Globalization and Global Strategy, analysing how macro forces like geopolitics, economic policies, and technological change reshape the fundamentals of global business; (2) Industry Competition and Core Competence, teaching how to assess international industry dynamics and build sustainable competitive advantages; (3) Institutional and Governance Focus, exploring differences in political, legal, and cultural institutions across countries, as well as corporate governance and compliance challenges in multinational operations; and (4) Frontier Practices in Global Strategy, addressing the internationalization strategies of emerging-economy firms (especially Chinese firms), global value chain configuration, strategic alliances, and corporate social responsibility. The course adopts an innovative “dual-expert relay teaching” format. The overseas expert leads the first half, delivering lectures in English on theoretical foundations such as globalization, industry competition, core competence, and institutional focus. The HIT faculty member then takes over the second half, integrating both Chinese and global business practices to cover foreign market analysis, strategic choices, multinational corporate structure and governance, and global social responsibility. Through case studies, interactive seminars, and group presentations, students critically examine frontier issues in global strategic management, with a strong emphasis on developing strategic thinking, cross-cultural understanding, and decision-making acumen. The final assessment is a group presentation, designed to evaluate students’ ability to comprehensively apply analytical tools and demonstrate effective teamwork. Upon completion of the course, students will have mastered the analytical frameworks of global strategic management and developed a broad international perspective, laying a solid foundation for future careers in multinational management, consulting, finance, and other related fields.'
