This course is given for students majored in bridge engineering and its main objective is to build basic concept of finite element method and make students initially achieve abilities of finite element analysis on bridge structures. In this course, with plenty formulas deviating, many concepts are introduced to students, including direct stiffness method, shape function and displacement shape, energy method and etc. Stability of bridge structure is specially discussed subsequently after fundamental concepts learning. With this course learning, the students are supposed to build fundamental background for actual bridge engineering structural analysis in the future.
