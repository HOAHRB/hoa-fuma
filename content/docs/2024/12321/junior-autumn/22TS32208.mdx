It aims to cultivate students'' comprehensive ability to solve practical problems in complex engineering practices by deeply integrating artificial intelligence technology with traditional structural design. The course takes the design of reinforced concrete structures as its core foundation and systematically integrates AI technology, breaking through the limitations of traditional design models that rely on experience-based trial and error and repetitive labor. It promotes an all-round improvement of students from theoretical cognition to practical innovation. The course lasts for a total of 32 class hours, covering the basic principles of structural design and the application logic of AI technology. The course content is divided into two core parts: basic theoretical learning and AI technology application. Basic theoretical learning focuses on the core knowledge system of reinforced concrete structure design, including limit state design methods, structural reliability analysis, bearing capacity and normal service limit state, as well as the mechanical properties of concrete and steel. Through in-depth explanations of the calculation of the bearing capacity of flexural members'' normal and inclined sections, the composite force analysis of compression and torsion members, and the modeling principle of the tension-compression bar model in deep beam design, students can systematically master the basic logic and normative requirements of structural design. On this basis, AI is introduced to explore the underlying logic and implementation paths of AI in structural design, including the development of AI-assisted programming tools, the construction of automated design processes, the simulation and optimization of complex force scenarios, and the application of virtual simulation experiments in the analysis of structural failure modes. By embedding AI technology into the entire design process, students are guided to explore the potential of intelligent algorithms in load combination optimization, parameter sensitivity analysis, and engineering problem diagnosis, cultivating their dual capabilities in technology development and engineering practice.'
