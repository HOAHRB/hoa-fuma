    en: Philosophy of Structural Design （2）A in prestressed concrete members as a fundamental object of study, combining the current specification, systematically introduces the basic principles and methods of the design of prestressed concrete structure, focused on the calculation of prestress losses, prestressed concrete structure persistent situation ultimate limit states and serviceability limit states, persistent and short situations stress checking of components. Among them, the prestress losses includes six items, that is loss of the friction, loss of the anchorage deformation, loss of elastic compression of concrete, loss of stress relaxation, loss of creep and shrinkage of concrete; for flexural members, the calculation of persistent situation ultimate limit states includes the normal section bearing capacity and inclined section bearing capacity, the calculation of persistent situation serviceability limit states includes the calculation of cracking resistance, crack width (partial prestressed concrete class B component) and deformations. The stress checking includes the stress checking of fully prestressed concrete in the use stag, partial prestressed concrete class A component in the use stage, partial prestressed concrete class B component after cracking, short situation of flexural prestressed concrete. Finally, a case study of prestressed simply supported beam, the design of main contents and calculated steps of prestressed concrete structure are described in detail, including the design of cross-sectional, reinforcement design, stress checking and calculation of bearing capacity in various states.
