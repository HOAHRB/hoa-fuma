    en: This course is a specialized course for the students of road and bridge engineering major. This course gives laboratory classes about replication experiments including beating experiment, unlimited compressive strength experiment, cement content of cement-stabilized materials and design of pervious concrete. This course adheres to the principle of student-centered approach. The teachers only provides the necessary explanations, and the students complete all the experimental projects by themselves. This course includes both validation experiments and design experiments. Verification experiments can help students strengthen their theoretical knowledge, familiarize themselves with the operation of instruments and equipment, and cultivate good experimental habits; Design experiments can fully tap into students' initiation ability. This course gives a chance for student to use the theory and knowledge they have learnt, and also makes student understand the relationship among compose, structure and properties. Furthermore, this course is to enable students to understand the test method of road base and pervious materials and the operational approach of equipment. The course provides the basement for the study in graduate design phase and work in transportation and roads.
