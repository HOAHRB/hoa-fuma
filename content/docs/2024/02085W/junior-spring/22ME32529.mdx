Pneumatic technology has been innovatively applied in the field of robotics due to its unique characteristics of flexibility and gas pressure isotropy. For example, pneumatic humanoid arm cooperative robot, various kinds of pneumatic soft robot and robot hand, pneumatic exoskeleton and so on. This course mainly teaches the basic theories of gas thermodynamic properties and gas flow related to pneumatic robot technology, the principle, structure and characteristics of air source generation ,air processing element, actuator element and control element. The design and computer simulation methods of pneumatic circuit and control in the field of robotics are also taught with examples of pneumatic humanoid arm cooperative , pneumatic soft robot and pneumatic exoskeleton. Enable students to have a deeper understanding of the professional knowledge of pneumatic technology in the field of robotics, and master the methods of system analysis and design. Through the classroom teaching of this course, combined with the experimental operation, the required training in the field of pneumatic robot is carried out, cultivate students' ability of comprehensive analysis and design of pneumatic robot technology, as well as their ability to solve practical problems with basic theoretical knowledge and design methods, and enhance their awareness of innovation. It lays the foundation for students to further study the relevant professional courses and engage in the field of robotics in the future.
