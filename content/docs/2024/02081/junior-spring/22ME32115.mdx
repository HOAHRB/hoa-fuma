    en: Control Technology for Mechatronics Integration is a comprehensive technical foundational course with a strong theoretical and practical focus. The course, centered around mechanical equipment, introduces the composition and design of integrated mechatronics systems, as well as mathematical modeling and analysis methods, which are constructed from power electronics technology, hydraulic transmission technology, and computer control technology. From the perspective of integrated mechatronics systems, the course combines analysis of engineering examples to explain the design, analysis, and implementation processes of integrated mechatronics systems. The main content of the course includes fundamental knowledge of integrative technology of mechatronics systems, automatic control technology of leveling mechanisms of the paver, automatic control technology of bucket trajectory for hydraulic excavators, adjustable-frequency variable-speed technology for crane machinery, safety monitoring and collaborative operation technology for crane groups. The main purpose of this course is to train students to master the design process and analysis methods of integrated mechatronics systems, to enhance their ability to design and conduct comprehensive analysis of mechatronics systems by integrating knowledge from various disciplines, and to equip them with the elementary capability to solve practical engineering problems.
