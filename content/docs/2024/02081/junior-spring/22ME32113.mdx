It is a knowledge expansion course for innovative design of industrial products. The main content of this course includes two parts. The first part mainly teaches the aesthetic principles of industrial modeling design, the method of form composition, the basic principles and methods of product color design, and ergonomic design, so that students can initially master the performance techniques of modeling design. The second part mainly teaches various concepts and methods of innovative product design, including creative thinking, functional design ideas, system design ideas, commercialized design ideas, humanized design concepts, evaluation methods. This course comprehensively analyzes and systematically considers various elements of product design aesthetics, functions, technology, economy, and their interrelationships. It cultivates students' ability to industrial design and evaluate industrial products, broadens their design knowledge, enhances their innovation ability, and designs products that meet user, market, and environmental requirements from a scientific perspective.
