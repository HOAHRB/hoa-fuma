    en: This course will introduce some important numerical methods and theory in the field of numerical algebra. The direct and iterative methods for solving linear and nonlinear system of equations will be discussed. Construction procedure, stability analysis and computational accuracy of numerical schemes will be explained in detail. In particular, based on the knowledge of vector and matrix norms, the elimination methods, the triangular decomposition methods, the conjugate gradient method as well as the orthogonal diagonalization methods will be concerned for solving linear systems of equations. The construction and convergence judgment of Jacobi and Gauss-Seidel iterative methods will also be presented. For nonlinear equations and systems, The Picard iterative process and its convergence analysis will be briefly given. The Newton method and the convergence order at single and multiple roots will be illustrated in detail. After auditing the current course, students are expected to grasp classical numerical methods and to enhance the ability in solving practical engineering problems.
