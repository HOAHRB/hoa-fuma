(5) Knowing the state-of-the-art AI algorithms. The teaching content mainly includes (1) Introduction to machine learning. (2) Deep learning (BP, CNN and RNN). (3) Indexing for unstructured data. (4) Big Data Applications (e.g., multimedia big data applications). (5) Trustworthy computing in machine learning/deep learning. (6) Privacy concerns and classification over encryption images. This is an undergraduate level course designed for students who are interested in big data processing and machine learning applications. Students are required to actively participate in the class discussions, lab operations. The final grade of this course is based on the course projects, and the students may conduct the course projects individually or in a group of two students.'
