Prime number is the most important notion in number theory, and its study is one of the two main themes in number theory. The other theme is the study of polynomial equations for their integer solutions, which we call Diophantine equations. For example, the Fermat equation is one special kind. Modern number theory is a vast area in Mathematics and elementary number theory is one of its branches. Here “elementary” means the tools needed are elementary, not involving more advanced theories such as Complex Analysis, Abstract Algebra, Algebraic Geometry. There are three goals in this course: 1. The students should understand systematically the basic notions, basic theories and important events and basic methods; 2. The students should obtain various abilities on abstract thinking, experimenting and problem solving; 3. By connecting with current number theory development and mentioning the modern tools, lay the foundation for students’ interest in studying more advanced Mathematics courses and potential interest in learning then researching on number theory.'
