    en: Decision-making is the only way for us to change our future lives. Simon, a master of management, also said, "Management is decision-making." We make decisions every day. Some decisions are routine, such as choosing the TV programs you want to watch. Occasionally, we also make decisions that have a profound impact on ourselves and the people around us. Gaining decision-making ability is an extraordinary achievement. Although many principles of high-quality decision-making have been widely circulated for centuries, this issue has rarely been emphasized throughout our educational career. The important concept of this course is not the extension of the traditional quantitative management method, but the practical method of future decision-making application. The purpose of this course is to provide an opportunity to master this ability, so that you can take clear action when making any decision of concern. One of the biggest obstacles to gaining decision-making ability is that we think we are good at decision-making. However, it is easy to prove that even in relatively simple decision-making situations, when people look carefully, they will think that the decisions made at that time are unwise. The most challenging phenomenon we face in the decision-making process is uncertainty. Assuming that each plan we face when making decisions has a video that can show our future life in detail, then we can easily make decisions. Uncertainty is inseparable from all major decisions. We must learn to surf in the ocean of uncertainty instead of drowning in it. We must form a clear concept that "uncertainty is a guide to decision-making", that is, to achieve clarity of thinking. The most important difference in decision analysis is the difference between decision-making and the resulting results. Good decisions can produce bad results, and bad decisions can also produce good results. The quality of decision-making only depends on the quality of the thinking and analysis you use when making decisions. The amount of analysis applicable to a decision can be from almost zero to a huge amount of computer modeling. This course provides the basic principles, ideas and methods that must be mastered in order to achieve this goal. The course requires mastering the necessary calculations and the implementation method of the computer with the help of a common language.
