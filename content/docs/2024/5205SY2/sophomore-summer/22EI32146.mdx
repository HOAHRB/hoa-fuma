    en: 'As the deep integration of internet and intelligent technologies continues to reshape modern society, computer networks have become a fundamental infrastructure of the information age. Guided by the philosophy of "Internet + Education," this course adopts a blended approach combining theory and practice, aiming to help students establish a systematic knowledge framework of computer networks, thoroughly understand the fundamental principles of the five-layer protocol architecture and core protocols, and master the complete process of data encapsulation and transmission. The course content covers the comprehensive five-layer protocol architecture: foundational physical layer data transmission, data link layer frame encapsulation and error control, network layer IP protocol and routing algorithms, transport layer TCP and UDP mechanisms, and application layer protocols including HTTP, DNS, FTP, and SMTP. Through carefully designed coursework, students will gain an overall understanding of how the internet operates, comprehend the end-to-end data packet transmission process, and develop essential capabilities in network analysis. The course emphasizes a dual-track approach to capability development. Goal 1 focuses on building a thorough understanding of network architecture and core protocol principles, cultivating students'' ability to apply network knowledge to analyze practical problems. Goal 2 emphasizes literature retrieval and technical review writing, guiding students to track cutting-edge developments in intelligent networking—particularly reinforcement learning-based network design—by consulting international frontier research papers and writing technical review reports, thereby enhancing their synthesis skills and academic perspective. The teaching methodology integrates heuristic, interactive, and case-based approaches. Teachers guide students through progressively structured question chains, transitioning them from passive knowledge reception to active exploration, thereby building systematic logical thinking frameworks. Collaborative learning through group discussions and presentations nurtures teamwork skills and communication abilities. Assessment is diversified to comprehensively evaluate students'' depth of understanding and engineering practical capabilities.'
