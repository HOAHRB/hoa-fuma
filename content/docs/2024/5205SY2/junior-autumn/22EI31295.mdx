    en: “Fundamental of Statistical Signal Processing and Stochastic Process’’ is a major foundation course for students from the Academician Featured Class in the School of Future Technology who majors in electronic information. This course rebuilds the signal processing framework from the aspect of the probbility theory. It introduces the basic theory of random signals, analysis methods of random signals, Markov Processes and basic theory of signal detection and estimation. Specifically, the characteristics of random signals in the time domain and in the frequency domain are also discussed in this course. Taking the electronic technology as background, the applications of many analysis methods for random signals are provided. Properties and applications of narrowband random processes, which are commonly used in electronic systems, are discussed. The basic konwledge of Markov processes, which is widely used in random process theory, is also introduced. The basic theory of signal detection and estimation are also discussed. The purpose of this course is to enable students to have a more comprehensive understanding of signal theory, lay a solid foundation for random signals and for future learning of other courses in the field of electronics and communication. This course will also cultivate the capability of solving complex engineering problems, so as to adapt to the development of modern signal processing.
