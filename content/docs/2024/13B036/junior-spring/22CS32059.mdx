It equips students with an understanding of the fundamental principles of cutting-edge technologies such as Variational Autoencoders, Generative Adversarial Networks, and Diffusion Models. Students will learn the basic principles and implementation methods of natural language generation, visual content generation and editing, as well as multimodal generation. The course will also investigate the wide-ranging applications and latest advancements of AIGC technologies in fields such as scientific computing, the metaverse, and embodied intelligence. Additionally, it will guide students to consider the social responsibilities of AIGC technologies, fostering awareness of safe usage and ethical considerations. Through this course, students will master the basic concepts, technical frameworks, and research hotspots of AIGC, and become familiar with its practical applications in text, image, and audio generation. They will develop the ability to abstract complex real-world problems, apply AIGC technologies for problem-solving and analysis, and design, develop, and optimize related systems to address complex challenges. Furthermore, the course will cultivate students' innovative capabilities and global perspectives, laying a solid foundation for further studies in related fields or research in artificial intelligence content generation.
