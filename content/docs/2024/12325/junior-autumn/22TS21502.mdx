    en: The course Database Technology and Applications starts from the fundamentals of database, especially focuses on basic theories and knowledge, such as database system structure, relational model, relational algebra, database Normal Form system, ER model. The main parts of database management system are query optimal, parallel control, recovery and complete constraint rules. There are lots of examples, which are realized by SQL, among the explanations during lectures. The target of choosing SQL is to build the ability to use the state of art database management system. In the experimental part, it can be divided into two parts. One part is seminars. The topics are given by the lecturer and students finish the researche. The other part is experimental classes, in which students have to finish the database design and realization.
