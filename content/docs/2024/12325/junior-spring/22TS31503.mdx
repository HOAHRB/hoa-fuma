    en: Geographic information systems for transportation (GIS-T) are application-oriented GIS, which are interconnected geographic information systems, transportation network properties and transportation analysis model. By far, GIS-T applications cover much of the broad scope of transportation. Transportation analysts and decision makers are using GIS tools in infrastructure planning, design and management, public transit planning and operations, traffic analysis and control, transportation safety and analysis, environmental impacts assessment, hazards mitigation, and configuring and managing complex logistics systems. GIS-T has owned unique architecture and contents of technology. The contents of this course consist of two parts. In the first part, concepts of GIS-T, methods of data collecting, data models, data organizing, spatial analysis based on GIS are introduced, and in the second part, how GIS-T has been applied in transportation planning, intelligent transportation systems, environmental impacts assessment, hazards management and logistics systems are focused.
