    en: Genetics is the science about biological heredity and the rules of variation, and the basic of molecular biology and gene engineering, thus it is a required curriculum in the department of biology. Genetics systematically expounds the basic principles of heredity and variation, the basic methods and theories of genetic analysis, and the application of genetics in the prevention and cure of human genetic diseases and the breeding of agricultural animals and plants. The development of Genetics is comprised of two stages，the classical genetics and the modern genetics. It introduces important concepts concerning Mendel genetic analysis（law of segregation, law of independent assortment and law of linkage）, genetic analysis of tetrad, molecular mechanisms of genetic recombination, extranuclear inheritance, immunogenetics, genic mutation, chromosomal aberration, genetic analysis of quantitative character and population, genetic and human health，epigenetics are also provided in the course. The students should master the elementary knowledge and methods of genetic researches and they should also be able to apply the knowledge and methods in practice. They will have a cognition to some complicated vital phenomena which fits the objective rule, and get the message of new achievements in the modern genetics. Combining with traditional skills in the field of agriculture、medicine science and industry, the learner will build a strong foundation to study biotechnology, gene engineering and many other branches about genetics.
