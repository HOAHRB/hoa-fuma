    en: Plant Biology Experiments is an experimental course that fits the curriculum of plant biology。 Course includes plant morphological structure, plant photosynthesis, plant transpiration, plant hormones and physiological performance of plants in adversity. Structure is the basis of physiological activity. The experimental course puts the observation of the structure of the six major plant organs in the first part, which adopts the combination of teachers' lectures and students' direct operation and observation, so that students can gain an intuitive understanding of plant organs and their microstructure and function. On the basis of a comprehensive grasp of the structure and function of plant organs, the role of chloroplast, the basic structural unit of photosynthesis, in photosynthesis was analyzed through the extraction and activity determination experiments of chloroplasts, so as to deepen students' understanding of photosynthesis. Through the measurement of water potential and transpiration intensity of plant tissues, students can intuitively understand the water metabolism in plants. Through the determination of indoleacetic acid and indoleacetic acid oxidase, the physiological changes and performance of plants in adversity, so that students can understand the hormone regulation behavior in plants and the means of adaptation in the face of adversity, and experience the unique charm of plants, as one of the main life forms on the earth, their physiological activities and operating mechanisms. Through this series of courses, students could master the basic experimental skills and methods of plant biology and get basic training in experimental skills. Cultivate student scientific literacy, expand students' interdisciplinary horizons, and exercise students' comprehensive ability to find information, summarize problems and report.
