    en: Biochemistry is one of the most important basic courses of Life Science. It is the study of the chemical constitution of biological molecules and chemical reactions which occur in living cells and tissues. This course is divided into two semester to study. In the first semester, the main content is to be focused on the static biochemistry including chemical constitution, biological character and functions of the main biological molecules. The main content of the second semester of Biochemistry is to be focused on the dynamic biochemistry, including the material and energy metabolism, delivery of genetic information, and related regulation. By the study of the second semester, students should master the important metabolic pathways, the process and mechanism of energy production, the common process of delivery of genetic information, and regulation of gene expression. Through the study of this course, basic principles and thinking strategy of biochemistry could be delivered to the students, and make them be able to explain and address some biochemistry problems by using the rules of biochemistry theories and experimental skills.
