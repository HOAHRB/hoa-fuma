Biosafety is a knowledge that must learned in majors such as biology, contemporary medicine, public health, epidemiology, and more, and a preventive awareness that social groups need to enhance. This course focuses on the basic theories and research progress in important fields such as genetically modified organisms, human infectious diseases, laboratory biosafety, invasive organisms, food safety, pesticide safety, and biosafety industries, which are currently the most concerned by society. Through this course’ learning, students can enhance their awareness of biosafety, understand relevant laws and regulations on biosafety in China, and regulate their personal health behavior and cultivate a correct biosafety concept and knowledge system for future students engaged in related majors, and to cultivate good biosafety habits.
