Course Description Biochemistry and Bio-manufacturing is a specialized course for majors including bioengineering, biopharmaceuticals, Biology and Medicine, and synthetic biology. It integrates classic fundamental biochemistry theories with cutting-edge modern bio-manufacturing technologies, breaking down the barriers between basic life science principles and industrial applications. This course systematically expounds the structure, physicochemical properties and biological functions of biomacromolecules (proteins, nucleic acids, carbohydrates, and lipids), and illustrates substance metabolism, energy metabolism, as well as the transmission and regulation of genetic information in organisms, consolidating students’ theoretical foundation for analyzing life phenomena at the molecular level. On this basis, the course interfaces with the modern bio-manufacturing industrial system and focuses on core technologies such as biomolecular modification, metabolic engineering regulation, biocatalytic transformation, biomedical development, and functional biomaterial preparation. Combined with frontier research directions including synthetic biology, precision bio-manufacturing and biomedical formulation development, this course presents the latest advances in cutting-edge biological fields represented by protein drugs, nucleic acid preparations, biological nanomaterials, and targeted formulations. Adhering to the three-in-one teaching model of fundamental theory, engineering application and industrial frontier, this course aims to equip students with a comprehensive core knowledge system of biochemistry and an in-depth understanding of the underlying biochemical principles of bio-manufacturing, while familiarizing them with key technologies and application scenarios of modern bio-manufacturing. It cultivates students’ competence to analyze and resolve practical problems in the research, development and production processes of bio-manufacturing, thereby laying a solid theoretical and applied foundation for students’ future scientific research and industrial practice in biopharmaceutical R&D, biomaterial creation, and synthetic bio-manufacturing.'
