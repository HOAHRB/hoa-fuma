    en: With the demand of the living standard of people moving steadily upward, people need more and more agricultural production with good quality. In order to produce plenteous food for people’s health, the significance of the biological control theory, either in theory or in practice, was not ignored. Biological control put main emphasis on the use of living organisms, such as predators, parasitoids, and pathogens, to control pest insects, weeds, or diseases. It is one of the indispensable components in the integrated pest control. The purposes of this course are to help the students to master the history and the trend of the biological control, and its significance in the integrated pest control; to help the students to master the basic principles and methods of biological control, which can widen the sphere of their knowledge and train many specialists for biological control in order to arise the level of the biological control in our country. The contents of this course involved the definition, the contents, the significance, the development history, and the methods of biological control, the theory basis of using biological control, the main groups and characters of the parasitic and the natural predators, the main principles in introduction, reproduction, and release of the natural predators, the theory and practice of using pathogenic microbes to control the quantity of the pests, the introduction of the research methods on pathogenic microbes, such as the characters of the groups, the mechanism of causing diseases, the production and use of such microbe preparation, and, finally, the application of biotechnology in biological control.
