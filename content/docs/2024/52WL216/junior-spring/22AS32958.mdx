    en: Machine vision cameras are the core hardware of intelligent vision engineering. Through this course, students will systematically master the design, analysis, and assembly of machine vision cameras, providing them with practical tools, analytical methods, and reference techniques for the real-world application of machine vision in future research, experimentation, and production processes. This enables students to design vision cameras that are more practical, operational, and engineering-oriented. By simulating the effects of non-ideal states of camera components on imaging, students will gain an intuitive understanding of the necessity and importance of vision camera design. Starting from the error design of the camera's optical system, the course introduces visual errors and guides camera design through error allocation methods. Through the study of design software usage, students will acquire fundamental camera design techniques and data conversion methods between mechanical design software and optical design software. Numerical analysis of machine vision cameras will equip students with performance evaluation methods, along with corresponding analytical tools and techniques. Additionally, by learning optical system alignment and mechanism design, students will grasp the principles of visual camera alignment and assembly as well as practical mechanisms, offering them design insights and references for future practical work.
