Based on the needs of cutting-edge fields such as intelligent manufacturing and artificial intelligence, it aims to cultivate students' abilities in system design, engineering application and innovation. Closely integrated with the core technology system of intelligent vision systems, this course design covers key modules including image acquisition, preprocessing, feature extraction, target detection and recognition, and system integration. It integrates core knowledge points such as sensor technology, CCD/CMOS imaging principles, digital image processing algorithms and embedded vision systems, realizing in-depth integration of theory and practice. Adopting a hierarchical teaching mode of "theoretical guidance - practical operation - project implementation", the course design guides students to fully master the design process and implementation methods of intelligent vision systems from hardware selection (cameras, lenses, light sources, etc.), software programming (based on tools such as OpenCV and PyTorch) to system debugging. By simulating real application scenarios such as industrial inspection, target positioning and intelligent recognition, students will complete comprehensive design projects in groups, deepen their understanding of the cross-integration of machine vision and artificial intelligence in practice, and improve their abilities in problem analysis, team collaboration and engineering innovation. This course design lays a solid practical foundation for students to engage in research and development, application and maintenance in intelligent vision-related fields, helping them adapt to the demand for compound technical talents in the intelligent manufacturing industry.
