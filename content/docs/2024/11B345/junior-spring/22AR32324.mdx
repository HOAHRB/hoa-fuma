Combining new methods and technologies of urban science, this course analyzes urban space and deepens the understanding of urban development formation, urban planning and design. The purposeful comparison of urban morphology concept orientation, the complexity comparison of urban morphology paradigm differences, and the perceptual comparison of urban morphology spatial order are taught from political, economic, and cultural perspectives. Using quantitative methods such as spatial syntax, spatial economics, and central place theory, this course analyzes the characteristics of urban spatial logic, spatial enclosure, and spatial integrity, explores the essence and laws of the generation, development, combination, and transformation of urban spatial forms, and reveals the roles of corresponding social and political systems, economic development levels, and scientific and technological factors hidden behind them.
