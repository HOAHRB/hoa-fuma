    en: 'Site Engineering Design is an important design course for graduate students in the specialty of Urban Planning. Through this course, students could know how to make urban site design and outdoor environment embellishment design by using relative knowledge in courses of “Theory of Residential Quarter Design”, “Surveying” and “Site Engineering Technology”. The aim of this course is to train students to own abilities to complete outdoor site design independently, to know basic methods about outdoor environmental design and street furniture design, and to master knowledge about site engineering facility construction and detailed design. The content of Site Engineering Design includes: site design of a residential quarter, site engineering facility design and landscape architecture design. The main goals of this course are three aspects: ability to complete the vertical design of outdoor space independently; master the basic methods of exterior design, sketches and street furniture design; master the basic knowledge of the construction and node design of outdoor engineering facilities. The content of teaching includes design concept and planning scheme. design concept is designated to understand the terrain characteristics of the site, and reasonably determine the total layout of the site, and carry out the design of the outdoor engineering facilities. And planning scheme is referred to students use the theoretical knowledge to follow the different design stages of the results required to complete the drawing of the drawings and a brief design description. In terms of teaching method, classroom teaching will use multimedia teaching equipment to combine teaching and practical case analysis. According to the contents of each part of the teachings, put forward the practical issues related to the detailed planning and construction of the teaching content, and let think, discuss, and explain on the one hand to improve students'' interest in learning, and on the other hand can make students learn the theory and detailed planning and construction practice. In the process of building design, site design is critical. China has vast territory, with north-south terrain so obvious. But in the construction, site design principles are the same, to the greatest extent to maintain the original site terrain style, and reduce the capital investment of field engineering. The importance of site design in architectural design is manifested in three aspects: firstly, the site design ensures the balance of earthwork in the construction area. In the process of design, there is the need try best to maintain the original style and landscape environment in the construction site, and make appropriate amendments in the original terrain and geomorphology. In other words, it is necessary to design the site to maximize the construction area to ensure the balance of earthwork to ensure that the building can be carried out in a relatively flat environment for construction operations. Secondly, the vertical design of the site design directly affects the spatial composition of the building, needing fully take into account the terrain, geological and specific environmental characteristics, to weaken the difficulty of vertical design. Thirdly, Road design is also an important part of the design of the site, which directly affects the overall layout of the building and the construction function requirements. Environmental protection is the key and important concern of site design. The process of site design requires the use of the basic principles of architectural composition, showing a flexible and diverse sense of contrast, and natural integration, fully embodying the principle of harmony between man and nature, to play the site design in the maintenance of environmental principles in the positive role.'
