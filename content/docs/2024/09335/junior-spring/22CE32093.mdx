    en: In recent years, intelligent information technology has gradually become the innovation propulsion and key development direction in engineering fields, aiming to establish a synchronous twin between physical entities and digital objects. In structural engineering field, the demand for digital and intelligent transformation runs spans the entire project lifecycle, including design, construction, operation and maintenance, and becomes the core driving force to promote industrial upgrading and technological innovation. The new generation of artificial intelligence (AI) technology with machine learning and deep learning as the core has attracted close attention from researchers and engineers due to its excellent computing efficiency and wide applicability. These technologies can not only greatly improve the accuracy and efficiency of structural design, achieve intelligent management and quality control in the construction stage, but also realize accurate assessment and prediction of structural health status through real-time intelligent monitoring in the operation and maintenance stage, effectively extending the service life of the project and reducing cost. To improve the professional knowledge of students in the field of AI and engineering structure, this course adopts the new mode of "lecture–learn–application" to systematically guide students to explore the principles and practices of AI technologies such as machine learning, deep learning and computer vision, and to help students get started with AI programming and understand the application of AI in engineering. This course will provide an in-depth introduction to AI methods and the application of AI in engineering.
