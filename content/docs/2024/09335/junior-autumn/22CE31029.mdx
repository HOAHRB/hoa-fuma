Through the study of macroeconomics, students will master the basic principles and analytical methods of macroeconomics, be familiar with fiscal and monetary policies, seek a balance between long-term stable economic growth, fairness and efficiency, and understand that various national policies are based on economic background. The goal is to cultivate students' observation and analytical skills of real economic behavior and economic phenomena, and train them to have economic awareness and thinking.
