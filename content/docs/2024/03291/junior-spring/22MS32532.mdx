    zh: 课程主要讲解以激光为工具在工业界中常用的几种激光制造技术相关基础知识与工业应用特点。课程开始，首先介绍激光物理相关的必备知识，如激光的理特性、激光器的工作原理与分类、激光操作安全知识等；以及激光加工中的专业基础知识，包括激光热源特性、激光与材料的相互作用、激光加工过程的传热传质特性、材料的快速凝固行为等。进一步，将分别介绍激光连接、激光表面处理、激光切割、激光烧蚀与钻孔、激光增材制造、超快激光微纳加工等几种激光加工技术的激光特性、工艺特点、物理现象及控制方法等。根据方法的不同，有针对性地分析不同工艺方法特有的专业知识、相关设备及工业应用情况，让学生更全面了解激光制造技术中光源、工艺、材料的重要性及其对加工质量的影响规律，掌握当前激光制造技术在工业领域的最新应用动态。最后，以实例介绍高功率激光在汽车、模具、造船等行业中用到的典型激光制造技术，讲解不同激光制造工艺在结构件生产中的考虑因素，对激光加工技术的工业应用潜力与发展趋势进行综合的分析与讨论。
    en: The course mainly explains the basic knowledge and industrial application of several laser manufacturing technologies. At the beginning of the course, we will introduce the necessary knowledge of laser physics, such as laser physic characteristics, laser operating principle, laser classification and laser safety, etc. as well as the basic knowledge of laser processing, including laser heat source characteristics, interaction between laser and material, heat and mass transfer of laser processing, rapid solidification of materials, etc. Further, the laser processing characteristics, physical phenomena and control methods of several laser technologies are introduced, including laser joining, laser surface treatment, laser cutting, laser ablation and drilling, laser additive manufacturing, Ultrafast laser micro-nano processing and so on. According to different laser processing method, the relevant professional knowledge, equipment and industrial application situation are targeted analysis, which to help student comprehensive understanding of the importance influence of laser source, processing and materials on the laser manufacturing quality. Finally, the examples of high laser manufacturing in the automotive, mould, shipbuilding industry are introduced comprehensively, the interpretation of the different considerations for the different laser manufacturing technology in the real production. Based on this, the potential industrial applications and the development tendency of laser manufacturing technology are analyzed and discussed.
