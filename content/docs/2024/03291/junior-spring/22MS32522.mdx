    en: Nondestructive characterization is the necessary technique to realize the inspection of welding quality and the structural health inspection of welding structures, among which acoustic based nondestructive characterization is the most widely used method. This course introduces nondestructive testing methods for welding defects, including It includes ultrasonic phased array method, ultrasonic TOFD method, ultrasonic guided wave detection method, laser ultrasound, electromagnetic ultrasound, acoustic emission and nonlinear ultrasonic technology. Through the course, students are required to master all kinds of detection and monitoring methods based on acoustics, especial for the principle and technical characteristics. The students can choose the appropriate test methods for characterize defects or damage state for welding with different structure. Using of modern detection technology, the students can design and analysis the quality of manufacturing. Nondestructive characterization methods have different applicability and technological limitations. Through this course study, students are required to understand the technical drawbacks of the methods. Moreover, students are required to master certain methods to solve the technical drawbacks. Using literature retrieval tools, students are required to know the current states and progress of nondestructive characterization of weld.
