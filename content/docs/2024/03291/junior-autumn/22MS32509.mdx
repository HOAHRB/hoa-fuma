    zh: 近十多年快速发展的芯片、航空航天和先进材料领域对表面技术提出了很高的需求，《先进材料表面改性》是针对该需求而开设的选修课程，使本科生能够对表面技术应用领域需求有较深的了解，掌握先进表面强化的相关知识，拓宽知识面。本课程主要讲述气体分子运动理论、等离子体放电原理、表面改性、薄膜技术、涂层技术、复合技术、强化层性能测试等相关技术领域的知识体系，着重从物理上讲清各种技术原理、材料强化机制、相关设备和优缺点以及应用领域，使学生掌握先进表面改性技术的选用原则，为将来的科研与工作拟解决的问题提供思路，并夯实相关物理基础。除了正常的课程讲授之外，本课程要求学生组成小组，针对当前先进表面强化技术的热点方向，对其材料特征、发展趋势及制备方法开展项目研究，进行研究报告撰写和答辩，从而培养学生的自主学习、团队协作以及表达与沟通能力。本课程最终成绩评定主要由三部分组成，其中，平时作业占20%，项目报告占30%，期末考试占50%。
    en: The course “Advanced Surface Modification Methods for Materials” mainly focuses on the knowledge of the fast development of surface engineering technologies recently. It will build the bases of the knowledge for surface engineer and expand the scope of the student. The contents of this curriculum include the molecular dynamics, gas discharge, plasma, surface modification, thin film deposition, coating technology, duplex processes for surface enhancement and characterization methods for surface properties. In addition, the principle of different surface modification methods and surface enhancement mechanisms will be discussed. After the curriculum, the students will understand the applications, disadvantages and advantages of different surface modification methods. In addition to lectures, the students will be divided to different groups, and each group will perform a project study on specific materials in advances of surface engineering applications. Each group will write a paper and give a presentation on the properties, development and processing method of this material. Therefore, the capability on self-study, team spirit, expression and communication will be development through this project study. The score of this curriculum is composed of exercise (20%), project study (30%) and final exam (50%).
