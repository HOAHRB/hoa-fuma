    en: The macromechanics, physical, and chemical properties of materials are determined by their microscopic morphology, crystal structure, and microchemical composition. “Electronic Micro-Analysis” reveals information about a material's morphology, structure, and micro-area composition through the interaction of electrons with the material. This course introduces the structures and principles of large electron microscopes, including transmission electron microscopes, scanning electron microscopes, and electron probes. Through classroom teaching and experiments, students gain a preliminary understanding of electron diffraction phase analysis, interpretation of electron micrographs, scanning electron microscopic analysis techniques, and spectrum analysis techniques. This course equips students to apply electron microscopic analysis techniques for scientific research, addressing practical engineering problems by examining the micro-mechanisms behind the service conditions and failure phenomena of various components.
