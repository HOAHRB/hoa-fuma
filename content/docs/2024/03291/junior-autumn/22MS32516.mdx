    en: Experiment design and data processing is a branch of natural science research methodology. It provides not only the correct methods of scientific experimental design and data collection, but also the methods about how to disposing and analyzing data and making objective and scientific conclusions. The correct methods for experiment design and the statistical analysis of experimental results are the basic training which every research workers must have. This course mainly includes the statistical analysis principle and method of experimental result, such as error theory and determination results expressions, statistical inference and significance testing and common experiment design principle and method. The experiment design methods mainly include orthogonal experimental design, uniform experimental design, optimized experimental design and regression orthogonal experimental design. Through the study of this course, the students can understand the basic principle and characteristic of experiment design and data processing methods, grasp the knowledge, methodology and technique in experimental design and data processing and use experimental data to make scientific conclusions. All of these would built a sound foundation for students’ theses and their future scientific research.
