    en: Welding quality testing and evaluation technology is an important means to control the quality of welding product and ensure the safe operation of the equipment. Non-destructive testing technology is increasingly showing its importance and necessity in the quality assurance system, and it becomes an important means to control the quality of products and ensure the safe operation of in-service equipment. The important role of welding quality testing depends on the selection of the test method and the reliability of the test results. In the welding quality inspection and evaluation system, different detection methods have different applicability and technological limitations. Through this course, the students can understand the characteristics of the welding defects in different welding joints. The students can understand the technical principles of the five conventional welding nondestructive testing methods, as well the technological features, commonly used equipment and application objects. This focused on mastering the craft of the two methods, i.e. ultrasonic and X-ray based NDT methods. Meanwhile, the student will understand the strength and density testing methods, the commonly used destructive testing method. The course requires the students can choose the appropriate detection methods for different types and structure of the welded joints for testing and evaluation, and can develop non-destructive testing craft according to the selected method. Through the course study, the students should understand the scientific and technical problems existing in the non-destructive testing methods, and the research frontiers as well.
