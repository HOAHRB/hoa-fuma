    en: Laser welding has been recognized as “one of the most promising and potential welding technologies in the twenty-first century”. For now, laser welding technology has gradually matured, and widely used in national defense and every field of the national economy. Also, as an important welding method in welding field, it has a tendency to replace other traditional welding methods. This course is a compulsory course in welding specialty, and also a compositive one involved multidisciplinary knowledge, such as optics, electricity, hydromechanics, thermodynamics, metallurgy, material science, automatic control and so on. This course mainly includes the application characteristics of laser welding method in industry, basic physical characteristics of laser beams, the interaction between laser beam and materials and their heat transfer characteristics, the working principle of laser, laser welding characteristics of different metal materials, and various advanced methods based on laser welding. In the process of teaching basic knowledge, the problems existing in the actual welding process of laser beam and their solutions are mainly emphasized. And the related technologies at home and abroad are introduced in detail. Through the study of this course, the students can have a detailed understand of the application characteristic and application area of laser beam which has a high-energy density and good application prospect, master the basic characteristics of laser, the physical mechanism of the interaction between laser and metal materials, the basic principle of laser welding and the processing characteristics of different kinds of laser beams, and know the advanced laser beam welding process and its application in the world. Furthermore, based on a practice case, the students can choose suitable methods and techniques of laser beam welding to provide solutions for practical engineering problems.
