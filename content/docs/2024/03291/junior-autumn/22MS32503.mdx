    zh: 电阻焊是工件组合后通过电极施加压力，利用电流通过接头的接触面及邻近区域产生的电阻热进行焊接的方法，属压力焊。电阻焊过程的物理本质是利用焊接区本身的电阻热和大量塑性变形能量使两个分离表面的金属原子之间接近到晶格距离形成金属键，在结合面上产生足够量的共同晶粒而得到焊点、焊缝或对接接头。电阻焊是一种焊接质量稳定，生产效率高，易于实现机械化、自动化的连接方法，广泛应用在汽车、航空航天、电子、家用电器等领域。据统计，目前电阻焊方法已占整个焊接工作量的1/4左右，并有继续增加的趋势。 通过本课程的学习，使同学能较好的掌握电阻焊的基础理论，并结合常用金属材料及典型零件焊接特点分析，培养具有制定电阻焊工艺及处理有关实际生产问题的能力。
    en: Resistance welding is a method of welding the workpiece combination by applying pressure through the electrode and using the resistance heat generated by the current through the contact surface of the joint and the adjacent area, which belongs to pressure welding. The physical nature of resistance welding process is to use the resistance heat of the welding zone itself and a large amount of plastic deformation energy to make the metal atoms on the two separated surfaces close to the lattice distance to form a metal bond, and generate enough common grains on the bonding surface to obtain solder joints, welds or butt joints. Resistance welding is a kind of stable welding quality, high production efficiency, easy to realize mechanization, automation connection method, widely used in automotive, aerospace, electronics, household appliances and other fields. According to statistics, the current resistance welding method has accounted for about 1/4 of the whole welding workload, and there is a trend to continue to increase. Through the study of this course, students can master the basic theory of resistance welding well, and cultivate the ability to formulate resistance welding technology and deal with practical production problems by combining with the analysis of welding characteristics of common metal materials and typical parts.
