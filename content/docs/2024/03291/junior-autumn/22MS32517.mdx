    en: The course “Principle and Design of A Vacuum System” mainly focuses on the fast development of vacuum systems in materials science and engineering applications. It will build the bases of a vacuum system design and expand the scope of the student. The contents of this curriculum include basic theories for vacuum, materials used in vacuum, vacuum seal design, working principle of different vacuum pumps, vacuum gauges, leak detection methods of a vacuum chamber and vacuum system design. Through the course, the students can understand basic concepts of a vacuum system, obtain the rules for selecting proper vacuum pumps and master the methods for vacuum characterization and leak detection. Furthermore, the students will understand how to design a vacuum system. In addition to lectures, the students will be divided to different groups, and each group will perform a project study on a specific vacuum system design for materials science and engineering applications. Each group will write a paper and give a presentation on the applications, principle and design of this vacuum system. Therefore, the capability on self-study, team spirit, expression and communication will be development through this project study. The score of this curriculum is composed of exercise (20%), project study (30%) and final exam (50%).
