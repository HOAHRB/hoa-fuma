    en: Electron beam welding is an important welding method in the material processing field. Electron beam welding has received increasing attention in the defense and civilian industry due to its characterization of high energy density, high ratio of depth-to-width, maximum penetration with minimal distortion and inert atmosphere (vacuum). This course mainly focuses on explaining of the formation mechanism of electron beam, the interaction between electron beam and material based on the introducing of the working principle of electron beam emission system. The course includes the explaining of both working principle of electron beam welding equipment and the electron beam welding foundation. The principle of electronic optics, the structure and function of electron gun and power supply are the main content of “working principle of electron beam welding equipment” part. The energy conversion and transmission during the interaction the process between electron and material, the forces and flow behavior of the molten pool, and the vacuum electron beam welding process are explained in the “electron beam welding foundation” part. Electron beam generation mechanism, the working principle of electron beam welding equipment, the relationship between the electron beam and the material between the physical metallurgy are the focus of this course. Students are required to grasp electron beam generation mechanism, know the structure and function of the electron beam gun and power supply, understand the thermo-force-metallurgical behavior between materials and electron beam during electron beam welding process, and gain the knowledge of whole electron beam welding process. The course, in a total of 16 hours, uses a combination of traditional blackboard and multimedia teaching methods.
