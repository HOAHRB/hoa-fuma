    zh: 非平衡凝固材料制备技术（熔体旋淬，雾化沉积，水冷铜模吸铸等）在推动新材料研发的同时促进了传统材料的升级换代，是国民经济发展和国防建设所需关键材料的重要制备技术。同时，金属的增材制造（如激光熔覆沉积、电子束熔丝沉积等）也受到越来越多的关注。这些制造技术的一个核心问题就是熔体金属非平衡凝固行为。金属的非平衡凝固过程直接决定了成形过程中的应力应变和变形开裂，材料组织形成与演变规律，最终决定了零件的力学与物理性能。 本课程详尽介绍了包括非晶、准晶、微晶、纳米晶在内的非平衡疑固新型金属材料、非平衡定向凝固与组织超细化、熔体热处理与非平衡凝固的新进展，并结合授课教师自身在上述领域的研究成果和研究体会的介绍，将材料与技术、理论与实验、基础与前沿有机结合，便于学生对当今世界新材料研究前沿领域的了解和对相关知识的理解。
    en: Non-equilibrium solidification techniques (melt spinning, gas atomization, water-cooled copper die casting, etc.) promote the development of new materials as while as the upgrading of traditional materials. It is an important fabrication approach for key materials required for the development of the national economy and national defense construction. At the same time, metal additive manufacturing (e.g., Laser Metal Deposition, Electron Beam Free Form Fabrication, etc.) is also receiving more and more attention. A central issue in these fabrication techniques is the non-equilibrium solidification behavior of molten metals. The non-equilibrium solidification process of the metal directly determines the stress and cracking during the processes, the formation and evolution of microstructures, and ultimately the mechanical and physical properties of the part. This course provides a detailed introduction to the new materials for non-equilibrium solidification, including amorphous, quasi-crystalline, micro-crystalline and nanocrystalline materials. Based on the lecture’s own research results and experiences in these fields, we will facilitate the students' understanding of the frontier of new materials research in the world.
