    en: This course is designed for undergraduates in Intelligent Medical Engineering majors. The primary goal is to enable students to have a holistic understanding of various aspects of clinical medicine, including symptoms, diagnosis and treatment strategies, prognosis, and rehabilitation of major diseases. On this basis, students will develop their ability to identify challenges in clinical medicine based on their engineering background, and to independently find solutions and propose initial plans, through application examples in the field of medical and industrial integration. This course is divided to the basic section and disease section. The former section introduces the logical framework and basic knowledge of clinical medicine, covering physical diagnosis, auxiliary examination, common operations, and critical illness, utilizes case studies of medical-engineering integration to guide and inspire students to focus on entry points for solving medical problems with engineering approaches in their future studies. The latter section focuses on the relationship between disease pathophysiology, laboratory and other examinations, and disease diagnosis and treatment, covering common and frequently-occurring diseases in various clinical departments including internal medicine, surgery, gynecology, pediatrics, and infectious diseases, allowing students to gain a concrete understanding of clinical application scenarios. The current course aims to enhance students' sensitivity to solving practical problems in clinical medicine with an engineering background, and guide students to establish correct values and a strong sense of social responsibility.
