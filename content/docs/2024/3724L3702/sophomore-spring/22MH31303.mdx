    zh: 《系统生物学》旨在为生物科学、生物技术和生物工程专业的学生提供深入了解生命科学新研究领域的机会。与传统的“老”系统生物学不同，该课程将焦点放在后基因组时代的系统研究上，将分子生物学作为知识基石，超越传统的“还原论”和分析方法，强调“整体论”和综合方法的应用。课程全面涵盖了系统生物学的演变背景、基本概念和关键技术。将深入介绍生物系统的组成和相互关系，涉及基因组学、转录组学、蛋白质组学等多组学分析。课程注重实践操作，使学生了解基因组测序、蛋白质质谱等技术，并培养数据分析能力。大规模蛋白质相互作用研究、基因功能富集分析等内容深入剖析生物网络特性。通过实践操作介绍使用Cytoscape软件构建蛋白质互作网络，强调网络可视化和网络药理学的关键技能，课程将在分子生物学基础上，探讨生物体系的整体性和相互作用。
    en: Systems Biology is designed to provide biological sciences, biotechnology, and bioengineering students with an in-depth understanding of new research areas in life sciences. Unlike traditional systems biology, this course focuses on systems research in the post-genomic era by using molecular biology as the cornerstone of knowledge, moving beyond traditional "reductionist" and analytical approaches, and emphasizing the application of "holistic" and integrative methods. The course provides a comprehensive coverage of the evolutionary background, basic concepts and key techniques of systems biology. The composition and interrelationships of biological systems will be introduced in depth, involving genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics and other multi-omics analysis. The course focuses on hands-on practice, enabling students to understand techniques such as genome sequencing and protein mass spectrometry, and to develop the ability to analyze data. The characteristics of biological networks will be analyzed through large-scale protein interaction studies, gene function enrichment analysis and other contents. By introducing the use of Cytoscape software in constructing protein interaction networks through hands-on practice, and emphasizing the key skills of network visualization and network pharmacology, the course will explore the wholeness and interactions of biological systems on the basis of molecular biology.
