    en: Intelligent diagnostics is an interdisciplinary concept that integrates medicine, computer science, and mathematical and statistical analysis to play an intelligent role in the development of medical services. Literally, intelligent diagnostics refers to the integration of advanced technologies such as artificial intelligence into the original diagnostic methods, making the original human-based diagnosis more intelligent, standardized, and personalized. Similar to precision medicine, the essence of intelligent diagnostics lies in the use of intelligent algorithms based on various types of multi-source data. The unique feature of this course is that, unlike traditional diagnostics limited to consultation or imaging interpretation, intelligent diagnostics the entire process of medical services, involving decision-making in pre-diagnosis screening, diagnosis, and post-diagnosis recovery. It not only provides intelligent and precise decision-making solutions during the diagnosis but also achieves intelligent early warning before the diagnosis, achieving the effect of preventing diseases. It can also achieve better recovery and intelligent home health management after recovery, enabling the whole population to take charge of their health management. Therefore, this course builds upon the medical theoretical foundation of diagnostics, integrating advanced digital technologies to provide intelligent diagnostic decision-making for each resident throughout the entire process, life cycle, and all aspects, aiming to safeguard residents' health and contribute to the national effort in promoting health. Focused on the major strategic deployment of new medicine and medical education in China, this course offers a diversified learning model through a combination of theory and case studies, as well as online and offline methods. It is divided into three sections. The first section, "Theory of Intelligent Diagnostics," introduces the fundamental concepts of intelligent diagnostics. The second section, "Technologies of Intelligent Diagnostics," covers typical emerging core digital technologies such as artificial intelligence, big data, and the Internet of Things. The third section, "Applications of Intelligent Diagnostics," explores the applications of intelligent diagnostics in different fields like neuroscience, imaging, and disease monitoring. By presenting new diagnostic models and approaches under the guidance of intelligent technologies, this course aims to create a cutting-edge and high-level textbook on intelligent diagnostics, promoting the cultivation of a high-level interdisciplinary talent pool in new medicine and medical education, and supporting the construction of a strong manufacturing and technological nation.
