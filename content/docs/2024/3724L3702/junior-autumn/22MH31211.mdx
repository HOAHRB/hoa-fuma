    en: 'Internet-Based Healthcare and the Medical Internet of Things (IoMT) is a core course for junior undergraduates majoring in Intelligent Medical Engineering. Positioned at the intersection of medicine and engineering, it provides students with systematic theoretical foundations and practical skills in internet-based healthcare and IoMT. The course follows a “policy–technology–scenario–data–security” framework and adopts a modular, progressive design. It introduces fundamental concepts, system architectures, key technologies, and data processing methods, helping students build an integrated knowledge chain that connects technical principles with real-world applications. Aligned with the training objectives of the Intelligent Medical Engineering program, the 32-hour course combines systematic lectures with scenario-based exploration and addresses three core learning objectives: Foundational Knowledge – Through modules on Fundamentals of Internet-Based Healthcare, Smart Healthcare Ecosystems and Medical Big Data, and Concepts and Characteristics of Medical IoMT, students develop an understanding of the industry’s development trajectory, policy environment, and basic theories. They explore how internet-based healthcare reshapes traditional medical models, gain familiarity with healthcare information systems (HIS, LIS, EMR), and acquire skills in medical big data classification and basic processing. Technical Implementation – Focusing on IoMT Architecture and Key Technologies, Medical Sensors and Wearable Devices, Telemedicine and Smart Hospitals, Mobile Health Management, and IoMT Data Processing, students examine IoMT’s three-layer architecture (perception, network, application), wireless communication protocols (Bluetooth, ZigBee, NB-IoT), and data acquisition principles for medical sensors (e.g., ECG, blood pressure, oxygen saturation). They also analyze practical workflows in scenarios such as smart hospitals, remote consultation, and chronic disease management, equipping them with end-to-end implementation capabilities. Intelligence and Security – In modules on AI-Enabled IoMT and IoMT Information Security, students explore the advantages of AI–IoMT integration, ethical and privacy challenges, and IoMT security frameworks and technologies. This builds a comprehensive literacy that balances technological innovation with risk awareness. Throughout the course, real medical scenarios and data-driven case studies are emphasized. Students analyze examples such as smart hospital construction, telemedicine workflows, and wearable data projects. They are guided to examine the full lifecycle of medical data—from sensor-based perception → big data processing → intelligent applications such as AI-assisted diagnosis—to form a systematic understanding of the path from perception to application and from data to intelligence. By the end of the course, students achieve a systematic mastery of core knowledge and technologies in internet-based healthcare and IoMT, while also strengthening their interdisciplinary skills in analysis, scenario-based implementation, and problem-solving. This provides a solid theoretical and practical foundation for future work in smart healthcare R&D, IoMT device development, and health big data analytics.'
