As one of main courses, the course mainly introduce the structure, properties, characterization and preparation methods of organic compounds. It takes an important position in the courses of materials physics and helps students to learn the synthesis of materials and polymer. The contents of organic chemistry are the structure, naming, physical properties and chemical properties of fat hydrocarbon, aromatic hydrocarbon, organic halogen compounds, organic oxygen-containing compounds, the organic nitrogen compounds, and heterocyclic compounds. The basic principle of infrared spectrum and the characteristic absorption peak of organic compound, the basic principle of nuclear magnetic resonance and the chemical displacement of organic compounds are included in the course. Free radical reaction mechanism, electrophilic addition reaction mechanism, electrophilic substitution reaction mechanism and nucleophilic substitution mechanism are also introduced. The target of organic chemistry is to cultivate students to master the structure and properties of organic compounds and reaction mechanism, and to understand the separation, purification and identification of organic compounds, so that the students have engaged in research and development, production and testing of materials. At the same time, the students will have the ability to analyze and solve complex problems in materials engineering, structure design of organic compound, production process design and independent learning, which will make them adapt to the rapid development in the field of materials science.
