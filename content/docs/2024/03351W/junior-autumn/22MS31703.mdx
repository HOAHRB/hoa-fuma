The purpose of course is to make the students master the nature, influencing factors and testing principles and methods of physical properties, such as electricity, optical, magnetism, thermal and elastic as well as internal friction, and make reasonable analysis of engineering problems based on the fundamental principles of mathematics, physical chemistry and engineering science by using the physical properties of materials, identify and judge the key links in engineering problems.
