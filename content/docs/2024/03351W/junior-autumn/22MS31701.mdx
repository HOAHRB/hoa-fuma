    en: This course mainly includes materials thermodynamics and phase transformation principles for the undergraduates in the major of Materials Physics and Chemistry. The lecture refers to different aspects of phase transformation but centers on the thermodynamics, kinetics and crystallographic of solid-state phase transformation. The basic contents of the course include phase transformation and microstructure evolution during thermal equilibrium and non- equilibrium conditions, origin, characterization and classification of transformation, nucleation-growth-coarsening-formal theory of kinetics, typical nucleation-growth phase transformation in materials, the theory of Spinodal decomposition, the thermodynamics, kinetics and crystallographic of martensite transformation and the recent development of the transformation theory. The lecture focuses on the interpretation of physical nature and mathematical description of phase transformation, the factors controlling microstructure and connection between microstructure and property. The purpose aims to conduct students to understand microstructural features of materials more in-depth, to train the ability of scientific research methods and logical thinking ability of materials research, and to solve the practical problems in material science and engineering for their future studies.
