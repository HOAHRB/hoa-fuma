    en: This course will systematically teach all aspects involved in the design and development of the cube satellite which is popular in international aerospace universities. With the help of the Lilac student nano/micro satellite laboratory, it will enable students to understand and master the development process of Lilacsat-1, exercise their ability to solve problems independently and cooperate with teams, and train high-level aerospace professionals for our university. The course focuses on the development of nano/micro satellite, the overall satellite and the design methods of integrated electronics, power system, structure and thermal control, attitude and orbit control, TT&C communication, payload & application and other subsystems. In the process of experiment, the students are divided into 4-6 groups to undertake the design and development of a subsystem independently. Finally, the prototypes of each subsystem are integrated and tested to form a 2U cube satellite (Lilacsat-1) designed, processed, developed, debugged and tested by the students themselves. This course is open to students majoring in aeronautics and astronautics, mechanics, control, communications, electromechanics, microelectronics, thermal energy, etc.
