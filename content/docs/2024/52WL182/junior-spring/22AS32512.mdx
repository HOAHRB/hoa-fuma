The course is an important supplement for the required course “spacecraft orbit dynamics”. It is an important component for training the talents working on studies and engineering design in the field of orbit dynamics. The course is based on the classic book in orbit dynamics, i.e., An Introduction to the Mathematics and Methods of Astrodynamics, Battin R.H., 1999. The main contents include the fundamental problem and the fundamental mathematical method in orbit dynamics and their applications in orbit transfer and rendezvous missions. The purpose of the course is to train the undergraduates to grasp the fundamental method in the field of orbit dynamics and to apply this technique into orbit mission analysis and design. Moreover, we will train the undergraduates' innovative thinking, which may be used in their future scientific research. In addition, the research hot topics and the methods in orbit dynamics will be introduced in the course.
