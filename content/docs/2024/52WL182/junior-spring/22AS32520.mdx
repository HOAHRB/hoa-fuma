System engineering is a comprehensive engineering technology that rationally develops, operates and innovates a large-scale complex system, especially the ideas, theories, procedures and methods required for the management system, starting from the whole. This course mainly teaches the basic concepts and characteristics of systems and systems engineering, system engineering methodology, system model and modeling, system evaluation principles and common methods and system engineering application examples. The main task of this course is to cultivate students' concepts of systems engineering through the overview of basic concepts and basic theories of systems engineering, so that they can establish the basic ideas of unifying engineering technology, organizational management and engineering philosophy; To help students master the system engineering methodology including overall planning, careful organization, weighing and selection, proper aftercare and other engineering strategic awareness, the focus is to guide students to consciously apply the basic ideas of systems theory to the entire work process of dealing with a series of complex engineering problems such as aircraft development (including planning, pre-research, design, manufacturing, testing, use and aftercare processing).
