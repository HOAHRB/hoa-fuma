The course equips students with an understanding of the neural mechanisms underlying human information processing, the origins of its unique advantages, and the principles for developing brain-inspired computational models. Course Objective 1: To study the historical development and fundamental theories of cognitive neuroscience, as well as the basic physiological foundations of neuroscience, and to establish a framework for understanding information processing in the human brain—specifically, to become familiar with fundamental cognitive architectures such as perception, memory, language, and motor control, and how they integrate to produce adaptive behavior.
