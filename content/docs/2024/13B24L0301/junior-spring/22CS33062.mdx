    en: 'Comprehensive Artificial Intelligence Project Practice" is a core practical course designed for students who already possess a foundational theoretical understanding of artificial intelligence. This course aims to bridge the gap between traditional theoretical teaching and engineering application. Through a complete project lifecycle that closely mirrors real-world industry scenarios, it comprehensively enhances students'' hands-on capabilities, innovative thinking, and team collaboration spirit in the AI field. The core teaching methodology is "Project-Driven Learning." Students will work in small groups to tackle a complex problem with practical application background (e.g., an intelligent recommendation system, computer vision application, natural language processing task, or intelligent decision-making model). They will experience the entire project workflow firsthand: from problem definition, data collection and processing, and model selection and training, to system integration, development, and final deployment. This process is not only an in-depth application of core technologies like machine learning and deep learning but also a comprehensive test of software engineering and project management knowledge. The course content covers several key phases. First, project initiation and requirements analysis, cultivating the ability to translate vague needs into specific technical specifications. Second, the data engineering phase, where students master crucial preprocessing techniques like data cleaning, labeling, and augmentation through practice. Subsequently, the core model building and optimization stage requires students to flexibly apply learned algorithms and pursue optimal performance through experimental design and hyperparameter tuning. Finally, the course focuses on guiding students on how to package trained models into usable services or applications and explores practical aspects of production environments like model deployment, performance monitoring, and iterative maintenance. A key feature of this course is its highly comprehensive and cutting-edge nature. It serves not only as a "touchstone" for testing theoretical knowledge but also as a "training ground" for stimulating students'' innovative abilities. By solving real-world challenges, students will deeply understand the limitations of theoretical models and the various trade-offs inherent in engineering practice. Simultaneously, the course emphasizes teamwork, project management, documentation, and presentation skills, holistically shaping AI talent that meets industry demands. Upon completion, students will be capable of independently or collaboratively completing a moderately complex AI project. They will possess the strong execution skills needed to transform innovative ideas into practical solutions, laying a solid practical foundation for future careers in AI-related research, development, engineering roles, or further academic pursuits.'
