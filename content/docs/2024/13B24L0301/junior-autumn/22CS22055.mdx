    en: Natural Language Processing C is a core course for undergraduate students majoring in Artificial Intelligence. Natural language understanding and generation is one of the cutting-edge issue in the AI domain. This course summarizes the development of natural language processing technology, introducing the fundamental tasks of natural language processing, the basic concepts and key technologies of statistical language models as well as large language models. Meanwhile, it discusses NLP technology application areas such as text classification, sentiment analysis, machine translation, and dialogue systems. The objective of this course is to enable students to understand the basic principles of how computers process human language, master common methods for modeling and computing natural language via deep learning, pre-training strategies, and large language models. It serves to provide a technical foundation for students to design and develop natural language related AI applications in the future, while enhancing their abilities in formal thinking and computational modeling.
