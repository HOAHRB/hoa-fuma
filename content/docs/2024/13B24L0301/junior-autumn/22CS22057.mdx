    en: The course "Knowledge Representation and Reasoning C" is offered to third-year students majoring in QiangJi program. This course aims to introduce the basic theories, basic knowledge and basic techniques of knowledge representation and reasoning. Knowledge representation and reasoning are typical problems in the field of artificial intelligence. Information in the information world is organized through knowledge, so that computer systems can solve complex tasks. There are tremendous unstructured texts in the web, which contains lots of useful knowledge. This kind of knowledge can help improve the capacities of many artificial intelligent systems. This course then focuses on a series of topics including how to acquire knowledge from web, how to organize knowledge, and how to represent knowledge. Via telling students the techniques about name entity recognition, relation extraction, and attribute completion, the students know how to use machine to construct medium-scale knowledge graph, and simultaneously, via telling students the techniques about knowledge representation and knowledge searching, the students know the strategy of using knowledge graph. Besides, via the told techniques, the students also understand how to make machine organize, manage, and search knowledge graph, and how to use knowledge graph to improve the performance of other intelligent applications. In summary, this course aims at revealing the organizing mode, the searching strategy and the using method of knowledge.
