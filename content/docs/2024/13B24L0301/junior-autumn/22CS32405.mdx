    en: This course is a specialized core curriculum designed for undergraduates in the AI and Advanced Technology Leadership Program. It delves into the core theories and cutting-edge applications of 3D spatial computing. Keeping pace with technological evolution, the course deeply integrates advanced methodologies like deep learning to systematically construct a complete knowledge framework encompassing "Data Acquisition - Quality Optimization - Semantic Understanding - Photorealistic Representation." It aims to cultivate students' core competency in solving complex 3D problems within the field of artificial intelligence, laying a solid theoretical and technical foundation for future R&D in frontier areas such as AR/VR, autonomous driving, and intelligent robotics. Regarding knowledge objectives, the course guides students to master the principles of 3D coordinate systems, 3D data acquisition, and reconstruction. It provides an in-depth understanding of the application logic of deep learning in 3D data processing, covers quality enhancement methods for data like point clouds and meshes, and offers detailed explanations of core concepts behind state-of-the-art photorealistic representation techniques such as NeRF (Neural Radiance Fields) and 3D Gaussian Splatting. Students will learn not only isolated technical points but also how to integrate knowledge, understanding the intrinsic connections between various technical modules and their applications in real-world engineering scenarios. In terms of capability objectives, the course emphasizes hands-on practice and application. Through a series of practical projects, students will be able to independently implement low-level 3D data processing (e.g., coordinate transformation, basic 3D reconstruction) and high-level semantic tasks (e.g., 3D object classification, scene understanding). They will become proficient in using mainstream tools and libraries like Open3D and PyTorch3D, and gain the ability to integrate advanced deep learning models seamlessly into 3D data processing pipelines to tackle practical engineering challenges. For competency development, the course focuses on cultivating rigorous scientific thinking and an innovative mindset. Students will learn to analyze and solve complex problems in 3D spatial computing based on scientific principles, establishing a professional ethos that values precision and innovation in engineering practice. Furthermore, the course aims to inspire students' autonomous learning capability to track emerging technologies like Gaussian Splatting. Through team-based projects, it also fosters comprehensive qualities such as effective communication and collaboration within interdisciplinary teams to accomplish complex R&D tasks.
