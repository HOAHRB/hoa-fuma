    en: As an elective course for majors, Graphics and Virtual Reality serve as the theoretical foundation for many subsequent courses, such as Image Processing, Pattern Recognition, Multimedia Technology, and Computer Vision. The course aims to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the research content and application directions of computer graphics and virtual reality technology. Students will explore the relationship between computer graphics and related disciplines such as image processing, pattern recognition, and computational geometry. They will also acquire knowledge of fundamental graphics generation algorithms, graphics transformation and clipping, graphics modeling and representation, as well as realistic graphics generation algorithms, and stay informed about the latest developments in computer graphics. Throughout the course, students will deepen their understanding of fundamental graphics concepts by implementing algorithms and enhancing their ability to apply theory to practice. The course aims to cultivate students' ability to design and implement graphics generation, processing, and display algorithms using computers, while mastering the thought process, techniques, and methods of "problem analysis—algorithm design—programming implementation". This will lay a solid foundation for students to study other related courses and engage in research in the field of computer graphics and its applications in the future.
