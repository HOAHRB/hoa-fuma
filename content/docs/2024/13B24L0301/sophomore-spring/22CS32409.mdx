    en: This course aims to systematically elucidate the core theories and key technologies of multi-agent systems (MAS) and intelligent robots. The course enables students to master the basics of distributed decision-making, collaboration, communication, and game theory in multi-agent systems, while also understanding the core modules of perception, planning, and control in intelligent robots. It focuses on exploring how multi robot systems can serve as concrete manifestations and extensions of MAS. Cultivate students' ability to design and implement multi-agent/multi robot collaborative solutions. Through simulation and practical projects, students are trained to use reinforcement learning, consensus algorithms, and other technologies to solve complex problems such as task allocation, path planning, and cluster collaboration. Guide students to understand cutting-edge applications and development trends, stimulate innovative thinking, critically analyze the advantages and challenges of multi-agent systems (such as security and ethical issues), and lay a solid foundation for future scientific research or career development.
