The course will provide a rigorous mathematical framework for formal description of computing and algorithms, and initially explore the basic capabilities and limitations of computers, and introduce some basic concepts of computability theory and computational complexity theory. In addition, the concepts and methods learned in this course are widely used in many fields of computer science and technology, such as compilation principles, algorithm design and analysis, software correctness verification, natural language processing, and digital circuit design.
