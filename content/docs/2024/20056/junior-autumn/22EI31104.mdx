    en: This course focuses on the fundamental conceptions of information theory, the components of communication systems, and the basic principle of each component. The students should understand how to design a basic communication system. The main contents include seven sections. (1) First of all, some fundamental conceptions of communication systems are introduced in details, including the basic composition, classification, communication mode, and the key performance parameters. (2) The mathematical model and characteristics of different channels are then presented. (3) In the baseband signal transmission section, we will introduce pulse code modulation, spectral characteristics of digital baseband signal, and the impact of noise on the transmission performances. (4) Digital modulation theory is one of the most important issues, and different modulation schemes and their corresponding BER (bit error rate) performances are deduced and analyzed in details. (5) Some optimal detection criterions are also presented for digital communications, for example, maximum likelihood (ML) criterion. (6) Synchronization issue is also analyzed in this course, including carrier synchronization, bit synchronization and frame synchronization. (7) Some basic conceptions of channel coding are introduced, for example, the encoding and decoding processing of linear block codes.
