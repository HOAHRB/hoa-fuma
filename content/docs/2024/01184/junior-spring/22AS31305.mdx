    zh: 近年来，复合材料实验的方法受到了很大的重视。许多测定金属特性的实验方法，已用于测试纤维增强材料。但是，由于纤维增强复合材料的非均质性和各向异性，在把从金属材料实验技术中的实验方法直接用于测定复合材料特性之前，必须对它们进行认真仔细地审查。另外，复合材料的各种损伤不可避免，如何对典型损伤进行检测，特别是无损检测是当前复合材料领域的热点和难点。因此，本课程在理解与消化传统的实验方法基础上，重点讲解近代力学的研究过程中广泛采用一些新的实验手段和方法，使学生了解近代力学实验方法的发展前沿与分析评价手段。 本课程包括测试理论、制备工艺和测试操作三部分：理论部分主要内容包括一般原理和展望，试样准备，误差分析与实验数据处理，电测技术理论，复合材料力学性能实验方法（拉伸实验、压缩实验、剪切实验、弯曲实验、横向性能测试、层间断裂韧性、冲击实验和疲劳实验），无损检测方法和现代光学测试方法等；制备部分主要包括热压成型工艺和液体成型工艺；测试部分包括纤维增强复合材料拉伸实验、压缩实验和弯曲实验。
    en: 'Experimental methods for mechanical properties of composite materials have received much more attention in recent years with the development of fiber reinforced composite materials. Many experimental methods used in determination of metal characteristics have been adopted to test the mechanical properties of fiber reinforced materials. Owing to the heterogeneity and anisotropy of fiber reinforced composite materials, the experimental methods used in metal materials should be carefully checked to determine the valid of the experimental methods. In addition, all kinds of composite damages are unavoidable. How to detect the typical damage, especially in nondestructive testing, is currently a hot topic in the field of composite materials. Therefore, based on understanding and digesting the traditional experimental methods, this course focuses on some new experimental means and methods used in advanced composite materials, and enables students to understand the development frontier and analysis evaluation method of modern mechanical test methods. The main contents of this course include three parts: experimental theory and method, preparation technology and experiment operation. The contents of theoretical parts involve: general principles and perspectives, specimen preparation, error analysis and experimental data processing, electrical measuring technology, experimental method of the mechanical properties (tension, compression, shear, flexure, through-thickness testing, interlaminar fracture toughness, impact and damage tolerance, fatigue), nondestructive testing method and modern optical testing method. The contents of manufacture processes involve: hot pressing process and resin molding process. The contents of experiment operation involve: bending test, tension test and compression test of fiber reinforced composite plates.'
