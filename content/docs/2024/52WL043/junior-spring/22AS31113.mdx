    en: The Project Practices of Intelligent Control takes the intelligent detection and control link in the industrial and medical fields as the course object, and develops the experimental design content of the high integration of theory and practice around its detection, control and application. In theory, it covers the modeling and composition of intelligent inspection and control system, the performance analysis of control system, the theoretical methods and design of inspection and control system, and focuses on the design methods of visual intelligent inspection and control system. The practice of industrial projects includes defect detection, process detection and size measurement, while the practice of medical projects includes histomorphology recognition, lesion detection and disease diagnosis. In addition, both projects cover visual intelligent algorithms in deep learning, and both require continuous dynamic search of targets with the help of mechanical arms or sliding platforms during the detection process. Through the study of this course, students can master the basic theoretical knowledge of detection and control systems; can correctly analyze the performance of the system; have comprehensive theoretical knowledge to plan, design, and develop the overall system; master the basic principles and methods of intelligent detection algorithms and deep learning networks in intelligent and unmanned applications.
