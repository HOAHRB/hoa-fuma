The course breaks through the traditional hydraulic control framework, builds a closed-loop architecture of "deep reinforcement learning training-environmental interactive feedback-real-time strategy optimization", and overcomes core problems such as autonomous gait generation of complex terrain, high-precision torque distribution and adaptive adjustment of shock load through AI special training modules such as multi-degree-of-freedom collaborative training of foot-type robots, training of hydraulically driven nonlinear compensation models and iteration of dynamic load anti-disturbance strategies. Based on the AI training system of virtual and real fusion, students will master key technologies such as deep reinforcement learning reward function engineering, end-to-end training of neural network controllers, and multimodal perception data-driven strategy optimization, so as to realize AI policy transfer and dynamic behavior generalization from simulated environments to physical systems. The course takes AI training to empower the intelligent upgrade of hydraulic system as the core path, strengthens the autonomous decision-making under dynamic disturbance, the compression and deployment of nonlinear electromechanical control models, and the real-time optimization ability of 100-kilogram loads, and provides a full-stack AI training methodology and practical framework of "algorithm-control-execution" for the field of Intelligent Heavy-load Legged Robots.
