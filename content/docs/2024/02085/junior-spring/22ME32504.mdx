    en: Micro manipulation robot has the micrometer scale precision manipulation capability and is widely applied in fields such as wafer detection, micro device assembly, and cell batch injection. It is a common support technology in high-end equipment fields such as semiconductor processing, micro nano manufacturing, and life sciences. Piezoelectric actuation technology has outstanding advantages of high accuracy, fast response, and large force density, and has become a core technology in the field of micro manipulation robot technology. Piezoelectric micro manipulation robots have been widely studied. The course Piezoelectric actuation and micro manipulation robot is open to Undergraduate student of School of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, and is one of the elective courses in the major of mechanical and electronic engineering. This course mainly includes an overview of micro actuation technology, piezoelectric materials and the foundation of piezoelectric actuation, ultrasonic resonant piezoelectric actuation technology, non-resonant precision piezoelectric actuation technology, structural design of micro manipulation robots based on piezoelectric actuation, actuation principles, and typical applications. The focus of the course is to teach students to understand the piezoelectric actuation technology and micro manipulation robot systems, master the basic design ideas, application fields, and development directions of piezoelectric micro manipulation robots, broaden their academic horizons and innovative thinking, cultivate the ability of critical thinking in the forefront of robotics technology, as well as their capabilities to think and solve problems in interdisciplinary fields. This course can stimulate the research interest in this field, cultivate basic skills in bibliographic retrieval and academic report writing.
