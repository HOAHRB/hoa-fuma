    en: Computational theory is the theoretical fundamental of computer science, while computability theory and computational complexity theory are two of the most important topics in computational theory. Based on abstract computing model, for all kinds of problems in real applications, computability theory studies whether there are corresponding algorithms, and computational complexity theory mainly studies whether there are efficient algorithms. Nearly half a century, there have been many influential results in this area which offer solid fundamentals and important guidance for theoretical computer science research, computer system research and software development. This course mainly teaches the classical computability theory and computational complexity theory based on Turing model, and is an important theoretical course for students studying computer science. The goal of this course is to help students understand the essential ideas of computer science, to improve their abstract thinking and logical mind, and to teach them the basic methods in theoretical computer science. To encourage the interest of students in studying and investigating in theoretical computer science, the course is designed based not only on essential theory and methods but also on the most popular topics and results.
