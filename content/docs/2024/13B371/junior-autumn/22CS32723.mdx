This course is practice-intensive with significant constractive learning, coding, experiments, writing, and presenting. NVIDIA GPU/Huawei Ascend AI Processor and the CUDA/CANN programming tools are used in the practice section of the course. Many have reported success in performing general-purpose parallel computation as well as neural network based artificial intelligence on them. Each programming assignment will involve successively more sophisticated programming skills. The experiments will be of your own design, with the requirement that the project must involve a demanding application such as data-intensive computation, followed by some form of visualization and display of results.
