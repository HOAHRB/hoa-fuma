    en: Programming is a practical course that emphasizes practical skills. Although first-year college students have completed courses related to programming such as C language and some data structures, most of them are beginners and have little exposure to large-scale programming practice and training. As a result, their programming skills remain at the initial stage, making it difficult to meet the requirements of practical engineering development. The purpose of offering this course is to provide students with practical guidance and training in programming design after completing basic courses such as C language. Combining the characteristics of real-time information processing software design, it introduces modular programming ideas and methods, teaches engineering creation, code writing, and debugging skills and techniques based on the Visual Studio integrated development environment, and introduces the concept of modular and standardized high-quality programming design. Through the design and practical application of large-scale programs, students are taught common programming skills and techniques, enabling them to truly master large-scale program design methods and possess the ability to design algorithms based on specific problem requirements, solve complex engineering problems through team communication and collaboration, and quickly become talents capable of undertaking engineering research and development tasks.
