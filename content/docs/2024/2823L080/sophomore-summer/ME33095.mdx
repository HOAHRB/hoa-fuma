By introducing smart materials (such as piezoelectric materials, flexoelectric materials, shape memory materials, electro/magneto/photo-responsive materials, electro/magneto-rheological materials, polyelectrolyte materials, pyroelectric materials, magneto-optical materials, superconductors, etc.) and their basic characteristics, design principles, control mechanisms, and development trends, this course enables students to establish a comprehensive concept of smart structure systems. It helps students understand the application and potential development directions of smart materials and smart structures in fields such as precision machinery, microelectronics, aerospace, and biomedical engineering. Students will be able to integrate their knowledge and master advanced methods and techniques for utilizing smart structure control technology in related research. In this course, students will learn the design and application examples of cutting-edge smart structures and structural electronics technology. They will engage in independent research and development using smart materials, structures, and their control methods in a collaborative team format. Through course design and other activities, students will enhance their abilities in innovation and teamwork.
