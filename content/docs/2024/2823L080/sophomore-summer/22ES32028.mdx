    en: Flow control technology has evolved alongside the development of the aviation industry. Advanced flow control technology made significant contributions to the progress of the aerospace industry in the 20th century. With aircraft flow control as the background, the course teaches how to use scientific methods to explore and analyze the influence laws of design variables such as control positions, control parameters and incoming flow conditions in basic research on flow control technology. It helps students understand and recognize the key control parameters, their basic characteristics and the database of control effects in flow control technology. Meanwhile, focusing on existing flow control technologies and developing new-concept ones, the course provides new ideas for their application in aircraft. This section will combine specific flow control methods to introduce how to improve existing technologies, enhance their control capability and reliability, and integrate with other disciplines to develop new, high-efficiency flow control technologies.
