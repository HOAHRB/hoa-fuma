    en: Introduction to system modeling and simulation is a core course for students majoring in automation. It mainly talks about the concept, method and tools of system modeling and simulation. It also helps the students to learn and master the method for system modeling and simulation, based on which they can model typical systems through mechanism modeling, modeling based on experimental data or the previous two method combined. Simulation experiments is also a topic of this course. This course consists of the following parts. (1) The concept of system, model and simulation, and the history, current status, future direction and application of system modeling and simulation. (2) Mechanism modeling method and modeling examples such as electrical system, mechanical system, electro-mechanical system; modeling method and modeling examples based on experimental data or the previous two methods combined. (3) Typical simulation method, such as Euler method, Trapezoidal method， Runge-Kutta method and Linear Multiple-step method, and how to choose simulation method and simulation step. (4) Simulation program design. The experimental parts of this course are system modeling and simulation experiment. The desire to do innovative work, the ability of system analysis and the knowledge of modeling and simulation will be cultivated through classroom teaching and computer experiment. This will lay the foundation of learning automatic control theory and automatic control experiments, provide them with powerful tool for study system problem, and cultivate them to do systematic thinking and help them to gain the ability of modeling and simulation.
