    en: Lighting systems account for nearly 20% of all the electrical energy consumed in the industrialized countries. Therefore, it can be understood that Lighting is an important topic for undergraduate engineering students. In this course, the basic concepts related to light, vision, color and lighting design will be treated in a first part. In a second part of the course, the different light sources will be presented, including incandescent lamps, discharge lamps and very popular LED or solid-state lamps. The different auxiliary equipment used to drive incandescent and discharge lamps will also be expounded, including most common electronic circuits. Since, LED lamps are very relevant nowadays in lighting applications, power converters for DC and AC supplied applications will be presented in detail. Finally, some design examples will be carried out, performing computer simulations to verify the correct operation of the developed designs.
