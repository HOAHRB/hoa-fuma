Molecular biology chiefly concerns itself with understanding the relationships between structure and function of biological molecules including DNA, RNA, protein and other biomolecules, as well as learning how these relations contribute to the operation and control of biological processes, of which the regulation and control of gene expression is particularly emphasized. The progress in molecular biology can deepen our understanding of the molecular mechanisms underlying inheritance, reproduction, growth and development, which will contribute to the development of novel concepts, strategies and techniques for life sciences.
