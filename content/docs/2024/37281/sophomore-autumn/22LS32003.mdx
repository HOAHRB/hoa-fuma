    en: Biomedical Engineering (BME) is a new interdisciplinary subject, which uses the disciplines and applications of modern science and technology, from the prospective of engineering, to investigate the structures, functions, and other phenomena of organisms, especially human beings, on multiple levels, reveal the regulations of life activities, getting a deeper insight into the living systems, provide novel methods for safety protection, decease prevention, treatment and rehabilitation, design new instruments for diagnosis and therapy. Besides biology, BME is also highly related to physics, chemistry, material science and computer science. A project of BME usually mixes many fields of science and technology. Therefore, the knowledge of BME is complex and unsystematic. This course, which is a brief introduction of BME, is a selective course for the students of Bioengineering. In this course, the basic concept, history, classification and development (domestic and international) of BME will be introduced, including cancer diagnosis & therapy, biomechanics, bioinformatics, biosensors & chips, biomaterials, tissue engineering and artificial organs. The aim of this course is broadening the students’ range of knowledge, making the students know the importance of BME in scientific research and clinic medicine, as well as the problem and challenge in the field of BME. Furtherly, this course is also expected to encourage the students to make a contribution in this field of our country in the future.
