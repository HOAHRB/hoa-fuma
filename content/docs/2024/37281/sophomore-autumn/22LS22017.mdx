    en: Microorganism is a kind of tiny creatures that are hardly visible to the naked eyes spreading all around us. On one hand, they improve our living standard by producing some useful products, such as beer, bread, and antibiotics. On the other hand, however, some of them are the threats to human health, such as AIDS and pulmonary tuberculosis caused by microbes. Thereby, it is necessary to study the microorganism thoroughly in order to use or control different microbes. Microbiology is one of the core courses in biology. This course puts main emphasis on irradiating five of the principle laws in microorganisms, namely the morphology and the structure of the microbes; the physiology and the metabolism of the microbes; the heredity and the variety of the microbes; the ecology distribution, the classification and the evolution of the microbes. This course contains ten chapters, which deal with the five common characters of microbes, the morphology and structure of microbes, the nutrition and the mediums, the metabolism and the fermentation, the growth of the microbes and its control, the heredity of microbes and microbial breeding, microbial ecology, the infection and immunology, and the classification and identification of the microbes, respectively.
