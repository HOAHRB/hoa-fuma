    en: The aim of this course is to introduce gene fragments of eukaryotic cells into prokaryotic cells for protein expression. First, we need to design specific primers for PCR amplification to obtain enough CDS fragment of eukaryotic gene. Prokaryotic expression vector was used as receptor. Both of the fragment and the vector were double-cut to form sticky ends, and ligase was used to link them into recombinants. The recombinants were transformed into prokaryotic cells, and the correct insertion of the fragments into the vector was confirmed by various methods to determine the correct recombinant plasmid that could express the target protein. The course consists of a continuous series of experiments, and today's experimental results are the next day's experimental materials. In order to exclude false results, sufficient controls must be carefully designed, and this becomes a great fun in the challenge. It enables students to grow up happily in learning, while mastering basic molecular biology experimental techniques, and improving experimental hands-on ability and analytical problem-solving ability. Those will lay a good foundation for independent scientific research of the students in the future. At the same time, the students will obtain rigorous training in scientific research thinking, and acquire the ability of cooperation after cooperated practice between classmates, and enhance the potential of scientific research management through the grasp of the experimental process.
