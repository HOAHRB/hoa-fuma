Therefore, jurisprudence holds a special position in the legal system and is the general theory, fundamental theory, and methodology of law. This course is one of the components of the jurisprudence course, mainly focusing on the fundamental and cutting-edge issues in the field of legal research, conducting in-depth thematic research, deepening students' theoretical knowledge of law, improving their practical ability to analyze and solve practical problems using theoretical knowledge, and forming rigorous legal thinking. This course is a coherent and complementary whole with the course "Jurisprudence" offered in the first semester of this major. Studying this course requires a good legal foundation, as well as corresponding knowledge of philosophy and sociology, as well as a certain level of theoretical abstraction and thinking ability.
