    en: Big data is the primary material for quantitative research in social sciences. The processing and analysis of big data across various social fields are essential methods and techniques that students in the humanities and social sciences must master. This course adopts an interdisciplinary academic perspective combining social sciences and computational sciences, aiming to equip students with the foundational knowledge of big data analysis. This includes understanding the fundamentals of big data, the basic logic and principles of big data analysis, the lifecycle of big data analysis, as well as the tools, platforms, and use cases for big data analysis. Building on this foundation, the course also aims for students to master the methods, techniques, and models of big data analysis, become proficient in using the common data analysis platforms in social sciences, and learn to establish and analyze big data models based on R language. Moreover, students will understand and master comprehensive techniques for analyzing social big data cases based on R language.
