    zh: 本门课程作为经典力学课程的延伸，将学生学到的理论动力学向具体应用场景推广。动力学与控制方向是分析由刚体和变形体组成的动力学系统在外载荷作用下的移动、转动和变形，并根据所建的数学模型对系统进行轨道、姿态和振动控制的学科。本课程将以拉格朗日力学为基本工具，介绍数学建模中的刚体动力学、柔性体动力学、约束库、环境载荷等的数学建模方法；介绍数学模型的简化与线性化方法，并基于此设计运载器系统的轨道、姿态与振动控制；进而介绍多体系统动力学的建模方法，并基于此实现复杂机械系统的仿真与控制以及工程应用等。通过本门课程的学习，学生可以学习和掌握力学、机械和航天工程上常用刚性、柔性部件的建模和计算仿真方法，能够设计和实现相关仿真算法，并在简化数学模型上设计相应的控制器，并应用到自己的科研实践和工程问题的解决中去。
    en: As a continuation of the course of Classical Mechanics, this course will extend the theoretical studies to practical engineering applications. The topics studied in dynamics and control include studies of the motions, rotations and vibrations of mechanical systems consists of rigid and deformable bodies, as well as the orbital, orientation and vibration control strategies to guarantee the system moves as expected. The main theoretical backgrounds of this course is the Lagrange mechanics, and the formulations of rigid bodies, flexible bodies, constraints as well as applied loads are provided in this course. The mathematical formulations will be simplified and linearized, and the control strategies are studied on those models. Frontier research topics, such as Lie group descriptions of rotations and real-time integrators will also be introduced. After study of this course, the students will be prepared for researches on vehicle dynamics of astronautics, aeronautics, ships, as well as railway engineering, and they will also be familiar with the applications of dynamics and controls on robotics and other machines.
