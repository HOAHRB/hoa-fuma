    en: Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics is an important branch of chemical engineering and the theoretical basis for research and development (R&D) in chemical processes. This course mainly covers the basic knowledge and concept of thermodynamics, and in particular, introduces the fundamental laws and their application in chemical processes. The thermodynamic analysis of energy conversion, transfer, use, and loss in the chemical process is comprehensively introduced to evaluate the energy efficiency of the process and equipment. At the end of this course, students will be able to master the laws and state properties of fluid state changes, the basic rules of thermodynamic function, and energy conversion. It will develop students' thermodynamic ideas and ways of thinking, train students' ability to analyze and solve practical problems, and lay a solid theoretical foundation for the study of chemical processes. It will also cultivate students' innovative spirit and self-learning ability to meet the future development of the chemical industry.
