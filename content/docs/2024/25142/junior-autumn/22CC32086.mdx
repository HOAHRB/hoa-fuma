The course focuses on the core role of AI in the field of electrochemistry - from accelerating the development of new materials (such as electrolytes、high-efficiency catalysts) to optimizing the performance and life of batteries, electrolyzers and fuel cells. At the same time, it uses intelligent algorithms to solve the problems of long R&D cycles and complex regulation of traditional energy equipment, and promotes the accelerated transformation of energy systems towards low-carbon and intelligent ones. Through this course, students will understand the application logic of tools such as machine learning and digital twins in electrochemical systems, such as building a battery life prediction model, designing a dynamic control strategy for water electrolysis to produce hydrogen, and understanding how AI empowers key technologies such as lithium/sodium ion batteries and hydrogen storage systems. The course also uses actual cases (cultivating interdisciplinary capabilities from micro-material design to macro-energy system integration, and ultimately guiding students to gain insight into the strategic significance of AI+electrochemistry in reducing carbon emissions across the entire industry chain and promoting a circular economy, and providing solutions that combine technological innovation and sustainable vision for my country's energy structure transformation under the "dual carbon" goal.
