    en: '"Electrochemical Innovative Design" includes two parts: calculation and design of materials for electrochemical power sources and theoretical design of electroplating systems. This course adopts a task-driven approach in teaching practice. In the teaching part, the basic operations and data analysis methods will be introduces, including the calculation and analysis methods of the electrochemical properties of materials related to fuel cells and lithium-ion batteries, as well as the finite element simulation method of electroplating additives adsorbed in the electroplating process. Based on the basics, two innovative design topics will be set, regarding materials for chemical power sources and electroplating. Students work in groups to determine the models, calculation methods, and parameters involved in the design, and then submit calculation tasks and summarize the result for analysis. The calculation results should be verified by listing physical characterizations and electrochemical methods in literatures. The design will be completed after finishing a design report and passing the defense. Through the course training, students are required to develop comprehensive abilities in chemistry, electrochemistry, basic theories of chemical power sources and electroplating, computer software simulation and calculation, data integration, and analysis. Students are expected to have the ability to apply their knowledge of electrochemistry and computational chemistry, simulation, and other related knowledge to solve practical problems when engaging in research, design, and development work related to batteries, electroplating, and other chemical and materials-related fields.'
