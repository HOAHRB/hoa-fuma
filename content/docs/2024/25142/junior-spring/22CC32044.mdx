As the largest branch of electrochemical engineering, this course is the supplement to the courses Chemical power supply technology and Electroplating technology. The purpose of this course is to pay attention to the integrity and to Make the students of energy chemical engineering master the comprehensive knowledge of electrochemical engineering, so then broad professional knowledge. The electrochemical engineering comprises electrolysis, electroplating and electrochemical power source and their industrial process. This curriculum involve the basis of electrochemical engineering, such as research field in electrochemical engineering and the history, status quo, direction of development, research field and application of electrolysis; figures of merit, analysis and calculation of mass transport, heat transport, potential and current density distribution on surface of electrode; sorts of electrochemical reactor, coupling and assembling in reactor, and reactor design; electrode and membrane materials。The curriculum also involve the application of electrochemical engineering, such as chlor-alkali industry, inorganic electrosynthesize, organic electrosynthesis, metal extraction and refining, and electrochemical machining.
