    en: '"Theory of Metal corrosion" is an elective course offered in the forefront track of the Chemical Engineering and Technology discipline. The course aims to equip students with an understanding of various corrosion types, characteristics of localized corrosion, and corrosion features under natural conditions. It further aims to familiarize students with the thermodynamics of electrochemical corrosion, electrochemical corrosion kinetics, and the mechanisms and influencing factors behind filamentous corrosion, intergranular corrosion, selective corrosion, and exfoliation in localized corrosion. The course emphasizes the mastery of characteristics related to hydrogen evolution corrosion, oxygen evolution corrosion, corrosion of passivatable metals, and the mechanisms and influencing factors behind intergranular corrosion, galvanic corrosion, and pitting corrosion. Additionally, students gain insights into common corrosion types and protective measures in chemical equipment and chemical production processes.'
