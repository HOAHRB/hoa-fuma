So, the exploitation of novel electrochemical power sources has been paid attention in the past decades. Its industrialization development is more and more fast. Its scale of industry is sharply increasing. Its applications are of diversification. Novel electrochemical power sources are playing an important role in national economy. Novel electrochemical power source is a course about the lithium ion battery, low-temperature fuel cells, redox flow battery, Na-S battery, and so on. Novel electrochemical power source is one of the elective courses for students studying in the specialty of chemical engineering and technology. This course expounds the production, storage, transportation and utilization of lithium ion battery; the detailed introduces the integrated utilization of lithium ion battery and the principle, structure of fuel cells, electrode materials and preparation technology, the generate electricity, getting heat and storage of redox flow battery, the primary principle, structure and performances of Na-S battery. Students having studied this course should grasp the basic concepts of lithium ion battery, low-temperature fuel cells, redox flow battery, and Na-S battery, master their important theories and performances, understand the general situation of the production, transformation, and utilization of different novel electrochemical power sources and their preparation technology. After having studied this course, students should make a stable foundation for engaging in the research and development and manufacture of novel electrochemical power sources.
