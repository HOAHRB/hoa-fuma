This 16-hour course provides a systematic introduction to the physical properties of semiconductor materials and the operational principles of key devices. Key topics cover the electronic states and energy band structures of semiconductors, statistical distribution and transport phenomena of carriers, behaviors of carriers under equilibrium and non-equilibrium conditions, the characteristics of PN junctions and metal-semiconductor contacts, as well as fundamental devices like MOSFETs. The course aims to equip students with the essential skills to analyze device performance, serving as a crucial foundation for integrated circuits and semiconductor technology.
