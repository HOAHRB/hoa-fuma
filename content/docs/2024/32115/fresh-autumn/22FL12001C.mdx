    en: College Foreign Language is a required foreign language course for freshmen. It contains an integrated foreign language skill training, progressing from listening and speaking to reading and writing, aiming to improve the overall foreign language proficiency and application ability of undergraduates. Listening skills are explicated in combination with text analyses as taught in reading. Authentic materials are used to motivate listening comprehension. Speaking ability is to be developed in thematic units. Through text reading and analysis, reading skills are consolidated, and reading efficiency and text-analysis ability are improved. Reading skills are subsequently applied to promoting writing ability as well as critical thinking ability. Thematic tasks are assigned to showcase rich multiculturalism, expand international perspectives, and cultivate Chinese sentiments. The course adopts the cumulative assessment method and the total score for one semester is composed of 50% of the course performance and 50% of the final proficiency test.
