    en: Most problem solving in science and engineering uses scientific computing. While sophisticated software is used for computations, it is important to understand the basic principles, accuracy and performance of our calculations. The course of Numerical Methods in Physics describes the main principles of the numerical computations, important computational algorithms and error estimates for them. The course describes methods for numerical interpolation and approximation, integration and differentiation, solution of non-linear equations and optimization. It introduces the main methods for solving systems of linear equations and finding spectrum of matrices. The numerical methods for the Cauchy problem for differential equations are also discussed. The main goal of the course is to provide the basic knowledge of the numerical methods and ability to use them for solving physical problems.
