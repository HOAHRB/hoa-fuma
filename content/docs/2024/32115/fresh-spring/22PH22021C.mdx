The course covers two aspects: heat and molecular kinetic theory. The study of the thermal part mainly establishes the basic concepts, laws and research methods to describe the macro thermophysical process, including the concept of temperature, the three laws of thermodynamics, the zeroth law of thermodynamics, entropy, enthalpy, phase change, etc; In the part of molecular kinetic theory, the relationship between microscopic phenomena and macroscopic thermal phenomena is mainly established. Through the preliminary establishment of the physical image of statistical physics, the basic physical concepts and processes such as particle distribution function, thermal balance, Maxwell distribution, Boltzmann distribution and transport process are learned. Through the course study, students can master the basic theory of heat, including the micro essence of thermal phenomena, the basic macro and micro description of thermal movement, understand the materiality of electromagnetic fields, understand the dialectical thinking and unity of opposites between micro and macro systems, lay the foundation for the subsequent general physics course of micro description of matter, and make necessary preparations for the theoretical physics course of thermodynamics and statistical physics. At the same time, students should master the knowledge and methods of applying thermal theory, analyze phenomena and solve problems, and have the preliminary ability to use thermal methods to study related problems.'
