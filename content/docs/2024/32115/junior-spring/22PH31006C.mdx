The course of Computational Physics mainly includes basic ideas of numerical methods applied to mathematical problems for ordinary and partial differential equations. Starting with the simplest Euler scheme applied to the initial value problems for ordinary differential equations, the course progressively describes more specific methods which can be applied to stiff problems and problems involving large parameters. The course describes also numerical methods for the integral equations and for the boundary value problems of partial differential equations. The main goal of the course is to provide basic knowledge of the methods which would allow further specialization in particular subjects of computational physics and understanding of modern finite element and boundary element methods and their realizations in quick solvers.
