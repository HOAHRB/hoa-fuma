    en: Fourier optics covers areas of optics, the description of which is closely related to Fourier analysis. The use of Fourier transforms makes it possible to significantly simplify the description of many optical systems and to better understand their operation principles. The Fourier Optics course addresses a number of such optical systems. At the beginning of the course, the necessary information from Fourier analysis is presented. Then the questions of describing light wave propagation are considered. Elements of the theory of radiation coherence are considered. The diffraction theory of the lens is presented and the idea of lens as an element of the optical Fourier transform is introduced. In the final part of the course, various schemes for using lens systems are considered. In particular, the problem of spatial spectral filtering, Fourier holography, matched filtering, pattern recognition, and holographic memory properties are considered. At the end of the course, correlative models of associative memory and connection with neural systems are represented.
