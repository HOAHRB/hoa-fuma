    en: Physical Optics as an important fundamental and specialized course holds an important position for students majoring in Electronic Science and Technology. It is a link between the previous and follow-up courses. Therefore, it becomes a necessary base for the students to learn later specialized courses and engage in future scientific researches. After the course, the students will master the basic ways of analyzing problems and gain the ability of solving the problems that may be encountered in later courses and research work. The main contents of this course fall into two parts. The first part, based on the electromagnetism theory of optics, is to discuss the propagation of optics, it puts much emphasis on the optical propagation law through isotropy medium and anisotropism medium and the optical characteristics of interference, diffraction and polarization. The second part is to discuss the interaction between optics and substances, including induction double refraction, absorption, dispersion and scattering of the optics. It will pay much attention to the interpretation of physical ideas and the introduction of new concepts and front projects of modern optics, such as the principle of laser, the basis of Fourier Optics and so on. In addition, the heuristic education way will be emphasized so as to foster the students’ ability of analyzing and solving problems.
