This course serves as an introductory course for learning hardware programming languages, the main teaching goal is to help students understand the principle of the design of FPGA, establish the concept of development and parallel processing of FPGA, grasp the basic method of developing the application of FPGA by EDA design software, be familiar with modular design and conversion between functional modeling and code description. Through the classroom teaching, experimental operation and computer simulation, this course focuses on training students'comprehensive analysis and design ability of software and hardware, and increasing their innovative consciousness. The main contents of the course are as follows. 1. Grasp the design method of Verilog HDL language, master the logical structure of Verilog HDL project, understand the corresponding relationship between hardware description language and actual hardware resources, and use Verilog HDL language to program according to the design requirements. 2. Understanding the development process of the FPGA, familiarizing with the functions of the development board of the FPGA, mastering the methods of using the design software of the FPGA, learning to use the design software of the FPGA to realize the specific functions and debug them. 3. Master the development process from theoretical description to functional modeling and realization of specific functions. Familiar with the use of the IP core resources of the FPGA, understand the development and design process of the FPGA. Master the decomposition method of engineering project, understand the modular design idea of FPGA, cultivate the ability of team cooperation to solve problems, and exercise the ability of design, organization and debugging in engineering project practice.
