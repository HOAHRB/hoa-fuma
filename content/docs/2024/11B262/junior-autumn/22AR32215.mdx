    en: '"Data Mining and Smart Energy Management" is a professional elective course for the major of Building Environment and Energy Application Engineering. The main purpose of the course is to cultivate students'' ability to use the cutting-edge technology of data mining to carry out the intelligent management of building energy system and expand their knowledge and technical vision under the background of the rapid development of smart city construction, internet and big data technology. The main task of the course is to make students generally understand the basic data mining algorithm applicable to the field of built environment and energy smart management through classroom teaching and case analysis, and have the ability to conduct preliminary data mining, knowledge discovery and result display of massive data generated in the field of built environment and energy smart management. Additionally, make the students have the ability to use data mining technology and professional skills to carry out simple evaluation and optimization of building energy system management schemes. Lastly, make the students have the ability to use data mining technology to analyze and explain the knowledge behind the massive data in field tests, and to obtain reasonable and effective conclusions through information synthesis. The course is generally divided into two parts: basic knowledge and professional application. The basic knowledge part mainly includes introduction, basic knowledge of data, data preprocessing, cluster analysis, classification algorithm, correlation and correlation; The professional application part takes urban heating and gas as the main energy intelligent management objects, including intelligent decision-making and intelligent regulation such as smart heating related concepts and standard interpretation, system simulation, load forecasting and fault diagnosis, basic concept of smart gas, SCADA system, GIS integrated management system of pipe network, and construction of smart gas system. The course is given mainly by classroom teaching. The assessment result of the course is the summation of in-class exam and writing technical report. The total score is 100 points.'
