    en: XX century rapid urbanization has brought many benefits like high productivity of industry, technology advances and improvement of public health. China is one of the most remarkable evidences. However urbanization process comes to a moment now when it inevitably collapses due to inherit contradictions between urban densities and human biology, unlimited growth of cities and shrinking of limited ecosystems supporting urbanized world. Thus XXI century needs to invent new urban spaces and buildings’ types that will coexist with the nature, combine autonomy in resource consumption and high connectivity in transport and information flows, and provide diversity of post-industrial lifestyles where all functions can be in one place. The course calls students to discuss, conceptualize and design solutions for Harbin future matching this new better world.
