It is a bridge between basic core courses and specialized courses. The course introduces the common and individual hydraulic characteristics of all kinds of engineering pipe networks, and its composition, design and analysis methods. Combined with working principles and performance of pumps and fans and its operation regulation method, the design and problem solving of typical engineering pipe network can be completed by using hydraulic calculation and working condition analysis software. Through the course study, help students to learn the basic theory and methods of designing, analyzing and commissioning for branched and loop pipe network, to have the ability of its engineering application and analysis by using design manual & reference materials, also to lay a preliminary foundation in design and operation management for other comprehensive and complicated pipe networks. Course contents mainly include: hydraulic characteristics of gas, liquid and multi-phase pipe networks——commonality and individuality; theoretical basis for pumps and fans; matching operation between pumps, fans and the pipe network system; hydraulic operating modes analysis and adjustments for the branched pipe network; theory of hydraulic calculating methods for the loop pipe network. The objective of the course is to widen professional field of view for undergraduates, to deposit necessary knowledge for future courses’ study, and furthermore to cultivate their practical ability in the pipe engineering applications. The results of the course evaluation are cumulative, including final exam, course assignments, experiment & software application, total scores are in percentage terms.'
