Heat Transfer plays an important role in the knowledge structure of the Architecture Environment and Energy Application Engineering. Most issues studied and solved by the specialty are temperature issues and humidity issues（cost some heat or cold to meet the building's temperature and humidity requirements）. With the climate changing, heat transfer happens all the time in the structure elements. Heat transfer is the main physical processes during heating, air conditioning and ventilating which could change indoor temperature and humidity conditions. So, the Learning levels of the curriculum can affect directly the level of students' expertise. Through this course, students can master system and complete basic heat transfer system, to understand the latest developments in heat transfer and development direction, laying a solid foundation for specialized courses of building heat transfer analysis, thermal load calculations, heat exchanger design calculations, equipment Selection of calculation, and energy consumption assessment. The primary contents are base theory of conduction, steady and unsteady conduction, base theory of convection, criterion expressions, base rules and calculation methods of thermal radiation, heat exchanger and knowledge application, numerical methods about finite difference and so on. The prime of heat transfer is mass conservation, energy conservation, momentum conservation. The conception of temperature field and heat current field is required to know. Students is expected to use the three conservation laws to create mathematic model of heat transfer questions, establishes and solves the equations with some simple boundary conditions, and to use the finite difference method to solve some normal heat transfer projects on computer.
