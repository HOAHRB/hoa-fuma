Energetic materials have always played a very important role in national defense and industrial production. The main task of this course is to introduce the synthesis, characterization, and combustion performance of energetic explosives, nanomaterials, and their composites, as well as the development trends and applications of energetic materials. In this course, the focus is on cultivating students' comprehensive analysis and design abilities as well as the application ability of synthetic chemistry knowledge, increasing their innovation awareness, supporting the achievement of corresponding indicators in the learning outcomes of applied chemistry. This course provides necessary basic knowledge for undergraduate students to understand the synthesis of energetic materials, and lays a foundation for further in-depth learning of materials chemistry in the future. The course mainly includes an introduction to energetic materials, application requirements for energetic materials, chemical foundations in the synthesis of explosives, green nitration technology, preparation methods for nanomaterials, various composite energetic materials and their applications in propellants. Students are required to systematically master the preparation, processing technology, engineering technology, structural analysis technology, key performance, and quality control of energetic materials, and possess certain scientific research and engineering practical abilities in the field of energetic materials.
