The numerical methods introduced in this textbook are nowadays very widely used computational techniques that it is essential for students to understand their basic solution algorism and applications of those techniques. The concept of finite element stiffness matrix and associated displacement equation in terms of interpolation functions are introduced first. The energy methods such as the first theorem of Castigliano, the principle of minimum potential energy and the method of weighted residual are described and illustrated with various examples. Finally, the applications to various engineering problems are illustrated. The main contents of finite difference method include: finite difference formats for hyperbolic, parabolic and elliptic equations. The material of this course of computational mechanics is expected to be useful for students who would like to learn the mathematical modeling, solution procedure and the computer programs used for solving practical problems. The objective of the course is to introduce various aspects of the numerical methods as applied to engineering problems in a systematic manner and to enable the students to understand the basic theory and computer implementation of the theory developed.'
