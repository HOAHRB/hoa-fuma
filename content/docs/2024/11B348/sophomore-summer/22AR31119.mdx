Co-taught with professors, professional designers and research scholars specializing in architecture, urban and rural planning, landscape architecture, design studies and other disciplines from world-renowned overseas universities, this two-week intensive English-taught program is delivered during the summer semester and features multicultural integration and interdisciplinary collaboration. Students enrolled in the course conduct research and design work in groups, carrying out design studies alongside faculty from both home and abroad. The International Joint Design Workshop lays a solid foundation for senior undergraduates of the School of Architecture and Design to expand their professional knowledge, broaden their global perspectives, pursue further studies at world-top universities, undertake internships at overseas design firms, and collaborate with international designers.
