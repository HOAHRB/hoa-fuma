It aims to cultivate students' innovative thinking ability in the dimensions of cutting-edge architectural design theory, methods, strategies, and technology based on digital technology. Through course theoretical learning and gradual thematic training, students can understand the needs of the digital architecture development era, familiarize themselves with the essence and characteristics of digital architecture, and master the logic of digital architecture design thinking; Cultivate students' sense of historical mission in conducting digital architecture learning based on the background of carbon neutrality and sustainable development. They will be able to apply digital modeling tools to practice building form, space, material, and structural information modeling, carry out digital building performance simulation practice, and carry out multi-objective optimization design and construction practice of digital buildings.
