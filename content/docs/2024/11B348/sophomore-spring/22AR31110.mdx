    en: Building Physics, which is an important part of architecture, focuses on the phenomenon and laws of acoustic, lighting and thermotics within building. It aims at enhancing building function and create well living or working environment. In this course, human’s reaction and feelings on the factors of acoustic, lighting and thermotics in building environment are studied, as well as how to make a comfortable building environment through technology and design method. Building Thermal Engineering (or named Thermal Physics of Building), is an important part of Building Physics, and is a fundamental course for advanced professional architect. In this course, some important topics will be lectured, such as the principles of heat and mass transfer occurred in building, how to do thermal insulation, heat shielding & moistureproof through architectural planing and design. Consequently, appropriate indoor thermal environment is formed, durability of building envelope is improved and building energy consumption is reduced. Through the course lectures and exercises, experimental training, the students will learn the basic knowledge, calculation skills and design method of building thermal engineering, and will construct their system concepts of building thermal technologies. Moreover, this course will help students to establish solid theoretical foundation for design and study on building energy efficiency and green building.
