The central parts of this course consist of industrial communication network of industrial control system, cyber security protection technology, intrusion detection technology, protocol reverse analysis and fuzzing test technology, and cyber security risk assessment technology. The purpose of this course is to enable students to master the basic knowledge of cyber security of industrial control system, grasp the development trend of cyber security technology of industrial control system, adapt to the development needs of industrial control system, and improve the level of the cyber security protection of industrial control system. The main teaching contents of this course include: industrial communication network of industrial control system, cyber security protection technology of industrial control system, intrusion detection technology and its application in commercial vehicle CAN(Controller Area Network) bus, protocol reverse analysis and fuzzing test technology and its application in vehicle CAN bus, cyber security risk assessment technology and its application in electric vehicle charging station, analysis of typical network attack events of industrial control system, and development trend of cyber security technology of Industrial Control Systems.'
