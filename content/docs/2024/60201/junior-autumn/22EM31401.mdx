After this course, students should master important management theories in each stage, and gain insight into the development direction in management, understand and analyze important phenomena in management practice. Important theories covered in this course include classical management theory, behavioral science theory, scientific management theory, interpersonal relationship school, decision theory, strategic management theory, marketing management theory, contingency management theory, quality management theory, cross-cultural theory, etc. The focus of this course is to understand the historical background of each theory, master the core content of each theory and its application in modern management practice, and deeply understand the relationship between management and culture, management and science.
