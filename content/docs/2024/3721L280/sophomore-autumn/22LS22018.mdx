Therefore, basic microbiology laboratory courses are of great importance. The course content is primarily fundamental, including the morphological structures of common bacteria, molds, and yeasts, routine slide preparation, size and quantity measurement, production of commonly used microbiological media, isolation and cultivation of soil microorganisms, isolation of bacteriophages, as well as physiological and biochemical reactions of microorganisms. The settings of the experimental content are closely related to the experimental conditions and are highly operable. Course Objective 1: Enable students to use routine equipment in microbiology laboratories and gain a preliminary understanding of different microbial groups. Course Objective 2: Provide training in the basic operational methods of microbiological experimental techniques, and gradually develop the ability to solve practical problems using modern biotechnology. Course Objective 3: Cultivate students’ scientific literacy, and exercise their comprehensive abilities to identify problems, research information, solve issues, and summarize findings.'
