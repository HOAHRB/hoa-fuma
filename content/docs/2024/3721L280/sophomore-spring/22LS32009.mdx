    en: Since the 21st century, the theory and experimental technology of life sciences have developed rapidly, producing a massive amount of research results and experimental data. A large amount of knowledge and data are stored in biological databases and analyzed by biological software. Understanding the principles of biological databases and software and mastering the application methods of common biological databases and software are essential qualities and abilities for talents in biotechnology and bioengineering disciplines. This course mainly teaches the application background and methods of biological databases and software, enabling students to understand the general characteristics and basic elements of biological databases and software. Students will be able to proficiently select and query common databases of genome sequences, genes, RNA, proteins, variations, diseases, etc., such as the NCBI RefSeq sequence database, GO gene ontology, GeneCard gene database, PDB protein database, dbSNP variation database, OMIM disease database, ClinVar variation-disease database, etc. The course also covers the deployment and use of genomic browsers, sequence alignment, sequencing data analysis, and other biological software methods, such as the UCSC Genome Browser, BLAST sequence alignment software, FASTA, SAM and other basic sequencing data formats, as well as software commonly used in sequencing data analysis steps. Additionally, the course will briefly introduce specific domain biological databases and software, enabling students to independently learn the usage of non-common biological databases and software. Through corresponding lectures and hands-on lab sessions, this course focuses on training students' practical skills in analyzing actual problems, selecting, and using biological databases and software to complete specific tasks. It effectively assists in the experimental design and data analysis of other courses in this discipline and cultivates students' ability to learn and apply advanced tools to solve practical problems.
