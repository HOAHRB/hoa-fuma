    en: This course focuses on the advanced theory and application of radiative heat transfer, providing students with a comprehensive and in-depth knowledge system. The course content covers the basic theory of radiative heat transfer, including the fundamental concepts of electrodynamics and optics, enabling students to understand the physical nature of radiative heat transfer. It further explores the theory of gas radiation, analyzing the energy level transitions, spectral line formation, and broadening mechanisms of gas molecules to help students grasp the characteristics of gas radiation. The course also delves into radiative heat transfer in non-homogeneous media, analyzing the radiative transfer processes in multiphase media and complex geometric structures, allowing students to understand the behavior of radiative heat transfer in practical engineering problems. In addition, the course introduces advanced Monte Carlo ray-tracing methods, including the basic principles, algorithm implementation, and application skills, to help students master numerical simulation tools. Through theoretical lectures and case analyses, students will gain a deep understanding of the core mechanisms of radiative heat transfer and learn how to apply this knowledge to practical engineering problems. Radiative heat transfer theory is of great significance in engineering and scientific research. It is not only a key component of heat transfer studies but also plays a crucial role in fields such as energy utilization, environmental science, and aerospace. By learning this course, students will gain a deep understanding of the basic principles and the latest research progress of radiative heat transfer, and master key concepts such as gas radiation and radiative heat transfer in non-homogeneous media. These theories not only provide students with tools for in-depth research on radiative heat transfer phenomena but also offer a scientific basis for solving practical engineering problems. For example, in solar energy utilization, radiative heat transfer theory helps design efficient solar collectors and photovoltaic systems; in the aerospace field, radiative heat transfer theory is essential for the design of thermal protection systems for spacecraft. Through this course, students will be able to apply theoretical knowledge to practical problems, enhancing their ability to solve complex engineering problems and laying a solid foundation for future research and career development.
