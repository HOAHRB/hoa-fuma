    en: Introduction to Launch Vehicle Engine Design is a specialized elective course for undergraduate students in energy, aerospace, mechanical, and materials engineering. The course systematically elaborates principles and key technologies of launch vehicle rocket engines. Through combinations of lectures and case study discussions, it helps students establish a foundational understanding of aerospace propulsion systems and master the structural characteristics, operational modes, and fundamental principles of world-renowned launch vehicle rocket engines. The course emphasizes the analysis of global typical engine cases, incorporating a comparative analysis of the developmental history and characteristics of Chinese and international launch vehicle rocket engines, as well as introducing the latest technological frontiers in commercial rockets. This approach deepens students' comprehension of engine design, while fostering innovative thinking and a global perspective. The course aims to bridge the gap between fundamental theoretical knowledge and engineering practical applications, spark students' research interest in the field of aerospace propulsion, and lay a solid foundation for future engagement in aerospace propulsion research.
