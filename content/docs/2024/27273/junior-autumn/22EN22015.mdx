    en: Environmental monitoring is a core component of undergraduate programs in Environmental Science and Environmental Engineering. It encompasses the surveillance of environmental indicators in the atmosphere, water bodies, soil/solid waste, and organisms (animals, plants), as well as the monitoring of physical parameters like noise levels and biological assessments of the ecological environment. Tasks include formulating monitoring strategies, conducting field sampling, preserving and transporting samples, performing laboratory analyses, interpreting data, and assessing environmental quality. Additionally, students delve into professional concepts and technologies such as network establishment, automated analysis techniques, instrument principles, and the operational mechanics of environmental atmosphere and river water quality monitoring systems.
