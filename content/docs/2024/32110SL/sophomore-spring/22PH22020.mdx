    en: The atomic physics is an important basic course for undergraduates. The atomic physics is also referred to as the quantum physics, or the modern physics. This course introduces the fundamental concepts and principles about micro-world in modern physics based on experiment laws. The atomic physics adopts the classical, the semi-classical and the quantum mechanics method to discuss the structure and changing rule of atoms and atomic nucleus, and interpret their macro properties. Atomic physics is the link for the transition from the classical theory to the quantum theory, it is the base for the students to learn quantum mechanics. The main content of this course includes energy levels and the radiation of the atom, the introduction of the quantum mechanics, alkali atoms and the electron spin, many-electron atoms, atoms in the magnetic-field, the shell structure of atoms, X-ray, molecular structures and molecular spectra, fundamental properties and structures of atomic nucleus.
