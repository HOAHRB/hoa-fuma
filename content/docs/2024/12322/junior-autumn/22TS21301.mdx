By studying this course, students could learn the composition, properties and application of construction materials. Students also could learn the relationship between composition and properties of building materials and the way to improve properties， and the relationship between properties of building materials and design parameters or construction methods. This course is the base for students’ future career in roads and bridges design, constructions or research of construction materials. The content of the course includes introduction, basic properties of building materials, stones, air hardening binding materials, cement and cement concrete, building steel, synthetic polymer waterproof materials, modified asphalt waterproof materials, construction plastic.
