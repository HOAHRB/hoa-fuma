    en: Thermoelectric generator (TEG) is made of thermoelectric materials and metal electrodes through advanced joining technology. TEG can directly convert heat energy into electrical energy using the Seebeck effect of thermoelectric materials. It is a static solid device with the advantages of stable heat source, compact structure, high energy density, no sound, no vibration, stable performance, long life, no maintenance, etc., which makes it has great potential application value in aerospace, aviation, advanced electronics and military fields. In the U.S. lunar program, the Apollo 17 spacecraft successfully transmitted data from the lunar surface to the Earth using the power provided by TEG; the Mars Curiosity rover realized a series of complex tests on the surface of Mars using thermoelectric power generation technology. In recent years, with the increasing environmental pollution and energy crisis, the use of thermoelectric devices to convert low quality heat directly into electrical energy has received widespread attention around the world. This course introduces students to the basic theories of thermoelectric phenomena, thermoelectric joining techniques, the latest advances in thermoelectric materials research, and the manufacture and application of thermoelectric devices.
