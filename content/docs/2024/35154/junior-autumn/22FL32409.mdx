It aims to provide students with the knowledge of secretarial occupation and other jobs related to human resources. Students are to mater the knowledge and skills of secretaries, public relations and human resources. It informs students of the beginning level knowledge such as the role and scope of secretaries, business etiquette, meeting organizations, telephone English and filing, and the advanced level knowledge such as marketing research, public relations, human resources management, job interview and leadership. This course focuses on improving students' oral proficiency of secretarial English and written skills of office documents such as telephone message, filing forms, meeting agenda, meeting minutes, marketing research plan, public relations scheme and human resources management scheme. Students will develop their competence of analyzing questions, solving questions, critical thinking, creative thinking and team work through a series of lectures, seminars and projects.
