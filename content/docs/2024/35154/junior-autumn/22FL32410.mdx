    en: Reading International Organization Documents and Writings is an elective course for students majoring in International Organizations and Global Governance. The course builds on the conception of international organization documents (IODs) as part and parcel of a semiotic system in which political, economic, cultural and ideological meanings represented by international organizations are activated and propagated, with IODs employed as a primary medium of communication that enables the international organizations to wield their power. On this basis, a syllabus has been designed to first develop students’ competence in understanding discourse embedded in IODs and choosing reading strategies essential for thorough comprehension of IODs. The course will then help students develop knowledge about IODs as interconnected genres with discoursal meanings to be fathomed and cognized at multiple depths and dimensions. It is also one of the intents of the course to promote students’ critical reading of IODs through comparison and context analysis, which will lead to their grasp of the institutional significance and administration of international organizations within a global context shaped by international relations.
