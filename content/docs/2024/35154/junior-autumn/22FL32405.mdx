Through elementary training in listening, speaking, reading, writing, and translating and so on, students shall master the basic knowledge, fundamental language skills, and learning strategy of Japanese, so as to lay a solid foundation for further Japanese learning. The course is mainly about Japanese phonology and basic grammer, including the phonological knowledge, the basic sentence patterns, the use of the word「こそあど」and its derivatives, the descriptive sentences, and basic grammatical phenomena such as nouns, adjectives and so on. By the end of the course, the Japanese skills of students shall be up to the standard of The Syllabus of College Japanese as a Second Language (for non-Japanese majors). The course adopts the cumulative assessment method, including 30% assessment at ordinary time（Classroom performance and homework after class are 20%, Classroom contribution is 10%）and 70% of the final grades.
