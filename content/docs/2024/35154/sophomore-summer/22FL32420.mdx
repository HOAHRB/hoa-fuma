    en: This course is designed to help students systematically master the core knowledge framework of intellectual property (IP) law in a relatively short period. It covers the main institutions of IP law—including copyright and related rights, patent law, trademark law, and the legal regulation of domain names—and places particular emphasis on legal issues arising in emerging technological fields of the digital era, such as software, artificial intelligence, the Internet of Things, and big data. By integrating legal theory, comparative jurisprudence, and current enforcement practices, the course guides students toward a deep understanding of the role and significance of IP law within the broader legal system. Upon completion, students will have acquired a solid grasp of international IP legal frameworks, relevant judicial precedents, and evolving trends; they will be able to accurately identify IP subject matter, interpret and apply both domestic and international legal norms, draft and analyze licensing agreements, justify legal decisions, anticipate and mitigate potential risks, and effectively address complex or atypical enforcement scenarios involving international rights protection—thereby developing comprehensive competencies for managing IP matters in the digital age.
