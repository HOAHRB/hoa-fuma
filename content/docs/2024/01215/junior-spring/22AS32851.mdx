These radiation sources can cause continuous and intermittent radiation damage to the electronic components of spacecraft, potentially leading to irreparable failures and performance degradation. Radiation effects and characterization techniques in aerospace integrated circuits are one of the new fundamental research fields that have emerged with the development of aerospace technology. The main task of this course is to enable students to understand and master the behavior and performance of integrated circuits in radiation environments. Students will understand the impact of radiation on semiconductor materials and integrated circuits, gain a deeper understanding of the physical mechanisms and related concepts of radiation effects, as well as methods for addressing and preventing radiation induced failures
