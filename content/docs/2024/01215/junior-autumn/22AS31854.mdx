The curriculum begins with the PN junction as its foundation, then systematically covers the core operating principles of the Bipolar Junction Transistor (BJT) and the Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Field-Effect Transistor (MOSFET). Furthermore, the course extends into advanced topics, including compound semiconductor devices and optoelectronic devices.
