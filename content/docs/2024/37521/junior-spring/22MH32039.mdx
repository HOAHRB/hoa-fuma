    en: Neuromodulation Technology is an interdisciplinary elective course offered by the School of Medicine and Health for undergraduate students majoring in Intelligent Medical Engineering. It is also suitable for students from related disciplines such as Biomedical Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, and Technology. This course focuses on the intersection of neuroscience, engineering technology, and artificial intelligence, aiming to help students master the core principles, key methodologies, and innovative applications of neuromodulation technology in healthcare through systematic theoretical teaching and cutting-edge practical case studies. The course content covers the fundamental theories and technical systems of neuromodulation, including neural electrical stimulation (e.g., deep brain stimulation, spinal cord stimulation), magnetic stimulation (e.g., transcranial magnetic stimulation), transcranial electrical stimulation, transcranial focused ultrasound stimulation, and other modulation techniques. Emphasis is placed on how to achieve precise and adaptive neuromodulation strategies through multimodal signal processing, artificial intelligence algorithms, and closed-loop feedback system design. Students will learn about neural circuit function analysis, stimulation parameter optimization, neural signal decoding and modeling, while gaining an in-depth understanding of the clinical applications of neuromodulation in treating diseases such as Parkinson's disease, depression, epilepsy, and sleep disorders. This course emphasizes the development of students' interdisciplinary integration and innovative practical abilities. Through case studies, simulation experiments, and project practices, students will attempt to design neuromodulation solutions for specific scenarios, thereby enhancing their ability to independently analyze problems, conduct engineering design, and translate medical applications.
