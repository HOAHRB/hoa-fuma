    en: Animation-2 software MAYA is a foundational course for media majors. Starting from a complete beginner level, students will systematically learn the use and operational techniques of Maya, including its model, materials, lighting, and rendering modules. The course primarily focuses on the basic operations of Maya, NURBS, Polygons, and Subdivision modeling techniques. From simpler solid modeling designs to more complex ones, the course gradually trains students in the basic operational skills of the software and their ability to use it for 3D design, expression techniques, and mastering the correct methods for solid modeling design. It also enhances students' creativity, expressiveness, and judgment during the design process. Students will easily understand how 3D animation software tools are applied in practice. The course is centered around learning software operations, while also incorporating the teaching of architectural knowledge, decorative materials knowledge, and artistic knowledge of light and shadow. It provides an in-depth introduction to Maya software operation, basic modeling, understanding camera types and common ergonomic knowledge, and a basic understanding of composition knowledge.
