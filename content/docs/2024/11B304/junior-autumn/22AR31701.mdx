This course uses case analysis and aesthetic analysis as methods to understand design from an aesthetic perspective, grasp the creative laws of design beauty in aesthetic appreciation, comprehend the essence of design beauty in aesthetic reflection, appreciate the power of design beauty in aesthetic experience, and summarize the development laws of design in historical reflection. The course mainly includes design and aesthetics, the essence of design beauty, the forms of design beauty, the media power of design, cultural differences in design beauty, technological revolution and design revolution, design transformation and aesthetic survival, and other contents. The final grades of this course are cumulative, including final exams, discussions, etc. In addition, students are required to independently complete two experiments. Through the exploration of design art case experiments, they can understand the relevant theories of design aesthetics and apply design aesthetics knowledge to solve creative design problems of cultural products.
