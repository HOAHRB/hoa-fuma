It is regarded as prerequisite knowledge and a necessary skill in the majors of almost all universities that are relative to economy and management. The course Intermediate Business English is elaborately designed to all the students of School of Management, HIT. The course is aimed to cultivate the communication skills and problem-solving ability for the students who are going to work in an international business environment. This course mainly comprises of two parts, namely business topics and business skills, involving knowledge and skills in listening, speaking, reading and writing of business English. The content of the course covers Job Interviews, Jobs and Responsibilities, Company Presentations, Business Meetings, Business Entertainment, Business Visits, Business Negotiations, Business Writing, Test training. The course is also helpful for those students who are going to apply for the certificates of either BEC or TOEIC.
