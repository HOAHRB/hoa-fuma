    en: Cross-border E-commerce is a 2-credit (32-hour) professional course offered by the School of Economics and Management for undergraduate students. The course provides a systematic introduction to the core theories and end-to-end operational practices of cross-border e-commerce, aiming to equip students with the comprehensive capabilities needed to engage in cross-border e-commerce business in the digital economy era. The course content follows a progressive structure of "theoretical foundations — platform operations — operational strategies — frontier technologies." It begins by building a holistic understanding of cross-border e-commerce, covering concepts and characteristics, development history, industry chain structure, and relevant legal frameworks and business models. The course then delves into global mainstream cross-border e-commerce platforms and regional market analysis, helping students develop platform selection and market assessment skills. Building on this foundation, students receive systematic training in core operational skills, including product selection analysis, store registration, product page creation, logistics management, and cross-border payment settlement. The course then focuses on advanced topics such as marketing and promotion, brand building, operational strategy optimization, and customer service. Finally, it explores cutting-edge applications of artificial intelligence in cross-border e-commerce, including intelligent product selection, dynamic pricing, content generation, customer service automation, smart logistics, and compliance management. Emphasizing the integration of theory and practice, the course adopts diverse teaching methods including lectures, case studies, and group discussions, with a strong focus on enhancing students' practical operational skills and business analytical capabilities. Upon completion, students will have systematically mastered the fundamental theories and operational practices of cross-border e-commerce, developed professional competencies in platform operations, product selection and promotion, and brand management, and gained an understanding of emerging technological trends in the industry, laying a solid foundation for future careers in cross-border e-commerce and related fields.
