The course is arranged according to the procedure of foreign trade business, systematically teaching various English business correspondences formats and writing methods. The content of the course includes English business correspondences formats, establishing business relations, inquiry and reply, offer and counteroffer, conclusion of business, modes of payment, contracts, letters of credit, packaging, shipping, insurance, complaints and claim. Through English Chinese translation exercises and then through designing and writing missing letters that are in a practical international trade case, students can systematically master and use the vocabulary, sentence patterns and writing skills of business letters, emails and faxes in foreign trade activities, so as to develop their ability and skills in translation and writing of English business letters that are in conformity with international business practices and clear and focused in ideas, reasonable in structure and decent in language.
