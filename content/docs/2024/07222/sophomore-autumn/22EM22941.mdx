    en: Based on basic economics, we will study the current development of the digital economy in-depth and examine the laws governing its operation, enabling us to apply fundamental economic principles to explain issues related to digital economy development. The course will proceed from shallow to deep. The purpose of the first chapter is to understand the basic principles and meanings of the digital economy, and to explore the central role of data as a factor of production. The second chapter explains digital economy technology and talent cultivation, the development of technologies such as big data and blockchain as well as the cultivation of digital literacy, how to cultivate relevant talents and their importance; the third chapter explores the study of the changes that the digital economy has brought about to the productivity and production relations. In the next two chapters, it extends to corporate governance, the impact of the digital economy in government governance, and discusses it with case studies. The final chapter will sort out global policies, including the digital economy strategies of international organizations and major economies such as the United States, Europe, and Japan, and summarize policy insights and trends. Help students build a comprehensive and systematic understanding of the digital economy from multiple perspectives.
