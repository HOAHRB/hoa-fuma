    en: Autonomous UAV flying is a key branch of autonomous intelligent system design, and also a hotspot study field nowadays. Muti-rotor flying vehicle (eg. Quad-rotor drone) possesses unbeatable advantages on stable hovering, precise waypoint control and flying flexibility. Because this kind of flying vehicle belongs to new concept drone, it is different from traditional flying vehicle on automation control. So it is necessary to study its control system’s design, and implement the controller to help the drone perform autonomous flying. This lecture focuses on the attitude control problem of multi-rotor flying vehicle, and help students master automation control design methods. The lecture encourages students to implement their own-designed controller, and run the system on real quad-rotor drone to verify the design. The lecture contains flying vehicle control, embedded system development, attitude/position sensor, automation control, multi-rotor drone’s 6 DOF (Degree of Freedom) kinetic control etc., and places physical system control experiments intermittently to help students train the ability of designing and implementing UAV’s flight control system. The lecture grows the student’s interest, and help to use the automation control theory, automation control elements etc. in the physical system. It is hopeful for this lecture to lead the students for using the automation control theory to solve practical problems, and to realize autonomous flying of UAV.
