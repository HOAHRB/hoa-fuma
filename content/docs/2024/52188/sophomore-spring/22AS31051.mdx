    en: Spacecraft orbit dynamics and control is a professional basic course for the small satellite class and the discipline of space vehicle design. This course mainly studies the basic concepts, principles and applications of spacecraft orbit dynamics and control. It is an important part of cultivating professionals engaged in the overall design of small satellites. Train students to master the basic theories and methods of spacecraft orbital dynamics and solve the basic skills of general spacecraft orbital dynamics application problems, so as to lay a preliminary theoretical foundation for future research on spacecraft orbital dynamics and control and other spacecraft application majors. The course is based on the classic book in orbit dynamics in Massachusetts Institute of Technology, i.e., An Introduction to the Mathematics and Methods of Astrodynamics, Battin R.H., 1999. The main contents include the fundamental problem and the fundamental theory method in orbit dynamics and their applications in orbit transfer and rendezvous missions. The purpose of the course is to train the undergraduates to grasp the fundamental method in the field of orbit dynamics and to apply this technique into orbit mission analysis and design. Moreover, we will train the undergraduates’ innovative thinking, which may be used in their future scientific research. In addition, the research hot topics and the methods in orbit dynamics will be introduced in the course.
