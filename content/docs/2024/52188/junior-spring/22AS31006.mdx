    en: 'As space missions such as deep-space exploration, on-orbit servicing, and autonomous mega-constellation operations grow increasingly complex, traditional ground-controlled spacecraft systems struggle to meet the demands for real-time responsiveness, reliability, and intelligence. This course focuses on the innovative design of autonomous intelligent systems for spacecraft, combined with the frontiers of aerospace engineering, artificial intelligence, control science and other disciplines, and trains students to design next-generation intelligent spacecraft systems with autonomous perception, decision-making, planning and execution capabilities. This course is designed from three levels: theoretical foundation, technical practice and innovative thinking, and on the basis of theory, students will master the basic architecture of spacecraft autonomous systems, core algorithms (such as state estimation, path planning, fault diagnosis) and the application principles of artificial intelligence technologies (machine learning, reinforcement learning, multi-agent collaboration). In terms of technical practice, through simulation platforms and project cases, students learn how to embed AI algorithms into spacecraft software and hardware systems to solve engineering challenges such as limited on-board resources and real-time requirements. In terms of innovative thinking, we will explore the design paradigm of autonomous systems in new mission scenarios (such as space manufacturing and autonomous mission planning of giant constellations), and cultivate interdisciplinary innovation capabilities. The core modules of this course include Spacecraft Autonomous System Architecture, Intelligent Perception and Environmental Understanding, Autonomous Decision-Making and Mission Planning, On-Orbit Learning and Adaptive Systems, as well as Cutting-Edge Case Studies and Innovative Design. The course emphasizes three distinctive features: project-driven learning, interdisciplinary integration, and industry-academia collaboration. The project-driven approach requires teams to complete full-process designs from algorithms to onboard code implementation. The interdisciplinary component incorporates knowledge from aerospace systems engineering, edge AI chips, cognitive science and other cross-cutting fields. The industry linkage involves inviting scholars deeply engaged in research missions to share practical engineering challenges. Upon completion, students will be able to design spacecraft autonomous system solutions tailored to specific mission requirements, develop lightweight AI algorithms and optimize their deployment on spacecraft computing units, and track international cutting-edge trends in the field. The teaching adopts a combination of theoretical teaching, simulation experiments (MATLAB/STK/Python), and industry case studies. Through this course, students will be at the forefront of the integration and innovation of aerospace and artificial intelligence, and lay the foundation for participating in the research and development of future intelligent spacecraft.'
