    en: 'With the development and maturity of digital technology, the automatic control system has been transferred from analog system to digital system. The ability of digital signal processing should be mastered by the undergraduate students. The course of Digital Signal Processing is designed for junior students of specialty of automation, detection, and guidance. This course is mainly about the basic theory and method of digital signal processing. The main contents of this course can be divided into two parts: the method of time-domain analysis and transform-domain analysis of discrete signal and system; and the designing method of discrete system. The time-domain analysis method mainly includes the analysis of typical sequences and their time-domain representation and operation; the time-domain representation and characteristics of systems; the solution of linear constant coefficient difference equation; the sampling and reconstruction theory of signal, etc. The transform domain analysis methods mainly include the discrete-time Fourier transform (DTFT), the discrete Fourier series (DFS), the discrete Fourier transform (DFT), and the fast Fourier transform algorithm (FFT); the design of the discrete system including the structure of digital filter; the designing methods of infinite impulse response (IIR) and finite impulse response (FIR) filter. In addition, the application of digital signal processing theory in the actual system will be introduced. The basic theory of digital signal processing involves many abstract mathematical derivations, in order to enable the students to have a more concrete understanding of theoretical knowledge, this course also introduces MWorks software to demonstrate graphics and animation, and the methods of simple digital signal processing using MWorks software.'
