At present, multi-body dynamics has formed a relatively systematic research method. Among them, there are mainly the methods of analytical mechanics represented by Lagrange''s equations commonly used in engineering, vectorial methods represented by Newton-Euler''s equations, graph theory methods, Kane''s methods and variational methods. However, at this stage, most of the courses only explain the theory and rely on formula derivation to complete the learning of relevant content, lacking practical aspects, students are difficult to grasp the abstract academic content in a short time. This course is for undergraduate third-year students, applicable to aerospace, mechanical, mechanics and other majors. This course explains the theoretical content and simulation practice technology of multi-body system dynamics and control, the course mainly teaches the introduction, double link robotic arm, single rigid body dynamics and control, computational multi-body system dynamics, floating base robot dynamics and control, fixed base robot dynamics and control, multi-body system dynamics simulation software (MBDyn) in seven parts. Compared with the traditional course lectures and setting programs, this course has explored and innovated in the following aspects: 1. The theoretical depth of the course is progressive. The theory of two-body dynamics is introduced through the simplest two-link mechanism, and the basic concepts of constraints, drive, and control are combined with a simple mechanism to explain. Subsequently, based on the fixed-base robotic arm and floating-base robotic arm system to explain and consolidate the multi-body system dynamics and control theory. The course content is from shallow to deep, from simple to complex, and builds a reasonable and clear theoretical system. 2. Combination of theory and practice. Each lesson is supplemented with accompanying cases, and the course cases adopt satellite, two-link, robotic arm and other institutions, which are typical and representative, and can integrate the theoretical structure explained in each lesson. Integration of theoretical content and actual cases, based on the actual system of abstract and complicated theory to explain, so that students can quickly master the theoretical knowledge and methods. 3. Adopt virtual prototype joint simulation technology. MBDyn, a multi-body system dynamics teaching software, has been developed to realize the verification of all the contents of this course. The software has completely independent intellectual property rights, and can realize the functions of multi-body system dynamics modeling and solving, control joint simulation, post-processing display and so on. Relying on this software, students can realize the classroom learning content by hands-on operation, and strengthen the mastery and understanding of knowledge points. This course is an offline teaching method to explain the theoretical knowledge, simulation cases and simulation methods. At the same time, detailed homework is set to help students consolidate what they have learned. Finally, the final examination is used to test the teachers'' teaching and students'' learning, and the content and score of the examination are set reasonably.'
