    en: 'Many important discoveries in the field of life science are closely related to the continuous innovation and development of cell biology experimental technology, so it is very necessary for workers who will engage in life science research to learn and master the most basic experimental technology of cell biology, such as observing cell structure with a microscope. It is of great research value and application prospect in the fields of life science, medicine, and agriculture to observe the structural characteristics of cells by microscopy and to study them qualitatively and quantitatively in combination with immune technology. In the fluorescence imaging experiment of cell structure, we observe live cells to gain an understanding of the morphological characteristics of cultured animal cells. Cells are stained with fluorescent markers, and the cell membrane and nucleus of animal cells are observed using a fluorescence microscope. Through this experiment, we aim to gain a preliminary understanding of the working principles and usage methods of fluorescence microscopy, as well as to master the application of immunofluorescence technology in the field of life sciences. Data in the field of life sciences has diverse source and complex in structure, including genomics data, proteomics data and clinical data. The data is vast in scale and dynamically evolving. Big data technologies can integrate and analyze these multi-sourced, heterogeneous data, deeply uncovering hidden patterns and making predictions for the future, providing critical support for scientific research. Mastering AI-based data mining methods has become a key skill for researchers in the life sciences. By combining lectures and hands-on practice, this course aims to help students gain a deep understanding of big data applications in life sciences and systematically learn the fundamental processes and techniques of data mining. Experiment 1: Understand artificial intelligence, big data technology, and their applications in the field of life sciences. Gain introductory experience with Python, and learn the association rule algorithm to grasp the basic data analysis workflow and replicate Python code. Experiment 2: Reinforce the basic data analysis workflow through an infectious disease prediction algorithm and implement Python code replication.'
