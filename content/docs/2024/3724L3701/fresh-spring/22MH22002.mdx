Among them, biosafety is a subject that studies the threat or potential impact of biological infectious agents on the ecological environment and human health, which is inseparable from agriculture and forestry, food, environment, medicine and other fields. Biosafety is not only a scientific problem, but also related to ecology, food and many other fields, affecting national economic development and social harmony and stability. With the development of society and science, human beings have gradually solved the problems of food, medicine, environmental protection and so on. But at the same time, it is also facing new challenges, such as various biological invasions, various infectious diseases, genetically modified organisms and food safety, which have attracted people's attention to these practical problems and potential risks. Medical ethics is an interdisciplinary subject with the closest connection between Humanities and natural science, the most profound and direct influence. It is the basis of policies, strategies and legislation for the development of life science and technology and health undertakings. It is practical, cross-cultural and global. This course aims to study the relationship and phenomenon of medical ethics in various times, reveal the origin, essence, function and development law of medical ethics, study the standards and methods of medical ethics evaluation, try to combine the new practical problems in the process of China's socialist medical system reform, try to explore various puzzling moral problems in modern medical life, and conduct various practical induction and deep-seated theoretical analysis, answer the ethical problems raised in modern medical practice and medical development.
