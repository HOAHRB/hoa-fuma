New energy materials are the key to achieving the transformation and utilization of new energy and developing new energy technologies. The development of the new energy industry cannot be separated from the development and application of new energy materials. This course aims to respond to national development strategies and focus on the development needs of strategic emerging industries. It aims to impart knowledge on clean production of new energy materials to students, in order to achieve energy conservation and emission reduction, and lay the foundation for scientific research, technological development, and practical production in the field of new energy conversion and utilization. It systematically introduces the basic theories and clean production technology development in the field of new energy material engineering. Emphasis is placed on cultivating students' comprehensive analysis and process design abilities in clean production of energy materials and new technologies for energy conservation and emission reduction, cultivating students' application ability in energy material science and engineering knowledge, increasing innovation awareness, and supporting the achievement of corresponding indicator points in professional learning outcomes.
