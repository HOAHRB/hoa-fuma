Based on this, it introduces the current research and development hotspots of solid oxide fuel cells, proton exchange membrane fuel cells, direct alcohol fuel cells, and alkaline fuel cells. Through the study of this course, students will master the basic theories related to chemistry, thermodynamics, and electrochemistry of fuel cells, which are electrochemical devices, and cultivate their initial ability to analyze and handle electrochemical problems; Enable students to understand the research and application frontiers of fuel cells, and cultivate their abilities to comprehensively apply knowledge such as electrochemistry, thermodynamics, and material sciences to solve practical problems; Enhance students' innovation awareness, personal communication skills, and group collaboration abilities through classroom discussions and other activities.
