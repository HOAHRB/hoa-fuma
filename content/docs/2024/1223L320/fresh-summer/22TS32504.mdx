    en: It focuses on the core principles and practical applications of intelligent driving technology. This course integrates knowledge from multiple disciplines such as vehicle dynamics, control theory, sensor technology, and computer simulation. The aim is to enable students to master the dynamic modeling, simulation analysis, and control strategy design of intelligent driving vehicles through theoretical lectures, experimental operations, and project practice. The course content starts from the basic concepts of intelligent driving systems, delves into the fundamental knowledge of vehicle kinematics and dynamics, introduces the working principles of various sensors and perception systems, and how to apply these technologies to the control and decision-making of intelligent driving vehicles. Students will learn how to construct dynamic models of intelligent driving vehicles and use professional simulation software for simulation and analysis to validate and optimize control algorithms.
