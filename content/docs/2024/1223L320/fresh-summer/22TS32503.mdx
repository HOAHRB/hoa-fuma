This course deeply discusses the core technology of auto drive system, with special emphasis on the application of synovial control and neural network control in automatic driving vehicles. Synovial control performs well in dealing with system uncertainties and external disturbances due to its strong robustness and adaptability. Neural network control utilizes its learning ability to model and control complex nonlinear systems. Through the study of this course, students will be able to understand the principles of these advanced control strategies, master their practical applications in autonomous vehicles, and lay a solid foundation for future research and work in the fields of intelligent transportation systems and autonomous driving.
