The victory of the socialist revolution in the relatively backward countries has opened up a new chapter in human history. How to understand the scientific nature of Marx and Engels' socialist theory and the achievements and twists of the International Communist movement? Especially the development and frustration of socialism in the Soviet Union and Eastern Europe. This course will discuss these problems. Through this course, students will learn and understand the history and reality of China's socialist construction, theory and practice comprehensively and systematically, so as to strengthen their confidence in the road, theory, system and culture of socialism with Chinese characteristics.
