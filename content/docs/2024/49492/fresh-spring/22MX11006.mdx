    en: Situation and Policy is a theory-based course centered up on ideology and politics taught at higher education institutes; it features high-level of theoretical timeliness, high accuracy and effectiveness in terms of doubt clearance, and high comprehensiveness in educational guidance. It is a core course that helps college students correctly understand domestic and overseas situations in the new era, equipping them with deep knowledge on the historical achievements, the historical changes, as well as the historical opportunities and challenges encountered during the pursuit of the Party’s and national causes since the 18th National Congress of the Communist Party of China. It is also an important approach to impart the Party’s latest theoretical innovation achievements to students and to guide college students to accurately comprehend the Party’s basic philosophy, basic development path, and basic strategy. The teaching content is arranged in accordance with the Main Points for the Teaching of Situation and Policy at Higher Education Institutes issued by the Ministry of Education each semester, closely revolving the learning and implementation of Xi Jinping’s thoughts on socialism with Chinese characteristics for the new era and in the principle of the “four traits of self-confidence”. The instructions are focused on the latest theoretical innovation achievements of the Party and the dynamic practices of carrying forward and developing socialism with Chinese characteristics in the new era, aiming to guide students towards the correct perception on the development situation of China and the world, Chinese characteristics and international comparison, the responsibility of the times and the historical mission, as well as the far-reaching aspirations and the implications of making earnest efforts. Special lectures on the situation and policy of comprehensively strengthening the Party’s discipline are given to explain the Party's political construction, ideological construction, organizational construction, style construction, and disciplinary construction, as well as elaborate the new measures and results of institutional construction running through the above processes.Special lectures on China's economic and social development situation and policy are given to impart the new decisions and new arrangements of the CPC Central Committee on economic construction, political construction, cultural construction, social construction, and ecological civilization construction. Special lectures on the situation and policy of Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan affairs are set up to update the new progress and situations on the compliance to the “one country and two systems” treaty and the advancement of national reunification. Special lectures on international situation and policy are given to elaborate the new concepts and contributions of China in following the path of peaceful development as well as promoting the building of a “community of shared future for mankind”. Furthermore, other targeted teaching contents are also arranged according to situational development requirements and student characteristics to timely analyze the popular issues that caught the most attention of the students.
