This course aims to help engineering students to grasp the basics about structural members and their strength, stiffness and stability. At the end of this course, students should be able to apply the equilibrium conditions, geometric relations and material constitutive relations to solve practical engineering problems involving stress and strain analysis in elementary structural members, such as bars and beams.
