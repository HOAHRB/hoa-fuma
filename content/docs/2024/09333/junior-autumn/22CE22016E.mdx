The main topics include an introduction to structural dynamic analysis, dynamic loads and dynamic degrees of freedom, formulation of equations of motion, free and forced vibration analysis of single-degree-of-freedom systems, dynamic response analysis of multi-degree-of-freedom systems, modal superposition method, damping models, practical calculation methods for natural frequencies and mode shapes, and step-by-step integration methods.
