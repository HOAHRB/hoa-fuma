    zh: 讲授预应力混凝土结构的基本概念和工作原理及适用范围。介绍预应力钢丝、预应力钢绞线、预应力FRP筋、预应力螺纹钢等预应力筋的基本力学性能。通过预应力筋的比例极限和条件屈服强度，阐述张拉控制应力及预应力筋材料分项系数的取值背景。通过对混凝土的徐变模型和预应力筋的松弛性能的分析，讲授预应力筋的松弛损失和收缩徐变损失的计算方法。通过对刮碰和摩擦物理内涵的阐释，讲授预应力筋摩擦损失和反摩擦损失的计算方法。通过弯矩和曲率关系，推导张拉预应力筋引起的等效荷载的计算公式。通过在等效荷载作用下变形受到荷载约束这一基本现象，介绍张拉预应力筋引起的次内力的基本概念。通过无约束预应力构件的受力和变形特性，讲解主内力的概念和计算方法。通过次内力与主内力之和等于总内力的基本原则，介绍次内力的通用计算方法。通过引入侧向约束影响系数和次内力，介绍预应力混凝土结构承载力计算的经典方法。通过讲授预应力筋的两阶段工作原理，阐述合理考虑侧向约束并实现两类极限状态设计计算相协调的预应力混凝土结构设计统一方法。介绍预应力在工程中应用的典型案例。
    en: This course teaches the basic concept, working principle and application scope of the prestressed concrete structures. The basic mechanical properties of prestressed tendons such as prestressed wires, prestressed steel strands, prestressed FRP bars and prestressed ribbed bars are introduced. Based on the proportional limit and offset yield strength of the prestressed tendons, the backgrounds on the evaluation of the tension control stress and materials partial factor of prestressed tendons are illustrated. Through the creep model of concrete and the relaxation characteristic of prestressed tendons, the calculation methods of relaxation loss, creep and shrinkage loss of prestressed tendons are taught. Through the interpretation of the physical connotation of scraping and friction, the calculation methods of friction loss and counter friction loss of prestressed tendons are taught. Based on the relationship between bending moment and curvature, the formula for calculating the equivalent load caused by tensioned prestressed tendons is derived. The basic concept of the secondary internal force caused by tensioned prestressed tendons is introduced. The concept and calculation method of the primary internal force are explained through the mechanical properties and deformation characteristics of the unrestrained prestressed members. According to the principle that the sum of the secondary internal force and the primary internal force is equal to the total internal force, the general calculation method of the secondary internal force is introduced. The classical method for calculating the bearing capacity of prestressed concrete structures is presented by introducing the influence coefficient of lateral constraint and the secondary internal force. By teaching the two-stage working principle of prestressed tendons, the unified design method of prestressed concrete structures is expounded with reasonable consideration of the lateral constraints and the coordination of the design and calculation at two limit states. The typical cases of prestress application in engineering are introduced.
