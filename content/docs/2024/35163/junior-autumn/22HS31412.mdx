The course uses “Readings in Chinese Literature for International Students” as a reference textbook to introduce Chinese literature and culture in an in-depth manner. Starting from Chinese idiom stories, the course gradually introduces classical Chinese poems, then analyzes classic modern and contemporary Chinese prose, and finally leads students into the world of modern and contemporary novels, so that they can read classic novels together, feel the charm of Chinese literature, and appreciate the elegance of Chinese culture.
