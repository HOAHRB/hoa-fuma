    zh: 政治经济学是以一定的社会生产关系为研究对象的经济学,即是对社会生产关系及其发展规律、生产和再生产中人和人的关系作为自己研究对象的学科。政治经济学不仅研究经济现象本身，还深入探讨经济现象背后的社会关系、阶级关系以及这些关系如何影响经济的发展和变化。它关注生产、分配、交换和消费等各个环节，分析这些环节中的矛盾和运动规律，从而揭示经济社会的本质和发展趋势。它的主要研究内容包括商品和货币、资本主义经济、社会主义经济、经济全球化和对外开放等内容。包括货币的流通量及其规律、市场经济和价值规律等诸多方面，体现了学科交叉的综合性，内容丰富，视野开阔。同时，为了突出现实性，着重对中国对外经济关系和国家经济安全等新现象、新变化和新问题做出了新的理论分析。重点是反映当代马克思主义政治经济学的新发展等内容。
    en: Political economy is an economics that focuses on certain social production relations as its research object. It is a discipline that studies social production relations and their development laws, as well as the relationships between people in production and reproduction. Political economy not only studies economic phenomena themselves, but also delves into the social and class relationships behind economic phenomena, as well as how these relationships affect economic development and change. It focuses on various links such as production, distribution, exchange, and consumption, analyzes the contradictions and laws of movement in these links, and thus reveals the essence and development trends of the economy and society. Its main research topics include commodities and currency, capitalist economy, socialist economy, economic globalization, and opening up to the outside world. Including the circulation of currency and its laws, market economy and value laws, and many other aspects, it reflects the interdisciplinary comprehensiveness, rich content, and broad vision. At the same time, in order to highlight practicality, new theoretical analyses have been made on new phenomena, changes, and issues such as China's foreign economic relations and national economic security. The focus is on reflecting the new developments in contemporary Marxist political economy.
