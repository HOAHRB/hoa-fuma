    en: The course aims to explore how countries can reallocate and optimize production resources through division of labor and cooperation in the context of globalization, in order to adapt to market changes and technological advancements. The course will introduce the concept, evolution, and main types of the global value chain, analyze the drivers of restructuring, such as technological progress, changes in trade policies, and shifts in market demand. Meanwhile, it will delve into the strategic options for restructuring, including vertical integration and regional integration, and highlight future development trends such as digitization, intelligence, and green transformation. Additionally, the course will focus on the impact of global value chain restructuring on the Chinese economy and corresponding strategies, such as strengthening technological innovation, optimizing industrial structure, and actively participating in international cooperation. Through this course, students will gain a comprehensive understanding of the current status and future of global value chain restructuring, providing theoretical support and practical guidance for addressing globalization challenges.
