    en: This course is a crucial part of the "Mathematics, Mechanics, and Structures" series of courses for undergraduate students majoring in Civil Engineering. It is also a required subject for the first stage of the national postgraduate entrance examination for most universities in China, covering the basic knowledge points of mechanics included in the prerequisite courses "Structural Mechanics" and "Structural Stability and Limit Analysis A."As the dynamic part of the prerequisite course "Structural Mechanics," this course mainly studies the internal force and deformation responses of engineering structures under typical time-varying dynamic loads such as earthquakes, strong winds, and ocean waves. It lays a foundation for further study of related professional courses, such as seismic，wind and impact design of engineering structures, and for future engineering design and scientific research. The course mainly includes an introduction to structural dynamic analysis, dynamic response analysis of single-degree-of-freedom systems, dynamic response analysis of multi-degree-of-freedom systems, and practical computational methods. These four parts are presented from shallow to deep. First, the difference between dynamic analysis and static analysis is introduced by defining and classifying dynamic loads. Then, the concept of dynamic degree of freedom is introduced to establish the equations of motion for structural systems. Using the two commonly used types of structural systems (single-degree-of-freedom and multi-degree-of-freedom) as examples, the solution of equations of motion and the characteristics of structural dynamic response are explained. The goal is to enable students to master the basic theory and analysis methods of structural dynamics. Finally, practical computational methods and discrete analysis are introduced to equip students with the ability to analyze and solve common engineering problems. Through this course, students should gain an in-depth understanding of the research objects and content of structural dynamics, systematically master the basic concepts, theories, and methods of structural dynamics, and be able to comprehensively apply the knowledge learned to solve common dynamic problems in engineering.
