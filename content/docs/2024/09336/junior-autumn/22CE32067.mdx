Many physical theories and sciences, such as atomic physics, solid state physics, nuclear physics, particle physics and other related disciplines, are based on it. Quantum mechanics is the established by study of the laws of motion of microscopic substances (such as atoms, molecules, electrons, etc.), which opened a new way for humans to understand the objective world. Quantum mechanics can provide a solid foundation for innovation and cross disciplinary research. This course focuses on introducing the basic concepts, principles, and methods of quantum mechanics. The course consists of six chapters. The first chapter is an introduction: the development history of quantum physics, the objects and characteristics of quantum mechanics research, and wave particle duality. The second chapter is about wave function and Schrodinger equation: statistical interpretation of wave function, state superposition principle, Schrodinger equation, conservation law of particle flow density and particle number, stationary Schrodinger equation, one-dimensional infinite potential well, linear harmonic oscillator, barrier penetration. The third chapter is about the mechanical quantities in quantum mechanics: operators representing mechanical quantities, momentum operators and angular momentum operator, hydrogen atoms, the orthogonality of Hermite operator eigenfunctions, the relationship between operators and mechanical quantities, the commutation relationship between operators. The fourth chapter is the representation of states and mechanical quantities. Chapter 5 is about Approximation methods (Perturbation theory and Variational principle). Chapter 6 is about spin and identical particles.'
