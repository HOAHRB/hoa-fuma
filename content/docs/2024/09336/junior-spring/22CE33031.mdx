    en: The intelligent construction and course design course mainly covers key topics such as steel structure design, reinforced concrete design, intelligent design and intelligent control of machinery. Its purpose is to deeply explore the core concepts, advanced technologies, and application methods in the field of intelligent construction. Intelligent design is a product of the integration between the construction industry and advanced technologies. By utilizing techniques such as artificial intelligence, modern graphic computing devices, and efficient optimization algorithms, it achieves automation and intelligence in structural design and optimization. Intelligent design provides more efficient, precise, and sustainable design solutions, fostering innovative development in the construction industry and offering architects and engineers higher levels of design and decision-making support. In the intelligent construction and course design course, the steel structure design primarily focuses on teaching the Steel roof truss structural system composition, Steel roof truss form and section design, internal force calculation methods, and component and node design methods for steel structures. This enables students to independently complete the design of steel roof truss structure projects. The reinforced concrete design mainly covers the structural layout and internal force calculation of integral one-way slab-ribbed beam floor systems, empowering students to independently accomplish the design of ordinary one-way slab-ribbed beam floor projects. The intelligent design primarily introduces cutting-edge principles and technologies of artificial intelligence, modern graphic computing devices, and explores programming in the Python language and writing programs for solid linear finite elements. This equips students with the ability to preliminarily implement intelligent design in structures. The intelligent control of machinery section mainly introduces the principles and practices of intelligent mechanical control, including intelligent control on platforms such as the ROS simulation system, quadruped bionic robots, wheeled robots, and handling robotic arms. Through this course design, students are expected to first acquire an understanding of steel structure design and reinforced concrete structure design methods, and become proficient in traditional structural design processes. Subsequently, they will be introduced to cutting-edge principles and technologies of artificial intelligence, modern graphic computing devices, efficient optimization algorithms, etc., mastering the Python language and developing general finite element and optimization programs, deep learning algorithms, etc., thus achieving preliminary structural intelligent design.
